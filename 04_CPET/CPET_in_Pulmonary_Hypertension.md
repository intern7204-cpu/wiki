# CPET in Pulmonary Hypertension

## One-Sentence Definition

CPET in pulmonary hypertension 是用 exercise gas exchange 與 cardiovascular response pattern，辨識 pulmonary vascular limitation、RV-arterial coupling failure、ventilatory inefficiency、risk 與 follow-up response 的評估框架。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 PH / PAH 的 CPET pattern。它不取代 [[CPET_in_Heart_Failure]]、right heart catheterization、PH guideline、或 PAH-specific treatment algorithm。

## 反對論點

「PH 的 CPET 就是 peak VO2 低；只要低 VO2 就代表 PH，正常 VO2 就排除 PH。」

## 反駁

Pezzuto & Agostoni 2023 的核心不是單看 peak VO2，而是整合 high VE/VCO2 slope、low PETCO2、increased VD/VT、abnormal P(a-ET)CO2 / A-a O2 gradient、reduced O2 pulse、flattened O2 pulse kinetics、reduced VO2/work slope、HR-dependent cardiac output response 與 clinical context。Peak VO2 也會受 HF、lung disease、deconditioning、muscle dysfunction、anemia 與 effort 影響。

## 結論

PH CPET 的價值在 pattern recognition 與 risk stratification；definitive diagnosis 仍需符合 guideline 的 clinical / echocardiographic / hemodynamic evaluation，特別是 RHC。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 解讀前要知道 PH subtype suspicion：PAH、SSc / CTD-PAH、CTEPH、PH-LHD、COPD-PH、IPF-PH 的 CPET meaning 不同。
- 若目標是 diagnostic support，CPET 必須與 echo probability、PFT、DLCO、V/Q imaging、CT、biomarkers 與 RHC decision pathway 合併。
- 若目標是 prognosis / follow-up，需固定 protocol 並確認測試品質與 expert interpretation。
- 若涉及 mPAP 20-25 mmHg 或 borderline PH，來源只提出 future application，不能當成已確立 treatment indication。

## Known Facts

- PAH pathophysiology 核心是 increased PVR / mPAP 造成 RV afterload mismatch。
- RV-arterial coupling 是 PAH symptoms、clinical status 與 prognosis 的主要 determinant。
- PAH effort dyspnea 的早期機制是 RV exercise reserve 不足，導致 CO 無法跟上 metabolic demand。
- PAH patients exercise 時可出現 VD/VT >30%；正常人 exercise 時 VD/VT 應下降。
- PAH CPET 可見 high VE/VCO2、high VE/VO2、low PETCO2、positive P(a-ET)CO2、higher P(A-a)O2、reduced peak VO2、low AT、reduced VO2/work slope、low peak HR、low O2 pulse。
- PAH O2 pulse 可呈 flattened slope，代表 stroke volume augmentation 受限，exercise CO increase 更依賴 HR。
- PAH 與 left HF 在同等 peak VO2 limitation 下可不同：PAH stroke volume response 較小，HR/VO2 slope 較陡。
- EOV 更常見於 post-capillary PH / HF，而非典型 PAH。
- High VE/VCO2 plus low PETCO2 raises likelihood of pulmonary vascular disease；both normal makes PH unlikely in the cited framework。
- 來源引用 ESC/ERS risk anchors：peak VO2 >15 mL/kg/min plus VE/VCO2 slope <36 = low risk；peak VO2 11-15 plus VE/VCO2 slope 36-44 = intermediate risk；peak VO2 <11 plus VE/VCO2 slope >44 = high risk。

## Mechanism

```text
pulmonary vascular disease
  -> elevated PVR and mPAP during exercise
  -> RV-arterial uncoupling
  -> stroke volume reserve limited
  -> CO increase becomes HR-dependent
  -> tissue O2 delivery insufficient
  -> earlier lactate / acid-base stress
  -> increased ventilatory drive and dead space fraction
  -> high VE/VCO2 slope and low PETCO2
  -> reduced peak VO2, low O2 pulse and abnormal gas exchange pattern
```

## Observable Patterns

| Pattern | Interpretation from this source |
|---|---|
| High VE/VCO2 slope | Ventilatory inefficiency; PH severity / prognosis marker |
| Low PETCO2 | V/Q mismatch, dead space and hyperventilation pattern; useful with VE/VCO2 |
| VD/VT >30% during exercise | Abnormal dead space response; supports pulmonary vascular limitation |
| Low O2 pulse / flattened O2 pulse | Limited stroke volume augmentation / RV reserve concern |
| Low VO2/work slope | Oxygen delivery / utilization limitation during incremental work |
| EOV | More suggestive of post-capillary PH / HF than typical PAH |
| Abrupt PETO2 rise + PETCO2 fall + RER and VE equivalents rise + SpO2 drop | Possible exercise-induced right-to-left shunt pattern |

## Clinical / Research Implication

- In symptomatic patients with intermediate echo probability of PH, CPET can refine likelihood and help decide further testing。
- In SSc / CTD, CPET may support RHC triage when combined with algorithms such as DETECT。
- After pulmonary embolism with persistent symptoms, high VD/VT, A-a gradient, VE/VCO2 abnormalities and PETCO2 abnormalities should raise CTEPH / CTED concern。
- In PAH follow-up, peak VO2 and VE/VCO2 slope should be read with RV / hemodynamic data rather than separately。
- Clinical trials should only use CPET endpoints when protocol execution and interpretation are standardized。

## Fact

- 本頁目前直接依據 Pezzuto & Agostoni 2023 單一 review。
- The review cites 2022 ESC/ERS PH guidelines as emphasizing CPET in diagnostic phase and follow-up。
- The review does not claim CPET replaces RHC。

## Inference

- CPET is best used as a probability-shifting and mechanism-localizing test in PH, not as an isolated diagnostic label。
- PH CPET interpretation should start from VE/VCO2 / PETCO2 / VD/VT pattern, then integrate peak VO2, O2 pulse and HR response。

## Assumption

- VE/VCO2 / PETCO2 pattern is interpreted only after adequate test quality and clinical context are confirmed。
- The source’s guideline-derived risk cutoffs should be cross-checked with the actual ESC/ERS guideline before formal clinical protocol writing。

## Uncertainty

- mPAP 20-25 mmHg treatment strategy remains unresolved in this review。
- PH subtype-specific cutoffs are not interchangeable。
- Sex-specific prognostic CPET markers remain based on limited data。
- CPET in PAH clinical trials needs standardization; prior inconsistent findings may reflect site expertise and method differences。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 high VE/VCO2 slope 單獨診斷為 PAH。
- 不可把 normal peak VO2 單獨用來排除 PH。
- 不可把 CPET 取代 RHC。
- 不可把 PAH、PH-LHD、CTEPH、COPD-PH、IPF-PH 的 CPET pattern 混為同一疾病。
- 不可把 borderline PH 的 CPET abnormality 直接寫成 PAH-specific drug indication。
