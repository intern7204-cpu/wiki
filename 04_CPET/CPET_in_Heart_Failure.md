# CPET in Heart Failure

## One-Sentence Definition

CPET in heart failure 是用 exercise gas exchange 量化 HF 的 cardiac reserve、ventilatory efficiency、exercise limitation pattern、prognosis 與 rehabilitation response 的動態評估框架。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 heart failure 中 CPET 的 interpretation framework。它不取代 HF guideline、advanced HF / transplant listing criteria、invasive hemodynamic testing、或完整 cardiac rehabilitation prescription。

## 反對論點

「HF 的 CPET 主要看 peak VO2；低於某個 cut-off 就能直接決定 prognosis 或 advanced therapy。」

## 反駁

Juarez et al. 2024 整理的 HF CPET 不只包含 peak VO2，還包含 VE/VCO2 slope、RER、VO2 at AT、PETCO2、exercise oscillatory ventilation、O2 pulse、HR / BP response、gas exchange、comorbidity contribution 與 effort validity。Peak VO2 的 cutoffs 也受 beta blocker use、age、BMI、predicted value、effort quality 與 HF phenotype 影響。

## 結論

HF CPET 應作為 pattern-based risk stratification 與 limitation localization，不應被簡化為單一數字決策。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 測試前需確認 indication、contraindication、medication context、ECG / BP safety、symptom-limited endpoint 與 expected exercise tolerance。
- Protocol 應讓 exercise time 大致落在 8-12 min，或至少 6-12 min 的臨床實務範圍；ramp slope 應依預期能力調整。
- Interpretation 必須先檢查 effort quality；RER >1.0-1.1 支持 maximal physiologic effort，但 HR >85% predicted 在 beta blockers 或 chronotropic incompetence 下不可靠。
- 若未達 maximal effort，peak VO2 的 prognostic interpretation 要降級，改看較不依賴最大努力的 OUES、VO2 at AT、VE/VCO2 slope 與整體 pattern。

## Known Facts

- HF patients 常達不到 true VO2max，報告上更常用 peak VO2。
- Peak VO2 in HF 與 peak cardiac output、muscle perfusion 與 systemic oxygen delivery / extraction 有關。
- VE/VCO2 slope 反映 ventilatory inefficiency，並可受 dead space ventilation、lung compliance、chemo / metabolic reflex sensitivity、early metabolic acidosis 與 abnormal pulmonary hemodynamics 影響。
- VE/VCO2 slope >34-36 可辨識 high-risk HF patients；>=45 屬 high / very poor risk pattern。
- Peak VO2 <14 mL/kg/min 是 poor prognosis marker；beta blocker patients 可使用 <12 mL/kg/min 作較低 threshold。
- Peak VO2 <=10 mL/kg/min、VE/VCO2 slope >=36、EOV presence、VO2 at AT <11 mL/kg/min 形成 very poor prognosis pattern。
- Excellent prognosis pattern 包含 peak VO2 >=20 mL/kg/min、VE/VCO2 slope <30、absence of EOV、VO2 at AT >11 mL/kg/min。
- HFpEF / HFmrEF / HFrEF 都可由 peak VO2 與 VE/VCO2 slope 提供 outcome information，但 CPET 不能穩定單獨分類 HF phenotype。
- HFpEF dyspnea 常有多重原因，包含 chronotropic reserve impairment、stroke volume reserve impairment、cardiac output reserve impairment、exercise pulmonary hypertension、impaired peripheral O2 extraction 與 pulmonary disorders。

## Mechanism

```text
HF pathophysiology
  -> limited stroke volume / cardiac output reserve or high filling pressure
  -> lower oxygen delivery to exercising muscle
  -> earlier metabolic acidosis and lower VO2 at AT
  -> increased ventilatory drive and inefficient CO2 clearance
  -> higher VE/VCO2 slope, lower exercise tolerance and possible EOV
  -> prognostic signal and clue to limiting system
```

## Observable Patterns

| Pattern | Interpretation from this source |
|---|---|
| Low peak VO2 | Reduced exercise capacity; prognosis marker after effort check |
| High VE/VCO2 slope | Ventilatory inefficiency; independent prognostic signal |
| Low VO2 at AT | Earlier transition to non-steady metabolic stress; poor prognosis when markedly low |
| EOV | Advanced disease severity / poor prognosis pattern |
| Low O2 pulse or flattened O2 pulse kinetics | Possible stroke volume limitation, but must be interpreted with HR response and comorbidity |
| Low PETCO2 | Possible V/Q mismatch or hyperventilation pattern |

## Clinical / Research Implication

- CPET is useful when HF symptoms, dyspnea, exercise intolerance, functional capacity or advanced therapy timing need objective physiologic clarification。
- In HFpEF, CPET should trigger broader differential thinking rather than forcing dyspnea into a single cardiac category。
- In cardiac rehabilitation, peak VO2、VO2 at AT、VE/VCO2 slope、BNP / NT-proBNP and functional tasks can be used as complementary response markers。
- In drug trials, CPET can quantify physiologic response, but this review does not support choosing individual HF medications solely by CPET changes。

## Fact

- 本頁目前直接依據 Juarez et al. 2024 單一 narrative review。
- The review states CPET provides diagnostic and prognostic information in HF, but requires specialized equipment, trained staff and expert interpretation。
- The review states portable metabolimeters and smart watches may support monitoring and daily-activity interpretation, but it does not establish them as CPET replacements。

## Inference

- HF CPET interpretation should be organized as: effort validity -> dominant limitation -> risk markers -> comorbidity search -> management implication。
- In advanced HF, ADL dyspnea may occur because daily tasks consume a high fraction of the patient’s peak VO2 reserve。

## Assumption

- Prognostic thresholds from this review are applied only as review-level anchors and require confirmation against current guideline and local advanced HF policy before high-stakes decisions。
- When CPET suggests pulmonary, muscular or peripheral extraction limitation, additional testing is needed before assigning causality。

## Uncertainty

- The source is not a guideline and contains some OCR-corrupted numeric passages, especially around HFpEF and BNP sections。
- HFpEF definitions are heterogeneous, making CPET findings and outcome estimates sensitive to classification criteria。
- miRNA monitoring in cardiac rehabilitation remains research-facing and is not routine clinical practice。
- Wearable-derived data require validation, workflow design and clinical governance before being used as formal treatment endpoints。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 peak VO2 cut-off 當作單獨 transplant listing rule。
- 不可把 VE/VCO2 slope 當成單一 pulmonary diagnosis。
- 不可把 low AT 直接等同 myocardial ischemia；lung disease、anemia、myopathy、deconditioning 也會影響。
- 不可把 smart watch 或 portable metabolimetry 當作 CPET 替代。
- 不可用 CPET 單獨分類 HFrEF vs HFpEF。

## 與 Pulmonary Hypertension 的邊界

- HF / post-capillary PH 可有 elevated VE/VCO2 slope 與 EOV；PAH 則更典型呈現 high VE/VCO2、very low PETCO2、increased VD/VT、limited stroke volume response 與 steeper HR/VO2 slope。
- Pezzuto & Agostoni 2023 指出 EOV 更常見於 post-capillary PH / HF，而非典型 PAH。
- 因此 HF CPET 若出現強烈 pulmonary vascular pattern，應連到 [[CPET_in_Pulmonary_Hypertension]]，而不是只用 HF 解釋。
