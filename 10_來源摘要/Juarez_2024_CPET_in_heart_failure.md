## One-Sentence Summary

Juarez et al. 2024 將 CPET 定位為 heart failure 中整合 exercise capacity、ventilatory efficiency、effort validity、comorbidity detection、risk stratification、cardiac rehabilitation response 與日常活動負荷評估的動態工具。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: CPET in Heart Failure

#### One-Sentence Definition

CPET in heart failure 是用 ramp / incremental exercise gas exchange 同時評估 cardiac output limitation、ventilatory inefficiency、gas exchange abnormality、peripheral / neuromuscular contribution 與 prognosis 的動態檢查框架。

#### Known Facts

- HF 是 heart 不能以符合 metabolic requirements 的速率供血，或只能靠 high filling pressures 達成供血的臨床症候群。
- CPET 可量測 exercise 中 respiratory gases、heart rate、blood pressure、oxygen uptake、carbon dioxide output、ventilation 與 gas exchange。
- CPET report 可分為 metabolics、cardiac、ventilation、gas exchange 四組資訊。
- Incremental / ramp protocols 常用於臨床 CPET；ramp grades 可包括 5、7、10、15 W/min，應依 expected exercise tolerance 選擇。
- 來源建議 ramp test duration 約 8-12 min；另提到 exercise time 6-12 min 作為取得有效 metabolic / functional information 的實務範圍。
- Cycle ergometer 的 workload linearity 較 treadmill 好；treadmill 改變 speed / slope 時較難取得線性 workload。
- HF patients 可有 cardiovascular、respiratory、muscular、sympathetic、neurohormonal system changes。
- CPET 不能穩定區分 HFrEF 與 HFpEF；若按 clinical characteristics 配對，兩者 CPET variables 可同樣 abnormal。
- Reduced VO2 可預測 HFrEF 與 HFpEF outcomes。
- Mild HF patients 的 resting cardiac output 可能正常，但 exercise 時不能充分增加 cardiac output。
- Peak VO2 in HF 與 peak cardiac output、exercising muscle perfusion 相關；cardiac output 不能上升會使 anaerobic metabolism 較早發生並造成 muscle fatigue。
- HF patients 常達不到 true VO2max，因此臨床上常報 peak VO2。
- VE/VCO2 slope 在 HF 中反映 ventilatory inefficiency，與 dead space ventilation、lung compliance、chemo / metabolic reflex sensitivity、early metabolic acidosis、abnormal pulmonary hemodynamics 相關。
- VE/VCO2 slope <30 被來源描述為正常；>34-36 可辨識 high-risk HF patients；>=45 被描述為 very poor prognosis / high risk。
- Ventilatory classification in HF：VC-I <30、VC-II 30-35.9、VC-III 36-44.9、VC-IV >=45。
- Peak VO2 <14 mL/kg/min 被來源列為 poor prognosis marker；在 beta blocker patients，cutoff 可降至 <12 mL/kg/min。
- Peak VO2 <=10 mL/kg/min 與最差 prognosis 相關；% predicted peak VO2 <50% 也提示 poor prognosis。
- RER >1.0-1.1 可作為 maximal physiological effort 的判斷線索，但 beta blockers / chronotropic incompetence 會影響 HR-based effort assessment。
- Excellent prognosis pattern 包含 peak VO2 >=20 mL/kg/min、VE/VCO2 slope <30、無 exercise oscillatory ventilation、VO2 at AT >11 mL/kg/min。
- Poor prognosis pattern 包含 peak VO2 <10 mL/kg/min、VE/VCO2 slope >=36、exercise oscillatory ventilation、VO2 at AT <11 mL/kg/min。
- 若未達 maximal effort，oxygen uptake efficiency slope <1.4 與 VO2 at AT <9 mL/kg/min 可提示 poor prognosis。
- HFpEF patients 常有 comorbidities；dedicated dyspnea clinic data 顯示 chronotropic reserve、stroke volume reserve、cardiac output reserve、exercise pulmonary hypertension、peripheral O2 extraction 與 pulmonary disorders 都可能貢獻 dyspnea。
- Exercise training in HF 可與 BNP / NT-proBNP 下降及 peak VO2 上升相關；一個 patient-level meta-analysis 中 exercise training reduced BNP by 28.3%、NT-proBNP by 37%、peak VO2 increased by 17.8%。
- CPET 需要 specialized lab、trained personnel 與 expert interpretation；availability 與 cost 是限制。
- Portable metabolimeters 可在 daily activities 中量測 oxygen uptake；advanced HF patients 某些 ADL 的 VO2 需求可接近其 peak VO2。
- Smart watches 可提供 heart rate、arrhythmia、blood pressure、oxygen saturation、sleep apnea related data，但屬於 monitoring / screening adjunct，不是 CPET 替代品。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
heart failure
  -> limited cardiac output reserve and/or high filling pressures
  -> reduced exercising muscle perfusion and early metabolic acidosis
  -> lower peak VO2 and lower VO2 at AT
  -> ventilatory inefficiency from V/Q mismatch, dead space, reflex drive and pulmonary hemodynamics
  -> elevated VE/VCO2 slope, abnormal PETCO2, possible exercise oscillatory ventilation
  -> risk stratification and comorbidity localization
  -> treatment planning, cardiac rehabilitation monitoring and advanced therapy discussion
```

#### Inferences

- CPET in HF should be interpreted as a pattern-based dynamic physiologic assessment, not as a single-number transplant trigger。
- HFpEF dyspnea should not be assumed to be purely cardiac; CPET may reveal pulmonary, peripheral O2 extraction, chronotropic, stroke volume or neuromuscular contributions。
- Daily activity intolerance in advanced HF can be mechanistically explained when ADL oxygen cost approaches the patient’s peak VO2 reserve。

#### Assumptions

- Risk cutoffs from this review are best used as screening / stratification anchors and should be calibrated with current HF guideline, transplant center policy, patient medication status and comorbidity profile。
- RER-based effort assessment is necessary before interpreting peak VO2, but it is not the only validity criterion。
- Portable metabolimetry and wearable data require validated workflows before being used for high-stakes clinical decisions。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- CPET does not consistently classify HFrEF vs HFpEF by itself。
- HFpEF definitions are heterogeneous and can change CPET results and outcome estimates。
- Drug-specific CPET changes are not sufficient to choose medications for an individual patient；the review itself states exercise testing is impractical for individual drug choice。
- miRNA biomarkers remain research-facing and should not be treated as routine rehabilitation monitoring。
- The raw Markdown has local OCR corruption in some numeric passages；those corrupted values were excluded from this summary。

## Clinically Useful Points

- In HF, report peak VO2 rather than casually calling it true VO2max unless maximal ceiling is demonstrated。
- Interpret peak VO2 only after checking effort quality, especially RER；HR criteria are weaker in beta blocker use or chronotropic incompetence。
- VE/VCO2 slope adds prognostic value beyond peak VO2 and is especially useful when effort-limited peak VO2 is uncertain。
- EOV, low VO2 at AT, low OUES, high VE/VCO2 slope and low peak VO2 should be read together as a risk pattern。
- CPET may identify non-cardiac contributors to dyspnea, especially pulmonary disease, anemia, myopathy, deconditioning, sleep apnea or skeletal muscle wasting。

## Research-Useful Points

- CPET can serve as an outcome measure in HF drug trials and cardiac rehabilitation studies。
- BNP / NT-proBNP and selected molecular markers may help monitor rehabilitation response, but they should be treated as adjuncts rather than substitutes for exercise capacity and clinical outcomes。
- Portable CPET / metabolimetry can connect laboratory peak VO2 with ADL oxygen cost, which may improve symptom-specific rehabilitation design。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「CPET 只要看 peak VO2」衝突：來源強調 VE/VCO2 slope、AT、RER、PETCO2、EOV、O2 pulse、HR response 與 comorbidity context。
- 與「CPET 可以直接分出 HFrEF vs HFpEF」衝突：來源指出 CPET 不穩定完成此分類。
- 與「HF dyspnea 一定主要來自 cardiac impairment」衝突：來源指出 pulmonary disease、muscle disease、anemia、sleep apnea、skeletal muscle weakness / wasting 都可貢獻。
- 與「wearables 可取代 CPET」衝突：來源把 smart watches 放在 daily monitoring 與 problem detection，而非 specialized CPET 替代。
