# Source Summary: Severinsen & Pedersen 2020 — Muscle-Organ Crosstalk: The Emerging Roles of Myokines

- Review article / high-quality narrative review.
- Citation: Severinsen MCK, Pedersen BK. Endocrine Reviews. 2020;41:594-609.

## Reliability Level

- 來源層級 4：high-quality narrative / expert review。
- 優點：Endocrine Reviews；作者群長期研究 muscle-derived IL-6 與 exercise immunometabolism；適合作為 myokine field map。
- 限制：非 systematic review；不同 myokines 的證據層級差異很大，從 human infusion / RCT 到 rodent / cell model 都有。
- 本文可支撐「skeletal muscle is a secretory organ」與「IL-6 是 human-supported myokine 主軸」；不可把所有列名 myokines 當作已驗證 clinical biomarkers。

## One-Sentence Summary

這篇 review 將 skeletal muscle 定位為可釋放 myokines 的 secretory organ，說明 exercise-induced muscle-organ crosstalk 可連到 brain、adipose tissue、liver、gut、pancreas、vascular bed、skin、immune system 與 cancer biology；但來源同時承認只有少數 myokines 已在 humans 被分配明確功能，其中 IL-6 是最強主軸。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Myokines and Muscle-Organ Crosstalk

#### One-Sentence Definition

Myokines 是由 muscle fibers 產生、表現並釋放，具有 autocrine、paracrine 或 endocrine effects 的 cytokines / peptides。

#### Known Facts

- 作者採用 2003 年定義：myokines are produced, expressed and released by muscle fibers and exert autocrine / paracrine / endocrine effects。
- 文中指出 skeletal muscle 可分泌 hundreds of peptides，myokinome 已識別超過 650 myokines。
- 但 biological function has been described for only about 5% of known myokines。
- Myokines 可媒介 muscle-muscle、muscle-brain、muscle-adipose、muscle-bone、muscle-liver、muscle-gut、muscle-pancreas、muscle-vascular bed、muscle-skin、muscle-immune inflammation 與 muscle-cancer crosstalk。
- Exerkines 是更廣義名詞，包含 exercise-induced peptides / nucleic acids from muscle and other organs，且可透過 extracellular vesicles / exosomes 搭載。

#### Mechanism Chain

Exercise / muscle contraction -> muscle fiber transcription / translation / secretion changes -> release of myokines -> local muscle remodeling or systemic organ signaling -> metabolic, inflammatory, structural, neural or tumor-related downstream effects。

#### Inferences

- Myokines 提供 exercise as medicine 的 mechanistic language，但不等於已可開立單一 molecule prescription。
- Muscle-organ crosstalk 應視為 systems response，而不是單一肌肉內 pathway。

#### Assumptions

- 將 secretome / circulating molecule 視為 myokine 時，需假設其來源真的是 muscle fiber，而不是 immune cell、adipose tissue、liver 或 extracellular vesicle cargo from other organs。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- 大多數候選 myokines 尚未有充分 human causal evidence。
- Acute exercise response、chronic training adaptation、baseline disease inflammation 不可混成同一訊號。

### Concept: Exercise-Induced IL-6 as the Best-Supported Human Myokine

#### One-Sentence Definition

Exercise-induced IL-6 是目前本文中 human evidence 最強的 myokine，具 metabolic、anti-inflammatory、visceral fat regulation、gut / liver / immune signaling 等作用，但不能等同慢性疾病中的 basal IL-6 elevation。

#### Known Facts

- 2000 年發現 human contracting skeletal muscle 可產生並釋放 IL-6 into circulation，足以解釋 exercise-induced plasma IL-6 increase。
- Physical inactivity is associated with high circulating basal IL-6 in humans。
- Acute exercise-induced systemic IL-6 and muscle IL-6 mRNA response are diminished after training；trained human muscle has elevated IL-6 receptor expression，suggesting increased muscular sensitivity。
- Recombinant human IL-6 infusion in healthy humans during hyperinsulinemic euglycemic clamp improved peripheral insulin-stimulated glucose uptake。
- Human studies show physiological IL-6 can increase lipolysis / fat oxidation and delay gastric emptying with postprandial glucose effects。
- IL-6 infusion stimulates IL-1ra / IL-10 and inhibits endotoxin-induced TNF production in humans。
- Abdominally obese humans randomized to tocilizumab vs placebo during 12-week aerobic exercise showed exercise-induced visceral fat loss was abolished by IL-6 receptor blockade。
- Corrigendum corrects figures: IL-6 inhibits appetite, not stimulates appetite.

#### Mechanism Chain

Muscle contraction -> IL-6 production / release -> IL-6 receptor signaling in muscle and other tissues -> AMPK-related glucose uptake and fat oxidation; IL-1ra / IL-10 increase and TNF suppression; gut GLP-1 / gastric emptying effects; visceral / cardiac fat response to training。

#### Inferences

- Transient exercise-induced IL-6 should be interpreted as a context-dependent signal, not simply as inflammatory harm。
- IL-6 provides the most defensible example when teaching myokines in humans.

#### Assumptions

- The IL-6 effects cited across infusion, blockade and exercise studies are sufficiently comparable to support a shared exercise-related IL-6 signaling framework.

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- IL-6 can have different actions depending on source, timing, receptor context and basal inflammatory state。
- IL-6 receptor blockade data support necessity for visceral fat loss in that cohort, but do not prove IL-6 alone is sufficient for all exercise-induced body composition changes。

### Concept: Organ-Axis Claims Require Evidence Stratification

#### One-Sentence Definition

The review lists many muscle-organ axes, but each axis has different levels of human support and should not be treated as equally established.

#### Known Facts

- Muscle-brain: BDNF is released from brain during bicycle exercise; muscle-derived BDNF has not been shown to enter blood and mediate direct muscle-brain signaling. Cathepsin B and irisin are proposed candidates; irisin increase in human plasma with exercise remains controversial.
- Muscle-adipose: IL-6 has strong human support for lipolysis / fat oxidation; irisin and Metrnl browning evidence is mostly rodent / debated in humans.
- Muscle-liver: human exercise studies suggest muscle-derived IL-6 contributes to endogenous glucose production during exercise.
- Muscle-gut / beta-cell: IL-6 can stimulate GLP-1 secretion and delay gastric emptying; conditioned media from human muscle cultures suggest beta-cell effects may depend on muscle insulin-resistant state.
- Muscle-skin: IL-15 / AMPK pathway and skin aging evidence is based on mice plus human endurance exercise observations.
- Muscle-cancer: epidemiology supports physical activity and lower cancer risk in multiple cancers; mechanistic IL-6 / NK cell tumor redistribution data are preclinical mouse data.

#### Mechanism Chain

Each proposed axis requires: muscle source verification -> exercise responsiveness -> circulation / local delivery -> receptor / target tissue action -> causal physiological outcome in humans。

#### Inferences

- A useful wiki page should rank myokines by evidence maturity rather than list them as equivalent facts.

#### Assumptions

- Candidate myokines from rodent and cell models may identify mechanisms, but do not automatically define human exercise adaptation.

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- The review explicitly supports biomarker / therapeutic target potential, but clinical deployment remains speculative for most myokines.

## Clinically Useful Points

- Myokines help explain why exercise affects metabolism, inflammation, body composition and organ function beyond calorie expenditure.
- IL-6 is useful for patient education only if framed as transient exercise-induced signaling, not chronic inflammatory elevation.
- Myokine testing is not routine clinical care; current use is mainly mechanistic explanation / research stratification.
- Claims such as irisin browning, cathepsin B cognition, musclin anti-cachexia or FSTL1 vascular repair need evidence qualifiers before clinical use.

## Research-Useful Points

- Study design must verify tissue source, exercise dose, sampling timing, acute vs chronic response, disease state and assay specificity.
- Exerkine / myokine research should separate muscle-derived factors from hepatokines, adipokines, batokines, cardiokines and extracellular vesicle cargo.
- IL-6 is the best anchor for human causal work; candidate myokines beyond IL-6 need stronger human mediation studies.

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「IL-6 = purely harmful inflammatory cytokine」衝突：本文區分 exercise-induced transient IL-6 與 chronic basal elevation。
- 與「exercise benefit = calories burned」衝突：本文支持 endocrine / paracrine / autocrine signaling layer。
- 與「myokine = exerkine」混用衝突：myokines 是 muscle-derived subset，exerkines 是多器官 exercise-induced factors。
- 與「irisin 已確定是 human exercise browning mediator」衝突：本文明確標示 human plasma irisin response 仍有爭議。
