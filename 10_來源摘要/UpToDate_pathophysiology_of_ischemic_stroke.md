## One-Sentence Summary

Ischemic stroke 的病理生理是 blood flow reduction 超過 autoregulation 與 collateral compensation 後，從 oxygen / glucose delivery failure 進入 ATP depletion、ion pump failure、glutamate excitotoxicity、calcium influx、oxidative / nitric oxide injury、inflammation、necrosis / apoptosis、BBB disruption and edema 的連續過程。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Cerebral Autoregulation 與灌流閾值

#### One-Sentence Definition

Cerebral autoregulation 是腦血管透過改變 vascular resistance 在一定 perfusion pressure 範圍內維持 cerebral blood flow 的能力；ischemic stroke 時這個能力受損，使 perfusion pressure 與 systemic BP 對 ischemic tissue 更重要。

#### Known Facts

- Cerebral blood flow 由 cerebral vascular resistance 與 cerebral perfusion pressure 共同決定。
- Autoregulation 通常在 mean arterial pressure 60-150 mmHg 範圍內維持相對穩定的 cerebral blood flow，但上下限有個體差異。
- Ischemic stroke 可 impaired cerebral autoregulation；chronic hypertension 可把 autoregulation shifted to higher arterial pressures。
- Source CBF thresholds: <50 mL/100 g/min protein synthesis inhibition；35 protein synthesis ceases；25 anaerobic glycolysis / acidosis；16-18 neuronal electrical failure；10-12 membrane ion homeostasis failure / typical infarct threshold。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
arterial occlusion, severe stenosis or systemic hypoperfusion
-> cerebral perfusion pressure falls
-> vasodilation and oxygen extraction increase
-> autoregulatory reserve fails
-> metabolic, electrical and membrane thresholds fail
```

#### Inferences / Assumptions / Uncertainties

- Inference: acute BP strategy should not simply normalize BP without considering perfusion-dependent tissue。
- Assumption: thresholds are physiologic guideposts, not bedside treatment cutoffs。
- Uncertainty: individual autoregulatory limits and optimal acute BP target are not resolved by this source。

### Concept: Ischemic Core 與 Penumbra

#### One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic core 是已死亡或注定死亡的 central infarct tissue；penumbra 是周邊仍有 partial oxygen / glucose supply、若及時恢復 blood flow 可能存活的組織。

#### Known Facts

- Human brain receives about 20 percent of cardiac output while being about 2 percent of body weight and has little or no energy stores。
- Most strokes are focal ischemia involving one vessel and downstream branches。
- Core tissue becomes irreversibly damaged and dies by necrosis if ischemia lasts long enough。
- Penumbra is potentially salvageable tissue that may receive partial oxygen / glucose through collateral vessels。
- ATP may fall to about 25 percent of normal in focal ischemic core models and about 50-70 percent in penumbra。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
focal arterial flow reduction
-> spatial perfusion gradient
-> severe ATP depletion in core
-> necrosis-dominant infarct
-> partial collateral support in surrounding tissue
-> time-limited penumbra
```

#### Inferences / Assumptions / Uncertainties

- Inference: "Time is brain" reflects progressive conversion of penumbra into core, modified by collateral flow。
- Assumption: focal arterial ischemic stroke model。
- Uncertainty: this source does not define CT perfusion or MRI mismatch thresholds。

### Concept: Ischemic Cell Injury Cascade

#### One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic cell injury cascade 是 oxygen / glucose deprivation 引發 ATP depletion、ion gradient failure、glutamate excitotoxicity、calcium influx、oxidative / nitric oxide injury、mitochondrial failure、inflammation and cell death 的連鎖反應。

#### Known Facts

- Brain ischemia initiates ATP depletion, sodium / potassium / calcium changes, lactate increase, acidosis, oxygen free radicals, intracellular water accumulation and proteolytic activation。
- Electrical failure increases synaptic glutamate release。
- NMDA receptors are the primary glutamate receptor subtype involved in ischemic damage; AMPA and metabotropic receptors may also contribute。
- Sodium influx draws water into cells and reverses astrocyte glutamate transport, amplifying excitotoxicity。
- Nitric oxide and reactive oxygen species may damage cellular components, mitochondria and DNA。
- Cell death can occur by necrosis or apoptosis；core low ATP favors necrosis, while penumbra can support apoptosis until energy depletion progresses。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
oxygen / glucose deprivation
-> ATP depletion
-> ion pump failure and depolarization
-> glutamate release plus impaired uptake
-> NMDA / AMPA activation
-> calcium and sodium influx
-> mitochondrial failure, NO / ROS injury, acidosis and edema
-> necrosis or apoptosis depending on energy state and duration
```

#### Inferences / Assumptions / Uncertainties

- Inference: neuroprotection has been difficult because ischemic injury is a network cascade rather than a single target。
- Assumption: mechanisms combine human pathology, animal model and cell model evidence summarized by the source。
- Uncertainty: human poststroke apoptosis evidence is limited and stage-dependent。

### Concept: Cerebral Edema and Hemorrhagic Conversion

#### One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic cerebral edema and hemorrhagic conversion arise when ischemia disrupts cellular ion transport, endothelial barrier function and extracellular matrix integrity, causing cytotoxic / ionic / vasogenic edema and, in severe vascular failure, blood-product extravasation into infarcted tissue。

#### Known Facts

- Ischemia and infarction lead to loss of structural integrity in brain tissue and blood vessels。
- Matrix metalloproteases can degrade basal lamina collagen and laminin。
- Loss of vascular integrity breaks down the BBB and contributes to cerebral edema。
- Severe vascular integrity failure is postulated to cause hemorrhagic conversion。
- Edema can increase ICP, reduce CBF, create mass effect and cause herniation。
- Cytotoxic edema results from ATP-dependent sodium / calcium transport failure。
- Ionic edema occurs between cytotoxic and vasogenic edema before BBB breakdown。
- Vasogenic edema results from BBB endothelial breakdown and macromolecule leakage。
- Roughly 10 percent of ischemic strokes are malignant or massive due to space-occupying edema severe enough to produce elevated ICP and herniation。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
ATP failure -> cytotoxic edema -> endothelial ion-water transport -> ionic edema -> BBB breakdown -> vasogenic edema -> ICP / mass effect risk
```

#### Inferences / Assumptions / Uncertainties

- Inference: large infarct deterioration can be edema / ICP-driven, not only recurrent ischemia。
- Assumption: management thresholds require separate neurocritical care sources。
- Uncertainty: hemorrhagic conversion mechanism is described as postulated when vascular integrity catastrophically fails。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Acute ischemic stroke physiology explains why rapid BP normalization may be unsafe in selected patients: autoregulation is impaired and chronic hypertension may shift the autoregulatory range。
- Penumbra is time-sensitive and perfusion-dependent; clinical symptoms alone cannot prove viable penumbra。
- Core versus penumbra can be modeled by ATP availability and cell death pattern, but requires imaging and clinical context for decision-making。
- Malignant edema is a secondary injury mechanism and must be monitored separately from the initial arterial occlusion。
- Hemorrhagic conversion risk has a vascular-barrier injury basis, not only medication-related risk。

## Research-Useful Points

- Separate hemodynamic thresholds, cellular cascade, edema biology and genetics when designing stroke mechanism notes。
- Avoid mixing animal / cell model mechanisms with directly proven human clinical facts。
- GWAS loci appear subtype-linked; mechanism classification matters before interpreting genetic associations。
- Genetics note: family/twin studies and GWAS support genetic contribution, but monogenic syndromes together account for only a small percentage of ischemic strokes and require dedicated sources。
