## One-Sentence Summary

Acute exercise molecular responses provide a plausible working model for how repeated exercise sessions remodel skeletal muscle, but single-session mRNA, phosphorylation, signaling, or MPS responses cannot be read as deterministic predictors of chronic phenotype without considering stimulus specificity, recovery, training status, tissue context, and long-term outcomes.

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept 1: Acute-to-chronic adaptation as a working model

#### One-Sentence Definition

Acute-to-chronic adaptation model 指每一次 acute exercise 造成 transient molecular response；在 repeated, progressive, recoverable training exposure 下，這些反應可能累積成 protein abundance / activity change 與 skeletal muscle phenotype，但這條鏈仍是 working model，不是完全驗證的線性公式。

#### Known Facts

- Repeated, episodic bouts of skeletal muscle contraction are the basic stimulus of structured exercise training。
- Skeletal muscle plasticity includes remodeling of muscle size, force, endurance, and contractile velocity。
- The review states that training adaptations are underpinned by activation and/or repression of molecular pathways and processes in response to individual acute exercise sessions。
- Acute exercise signals arise from neuronal, mechanical, metabolic, and hormonal stimuli。
- Acute molecular responses include signal transduction, regulation of pre- and posttranscriptional processes, protein translation, and protein degradation。
- Early rodent and human work showed acute transient increases in mRNA abundance during recovery after exercise, often returning toward baseline within about 24 h。
- The review explicitly states that the acute-to-chronic model remains a working model and is not fully validated。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
single exercise session
→ homeostatic perturbation
→ transient molecular signals and regulatory processes
→ recovery and protein turnover
→ repeated sessions under progressive overload
→ altered protein abundance / activity
→ skeletal muscle phenotype change
```

#### Inferences

- Acute molecular data are useful for mechanistic literacy, but not sufficient to predict long-term adaptation in a patient or athlete。
- A clinical program should use molecular logic to refine hypotheses, while still tracking function, symptoms, safety, recovery, and performance outcomes。

#### Assumptions

- Repeated acute responses can accumulate only when recovery, nutrition, progression, and adherence are adequate。
- Molecular measures are interpreted in the correct sampling window and tissue context。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Which acute molecular responses are causal drivers versus homeostatic repair signals remains incompletely resolved。
- The degree to which rodent, in vitro, and untrained-human first-bout data explain long-term trained-human adaptation remains limited。

### Concept 2: Exercise stimulus specificity and overlap

#### One-Sentence Definition

Exercise stimulus specificity means aerobic, resistance, HIIT, SIT, sprint, and concurrent training differ in force, velocity, duration, frequency, contraction number, and recovery, but their molecular and phenotypic adaptations overlap rather than forming perfectly separate boxes.

#### Known Facts

- The review categorizes exercise into aerobic / endurance, resistance / strength, and intermediate combinations including circuit training, concurrent training, HIIT, sprint interval training, and sprint training。
- Aerobic exercise generally uses longer duration, repetitive, lower-resistance activity and is often prescribed relative to VO2max, Wmax, vVO2max, lactate threshold, critical power, or intensity domains。
- Resistance training is expressed relative to 1RM or MVIC and differs by repetitions, sets, load, and exercises per session。
- Aerobic exercise has higher contraction frequency and lower force demand; resistance exercise has lower contraction frequency and higher force demand。
- Training volume and the characteristics of individual sessions help determine type and magnitude of adaptation。
- Aerobic training can produce modest hypertrophy and neural adaptations；resistance training can improve mitochondrial function and sometimes VO2max。
- Repeated sprint / SIT can produce endurance-like skeletal muscle adaptations。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
mode + intensity + duration + contraction force + contraction number + recovery
→ distinct but overlapping perturbation pattern
→ partially shared and partially divergent molecular response
→ relative weighting of endurance, hypertrophy, strength, fatigue resistance, and metabolic phenotypes
```

#### Inferences

- “Aerobic vs resistance” is a useful teaching axis, but too simple for predicting molecular response or long-term adaptation。
- Concurrent and interval training should be interpreted by actual session structure, not just label。

#### Assumptions

- Exercise prescription variables are documented precisely enough to compare stimuli。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- The review states that molecular responses show some specificity, but they do not fully explain divergent adaptations to different exercise training types。

### Concept 3: Signals, sensors, transduction, and effectors

#### One-Sentence Definition

Acute exercise molecular response can be organized as a sequence of exercise signals, sensor proteins / receptors, signal transduction networks, and downstream effector processes that regulate transcription, translation, degradation, and remodeling.

#### Known Facts

- Intrinsic signals include Ca2+ flux, ATP / ADP / Pi changes, NAD+ / NADH redox state, glycogen depletion, pH decline, intracellular PO2 decline, RONS, temperature, mechanical load / tension, and sarcolemmal disruption。
- Extrinsic factors include catecholamines, cytokines such as TNF-alpha and IL-6, GH, IGF-I, testosterone, glucose, amino acids, free fatty acids, and autocrine / paracrine factors。
- Sensor proteins include AMPK for energy status, sirtuins for NAD+ / NADH redox state, calmodulin for Ca2+, HIF for intracellular PO2, mechanosensors, beta-adrenergic receptors, androgen receptors, GH receptor, and IGF-I receptor。
- Signal transduction includes kinase / phosphatase cascades, posttranslational modifications, protein-protein interactions, and protein translocation。
- Effector processes include transcription factors and coregulators, epigenetic regulation, mRNA processing / degradation, translation initiation / elongation, protein degradation, ribosomal biogenesis, autophagy, mitophagy, mitochondrial biogenesis, and satellite-cell activity。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
exercise perturbation
→ intrinsic and extrinsic signals
→ sensors and receptors
→ kinase / phosphatase / PTM / translocation networks
→ transcriptional, translational, degradation, and organelle remodeling processes
→ potential phenotype change after repeated exposure
```

#### Inferences

- A pathway being activated after exercise is not proof that it caused the eventual phenotype。
- “Master regulator” language should be used cautiously because adaptation emerges from networks and processes rather than isolated targets。

#### Assumptions

- Measured molecular signals reflect relevant muscle tissue response and not sampling artefact, fiber-type composition, or unrelated stress response。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Many signaling networks are redundant, overlapping, and context-dependent。
- Human biopsy sampling provides limited time points and cannot fully represent subcellular spatial dynamics。

### Concept 4: Training status, first-bout effect, and response attenuation

#### One-Sentence Definition

Training status changes the acute molecular response to the same exercise; a first or unfamiliar bout can show generalized stress / damage responses, whereas trained muscle may show attenuated or more refined responses that should not be misread as lack of stimulus.

#### Known Facts

- Training status affects acute molecular response。
- After a training period, many acute signaling or mRNA responses at the same absolute or relative workload can be attenuated, but not all responses decline。
- The review discusses first-bout / repeated-bout concepts: unfamiliar exercise can evoke a broad stress response。
- Low-load resistance exercise to failure can produce hypertrophy similar to higher-load training over ≥6 weeks in some studies, though strength gains are likely greater with higher loads。
- Adaptations depend on exercise type, volume, intensity, duration, mode, recovery, and training status。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
untrained or unfamiliar bout
→ large perturbation + damage / inflammation / stress signaling
→ broad acute response
repeated exposure and adaptation
→ reduced perturbation at same task
→ attenuated or refined acute molecular response
→ improved phenotype or efficiency
```

#### Inferences

- Strong first-bout molecular response may indicate novelty or stress, not necessarily an optimal adaptation stimulus。
- Attenuation after training may represent adaptation, not failure of the program。

#### Assumptions

- Exercise sessions are compared at clearly defined absolute and relative intensities。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- How to use acute molecular attenuation to adjust clinical or training prescription remains unresolved。

### Concept 5: Translational limits and exercise mimetics

#### One-Sentence Definition

Exercise mimetics and druggable molecular targets are attractive but limited because exercise training is a systemic, multi-organ, repeated behavioral exposure that cannot be fully reproduced by activating one pathway.

#### Known Facts

- The review states that understanding molecular networks may support personalized exercise medicine and druggable adjunct targets。
- It also warns that acute molecular responses and chronic adaptation continuity are incomplete。
- Exercise mimetics / exercise-in-a-pill remain limited by the multiorgan and systemic effects of exercise。
- Sarcopenia-related pharmacologic hypertrophy does not necessarily translate into strength or physical function improvement。
- The review identifies biological sex, circadian rhythm, gut microbiome, multi-omics, and small extracellular vesicles as future directions。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
exercise = repeated systemic stimulus
→ neural + mechanical + metabolic + hormonal + immune + vascular effects
→ multi-tissue adaptation
single molecular target activation
→ partial pathway effect
→ may fail to reproduce function, performance, or health outcome
```

#### Inferences

- Pharmacologic adjuncts should be tested as adjuncts to exercise or disease-specific interventions, not assumed to replace exercise。
- In rehabilitation, patient-relevant outcomes such as function, strength, endurance, symptoms, falls, participation, and safety remain essential。

#### Assumptions

- A druggable pathway must be shown to change clinically meaningful outcomes, not only molecular markers。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- It remains unclear which molecular targets, if any, can safely and meaningfully mimic selected exercise benefits in clinical populations。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Do not prescribe exercise from mRNA or phosphorylation data alone。
- Use molecular response concepts to understand why mode, intensity, volume, recovery, nutrition, training status, circadian timing, and disease context can change adaptation。
- For rehabilitation, molecular adaptation remains a mechanism explanation; exercise prescription still requires FITT, safety screen, progression logic, functional outcome tracking, and patient goals。
- Be careful with first-bout responses in deconditioned or disease populations; strong molecular stress does not automatically mean optimal training dose。

## Research-Useful Points

- Exercise studies should report mode, intensity, duration, volume, recovery, training status, sampling time, nutritional state, sex, and disease context。
- Acute-to-chronic continuity requires longitudinal designs linking molecular time course to protein-level and phenotype-level outcomes。
- Multi-omics can expand discovery but does not remove the need for causal testing and human validation。
- MetaMEx and similar resources can help integrate human skeletal muscle transcriptomic data, but database-derived patterns still require experimental interpretation。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- Conflicts with simplistic “acute biomarker = chronic adaptation” interpretation。
- Conflicts with rigid aerobic/resistance molecular dichotomy；the source supports overlap and continuum.
- Conflicts with “exercise mimetic can replace exercise” framing；the source supports possible adjunct / target discovery but not full replacement.
