# Source Summary: Complex regional pain syndrome in adults: Treatment, prognosis, and prevention

## Source Type

UpToDate topic review. Literature review current through 2026-04；topic last updated 2025-12-17。

## Reliability Level

Tier 6。這是臨床 topic review，適合整理 practical management framework，但不是 primary guideline，也不是 individual procedure protocol。對藥物、block、neuromodulation 的敘述多處承認 evidence quality low / conflicting，因此不可把本來源改寫成強 recommendation。

## One-Sentence Summary

Adult [[CRPS_臨床辨識與治療限制|CRPS]] treatment 應採 multidisciplinary、rehabilitation-first、功能導向的升階流程：先做 education、PT/OT、psychosocial assessment 與低風險 pain control，若 progressive、severe、chronic 或 initial treatment response 不佳，再轉 pain specialist 考慮 interventional options；prognosis variable，recurrence 不少見，vitamin C prophylaxis 只適合低風險但證據有限的選擇性情境。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: CRPS Multidisciplinary Treatment Escalation

#### One-Sentence Definition

CRPS treatment 的核心不是完全止痛後才復健，而是用 education、PT/OT、psychosocial support 與 individualized analgesia 讓病人能恢復 affected limb use，並在 response 不佳時升階到 experienced pain management。

#### Known Facts

- All patients with CRPS should receive patient education、PT/OT as soon as practical、psychosocial assessment, and symptomatic pain management。
- Treatment goals are restoration of affected-limb function、decreased pain and disability、improved quality of life、and minimized medication toxicity。
- Treatment appears more effective when started early, ideally after diagnosis and before radiographic changes appear。
- PT/OT are first-line, but supporting studies are methodologically limited。
- Pharmacologic pain management is intended to allow active participation in rehabilitation and restore movement / strength。
- Early CRPS medication options in the source include NSAID、neuropathic pain adjuvant such as gabapentin / pregabalin / TCA、short bisphosphonate course in selected early CRPS with abnormal bone scan uptake、and topical lidocaine or capsaicin。
- The source grades early medication options as Grade 2C。
- Referral to pain management is suggested for unsatisfactory response to initial treatment、progressive symptoms/signs、severe CRPS、or chronic CRPS。
- Interventional options include trigger/tender point injections、sympathetic nerve blocks、SCS / PNS / DRG stimulation、epidural clonidine、sympathectomy、intrathecal baclofen for dystonia, and selected modalities such as hyperbaric oxygen in skin breakdown。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
CRPS diagnosis with mimic exclusion
-> education that pain does not equal ongoing tissue damage in the hyperalgesic region
-> early PT/OT and affected-limb use
-> analgesia chosen to permit participation, not replace rehabilitation
-> monitor pain, function, side effects and progression
-> escalate to pain specialist / interventional options when refractory or progressive
```

#### Inferences

- A CRPS plan that only lists medication without functional goals is incomplete。
- Delaying movement until pain disappears conflicts with the source's rehabilitation-first framing。
- Interventional pain procedures are best framed as enabling participation and reducing refractory pain, not confirming the diagnosis or curing the syndrome。

#### Assumptions

- Adult CRPS diagnosis has already been made using clinical criteria and mimic exclusion。
- Therapy access, medication safety screening, and pain specialist access are available enough to implement a staged plan。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- PT/OT are first-line, but comparative evidence among specific PT/OT techniques is not definitive。
- Graded motor imagery has small-trial support and low-quality meta-analytic support, but may not translate reliably into practice。
- Evidence for many pharmacologic and interventional treatments is weak, conflicting, or based partly on expert experience。
- Ketamine dosing, delivery, and patient selection are not standardized。

### Concept: CRPS Prognosis, Recurrence, and Prevention

#### One-Sentence Definition

CRPS prognosis is variable：improvement within six months is common, but prolonged disability and recurrence can occur; prevention evidence is limited and supports selective rather than universal vitamin C prophylaxis。

#### Known Facts

- Symptomatic improvement within six months is common, but a substantial proportion of patients have prolonged disability。
- One population-based Dutch cohort followed 102 patients for 5.8 years：64 percent still fulfilled diagnostic criteria, 30 percent considered themselves recovered, and 31 percent were unable to work。
- A retrospective population-based study of 74 cases found symptom resolution in 74 percent, sometimes spontaneously。
- Recurrence estimates range approximately 10 to 30 percent; one 1183-patient study found recurrence in 10 percent and an estimated incidence of 1.8 percent per patient-year。
- Recurrence may occur spontaneously, with cold exposure, after trauma, after surgery, or after emotional trauma。
- Vitamin C evidence after distal radius fracture is inconsistent：one trial favored vitamin C, another found no difference, and meta-analysis showed nonsignificant trend with low-quality evidence。
- The source does not find vitamin C evidence compelling enough for routine universal use after all distal fractures or high-risk injuries。
- Whole-food protein, vitamin C, calcium, and vitamin D intake for six to eight weeks after fracture is advised for healing；vitamin C 500 mg daily for six to eight weeks is reasonable when distal radius fracture patients have poor baseline nutrition or cannot maintain whole-food intake。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Fracture / limb trauma / surgery / immobilization risk
-> CRPS onset or recurrence risk
-> balance fracture-healing immobilization against prolonged disuse
-> maintain nutrition and early safe mobilization when healing allows
-> in prior CRPS, defer elective surgery until stable and avoid surgery during exacerbation
```

#### Inferences

- "Vitamin C prevents CRPS" is too strong; the supported statement is selective low-risk supplementation under limited, inconsistent evidence。
- Prognosis should not be framed as uniformly benign or uniformly disabling；both spontaneous / early improvement and long-term disability occur。
- Prior CRPS history should be treated as perioperative risk context, not an absolute contraindication to future surgery。

#### Assumptions

- Prevention discussion applies primarily to adults with fractures or adults with prior CRPS facing later injury/surgery。
- Surgical risk mitigation requires coordination with the surgical and anesthesia teams。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Recurrence-prevention strategies are supported mostly by small series and nonrandomized evidence。
- Pediatric CRPS recurrence and prevention cannot be generalized from this adult topic。
- Vitamin C trial results are inconsistent and event rates are low。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Start PT/OT immediately after diagnosis when practical; ideally before motion limitation and contracture develop。
- Pain medication should be titrated to enable rehabilitation participation, not to create passive analgesic dependence。
- Psychosocial / behavioral therapy is particularly relevant when CRPS duration exceeds two months at presentation, treatment response is insufficient, or psychologic / psychiatric comorbidity is suspected。
- Consider pain specialist referral when initial treatment is unsatisfactory, symptoms/signs progress, or CRPS is severe/chronic。
- Avoid interpreting sympathetic block response as diagnostic confirmation; this source treats blocks as refractory-pain interventions, while the diagnosis source states block response is not diagnostic。

## Research-Useful Points

- Many CRPS treatment trials have small sample size, heterogeneity, lack of controls, short follow-up, or risk of bias。
- Bisphosphonate evidence is strongest in selected early CRPS with abnormal bone scan / osteopenic change, but trials remain small and drug availability varies。
- SCS may improve pain and quality of life for up to two years in small studies, but long-term benefit and functional outcomes are less secure。
- Vitamin C prevention evidence has inconsistent RCT results and low-quality meta-analytic certainty。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「CRPS 治療等於 opioid escalation」衝突。
- 與「sympathetic block response 可證明 CRPS」衝突；本來源只把 block 放在 refractory pain treatment，診斷來源則明確說不能用來確診。
- 與「PT/OT 要等疼痛控制好才開始」衝突。
- 與「ketamine / SCS / sympathectomy 是 routine early CRPS treatment」衝突。
- 與「distal radius fracture 後所有人都應 routine vitamin C prophylaxis」衝突。
