## One-Sentence Summary

Aneurysmal SAH treatment 的核心是把 patient 放進 high-volume neurocritical workflow，先穩定 airway / circulation / seizures、停用並逆轉 antithrombotics、避免 hypotension 與 hypovolemia、給 oral/NG nimodipine、盡早 secure aneurysm（preferably within 24 hours），再密集監測與處理 rebleeding、delayed cerebral ischemia / vasospasm、ICP / hydrocephalus、hyponatremia、seizures 與長期 neurocognitive morbidity。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Aneurysmal SAH 急性治療與預後管理

#### One-Sentence Definition

Aneurysmal SAH 急性治療與預後管理是 ruptured intracranial saccular aneurysm 造成 SAH 後，從 stabilization、aneurysm securement、neurocritical monitoring、DCI/vasospasm treatment、ICP/hydrocephalus management 到 prognosis framing 的連續流程。

#### Known Facts

- 本來源只處理 aneurysmal SAH treatment and prognosis；epidemiology、risk factors、clinical manifestations and diagnosis、unruptured aneurysm、nonaneurysmal SAH 屬其他來源。
- Initial care 需辨識 clinical severity 並穩定 life-threatening conditions，尤其 comatose patients；重要步驟包含 secure airway、normalize cardiovascular function、treat seizures。
- Endotracheal intubation indications 包含 GCS <=8、elevated ICP、poor oxygenation or hypoventilation、hemodynamic instability、需要 heavy sedation/paralysis。
- Admission severity 與 subarachnoid bleeding extent 是 neurologic complications and outcomes 的重要 predictors；Hunt and Hess、WFNS、Fisher / modified Fisher 等量表用於分層。
- Aneurysmal SAH 最適合在 high-volume centers with dedicated neurocritical care units and experienced neurovascular / neurocritical staff 照護；observational data suggest lower mortality。
- Initial symptomatic management 包含 BP control、euvolemia、nimodipine、hemodynamic/neurologic monitoring、bedrest、analgesia、VTE prophylaxis、停用 antithrombotics 與必要 reversal。
- 所有 antithrombotic agents 應停用，所有 anticoagulation 應 reversal until aneurysm is definitively repaired。
- Antifibrinolytic agents should not be used routinely；2022 meta-analysis 顯示未降低 poor outcome，雖可能降低 rebleeding，但 heterogeneity 與大型 trial 結果限制確定性。
- Unsecured ruptured aneurysm 且 BP elevated 時，source 建議 gradual reduction；most patients target SBP <160 mmHg or MAP <110 mmHg，需避免 hypotension。
- BP lowering 可降低 rebleeding risk，但若 ICP high，過度 lowering 可能降低 CPP 並增加 infarction risk。
- Hypovolemia 是 ischemic complication risk factor；目標是 euvolemia，通常使用 normal saline，並至少每日監測 sodium。
- ICU monitoring 包含 frequent neurologic checks、TCD、selected CTA/CTP/MRI perfusion、continuous EEG in poor-grade or unexplained deterioration、troponin/ECG and echocardiography when indicated。
- Neurologic change 應以 urgent head CT、angiography and/or EEG 評估 rebleeding、infarction、hydrocephalus、vasospasm 或 subclinical seizures。
- Nimodipine 60 mg every four hours is administered to all patients with aneurysmal SAH within 48 hours of symptom onset or sooner once stabilized；given orally or via NG tube, continued 21 days。
- Nimodipine improves outcome but source states there is no convincing evidence that it changes angiographic or symptomatic vasospasm incidence；mechanism of benefit remains uncertain。
- Seizure prophylaxis after SAH is controversial；unsecured aneurysm / poor grade / ICH / hydrocephalus 等 high-risk 情境可考慮，但 exposure should be minimized and phenytoin generally avoided。
- In absence of acute seizures, antiseizure medication should be discontinued in most patients after aneurysm is secured。
- DVT prophylaxis with intermittent pneumatic compression starts before aneurysm treatment；subcutaneous unfractionated heparin or LMWH added once aneurysm is secured。
- Aneurysm repair by surgical clipping or endovascular coiling is the only effective treatment to prevent rebleeding and should be performed as early as feasible, preferably within 24 hours。
- Early rebleeding risk is 4-14% in first 24 hours, maximal in first 2-12 hours；rebleeding mortality reported as high as 70%。
- Predictors of rebleeding include longer time to aneurysm treatment、worse neurologic status、larger aneurysm、high SBP、intracerebral/intraventricular blood、acute hydrocephalus、incomplete obliteration。
- DCI occurs in approximately 30% of aneurysmal SAH and contributes substantially to morbidity and mortality。
- DCI clinical definition includes focal neurologic impairment or GCS decrease >=2 for at least one hour, not attributable to other causes after assessment。
- Vasospasm typically occurs days 4-14 after symptom onset and peaks at days 7-8, but can occur earlier。
- DCI prevention includes nimodipine and euvolemia；source does not use prophylactic hemodynamic augmentation。
- Aggressive vasospasm treatment can be pursued only after aneurysm has been clipped or coiled。
- New-onset DCI first-line treatment after aneurysm occlusion is stepwise hemodynamic augmentation with vasopressors while maintaining euvolemia。
- Hypervolemia should not be routinely used to prevent or treat DCI；older triple-H therapy included hypervolemia, but later data suggest no benefit and possible harm。
- Focal large-vessel vasospasm refractory to hemodynamic augmentation may be treated with balloon angioplasty；smaller or diffuse vasospasm may use intra-arterial vasodilators。
- Hydrocephalus affects 20-30% of SAH patients and usually presents within minutes to hours, but later hydrocephalus can occur。
- Immediate CSF diversion with EVD or lumbar drainage is indicated for deteriorating consciousness with elevated ICP and/or hydrocephalus；EVD is always indicated for noncommunicating hydrocephalus。
- Lumbar drainage is an alternative for communicating hydrocephalus but contraindicated in obstructive hydrocephalus or intraparenchymal hematoma。
- Hyponatremia develops in up to 30% of SAH patients；fluid restriction is undesirable because hypovolemia increases vasospasm-related ischemic injury risk。
- Acute seizures occur in 8-15% of SAH；levetiracetam is typically used, phenytoin avoided due association with worse cognitive outcomes。
- Most patients with acute seizures after SAH can wean antiseizure medication after one week unless seizures recur。
- Pulmonary edema and cardiac arrhythmias complicate 23% and 35% of SAH cases respectively；ECG/troponin/echo abnormalities can mimic primary cardiac disease。
- Early mortality remains high；population studies found 18-24% died suddenly before hospital evaluation, and historical case-fatality has declined toward approximately 30% in later eras。
- Survivors have increased long-term mortality compared with general population and increased nonfatal vascular events。
- Functional recovery is fastest in first 6-12 months but may continue beyond this；long-term complications include neurocognitive dysfunction, epilepsy, focal deficits, mood/sleep disorders, anosmia, aneurysm recurrence and late rebleeding。
- First-degree relatives of SAH patients have a two- to fivefold increased SAH risk；selected screening may be reasonable, especially when multiple first-degree relatives are affected。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Aneurysmal SAH
→ acute blood in subarachnoid space + unsecured ruptured aneurysm
→ high early rebleeding risk, ICP surge, hydrocephalus, seizures, autonomic/cardiopulmonary instability
→ stabilize ABC / seizures + reverse antithrombotics + BP control without hypotension + euvolemia
→ early clipping/coiling secures aneurysm and reduces rebleeding risk
→ days 4-14 vasospasm/DCI risk emerges
→ nimodipine + euvolemia + monitoring; treat DCI after securement with induced hypertension, angioplasty or intra-arterial vasodilators
→ manage ICP/hydrocephalus, hyponatremia, seizures and cardiopulmonary complications
→ prognosis depends on initial grade, blood burden, complications and long-term cognitive / mood / epilepsy sequelae
```

#### Inferences

- SAH treatment should be represented as a neurocritical workflow, not just a medication list。
- For rehabilitation handoff, the key safety questions are whether aneurysm is secured, whether vasospasm/DCI window is active, whether EVD/shunt or ICP risk persists, and whether cognition / mood / epilepsy risks require screening。
- Acute stroke hub should separate SAH diagnosis from SAH treatment: diagnosis identifies bleeding source; treatment prevents rebleeding and manages DCI / ICP complications。

#### Assumptions

- Adult aneurysmal nontraumatic SAH。
- Neurocritical care, vascular neurosurgery/endovascular treatment, CT/CTA/DSA, TCD/EEG, EVD capability and ICU monitoring are accessible directly or via transfer。
- DCI treatment is discussed after aneurysm securement；unsecured aneurysm changes BP and activity risk logic。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Optimal BP target in unsecured SAH is not fully settled and must balance rebleeding vs cerebral perfusion。
- Nimodipine outcome benefit mechanism is uncertain and does not equal proven vasospasm incidence reduction。
- Antifibrinolytic therapy, lumbar drainage, clazosentan, EVD weaning strategy, transfusion thresholds and routine imaging schedules remain areas with incomplete or mixed evidence。
- UpToDate topic review summarizes but does not replace formal guideline review。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Transfer/admit aneurysmal SAH to experienced neurocritical / neurovascular center when possible。
- Secure airway in poor-grade or unstable patient；grade severity after stabilization。
- Stop antithrombotics and reverse anticoagulation until aneurysm is secured。
- Avoid both severe hypertension and hypotension；most patients use SBP <160 or MAP <110 before securement, individualized to ICP/CPP and premorbid BP。
- Maintain euvolemia; avoid fluid restriction for SAH hyponatremia。
- Give nimodipine orally/NG, not IV, for 21 days。
- Secure aneurysm early, preferably within 24 hours；rebleeding risk is highest in first day。
- Watch days 4-14 for vasospasm/DCI, especially days 7-8。
- Treat DCI after aneurysm occlusion with stepwise hemodynamic augmentation; do not use routine hypervolemia/triple-H。
- Consider EVD/CSF diversion when consciousness deteriorates with hydrocephalus or elevated ICP。
- Long-term follow-up should include cognition, mood/sleep, epilepsy, focal deficits, anosmia and recurrent aneurysm/late rebleeding risk。

## Research-Useful Points

- Outcome studies should separate angiographic vasospasm, symptomatic vasospasm and DCI。
- Nimodipine effect should not be treated as proof of vasospasm prevention mechanism。
- Studies of DCI treatment need distinguish prophylactic hemodynamic augmentation from treatment after clinical decline。
- Long-term SAH outcomes require neurocognitive, mood/sleep and participation outcomes, not only mortality or mRS。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「SAH 給 nimodipine 是因為它直接防 vasospasm」衝突：source 說 outcome benefit established，但 vasospasm incidence reduction 不確定。
- 與「tranexamic acid 可 routine 防 rebleeding」衝突：source 不支持 routine antifibrinolytic use because poor outcome benefit 未建立。
- 與「triple-H therapy 是標準 vasospasm prevention」衝突：source 不 routine 使用 prophylactic hemodynamic augmentation，也不 routine hypervolemia。
- 與「aneurysm secured 前後 BP / vasospasm treatment 一樣」衝突：source 指出 aggressive vasospasm therapy should be after clipping/coiling。
