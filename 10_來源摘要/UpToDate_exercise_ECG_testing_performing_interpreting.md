## One-Sentence Summary

Exercise ECG testing 是以 symptom-limited incremental exercise 同時監測 ECG、HR、BP、symptoms and recovery response 的 cardiac stress test；它只有在病人能充分運動、resting ECG 可判讀且沒有重大 contraindications 時，才適合作為不加 imaging 的 ischemia / functional capacity 評估。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Exercise ECG Testing 適用條件與安全流程

#### One-Sentence Definition

Exercise ECG testing 適用條件與安全流程，是在測試前先確認 indication、exercise capacity、resting ECG interpretability and contraindications，再用 symptom-limited protocol 監測 hemodynamic、ECG and symptom endpoints。

#### Known Facts

- Exercise ECG 可用於 CHD diagnosis / prognosis 與 exercise capacity assessment。
- 適用情境包括：症狀疑似 myocardial ischemia、ACS / MI 已排除後的 acute chest pain、近期 ACS 未接受 coronary angiography、known CHD clinical status change、prior revascularization、abnormal or equivocal CCTA、valvular heart disease、certain arrhythmias、non-cardiac surgery 前 cardiac assessment。
- Serious complications 約 1 in 10,000 tests。
- Absolute contraindications 包含 acute MI within 2 days、ongoing unstable angina、hemodynamically significant uncontrolled arrhythmia、symptomatic severe valvular stenosis、decompensated heart failure、active endocarditis、acute myocarditis / pericarditis、acute aortic dissection、acute PE / pulmonary infarction / DVT、以及無法安全且充分測試的 physical disability。
- Relative contraindications 可在 benefit outweigh risk 時被 superseded；例子包括 left main coronary stenosis、moderate to severe aortic stenosis with uncertain symptom relation、uncontrolled tachyarrhythmia rate、advanced heart block、severe-gradient hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy、recent stroke / TIA、limited cooperation、resting BP >200/110 mmHg、significant uncorrected anemia / electrolyte imbalance / hyperthyroidism。
- Exercise ECG 不適合用於無法充分運動者；若 test 未達約 85 percent predicted maximal HR，negative test 的 ischemia sensitivity 會下降。
- Resting ECG 若有 WPW pattern、ventricular paced rhythm、LBBB、>1 mm resting ST depression、digoxin-related ST-T abnormality、LVH with ST-T abnormality、hypokalemia with ST-T abnormality，會干擾 ischemia 判讀，通常需考慮 imaging modality。
- RBBB 或 resting ST depression <1 mm 在適當 clinical setting 下仍可做 diagnostic exercise ECG。
- 多數 exercise testing 應依症狀與臨床 endpoint 停止，不應只因達到 85-90 percent predicted maximal HR 就停止。
- Standard report 至少應包含 indication、protocol、exercise duration、baseline / maximum HR、BP、workload、symptoms / RPE、stop reason、ECG changes / arrhythmias、functional capacity。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Suspected ischemia / need functional capacity estimate
→ pretest indication + contraindication screening
→ determine adequate exercise capacity and interpretable resting ECG
→ choose treadmill / cycle / arm ergometry and individualized protocol
→ incremental exercise increases myocardial work and oxygen demand
→ continuous ECG + staged BP + symptom / RPE monitoring
→ stop by symptom-limited, patient-determined, provider-determined, ECG, BP, or protocol endpoint
→ recovery monitoring captures delayed ischemic ST changes and arrhythmias
→ report integrates ECG, symptoms, BP, workload and functional capacity
```

#### Inferences

- 對 rehabilitation / exercise prescription 而言，exercise ECG 的價值不只是 ischemia positive / negative；functional capacity、chronotropic response、BP response、symptom reproduction and arrhythmia behavior 也會改變安全分層。
- 若病人因 gait、balance、orthopedic limitation 或 frailty 無法 treadmill 充分運動，換成 bicycle / arm ergometry 或 pharmacologic / imaging stress test 比硬跑 Bruce protocol 更合理。

#### Assumptions

- 病人有可評估的 cardiac indication，且測試在有 trained personnel、continuous ECG monitoring and resuscitation capacity 的環境執行。
- 本來源的 practical workflow 適用於 adult clinical exercise stress testing；pediatric、elite athlete、advanced heart failure、post-transplant 或 disease-specific rehabilitation 需另查 dedicated source。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Beta blockers 或其他會影響 ischemic / HR response 的藥物是否停用沒有單一 consensus；來源描述其 practice 通常不要求停藥，但需 individualized。
- Predicted maximal HR formula 有年齡與性別誤差；220-age 被來源用作 practical consistency，而不是精準 physiological truth。
- 本來源不詳細處理 stress imaging selection、Duke treadmill score、post-test probability 或 prognosis modeling。

### Concept: Exercise ECG ST Segment 判讀

#### One-Sentence Definition

Exercise ECG ST segment 判讀，是在 resting ECG 可判讀的前提下，依 exercise and recovery phase 的 ST depression / elevation、lead distribution、symptoms and arrhythmias 判斷 myocardial ischemia signal 與風險訊號。

#### Known Facts

- Exercise-induced ischemia 的 ECG 變化主要與 ST segment shift 相關，也可涉及 T wave and U wave。
- 一般 abnormal ischemia criterion 是 ≥1 mm horizontal or downsloping ST depression in one or more leads persisting 80 ms after the J point。
- Upsloping ST depression 較早出現但較不 specific；納入 upsloping criteria 會增加 sensitivity 但降低 specificity。
- V4、V5、V6 對 subendocardial ischemia 的 ST depression 最敏感，V5 常是單一最佳 lead。
- Lateral precordial ST depression 通常不能 localize culprit coronary artery；inferior-only ST depression 常是 false positive。
- Horizontal / downsloping ST depression of ≥1 mm 的 mean sensitivity and specificity 在一個 meta-analysis 中約 60 percent and 90 percent；不同 abnormal criteria 會明顯改變 sensitivity / specificity。
- Recovery-only ST depression 仍有 diagnostic / prognostic significance；一個 review 中 recovery-only ST depression 的 positive predictive value 接近 exercise-phase ST depression。
- ST elevation in leads without pre-existing Q waves and not in aVR suggests transmural ischemia, variant angina, or severe / multivessel CHD；與 ST depression 不同，ST elevation lead location may localize culprit artery。
- Exercise-induced ST elevation in aVR 在一項研究中強烈預測 left main or ostial LAD severe obstructive CAD。
- Exercise-induced ventricular ectopy occurred in 7-20 percent of patients tested for known or suspected CHD；mortality association may be most relevant when frequent ventricular ectopy occurs during recovery。
- Baseline LBBB 或 paced rhythm 會遮蔽 ischemia 判讀；baseline RBBB with upright ST/T in V5-6 時，V5-6 horizontal / downsloping ST depression 可作 ischemia indicator。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Exercise raises HR, BP and myocardial work
→ oxygen demand may exceed coronary oxygen delivery
→ subendocardial ischemia alters action potentials and injury currents
→ TQ / ST segment displacement appears on surface ECG
→ horizontal/downsloping ST depression is more specific for subendocardial ischemia
→ recovery monitoring may reveal delayed ST depression or high-risk ectopy
```

#### Inferences

- Exercise ECG 判讀應避免把單一 ST depression 數字孤立化；symptoms、workload、BP response、recovery pattern、baseline ECG and lead distribution 都會改變意義。
- Recovery phase 不是測試結束後的形式流程；它可揭露 exercise phase 沒出現的 ischemic ST depression 或 prognostic arrhythmia signal。

#### Assumptions

- ECG acquisition quality 足夠，且 interpretation 對照適當的 resting / standing baseline。
- Abnormal ECG criterion 用於 suspected obstructive CHD 的 adult diagnostic context，不自動外推到 asymptomatic screening、athlete screening 或 nonischemic cardiomyopathy。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- ST depression sensitivity / specificity 高度依 criteria、pretest probability、baseline ECG、population and angiographic reference standard 而變。
- ST elevation in aVR 的 left main / ostial LAD signal 來自特定研究；不可單獨當成 invasive decision 的唯一依據。
- Ventricular ectopy 的 prognostic weight 需結合 frequency、timing、underlying disease and broader stress-test prognosis source。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Exercise ECG without imaging 需要同時符合：能充分運動、resting ECG 可判讀、沒有 absolute contraindication、pretest clinical question 適合以 exercise ECG 回答。
- Symptom-limited maximal test 是多數情境的 default；target HR 可作 adequacy reference，但不應作唯一 stop rule。
- 若 exercise phase 沒有 ischemic ECG change，diagnostic test recovery 可考慮 supine monitoring；若 exercise phase 已有 ischemic changes，sitting recovery 可能較安全。
- Summary report 應寫 normal / abnormal，比單純 positive / negative 更能傳達資訊。

## Research-Useful Points

- 研究或報告應明確列出 protocol、modality、achieved workload / METs、HR / BP response、medication status、baseline ECG interpretability、ST criteria and recovery findings。
- 若用 exercise ECG 作 outcome 或 safety screen，必須先界定是否為 treadmill / cycle / arm ergometry，因為 modality 會改變 maximal workload、ECG artifact and BP measurement quality。
