## One-Sentence Summary

Symptomatic intracranial large artery atherosclerosis (ICAS) 的治療核心是 intensive medical therapy：依 stenosis severity 與 event phase 選擇 short-term DAPT / aspirin、控制 SBP 與 LDL-C、提高 physical activity；routine intracranial stenting 不建議，介入治療只屬 recurrent stroke despite maximal medical therapy 時的未定 rescue option。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Symptomatic ICAS secondary prevention and DAPT boundary

#### One-Sentence Definition

Symptomatic ICAS secondary prevention 是對近期 TIA / ischemic stroke 歸因於 intracranial large artery stenosis 的病人，使用 antiplatelet therapy、SBP control、LDL-C lowering、physical activity and lifestyle modification 的 intensive medical therapy，而 DAPT duration 由 stenosis severity 與 event risk 決定。

#### Known Facts

- Acute stroke / TIA due to ICAS 的 hyperacute management 與其他 ischemic stroke / TIA 類似，仍需快速判斷 IV thrombolysis / mechanical thrombectomy eligibility。
- Recent stroke / TIA attributed to 70-99% atherosclerotic intracranial large artery stenosis：source suggests aspirin plus clopidogrel DAPT for up to 90 days rather than aspirin monotherapy，之後轉 long-term aspirin monotherapy。
- Brain ischemia attributed to 50-69% stenosis：low-risk TIA（ABCD2 <4）或 moderate-to-major ischemic stroke（NIHSS >5）以 aspirin alone 起始；high-risk TIA（ABCD2 >=4）或 minor stroke（NIHSS <=5）可用 aspirin plus clopidogrel DAPT for 21 days。
- Long-term prevention beyond 21 or 90 days 以 aspirin monotherapy 為主；clopidogrel monotherapy 或 aspirin-extended-release dipyridamole 是 reasonable alternatives，但未在 ICAS specific trials 中充分研究。
- WASID trial 中 warfarin 對 50-99% symptomatic ICAS 沒有較好效果，且 aspirin treatment mortality lower than warfarin；source concludes no role for routine oral anticoagulation。
- Risk factor control 是 treatment core：hypertension、hyperlipidemia、physical inactivity、obesity、diabetes and smoking。
- For hypertension with stroke / TIA attributed to 50-99% intracranial stenosis，source suggests SBP target <140 mmHg rather than lower target。
- Source advises caution with SBP <130 or <120, especially fluctuating neurologic symptoms、recent stroke or documented low flow。
- For atherosclerotic ischemic stroke / TIA including symptomatic ICAS，source uses high-intensity statin and LDL-C target <70 mg/dL。
- Physical activity was independently associated with lower recurrent stroke / MI / vascular death in medically treated SAMMPRIS patients。

#### Mechanism Chain

Intracranial atherosclerotic stenosis
-> local thrombus / artery-to-artery embolism and sometimes low-flow vulnerability
-> high early recurrent stroke risk
-> time-limited platelet inhibition plus strict vascular risk control
-> lower recurrent vascular event risk without adding stent-related periprocedural harm.

#### Inferences

- ICAS is a mechanism-specific exception inside general secondary prevention: DAPT may be longer than the ordinary 21-day minor stroke / high-risk TIA logic when stenosis is severe and recent.
- BP management in symptomatic ICAS should not automatically inherit the general post-stroke <130/80 target, because perfusion vulnerability can make excessive lowering unsafe in selected patients.

#### Assumptions

- The index event is truly attributable to ICAS, not AF、lacunar infarct、dissection、moyamoya、vasculitis or another competing mechanism。
- Hemorrhage has been excluded and antiplatelet safety has been assessed。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Exact BP lower boundary in ICAS remains uncertain。
- Clopidogrel alternatives and pharmacogenomic tailoring are not fully resolved in this source。

### Concept: ICAS stenting and intervention boundary

#### One-Sentence Definition

ICAS intervention boundary is that intracranial stenting / angioplasty should not be initial treatment for recently symptomatic ICAS because randomized trials show harm or no benefit; rescue intervention remains unproven and should be restricted to highly selected medical failures or research settings。

#### Known Facts

- Source states intensive medical therapy is superior to intracranial arterial stenting for recent stroke or TIA attributed to severe ICAS。
- Intracranial arterial stenting and other interventional procedures are not recommended for first stroke / TIA attributable to severe intracranial stenosis。
- SAMMPRIS enrolled 70-99% stenosis with TIA / ischemic stroke within 30 days; 30-day stroke/death was higher with angioplasty/stenting than medical therapy alone（14.7% vs 5.8%）。
- SAMMPRIS final follow-up still favored medical management; no long-term benefit from stenting beyond the early harm。
- VISSIT also found higher early safety events and 12-month territory stroke/TIA with stenting。
- CASSISS, despite lower-risk selection and later enrollment, found no benefit of stenting over medical therapy。
- 2023 meta-analysis cited by the source found endovascular therapy plus medical treatment had higher death or stroke at 30 days and one year than medical therapy alone。
- Source recommends against intracranial stenting for recent stroke / TIA attributed to ICAS。
- Submaximal balloon angioplasty is promising in BASIS but source says more research is needed before wide clinical use。
- AHA/ASA recommendations summarized in the source state angioplasty or stenting should not be initial treatment for severe 70-99% symptomatic stenosis, even if the patient was taking antithrombotic therapy at the time of stroke / TIA。

#### Mechanism Chain

Stenotic intracranial artery
-> stent / angioplasty mechanically opens lumen
-> periprocedural perforator occlusion, vessel injury, hemorrhage, restenosis or reperfusion complications
-> early harm can outweigh any theoretical long-term flow benefit
-> medical therapy remains default.

#### Inferences

- "Severe stenosis" alone is insufficient to justify stenting; severity must be interpreted with phase, medical therapy status, recurrent events and trial evidence。
- Rescue intervention should be framed as evidence-limited last resort, not as routine escalation after one event。

#### Assumptions

- The clinical question is stroke prevention in symptomatic ICAS, not thrombectomy for an acute large vessel occlusion。
- Local neurointerventional expertise and trial availability may alter discussion but not the evidence boundary。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- The role of submaximal balloon angioplasty after BASIS remains unsettled due to trial design, delayed enrollment, site concentration and generalizability concerns。
- Whether any biomarker-defined subgroup clearly benefits from intervention is not established。

### Concept: ICAS recurrent risk and hemodynamic boundary

#### One-Sentence Definition

ICAS recurrent risk is high, especially with severe stenosis, recent symptoms, borderzone infarct / impaired collateral flow, clinically hemodynamic symptoms or female sex, but hemodynamic testing and symptom markers inconsistently predict who will recur。

#### Known Facts

- WASID reported one-year territory ischemic stroke rate of 11-12% in symptomatic 50-99% stenosis under aspirin or warfarin-era therapy。
- Modern intensive medical therapy appears to lower recurrence compared with older aspirin-alone era cohorts。
- Severe stenosis（>=70%）had higher same-territory stroke risk than <70% stenosis in WASID。
- Recent ischemic symptoms within 17 days were associated with higher same-territory stroke risk。
- Borderzone infarcts plus impaired collateral flow in a SAMMPRIS MCA subgroup had the highest recurrent stroke risk reported in that analysis。
- Women in WASID had higher combined stroke / vascular death and recurrent ischemic stroke than men。
- SAMMPRIS medical arm high-risk features included old infarct in the territory、presentation with stroke and absence of statin use at trial entry。
- Clinically hemodynamic stenosis in GESICA was defined by symptoms with positional change、effort/exertion or antihypertensive initiation / increase；it was associated with more recurrent territory ischemic events。
- VERITAS found low posterior circulation flow by QMRA associated with subsequent vertebrobasilar stroke，but MyRIAD did not show a comparable association between low flow and recurrent infarcts。

#### Mechanism Chain

Severe ICAS
-> reduced vascular reserve and/or unstable plaque embolization
-> recurrence risk rises when symptoms are recent, stenosis is severe, collaterals are impaired or low-flow physiology is present
-> risk stratification can guide monitoring and BP caution
-> current hemodynamic markers remain insufficient as definitive treatment-selection tools.

#### Inferences

- In rehab or ward handoff, ICAS patients with fluctuating symptoms, exertional/positional symptoms, borderzone infarct pattern or known low flow should be flagged for recurrence surveillance and cautious BP titration。
- Hemodynamic risk is a clinical warning, but not a stand-alone indication for stenting。

#### Assumptions

- The stenosis is symptomatic and anatomically tied to the event territory。
- Imaging and cardiac evaluation have excluded competing mechanisms adequately enough to make ICAS attribution plausible。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Hemodynamic instability measures are inconsistent across studies。
- Sex-specific recurrence differences need cautious interpretation and are not an independent treatment algorithm in this source。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Severe recent symptomatic ICAS（70-99% within 30 days）is one of the few post-stroke contexts where aspirin plus clopidogrel up to 90 days is specifically supported by this source。
- Moderate ICAS（50-69%）does not automatically get 90-day DAPT；DAPT duration depends on TIA/stroke severity and risk。
- Long-term DAPT is still not the default; after 21 or 90 days the source returns to single antiplatelet therapy。
- Warfarin is not routine ICAS therapy and was harmful in WASID compared with aspirin。
- SBP <140 mmHg is the ICAS-specific target suggested in this source for 50-99% symptomatic stenosis；lower targets require caution in recent stroke, fluctuating deficits or documented low flow。
- Routine stenting is actively discouraged; intervention belongs to research / last-resort discussion, not default escalation。

## Research-Useful Points

- Major trial anchors in the source: WASID, SAMMPRIS, VISSIT, CASSISS, THALES, CHANCE / CHANCE-2, CLAIR, BASIS, VERITAS, MyRIAD。
- Open questions: optimal BP lower bound、submaximal angioplasty generalizability、hemodynamic biomarker selection、DAPT alternatives and genotype-guided antiplatelet decisions。
