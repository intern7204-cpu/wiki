# Neurodevelopmental and Executive Function and Dysfunction

## 一句話定義

這章把 Executive Function (EF) 放回 broader neurodevelopmental framework，強調 EF 是 **goal-directed regulation of thought and action**，不是單一疾病名稱。

## 核心機制

### 1. EF 是 neurodevelopmental domain 之一

- 核心 domain 包含 sensory、motor、language、visual-spatial、intellectual、memory、social cognition 與 executive function。
- EF 不應脫離整體 neurodevelopmental profile 單獨看。

### 2. EF 的核心組件

- inhibition / impulse control
- cognitive flexibility
- emotional control
- initiation
- planning
- organization
- working memory
- self-monitoring

### 3. variation 不等於 pathology

- neurodevelopmental variation 在正常人群本來就存在。
- 何時構成 dysfunction，要看跨情境的功能損害。

### 4. 病因非常廣

- genetic、medical、psychologic、environmental、sociocultural
- perinatal insult、toxins、trauma、deprivation、parent/caregiver EF 都可能參與

### 5. EF 發展是長軸過程

- infancy 已開始萌芽。
- preschool 是快速成形期。
- school age 到 adolescence 持續成熟。

### 6. assessment 不能只看 clinic impression

- family history、school report、teacher comment、vision / hearing、sleep、substance use、neuro exam、dysmorphology 都重要。
- 複雜個案常需 neuropsychological evaluation。

### 7. intervention 要跨 setting 整合

- demystification + strengths-based framing
- accommodations / modifications
- behavior / routine scaffolding
- school-based support
- targeted therapy
- parent training

## 臨床決策點

### 核心 caveat

- 這章最重要的界線是：**EF problem 可以出現在很多 neurodevelopmental / psychosocial 情境，不可直接等同 ADHD。**

## 限制與未定論

### frontmatter contradictions

- Executive function variation is not synonymous with ADHD and should not be reduced to one diagnosis.
- Medication may help selected attention-related symptoms but does not treat the whole executive-function profile.
- Unsupported cognitive, sensory, or device-based therapies should not be framed as consensus treatment.

## 理解缺口

- 哪些 EF complaint 其實是 sleep、language、anxiety 或 school mismatch 的表現？
- 哪些 school support 最能把 EF 概念轉成可操作介入？
- 何時該由 general developmental evaluation 升級到 neuropsychological testing？

## 臨床使用版

- 若孩子被描述成「不專心、沒組織、很拖」，先回到 EF component 與功能情境拆解問題。
- 若沒先分清 component，後續介入很容易變成籠統勸說。
