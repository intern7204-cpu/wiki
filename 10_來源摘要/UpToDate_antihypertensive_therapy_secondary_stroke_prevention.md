## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Stroke Antihypertensive Therapy for Secondary Prevention

#### One-Sentence Definition

Stroke antihypertensive therapy for secondary prevention 是在 stroke / TIA 急性期後，用正確時機、藥物類別、降壓速度與個別化 BP target 來降低 recurrent stroke and vascular events，同時避免 cerebral hypoperfusion。

#### Known Facts

- Hypertension is a major risk factor for stroke and TIA。
- Among patients who have had a stroke or TIA, antihypertensive therapy can reduce recurrence。
- Acute stroke BP management differs from chronic secondary prevention therapy。
- Previously treated, neurologically stable patients with known hypertension should resume antihypertensive therapy for recurrent stroke and vascular event prevention。
- Previously untreated, neurologically stable patients with any stroke type or TIA and established BP above goal should start antihypertensive therapy。
- For TIA, oral antihypertensive therapy is initiated or reinstated without further delay。
- For acute ischemic stroke, permissive hypertension is typical during the first 24-48 hours except for extreme hypertension, end-organ failure, active coronary ischemia, aortic dissection, preeclampsia/eclampsia, or reperfusion eligibility threshold。
- Stable or improving ischemic stroke patients able to receive oral / enteral medication generally start or resume antihypertensive therapy 24-48 hours after onset and during hospitalization。
- Neurologically unstable ischemic stroke patients with fluctuating deficits or progressive deterioration should delay antihypertensive therapy until deficits stabilize or reach nadir。
- Spontaneous ICH often requires IV antihypertensive treatment during the acute phase, then transition to oral therapy when appropriate。
- SAH secondary prevention antihypertensive therapy is started when cerebral perfusion pressure is judged adequate。
- ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel blockers and diuretics are reasonable options for initial monotherapy。
- Unless there is a compelling indication, beta blockers should not be used for prevention of recurrent stroke。
- Combination therapy is recommended when BP is at least 20/10 mmHg above goal; this source uses an angiotensin inhibitor plus a long-acting dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker。
- A meta-analysis of eight trials and more than 35,000 prior stroke/TIA patients found antihypertensive therapy reduced stroke rate and cardiovascular death。
- For ischemic stroke/TIA with atherosclerotic disease, a more intensive BP goal is used if tolerated。
- Cardioembolic stroke or paradoxical embolus is not, by itself, considered evidence of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease; if not otherwise high risk, a less intensive BP goal may be used。
- For spontaneous ICH recurrence prevention, this source suggests a more intensive BP goal similar to ischemic stroke due to atherosclerotic disease。
- Optimal goal BP for recurrent SAH prevention is unknown。
- Gradual BP reduction, approximately 10 percent per day though not consensus-defined, is recommended in known cerebrovascular disease or long-standing uncontrolled hypertension unless hypertensive emergency exists。
- Chronic hypertension shifts cerebral autoregulation, making overly aggressive acute BP reduction more likely to cause ischemic symptoms。
- AHA/ASA 2021 secondary stroke prevention guideline is summarized as recommending BP therapy for untreated ischemic stroke/TIA patients with established BP >=130/80 mmHg after the first few days, resumption after the first three days in previously treated patients, and a reasonable goal <130/80 mmHg。
- AHA/ASA 2022 ICH guideline is summarized as suggesting <130/80 mmHg for ICH patients。

#### Mechanism Chain

1. Chronic hypertension damages cerebral and systemic vasculature and increases recurrent ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke risk。
2. Post-stroke patients have high baseline recurrence and vascular event risk。
3. Controlled long-term BP lowering reduces vascular stress and recurrence risk。
4. But acute ischemic tissue and chronically hypertensive cerebral circulation may rely on higher perfusion pressure。
5. Therefore BP treatment must separate acute-phase perfusion logic from chronic secondary prevention target logic。

#### Inferences

- Discharge or rehab planning should document both BP phase and BP target, not simply list an antihypertensive drug。
- For rehabilitation clinicians, neurologic stability and orthostatic / therapy tolerance are part of BP management, not separate issues。
- "Stroke history" alone is not enough to define BP goal intensity; mechanism and ASCVD risk still matter。

#### Assumptions

- Adult patients with stroke or TIA。
- Stroke type, acute reperfusion status, neurologic stability, swallowing / enteral access, comorbid cardiac disease, renal function, frailty and medication tolerance are assessed。
- BP is measured by reliable office, home or ambulatory methods。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- UpToDate agrees with guideline BP goals but states its goal approach is more nuanced。
- The optimal BP target for recurrent SAH prevention is unknown。
- The exact definition of gradual BP lowering has no consensus。
- Intensive targets may need relaxation in symptomatic hypotension, postural hypotension, severe frailty, advanced dementia, limited life expectancy, institutionalized status or medication side effects。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Do not apply long-term BP target reflexively during the acute ischemic stroke perfusion window。
- Do not let permissive hypertension persist after the patient is stable and past the acute phase。
- In ischemic stroke, stable/improving patients generally start or resume therapy 24-48 hours after onset; unstable patients wait until neurologic deficits stabilize。
- For long-term regimen, ACE inhibitor / ARB / calcium channel blocker / diuretic are reasonable; beta blockers require a separate indication。
- Home BP monitoring can support gradual titration and avoid overshoot。

## Research-Useful Points

- Implementation research should measure time from stroke to BP plan, not just discharge prescription。
- BP target studies should stratify by stroke mechanism, ASCVD risk, chronic hypertension, frailty, orthostatic symptoms and achieved BP。
- Cognitive outcomes after vascular brain injury should not be inferred directly from recurrent stroke prevention evidence.

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「acute stroke BP 高就要壓到 <130/80」衝突。
- 與「permissive hypertension 可一路延續到出院」衝突。
- 與「beta blocker 是 post-stroke 防復發首選」衝突。
- 與「所有 ischemic stroke / TIA 都屬同一個 ASCVD risk target」衝突；cardioembolic / paradoxical embolus 需另看是否有其他 high-risk factor。
- 與「VaD 或 symptomatic cerebral hypoperfusion 情境越低越好」需區分；本來源處理 stroke secondary prevention，不是 VaD cognition treatment。
