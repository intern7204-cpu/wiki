## One-Sentence Summary

Spontaneous ICH diagnosis 的核心不是靠症狀猜 hemorrhage，而是用 CT/MRI mandatory confirmation 後，同步判讀 location、volume、IVH / edema / herniation、underlying etiology clues 與 expansion-risk markers，因這些資訊會直接改變 acute monitoring、reversal、BP、neurosurgical triage 與後續 recurrence prevention。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Spontaneous ICH 診斷與病因分層

#### One-Sentence Definition

Spontaneous ICH 診斷與病因分層是 adult atraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage 的 initial diagnostic framework：先以 CT/MRI 確認出血並排除 ischemic stroke mimic，再用 location、clinical course、labs、CTA/MRI findings 與 expansion-risk markers 判斷病因、嚴重度與需不需要更密集監測或後續影像。

#### Known Facts

- 本來源處理 spontaneous atraumatic ICH 的 pathogenesis、epidemiology、clinical features 與 diagnosis；acute treatment / prognosis、secondary prevention / long-term prognosis、pediatric hemorrhagic stroke、newborn stroke 另文處理。
- ICH brain injury 包含 primary mechanical injury from hematoma expansion and perilesional edema，以及 BBB breakdown 後的 excitotoxic / inflammatory secondary injury。
- Hematoma enlargement 與 neurologic deterioration、increased ICP、worse outcomes 相關；多數 hemorrhage expansion 發生在 onset 後前數小時。
- Perihematomal edema 常見，影像初次檢查時至少半數患者可見，通常 7-12 天達最大量，前 48 小時擴大最快。
- 常見 underlying etiologies 包含 hypertensive vasculopathy、cerebral amyloid angiopathy、ruptured vascular malformation；其他包括 cerebral venous thrombosis、vasculopathies、tumor、coagulopathies、RCVS、moyamoya、CNS infection、drug-related vasculopathy 等。
- Hypertensive hemorrhage 常發生於 penetrating artery territory，典型位置包含 putamen、thalamus、pons、midbrain、basal ganglia、cerebellar nuclei。
- CAA 是 older adults primary lobar ICH 的重要原因，常以 lobar hemorrhage 與 lobar cerebral microbleeds 表現。
- CMB distribution 可提示 microangiopathy type：hypertensive microbleeds 常在 basal ganglia、thalamus、pons、cerebellar nuclei；CAA-related microbleeds 多在 superficial lobar regions。
- Major risk factors for spontaneous ICH include older age, hypertension, and antithrombotic therapy。
- Warfarin 增加 ICH risk，且 INR >3 與 larger initial hemorrhage volume / poorer outcomes 相關；DOACs 也增加 ICH risk，但 nonvalvular atrial fibrillation 中低於 warfarin。
- Antiplatelet monotherapy 的 primary ICH absolute risk 較小；dual antiplatelet therapy 可能高於 monotherapy，但不同 trial 結果受族群與期間影響。
- ICH symptoms/signs 依 location and size 而變；可急性 focal neurologic deficit，也可在 minutes to hours progressive worsening。
- Headache、vomiting、decreased level of consciousness 常見於 large hemorrhage，但小 hemorrhage 可缺乏這些症狀；headache may be absent。
- In one study, bleeding locations 約 putamen 35%、cerebral lobes 30%、cerebellum 16%、thalamus 15%、pons 5-12%；INTERACT2 中 intraventricular extension 29%。
- Putaminal hemorrhage 常造成 hemiplegia、hemisensory loss、homonymous hemianopsia、gaze palsy；large hemorrhage 可 stupor/coma。
- Cerebellar hemorrhage 常造成 inability to walk due to imbalance、vomiting、occipital headache，且可能沒有 hemiparesis；可因 obstructive hydrocephalus 或 brainstem compression deterioration。
- Lobar hemorrhage 依位置表現，parietal/occipital 常見，且 seizure incidence 較高。
- Pontine hemorrhage 可快速 deep coma、bilateral paralysis、pinpoint pupils、horizontal gaze impairment。
- Seizures in the first days after ICH 約 15%，lobar hemorrhage 比 deep/cerebellar ICH 更常見。
- ICH 可伴 neurogenic cardiac abnormalities，例如 prolonged QT、ST-T changes、troponin / BNP mild elevation、wall motion abnormalities；可能與 ICP/autonomic disturbance 造成 catecholamine surge 有關。
- CT 或 MRI 都是 emergency diagnosis and assessment of ICH 的 first-choice imaging options。
- Noncontrast head CT 可準確辨識 acute ICH 並和 ischemic stroke 區分；CTA 可同時辨識 vascular cause 與 hemorrhagic expansion propensity。
- Brain MRI 對 acute ICH detection 與 CT roughly equivalent，對 chronic ICH 更準確，但 emergency availability、protocol time、metal implants 會限制使用。
- ABC/2 可估 ICH volume：A = 最大 hemorrhage diameter；B = 與 A 垂直最大 diameter；C = 依 slice count 和 slice thickness 估計的 cranio-caudal extent；ABC/2 得到 cubic centimeters。
- Hemorrhage expansion predictors include shorter time from symptom onset to initial imaging、ICH volume、antiplatelet/anticoagulant use、CTA contrast extravasation such as spot sign。
- Initial CT/MRI 後若 clinical deterioration，需 additional imaging 評估 expansion or rebleeding；也可能需要 follow-up imaging 找 underlying cause。
- Clinical suspicion for ICH 可由 gradually worsening symptoms、severe headache、vomiting、severe hypertension、decreased consciousness/coma 提高，但不能靠 clinical characteristics alone 區分 brain hemorrhage and ischemia。
- CT or MRI is mandatory to confirm ICH and exclude ischemic stroke / stroke mimics。
- Routine laboratory evaluation 包含 CBC、electrolytes、BUN/creatinine/glucose、PT/INR、aPTT、DOAC-related tests when relevant、troponin、toxicology screen、urinalysis、pregnancy test where relevant。
- EEG reserved for seizures or encephalopathy not explained by ICH location/size。
- Follow-up imaging is indicated when underlying cause is suspected by clinical features or initial imaging，或 hematoma/edema 可能遮蔽 structural lesion。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Spontaneous vessel rupture or hemorrhagic transformation
→ parenchymal blood collection
→ hematoma volume + perihematomal edema create mass effect and ICP risk
→ clinical deficits reflect location/size but overlap with ischemic stroke
→ CT/MRI confirms hemorrhage and maps location, volume, IVH, edema, herniation
→ CTA/MRI/labs identify etiology clues and expansion risk
→ risk stratification guides monitoring, reversal, BP, neurosurgical triage and follow-up imaging
```

#### Inferences

- ICH diagnostic page should sit upstream of [[Spontaneous_ICH_急性抗擴大與Neurocritical_Stabilization]]：treatment intensity depends on imaging-confirmed hemorrhage, expansion risk and etiology clues。
- Location is not just anatomy teaching；deep vs lobar vs cerebellar vs pontine hemorrhage changes suspected etiology, seizure risk, surgical urgency and prognosis conversation。
- For rehab-facing use, early ICH diagnosis matters because deficits may worsen over minutes/hours and deterioration may reflect expansion, seizure, ICP or hydrocephalus rather than static stroke deficit。

#### Assumptions

- 本頁適用 adult spontaneous atraumatic ICH。
- 內容假設可取得 emergency CT/MRI、basic labs、coagulation testing、CTA/MRA/DSA 或 follow-up MRI when clinically indicated。
- Pediatric ICH volume、etiology and workup 不可直接套用 adult ABC/2 and adult etiologic probabilities。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Secondary injury mechanisms after BBB breakdown remain incompletely defined。
- Small DWI lesions in primary ICH occur, but mechanism and prognostic meaning remain undefined。
- Hematoma expansion markers improve risk stratification, but all ICH patients still need treatment to limit expansion risk；spot sign should not become the only trigger for care。
- MRI can identify chronic hemorrhage and underlying lesions better than CT in some settings, but emergency feasibility is lower。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Do not diagnose ICH clinically alone；CT or MRI is mandatory。
- Absence of headache does not exclude ICH。
- Noncontrast CT answers “is there hemorrhage?”; CTA/MRI can answer “why did it bleed?” and “is it likely to expand?”。
- Cerebellar hemorrhage can present with inability to walk, vomiting and occipital headache without hemiparesis；this should trigger posterior fossa vigilance。
- Lobar hemorrhage plus lobar microbleeds in older adults raises CAA suspicion；deep hemorrhage plus deep CMBs favors hypertensive vasculopathy。
- ABC/2 is a communication and severity tool, not a replacement for clinical judgment or serial imaging when deterioration occurs。
- EEG is for seizures or unexplained encephalopathy, not routine screening for every ICH。

## Research-Useful Points

- Hematoma expansion is a key early outcome bridge between imaging and treatment trials。
- Spot sign, noncontrast CT heterogeneity/irregularity, subarachnoid extension and intraventricular extension are imaging biomarkers, but clinical utility depends on treatment response and patient-centered outcome。
- DWI lesions in primary ICH suggest perfusion/ICP/autoregulation mechanisms beyond the visible hematoma, but causal meaning remains unresolved。
- Etiology-specific imaging signatures should be separated from recurrence-prevention evidence, which requires secondary-prevention sources。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「頭痛嘔吐才是 ICH」衝突：large ICH 常見 headache/vomiting，但 headache may be absent。
- 與「CT 只用來排除出血」衝突：source 支持 CT/MRI 同時提供 volume、IVH、edema、herniation、underlying etiology and expansion risk。
- 與「臨床看起來像 ischemic 就不用急影像」衝突：source 明確指出 clinical characteristics alone 不能區分 hemorrhage 與 ischemia。
- 與「所有 lobar ICH 都是 CAA、所有 deep ICH 都是 hypertension」衝突：location 是 etiologic clue，不是 definitive diagnosis；仍需 clinical/lab/imaging context。
