## One-Sentence Summary

Aneurysmal SAH diagnosis 的核心是：任何 sudden or rapid onset severe headache 都要先考慮 SAH，從 noncontrast head CT 開始；若 CT negative 且不符合嚴格 early-CT rule-out 條件，多數仍需 lumbar puncture；確認 SAH 後再用 CTA / DSA 找出 bleeding source，且 negative angiography 常需 repeat study。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Aneurysmal SAH 臨床表現與診斷分流

#### One-Sentence Definition

Aneurysmal SAH 臨床表現與診斷分流是針對 sudden or rapid onset severe headache 的 emergency workflow：辨識 thunderclap headache 與可能輕微或 neurologically intact 的 presentation，依序使用 noncontrast CT、必要時 lumbar puncture、再以 CTA / DSA 找出 ruptured aneurysm。

#### Known Facts

- SAH 指出血進入 arachnoid 與 pia mater 之間的 subarachnoid space；多數 spontaneous nontraumatic SAH 由 intracranial saccular aneurysm rupture 造成。
- Classic presentation 是 sudden-onset severe headache，常被描述為 worst headache of life；這類 thunderclap headache 應評估 SAH。
- 在 neurologically intact、severe-onset headache within one hour 的患者，三個大型 sequential studies 共 5283 人中，329 人（6%）有 SAH。
- Headache onset 不一定被患者或醫師記成 instantaneous；location 對判斷 SAH 幫助不大。
- Common associated symptoms 包含 brief loss of consciousness、vomiting、neck pain or stiffness。
- Meningismus 可在 bleeding 後數小時因 CSF blood breakdown products 導致 aseptic meningitis 而出現。
- Seizures in first 24 hours occur in less than 10% but predict poor outcome。
- SAH 可 sudden death；source 引用數據指出 as many as 22% die before reaching hospital。
- Sentinel headache 可能在 major SAH 前 days to weeks 出現，但 retrospective data 有 recall bias，warning leak 概念仍有不確定性。
- Physical exam 可見 hypertension、meningismus、Terson syndrome；Terson syndrome imply poorer prognosis。
- Pupil-involving third nerve palsy mandates aneurysm workup, but its absence does not lower SAH likelihood in acute headache。
- Hunt and Hess / WFNS 用於 clinical grade；Fisher / modified Fisher 用 CT hemorrhage pattern 估 vasospasm / delayed cerebral ischemia risk。
- Sudden or rapid onset severe headache is sufficient to trigger immediate SAH evaluation, even if the patient is alert and neurologically intact。
- Ottawa SAH Rule in selected neurologically intact acute nontraumatic headache maximal within one hour had 100% sensitivity but low specificity in the derivation/validation context。
- Missed/delayed SAH diagnosis often comes from not appreciating presentation spectrum、not obtaining/understanding CT limitations、not performing/interpreting LP correctly。
- Diagnostic approach generally requires noncontrast head CT and lumbar puncture in patients with negative head CT and ongoing suspicion。
- Noncontrast head CT is the cornerstone test; sensitivity is highest within the first six hours and declines over time。
- Noncontrast CT reveals diagnosis in >90% if performed within 24 hours of bleeding onset。
- CT sensitivity is reduced with delayed imaging, low-volume bleed, or significant anemia。
- Lumbar puncture is typically required for most patients with normal head CT, except selected patients with isolated headache, normal exam, optimal-quality CT within six hours, and expert interpretation。
- Classic LP findings include elevated opening pressure, elevated CSF RBC count that does not diminish from tube 1 to tube 4, and xanthochromia。
- Clearing of blood across CSF tubes is unreliable unless the final tube normalizes in a strong context；RBC count and xanthochromia must be interpreted carefully。
- Xanthochromia can be absent very early; by 12 hours post-SAH, visual xanthochromia is expected in essentially all patients in the cited data and can last two weeks or more。
- CT followed by CTA after negative CT is an alternative advocated by some, but source recommends standard CT then LP if CT negative, reserving CTA to identify symptomatic aneurysm when CT or CSF is positive。
- MRI is not usually first-line for suspected acute SAH because availability and speed are worse than CT；LP should follow negative MRI if suspicion remains。
- Once SAH is diagnosed, vascular imaging is required to determine the source of bleeding。
- DSA has highest resolution and remains gold standard for aneurysm detection/anatomic definition；CTA is increasingly used first-line because it is fast and accessible。
- CTA/MRA can detect aneurysms >=3 mm with high sensitivity, but small aneurysms can be missed; DSA should be performed if CTA does not reveal aneurysm in a patient with SAH。
- Negative initial angiography occurs in 14-22% of SAH cases; repeat angiography in 4-14 days is critical for most, usually with DSA, unless perimesencephalic pattern makes repeat optional。
- Nonaneurysmal SAH accounts for an estimated 15-20% of SAH cases；perimesencephalic hemorrhage is a major subgroup。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Ruptured intracranial saccular aneurysm
→ blood enters subarachnoid space
→ abrupt meningeal irritation, ICP surge, vomiting/LOC/neck stiffness possible
→ presentation may still be isolated headache and neurologically intact
→ noncontrast CT detects acute cisternal blood best early
→ if CT negative but suspicion persists, CSF testing detects blood breakdown products
→ confirmed SAH requires angiography to identify aneurysm source
→ negative angiography may require repeat study because small/obscured aneurysm can be missed
```

#### Inferences

- SAH should be represented in the wiki as a diagnostic pitfall page before treatment page, because the highest-risk error is missing a neurologically intact thunderclap headache.
- The acute stroke page should not treat SAH only as vasospasm management；SAH workflow begins with headache-triggered diagnosis and source-of-bleeding identification.
- Ottawa SAH Rule is a high-sensitivity screening tool for a narrow population, not a broad discharge rule for all headaches.

#### Assumptions

- Adult nontraumatic suspected aneurysmal SAH。
- Emergency setting with access to CT, LP, CTA/MRA and neurology/neurosurgery consultation。
- The source assumes interpretation by appropriately trained radiology/neurovascular teams when early CT or angiography is used for rule-out decisions。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Sentinel headache incidence and mechanism are uncertain because retrospective data can be biased。
- The exact role of CT-CTA instead of CT-LP remains debated；incidental aneurysm discovery is a meaningful downstream harm。
- Six-hour CT rule-out depends on clear onset time, modern scanner, technical adequacy, expert interpretation, normal neurologic exam and absence of caveats such as anemia or meningismus。
- Spectrophotometry is more sensitive for xanthochromia but often unavailable in North American hospitals and has limited specificity。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Sudden or rapidly peaking severe headache should trigger SAH evaluation regardless of prior migraine/tension headache history。
- Do not dismiss SAH because the patient is awake, alert or neurologically intact。
- First test: noncontrast head CT。
- CT negative does not automatically end evaluation；LP is still typical unless strict early-CT exception criteria are met。
- LP interpretation must consider opening pressure, RBC count pattern and xanthochromia timing。
- After SAH is confirmed, vascular imaging is mandatory: CTA is fast, DSA is gold standard。
- Negative first angiography is not always reassuring；repeat DSA in 4-14 days is needed for most nonperimesencephalic patterns。

## Research-Useful Points

- Diagnostic studies should separate rule-out strategies by time from onset, scanner generation, radiologist expertise, anemia and exam status。
- Ottawa SAH Rule is sensitive but low specificity; research utility is in minimizing missed SAH, not reducing most testing。
- CT-LP versus CT-CTA comparison must include incidental aneurysm detection, downstream procedures and patient-centered outcomes。
- Angiography-negative SAH is heterogeneous; perimesencephalic pattern should be separated from diffuse aneurysmal-pattern SAH.

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「SAH 一定看起來很病重」衝突：source 指出不少患者可 awake, alert, neurologically intact。
- 與「CT negative 就排除 SAH」衝突：大多數 CT negative 且仍有 suspicion 的患者仍需 LP；六小時 rule-out 有嚴格條件。
- 與「頭痛位置可判斷 SAH」衝突：source 指出 headache location 不有用。
- 與「CTA 可完全取代 LP」衝突：source 偏好標準 CT→LP workflow，CTA 主要在 CT/CSF positive 後找 symptomatic aneurysm。
