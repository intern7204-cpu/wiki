# Source Summary: Goulding et al. 2023 - Priming Exercise and VO2 Kinetics

## Source Type

- Review article.
- Journal: *Sports Medicine*.
- Citation: Goulding RP, Burnley M, Wust RCI. *How Priming Exercise Affects Oxygen Uptake Kinetics: From Underpinning Mechanisms to Endurance Performance.* 2023.
- DOI: `10.1007/s40279-023-01832-1`.
- Accepted: 2023-02-27; published online: 2023-04-03.
- 原始檔：`C:\原始資料\s40279-023-01832-1\s40279-023-01832-1.md`
- 本輪只處理此一篇來源；未混入 Goulding 2021、Goulding & Marwood 2023、Caen 2021、Skiba / W'BAL 或其他 VO2 kinetics / critical power 來源。

## Reliability Level

- Evidence tier: Tier 4 high-quality narrative / mechanistic review.
- 可信度：medium-high。
- 理由：本文整理約 25 年 priming exercise literature，並以 43 篇 prior leg exercise studies 回顧性分析 baseline `tauVO2` 與 priming-induced `tauVO2` change 的關係；但它不是 systematic review 或 meta-analysis，performance recommendation 有一部分是 authors' hypothesis。

## One-Sentence Summary

Prior heavy / severe exercise 可透過降低 `tauVO2`、增加 fundamental phase amplitude 或降低 `VO2 slow component` 改變後續 VO2 kinetics；最可能的核心機制是 intracellular O2 utilization upregulation 與 altered motor unit recruitment，而 performance benefit 需依 CP / W' 框架與 recovery duration 解讀。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Priming Exercise Effect on VO2 Kinetics

#### One-Sentence Definition

Priming exercise effect on VO2 kinetics 指 prior heavy / severe exercise 改變後續運動開始時 pulmonary / muscle VO2 response 的相位組成，而不是單純「熱身後 VO2 一定比較快」。

#### Known Facts

- 1996 年 Gerbino et al. 報告 prior heavy-intensity warm-up 可加快後續 heavy exercise 的 pulmonary VO2 kinetics，後續文獻稱為 priming effect。
- `VO2` on-transient 需分成 cardiodynamic phase、fundamental phase 與 slow component；若只用整段 monoexponential fit，可能把 slow component 減少誤讀成 `tauVO2` 真的變快。
- Priming 後的 overall VO2 response 可由三種相位改變造成：
  - lower fundamental phase `tauVO2`
  - increased fundamental phase VO2 amplitude
  - decreased VO2 slow component amplitude
- 在 healthy young active upright cycling 中，典型 priming effect 常是 increased fundamental amplitude + reduced slow component，而不一定是 true `tauVO2` reduction。
- 在 supine / prone exercise、healthy elderly、type 2 diabetes mellitus、heart failure，或 baseline `tauVO2` 較慢的人，prior heavy exercise 較可能降低後續 `tauVO2`。
- 作者回顧 43 個 1996-2021 年 prior leg exercise studies，指出 non-primed `tauVO2` 約 `<= 25 s` 時，bout 2 `tauVO2` 常接近 line of identity；當 non-primed `tauVO2` 約 `> 25 s` 時，bout 2 `tauVO2` 較常低於 bout 1。
- Residual lactic acidosis unlikely 是主要因果機制：priming effect 可在沒有 systemic lactic acidosis 的情境出現，且 prior exercise 換成不同 muscle group 時效果減弱，不能只用 systemic lactate 解釋。
- Increased muscle temperature 可在 isolated fibre 或 cooling / heating manipulation 中影響 mitochondrial respiration，但 human in vivo passive heating 多數無法重現 priming-like pulmonary VO2 kinetics change。
- Priming 多數會增加 convective / microvascular O2 delivery；這可能與 reduced VO2 slow component 有關，也可能在 O2 delivery constrained populations 中較重要。
- 但 increased O2 delivery alone 不足以解釋 `tauVO2` priming effect；single-fibre、rat hindlimb 與部分 human studies 顯示不改變 O2 delivery-to-utilization ratio 仍可出現 priming-like kinetics change。
- Altered motor unit recruitment 可解釋 increased fundamental amplitude 與 reduced slow component：prior exercise 可能讓較多 motor units 在 exercise onset 即被招募，減少後續 progressive recruitment。
- Motor unit recruitment evidence 主要來自 surface EMG 與少數 31P-MRS / EMG study；作者明確提醒 bipolar EMG 對 true motor unit pool output 的解釋能力有限。
- Enhanced intracellular O2 utilization 有較強 mechanistic support：single muscle fibre 和 animal data 顯示第二次 contraction onset 可更快調整 intracellular PO2 / microvascular PO2，即使 baseline PO2 或 delivery ratio 沒變。
- PDH activation / substrate availability 是可能機制，但 human DCA studies 即使增加 PDH activation，仍未一致改變 pulmonary VO2 kinetics；isolated PDH activation 的因果關係不確定。
- Parallel activation of ATP-consuming and ATP-producing processes 以及 mitochondrial calcium accumulation 被作者列為 plausible mechanism；但 calcium role 仍屬 speculative，需要 single fibre、serial biopsy 或 simulation studies 驗證。
- Performance effect 取決於 prior exercise intensity、criterion exercise intensity、recovery duration，以及兩者相對於 critical power 的位置。
- Prior severe-intensity priming 在 recovery 太短時可能降低後續 performance；prior heavy-intensity exercise 且只造成 modest lactate increase 約 `2-3 mmol/L` 時，文獻中較常見穩定 improvement。
- 若 priming 降低 `tauVO2` 而 phase amplitudes 不變，critical power 較可能增加。
- 若 priming 主要增加 fundamental amplitude 並降低 slow component，且 `tauVO2` 不變，`W'` 較可能增加而 critical power 不變。
- 作者推測 high aerobic fitness / rapid baseline kinetics 的人，priming benefit 可能主要限於 supra-CP、約 2-30 分鐘事件中的 `W'` improvement；untrained 或 recreationally active individuals 若 `tauVO2` 較慢，可能透過 CP increase 受益於更長事件。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
prior heavy / severe exercise
-> residual intracellular activation plus altered muscle recruitment state
-> possible improvement in microvascular O2 availability
-> faster or larger early oxidative response during criterion exercise
-> either lower tauVO2 or larger fundamental amplitude
-> reduced O2 deficit and/or smaller VO2 slow component
-> lower fatigue-related metabolite accumulation
-> performance effect maps to CP if tauVO2 falls, or to W' if amplitude / slow component changes dominate
```

#### Inferences

- `Priming effect` should be reported by the affected VO2 phase, not just as "faster VO2 kinetics."
- For athletes with already rapid baseline `tauVO2`, priming may be more useful for short supra-CP work capacity than for raising CP.
- For people with slow baseline `tauVO2`, including some older or chronic disease groups, priming could have a larger `tauVO2` effect, but this review does not establish a routine clinical prescription.
- Performance studies that do not locate the prior and criterion bouts relative to CP can easily misclassify why priming helps, fails, or harms performance.

#### Assumptions

- The fundamental phase / slow component partition adequately captures the physiologically meaningful VO2 response.
- CP is the best available boundary for interpreting heavy vs severe domain exercise in performance studies.
- `tauVO2 < 25 s` is a practical threshold from the authors' retrospective synthesis, not a universal individual diagnostic cutoff.
- Review-level inference from single-fibre, animal, and in vivo human evidence can identify plausible mechanisms, but not final causality.

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- This is a narrative / mechanistic review, not a systematic review or meta-analysis.
- Optimal priming intensity and recovery duration remain unclear.
- Mitochondrial calcium and parallel activation mechanisms are plausible but not directly proven in human dynamic exercise.
- Surface EMG evidence for altered motor unit recruitment is limited by sampling and signal interpretation problems.
- Clinical translation to heart failure, type 2 diabetes, older adults, pediatric patients, neurologic disease, or rehabilitation populations remains conditional.
- The suggested event-duration mapping, such as 2-30 min for trained individuals and >30 min for untrained / recreational individuals, is explicitly hypothesis-generating.

## Clinically Useful Points

- In CPET interpretation, do not interpret prior exercise as a generic warm-up state; ask whether it changed `tauVO2`, fundamental amplitude, or slow component.
- In clinical exercise prescription, this review should not be used to justify severe priming bouts in vulnerable patients.
- If a patient or athlete has slow baseline VO2 kinetics, prior exercise may alter `tauVO2` more than it would in a highly trained athlete with already fast kinetics.
- For rehabilitation, any priming-like strategy would need safety screening, domain-specific intensity control, recovery duration control, and monitoring of symptoms, not just physiology appeal.

## Research-Useful Points

- Future priming studies should measure CP / W' or otherwise define the heavy-severe boundary.
- VO2 kinetics fitting should partition phase II and slow component instead of relying on whole-response monoexponential summaries.
- Studies should stratify by baseline `tauVO2`, training status, posture / modality, and clinical O2 delivery limitation.
- Mechanistic work needs methods that can separate O2 delivery, intracellular utilization, and motor unit recruitment.

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「priming exercise = lactate / acidosis facilitation」衝突；本文認為 lactic acidosis 不是 quantitatively large causal mechanism。
- 與「warming muscle reproduces priming」衝突；human passive heating literature 多數沒有產生相同 pulmonary VO2 kinetics response。
- 與「O2 delivery alone explains faster kinetics」衝突；本文支持 O2 delivery contributes，但 intracellular utilization 和 motor unit recruitment 更能解釋主要現象。
- 與「overall mean response time 變短 = tauVO2 變快」衝突；未拆 fundamental phase 與 slow component 時，這個判讀不成立。
- 與「priming 一定提升 performance」衝突；benefit depends on intensity relative to CP and recovery duration，severe priming recovery 太短時可能有害。
