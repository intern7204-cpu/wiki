## One-Sentence Summary

Adult ischemic stroke prognosis 不能只用單一 NIHSS 或單一 imaging result 判斷，而要把 acute severity、age、infarct size/location、mechanism、comorbidities、complications、intervention response、recovery pace and domain-specific function 放在同一個動態模型。

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Adult Ischemic Stroke Prognosis Framing

#### One-Sentence Definition

Adult ischemic stroke prognosis framing 是在急性期與早期復健期，用 neurologic severity、age、neuroimaging、stroke mechanism、comorbidity、complications and evolving recovery trajectory 估計 mortality、disability、community discharge and domain-specific function 的臨床推理框架。

#### Known Facts

- Source states worldwide 30-day case fatality after first ischemic stroke ranges roughly 13.5-23 percent；summary section gives 16-23 percent。
- Even minor ischemic stroke can carry diminished long-term prognosis；one 10-year follow-up reported 32 percent cumulative mortality after minor ischemic stroke。
- Six-month deficits in an older community sample included hemiparesis 50 percent, cognitive deficits 46 percent, hemianopia 20 percent, aphasia 19 percent and sensory deficits 15 percent。
- Six-month disability measures included depression symptoms 35 percent, inability to walk unassisted 31 percent, social disability 30 percent, institutionalization 26 percent and bladder incontinence 22 percent。
- Acute-phase strongest predictors are stroke severity and age。
- Stroke severity should be judged clinically and by infarct size/location on MRI or CT；NIHSS is useful but misses some functionally important deficits。
- Each additional NIHSS point in one trial analysis reduced odds of excellent three-month outcome; NIHSS <=6 predicted good recovery, whereas NIHSS >=16 was associated with high probability of death or severe disability in that study context。
- Prognostic value of NIHSS changes over time; later assessment from 1-10 days may be more reliable than first presentation alone。
- Post-treatment NIHSS after IV thrombolysis or mechanical thrombectomy may predict outcome better than pretreatment NIHSS。
- Infarct volume, large artery occlusion, poor collateral flow, diffusion-perfusion mismatch, cerebral edema, cardioembolic or large artery mechanism, comorbidities and complications can worsen prognosis。
- Most recovery occurs during the first three to six months, but some patients continue to improve up to 18 months。
- Early voluntary finger extension / shoulder abduction predicts better arm and hand recovery; early sitting balance and paretic leg contraction predict independent ambulation。
- OPS and Reding three-factor approach are used in rehab settings; other global models exist but are not established as generally valid for routine clinical practice。
- Outcome-improving interventions include appropriate reperfusion therapy, stroke unit care, rehabilitation and secondary prevention。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
arterial occlusion / stenosis / embolism
-> infarct burden + location + collateral / reperfusion status
-> neurologic impairment severity and early instability
-> complications, comorbidity burden and treatment response modify trajectory
-> early impairment recovery and domain-specific function
-> ADL independence, walking, communication, swallowing, return to work, participation and survival
```

#### Inferences

- Prognosis should be updated repeatedly rather than stated once at ED presentation.
- Rehab planning should use both global scales and domain-specific assessments because NIHSS and mRS underrepresent hand function, balance, cognition, work and participation。
- Family counseling should distinguish population-level risk from individual uncertainty。

#### Assumptions

- This page applies to adult ischemic stroke, especially acute to early rehabilitation phase。
- The local clinical setting can access neuroimaging, serial neurologic examination and rehabilitation-domain assessment。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Prognostic models may not generalize across posterior circulation / infratentorial stroke, pediatric stroke, different health systems or rehabilitation access。
- Socioeconomic, race/ethnicity and sex associations may be confounded by age, severity, comorbidity, care access and rehabilitation dose。
- Corticospinal tract integrity markers are promising but not widely used clinically。

## Clinically Useful Points

- Do not give a definitive prognosis from the first NIHSS alone.
- Reassess after reperfusion therapy and after the first several days because the neurologic deficit may improve, deteriorate or reveal complications。
- Use prognosis to structure goals, discharge planning and time-limited trials, not to prematurely withdraw appropriate acute care or rehabilitation。
- For upper limb prognosis, early finger extension, grasp release and shoulder abduction matter more than a vague note of "arm weakness"。
- For walking prognosis, sitting balance, leg contraction / strength, cognition, neglect and continence should be checked early。
- mRS / Barthel help global outcome tracking, but domain-specific scales remain necessary。

## Research-Useful Points

- Studies should separate mortality, mRS / Barthel, walking, arm dexterity, aphasia, swallowing, neglect, return to work and participation。
- Prognostic model validation should specify timing of assessment, treatment received, stroke territory and care setting。
- Trial outcomes relying only on mRS may miss meaningful domain-specific recovery。
