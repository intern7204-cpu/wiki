# Moyamoya Disease與Moyamoya Syndrome 病因分層

## One-Sentence Definition

Moyamoya disease (MMD) 與 Moyamoya syndrome (MMS) 的病因分層，是在確認 moyamoya angiographic pattern 後，判斷它是 genetic susceptibility without associated condition 的 MMD，還是由 underlying medical condition 伴隨產生的 MMS。

## Definition and Boundary

Moyamoya 本身是 distal ICA / circle of Willis stenosis or occlusion 加上 prominent collateral vessels 的血管影像型態。MMD 指 primary / idiopathic moyamoya，MMS 指 same angiographic pattern 出現在 sickle cell disease、cranial irradiation、NF1、Down syndrome、thyroid disease、autoimmune disease 等 associated condition 脈絡中。

本頁只處理 terminology、etiology、associated conditions 與分類邊界。臨床觸發與影像診斷見 [[Moyamoya_臨床辨識與診斷影像]]；治療決策、revascularization 與 antithrombotic boundary 見 [[Moyamoya_治療決策與Revascularization]]、[[Moyamoya_Antithrombotic與急性支持管理]]。

## Why It Matters

把所有 moyamoya 都當成 idiopathic disease 會漏掉 MMS 的 underlying condition，也會錯過 secondary prevention、genetic counseling、family history、sickle cell disease、autoimmune / thyroid disease 或 radiation vasculopathy 的處理脈絡。相反地，只看到一個 associated condition 就忽略 angiographic criteria，也會把其他 intracranial stenosis mimic 誤分到 MMS。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 已有或疑似 moyamoya angiographic pattern。
- 有 CTA / MRA / DSA 或待安排 vascular imaging。
- 需同時取得：
  - 年齡、族群與 family history。
  - prior cranial irradiation / tumor / meningitis / trauma。
  - sickle cell disease 或其他 hematologic / hypercoagulable condition。
  - thyroid / autoimmune / vasculitic symptoms。
  - NF1、Down syndrome、Noonan syndrome、Turner syndrome 等 genetic / developmental disorder。
  - renal artery stenosis、fibromuscular dysplasia、congenital heart disease 等 systemic vascular clues。

## Mechanism

```text
Susceptibility or associated condition
-> abnormal vascular wall response with intimal thickening and angiogenesis
-> distal ICA / proximal ACA-MCA stenosis or occlusion
-> basal, leptomeningeal and transdural collateral formation
-> chronic hypoperfusion plus fragile collateral vessels
-> ischemic or hemorrhagic cerebrovascular presentation
```

## Observable Patterns

- MMD：moyamoya angiographic pattern plus genetic susceptibility / family tendency, without underlying contributing medical condition。
- MMS：moyamoya angiographic pattern plus associated condition implicated in vascular change。
- East Asian ancestry and family history raise MMD probability。
- RNF213 p.R4810K is a major East Asian susceptibility factor and is associated with earlier onset / more severe progression in this source。
- Pediatric / young adult cases with recurrent ischemic events, syndromic features or hematologic disease should trigger MMS search。
- Adult cases with unilateral findings or atherosclerotic risk factors need careful mimic exclusion before assigning MMD。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Classification should be documented explicitly as MMD, MMS, or moyamoya vasculopathy pending etiologic workup。
- MMS workup should be individualized by age and clinical clues rather than using one universal lab panel。
- Genetic counseling/testing is appropriate when known genetic predisposition exists and may be considered when MMS evaluation is unrevealing。
- Studies should avoid mixing MMD and MMS without stratification。

## Fact

- Moyamoya refers to unilateral or bilateral stenosis / occlusion around the circle of Willis with prominent arterial collateral circulation。
- MMD is moyamoya angiographic findings with associated genetic susceptibility but no underlying contributing medical condition。
- MMS is moyamoya angiographic findings with an associated medical condition implicated in vascular change。
- The terminal ICAs and proximal MCA / ACA are most involved。
- Some unilateral cases progress to bilateral involvement in approximately one-third of patients over two to eight years。
- Collateral patterns include basal moyamoya vessels, leptomeningeal collaterals and transdural anastomoses。
- Small-vessel aneurysms are a major cause of ICH and IVH in moyamoya。
- MMD is more common in Japan, China and Korea than elsewhere。
- Familial occurrence is approximately 10 to 15 percent。
- Annual incidence rates cited in this source range from 0.35 to 0.94 per 100,000 and prevalence from 3.2 to 10.5 per 100,000。
- Age distribution is bimodal: 5 to 10 years and 30 to 50 years。
- Associated MMS conditions include cranial irradiation、sickle cell disease、thyroid / autoimmune disease、NF1、Down syndrome and multiple other systemic conditions。

## Inference

- A young patient with triggered recurrent TIA and no classic vascular risk factors should be evaluated for moyamoya pattern and associated conditions before being labeled cryptogenic。
- An adult with unilateral stenosis and diabetes / tobacco / dyslipidemia needs careful distinction from intracranial atherosclerosis。
- A pediatric patient with NF1 or sickle cell disease plus stroke symptoms should raise MMS suspicion earlier than a typical adult stroke pathway。

## Assumption

- Vascular imaging is interpretable enough to confirm or refute moyamoya pattern。
- Associated conditions are not assumed from demographics alone。
- The clinician separates diagnostic classification from treatment indication。

## Uncertainty

- MMD etiology is still unknown despite RNF213 and other genetic associations。
- Inheritance pattern is not established。
- The causal strength of each MMS-associated condition varies。
- The source does not define treatment benefit by etiologic category。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「moyamoya = MMD。」正確是先確認 angiographic pattern，再分 MMD vs MMS。
- 誤讀：「RNF213 positive 就能診斷 MMD。」此來源仍以 angiographic findings 為診斷核心。
- 誤讀：「adult onset 就不是 moyamoya。」此來源指出 adult peak 在 30 to 50 years。
- 誤讀：「unilateral moyamoya 不算。」DSA criteria 可診斷 unilateral findings；但 noninvasive unilateral findings 通常需要 DSA。
