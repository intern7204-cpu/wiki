# AF Stroke Anticoagulation Failure

## One-Sentence Definition

AF stroke anticoagulation failure 是 AF patient 在 DOAC 或 VKA therapy 期間仍發生 ischemic stroke / TIA，需重新檢查 dose、adherence、renal function、drug interactions、LAA thrombus 與 competing stroke mechanism，而不是直接把事件等同藥物無效。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 breakthrough ischemic stroke / TIA during anticoagulation for AF。

不處理：

- primary prevention 的 anticoagulation initiation。
- acute stroke reperfusion protocol。
- non-AF antiplatelet failure；見 [[Ischemic_Stroke_Long_term_Antithrombotic_Therapy]]。
- general AF anticoagulant dosing table。

## Why It Matters

"Anticoagulation failure" 這個標籤很容易導致錯誤處置：任意換 DOAC、把 warfarin 改成 antiplatelet add-on、或忽略 carotid stenosis / lacunar infarct / malignancy 等 competing mechanisms。正確流程是先判斷病人是否真的有 therapeutic exposure，以及這次 stroke 是否真的是 AF cardioembolism。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Confirmed ischemic stroke / TIA。
- Patient was prescribed DOAC or VKA for AF before event。
- 可取得 medication history、renal function、interaction list、INR if VKA、brain / vascular imaging，並能在需要時安排 TEE。

## Mechanism

```text
breakthrough stroke on anticoagulation
-> four broad possibilities:
   1. no / low anticoagulant exposure
   2. wrong dose, renal issue, or drug interaction
   3. persistent cardioembolism / LAA thrombus despite therapy
   4. competing noncardioembolic mechanism
-> targeted correction beats reflex drug stacking
```

## Evaluation Workflow

1. Confirm event: ischemic stroke / TIA vs mimic vs hemorrhage.
2. Confirm exposure:
   - DOAC: missed doses, refill gap, dosing frequency, renal function, body weight, age-based reduction criteria, P-gp / CYP3A4 interactions.
   - Warfarin: INR at event, time in therapeutic range, diet/drug interaction, adherence.
3. Reassess stroke mechanism:
   - brain imaging pattern.
   - neurovascular imaging for stenosis, occlusion, dissection or other vascular disease.
   - TTE / TEE if cardiac/aortic source would change management.
   - selected labs if malignancy, APS, inflammation or other cause is suspected.
4. Look for LAA thrombus:
   - LAA thrombus suggests inadequate exposure, adherence problem or persistent high-risk substrate.
5. Decide intervention:
   - optimize current regimen if exposure was low.
   - address competing mechanism separately.
   - consider agent change only after cause review.

## Observable Patterns

### DOAC breakthrough

- Source states ischemic stroke during DOAC therapy is associated with lower-than-recommended dose and/or poor adherence.
- LAA thrombus should trigger dose and compliance reassessment.
- Off-label DOAC underdosing, atrial enlargement, hyperlipidemia and higher CHA2DS2-VASc score were associated with events in a cited study.
- If thrombus is present despite appropriate dosing and compliance, changing to another DOAC may be reasonable, but optimal treatment is uncertain and no consensus exists.
- A retrospective cohort cited by the source found higher recurrent ischemic stroke risk after switching to another DOAC or warfarin compared with unchanged DOAC; this does not prove switching causes harm, but prevents treating switching as automatic.

### Warfarin breakthrough

- Subtherapeutic INR is common.
- If ischemic stroke occurs with therapeutic INR 2 to 3, source favors switching to DOAC rather than routine antiplatelet addition when DOAC is not contraindicated.
- If DOAC is contraindicated, options include increasing INR target to 2.5 to 3.5 or LAA occlusion.
- Routine antiplatelet addition increases major hemorrhage, especially brain hemorrhage, and its benefit is less defined than optimizing anticoagulation.

### Competing mechanism

- In a source-cited analysis, DOAC breakthrough etiology was cardioembolism 49 percent, poor adherence or insufficient dose 23 percent, and competing mechanism 28 percent.
- In VKA breakthrough, etiology was cardioembolism 37 percent, poor adherence or insufficient dose 43 percent, and competing mechanism 20 percent.
- Therefore a large minority of cases are not solved by simply changing anticoagulant.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Medication reconciliation after stroke on anticoagulation should include "exposure audit" rather than only "continue vs switch."
- TEE is useful if it changes whether LAA thrombus, aortic plaque or another cardiac embolic source is suspected.
- If a competing mechanism is found, AF anticoagulation usually still remains indicated, but the competing cause may require separate treatment such as revascularization, antiplatelet strategy, malignancy workup or risk-factor control.

## Fact

- The source states all AF patients with ischemic stroke despite VKA or DOAC should have thorough evaluation for cardioembolic and noncardioembolic mechanisms.
- The source states missed DOAC doses and subtherapeutic VKA intensity are common causes.
- The source provides etiologic proportions for breakthrough stroke on DOAC and VKA as listed above.
- The source states LAA thrombus suggests reassessment of dosing and compliance.
- The source states correct DOAC dose and compliance should be verified.
- The source states changing DOAC is reasonable in selected thrombus-present cases but optimal treatment is uncertain.
- The source states warfarin breakthrough at therapeutic INR favors switch to DOAC rather than routine antiplatelet addition when DOAC is not contraindicated.
- The source states antiplatelet addition increases major hemorrhage, particularly brain hemorrhage.

## Inference

- "Treatment failure" should be reserved for cases where exposure was appropriate and competing mechanisms were reasonably excluded.
- Dose reduction for frailty or age should be checked against agent-specific criteria; underdosing can create false safety.
- A recurrent event while anticoagulated should prompt interdisciplinary review, not purely automatic pharmacy substitution.

## Assumption

- The patient had an actual prescribed anticoagulant before the event.
- Event timing relative to last dose or INR is available or reconstructable.
- Imaging and vascular workup are sufficient to identify common competing mechanisms.

## Uncertainty

- Optimal DOAC switching strategy after confirmed adequate exposure remains unsettled.
- Observational data comparing switching versus unchanged DOAC may be confounded.
- Whether LAA occlusion improves outcome in all anticoagulation failure phenotypes requires separate source review.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 "stroke on DOAC" 直接寫成 DOAC failure；先查 missed dose、dose reduction、renal function and interactions。
- 不可把 "therapeutic INR" 等同沒有其他病因；仍需 vascular and cardiac reevaluation。
- 不可 routine 加 antiplatelet 來補強 anticoagulation；bleeding risk 很可能上升。
- 不可在找到 competing mechanism 後忘記 AF 仍可能需要 chronic anticoagulation。
