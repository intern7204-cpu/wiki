# Aneurysmal SAH 急性治療與預後管理

## One-Sentence Definition

Aneurysmal SAH 急性治療與預後管理是 ruptured intracranial saccular aneurysm 造成 subarachnoid hemorrhage 後，從 stabilization、aneurysm securement、neurocritical monitoring、DCI / vasospasm treatment、ICP / hydrocephalus management 到 long-term morbidity surveillance 的連續流程。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 **aneurysmal SAH treatment and prognosis**。

不處理：

- Aneurysmal SAH epidemiology / pathogenesis / risk factors。
- Aneurysmal SAH clinical manifestations and diagnosis；見 [[Aneurysmal_SAH_臨床表現與診斷分流]]。
- Unruptured intracranial aneurysm。
- Nonaneurysmal SAH / perimesencephalic SAH。
- Ruptured mycotic aneurysm。
- Full technical selection between clipping and coiling。

## Why It Matters

Aneurysmal SAH 不是「診斷後給 nimodipine」這麼單純。早期死亡與 disability 來自多條同時進行的路徑：

- unsecured aneurysm rebleeding。
- ICP surge / hydrocephalus。
- seizure、cardiopulmonary instability、hyponatremia。
- days 4-14 的 delayed cerebral ischemia / vasospasm。
- long-term cognitive、mood、sleep、epilepsy and participation sequelae。

因此治療頁的核心不是單一 drug，而是 neurocritical workflow。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult aneurysmal nontraumatic SAH 已診斷或高度懷疑。
- Ruptured aneurysm 需由 CTA / DSA 等 vascular imaging 定位。
- 若所在醫院缺乏 neurocritical care、vascular neurosurgery / endovascular specialists，需在 stabilization 後考慮轉至 high-volume center。
- Aneurysm 是否已 secured 會改變 BP、activity、VTE prophylaxis 與 vasospasm treatment 邏輯。
- 若 SAH 造成 mechanical ventilation、預期 major functional/cognitive dependency 或 long-term artificial nutrition，需同步啟動 [[Stroke_Neuropalliative_Care與Goals_of_Care]]；這不等於放棄 aneurysm repair 或 neurocritical care。

## Mechanism

```text
Aneurysmal SAH
→ subarachnoid blood + unsecured ruptured aneurysm
→ early rebleeding risk + ICP/hydrocephalus + seizures + autonomic/cardiopulmonary instability
→ stabilize airway/circulation/seizures
→ stop antithrombotics + reverse anticoagulation + control BP without hypotension + maintain euvolemia
→ early clipping/coiling secures aneurysm and reduces rebleeding risk
→ days 4-14 vasospasm/DCI risk
→ nimodipine + euvolemia + monitoring; treat DCI after aneurysm securement
→ long-term recovery shaped by initial grade, blood burden, complications and neurocognitive / epilepsy / mood sequelae
```

## Acute Workflow

### 1. Stabilize and Grade

- Initial care prioritizes airway、cardiovascular function and seizures。
- Intubation is considered with GCS <=8、elevated ICP、poor oxygenation / hypoventilation、hemodynamic instability 或需要 heavy sedation/paralysis。
- Severity should be graded after stabilization using clinical grade and hemorrhage burden。
- Hunt and Hess / WFNS reflect neurologic grade；Fisher / modified Fisher estimate vasospasm / DCI risk from CT blood pattern。

### 2. Transfer or Admit to Expert Center

- Aneurysmal SAH is best managed in high-volume neurocritical centers with neurovascular surgery、endovascular and neurocritical care capability。
- This matters because aneurysm repair, EVD/ICP care, DCI monitoring and rescue treatment are time-sensitive and resource-dependent。

### 3. Initial Symptomatic Management

- Stop all antithrombotics and reverse anticoagulation until aneurysm is repaired。
- Before aneurysm securement, treat marked hypertension gradually while avoiding hypotension。
- Source uses SBP <160 mmHg or MAP <110 mmHg for most patients, individualized by brain swelling、renal risk、baseline BP and ICP/CPP concerns。
- Maintain euvolemia, usually with normal saline；hypovolemia increases ischemic complication risk。
- Avoid fluid restriction for SAH hyponatremia because it can worsen intravascular volume contraction and ischemic risk。
- Give nimodipine 60 mg every 4 hours orally or via NG tube for 21 days; never IV。
- Bedrest、analgesia、stool softener、VTE prophylaxis and frequent neuro checks are supportive measures, not substitutes for aneurysm repair。

### 4. Secure the Aneurysm

- Surgical clipping or endovascular coiling is the only effective treatment to prevent rebleeding。
- Repair should occur as early as feasible, preferably within 24 hours。
- Rebleeding risk is highest early: 4-14% in the first 24 hours, maximal in the first 2-12 hours；mortality after rebleeding may be as high as 70%。
- Longer time to treatment、worse neurologic status、larger aneurysm、high SBP、intracerebral / intraventricular blood、acute hydrocephalus and incomplete obliteration increase rebleeding risk。

### 5. Monitor for DCI / Vasospasm

- DCI occurs in approximately 30% of aneurysmal SAH。
- Vasospasm typically occurs days 4-14 and peaks around days 7-8。
- Monitoring may include frequent neuro checks、TCD、repeat CT / CTA / CTP in selected protocols、DSA when needed and EEG in poor-grade or unexplained deterioration。
- A new focal deficit, reduced consciousness, seizure, worsening confusion or inattention should trigger evaluation for rebleeding、infarction、hydrocephalus、vasospasm、subclinical seizure and medical complications。

### 6. Treat DCI / Vasospasm After Aneurysm Securement

- Aggressive vasospasm therapy is pursued after aneurysm clipping/coiling。
- First-line treatment for new DCI is stepwise hemodynamic augmentation with induced hypertension and euvolemia, assessing response at each MAP level。
- Hypervolemia and classic triple-H therapy should not be routine。
- Refractory focal large-vessel vasospasm may require balloon angioplasty。
- Smaller or diffuse vasospasm may use intra-arterial vasodilators。

### 7. Manage ICP / Hydrocephalus

- Hydrocephalus affects 20-30% of SAH patients, often within minutes to hours but can be delayed。
- Deteriorating consciousness with ventricular enlargement / elevated ICP requires urgent CSF diversion。
- EVD is indicated for noncommunicating hydrocephalus；lumbar drainage is an alternative only for communicating hydrocephalus and is contraindicated with obstructive hydrocephalus or intraparenchymal hematoma。
- Osmotic therapy may be used when ventriculostomy is unavailable or insufficient; hyperventilation is generally avoided except temporizing herniation physiology。
- Some patients need long-term shunt after failed drainage weaning or delayed recurrent hydrocephalus。

## Observable Patterns

- Early deterioration can indicate rebleeding, hydrocephalus, ICP elevation, seizure or cardiopulmonary complication。
- Days 4-14 neurologic decline raises concern for DCI / vasospasm, especially around days 7-8。
- Hyponatremia with high urine sodium/osmolality can reflect SIADH or cerebral salt wasting; volume status is the key distinction。
- ECG changes, troponin elevation, LV dysfunction, pulmonary edema and arrhythmias may occur after SAH and can mimic primary cardiac disease。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Acute stroke pages should separate SAH diagnostic gate from SAH neurocritical treatment。
- Rehabilitation handoff after SAH should record:
  - aneurysm secured or unsecured。
  - repair method and date。
  - current DCI/vasospasm window status。
  - EVD / shunt / ICP issue。
  - seizure status and antiseizure medication plan。
  - cognition、mood、sleep、anosmia and epilepsy follow-up needs。
  - goals-of-care status、surrogate decision-maker、time-limited trial endpoint and caregiver support needs。
- Research should not collapse angiographic vasospasm, symptomatic vasospasm and DCI into one endpoint。

## Fact

- Source states patients with aneurysmal SAH should receive intensive hemodynamic、cardiac and neurologic monitoring。
- Source recommends discontinuing antithrombotics and reversing anticoagulation until aneurysm repair。
- Source uses SBP <160 mmHg or MAP <110 mmHg for most acute SAH patients while avoiding hypotension。
- Source states nimodipine improves outcomes and is standard of care, continued for 21 days。
- Source states aneurysm repair with clipping or coiling is the only effective rebleeding prevention and should preferably occur within 24 hours。
- Source states DCI occurs in approximately 30% and vasospasm usually occurs days 4-14。
- Source states hypervolemia should not routinely be used to prevent or treat DCI。
- Source states acute hydrocephalus affects 20-30% of SAH patients。
- Source states 30-day mortality approaches 30% and long-term complications include neurocognitive dysfunction, epilepsy, focal deficits and late rebleeding。

## Inference

- SAH treatment should be documented by phase: unsecured aneurysm, secured aneurysm but DCI window active, subacute complication management, recovery/follow-up。
- In rehab planning, mild motor recovery does not rule out meaningful neurocognitive or mood/sleep morbidity after SAH。
- Nimodipine should be described as outcome-improving standard therapy, not as a simple vasospasm-prevention drug。
- Severe SAH communication should avoid premature closure: palliative care can run concurrently with aneurysm securement, DCI monitoring and rehabilitation planning。

## Assumption

- Adult aneurysmal SAH。
- Treatment occurs in or with transfer to a center capable of neurocritical care and aneurysm repair。
- Patient-specific BP and DCI treatment decisions require bedside assessment of ICP/CPP, cardiac status and aneurysm securement。

## Uncertainty

- Optimal BP target before aneurysm securement remains uncertain。
- Nimodipine mechanism of benefit remains uncertain。
- Routine antifibrinolytics, lumbar drainage selection, clazosentan, transfusion thresholds and EVD weaning strategy have incomplete or mixed evidence。
- Long-term cognitive and quality-of-life outcomes vary by complications, premorbid status and measurement method。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not treat nimodipine as proof that vasospasm incidence is prevented。
- Do not use tranexamic acid or aminocaproic acid routinely as an outcome-improving standard。
- Do not apply induced hypertension for DCI before the aneurysm is secured。
- Do not use hypervolemia/triple-H as default DCI prevention。
- Do not treat good motor recovery as absence of SAH morbidity; cognitive、mood、sleep and epilepsy risks remain clinically relevant。
- Do not equate palliative care consultation with hospice or withdrawal of aneurysmal SAH treatment.
