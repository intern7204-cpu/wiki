# Ischemic Stroke Cerebral Autoregulation 與灌流閾值

## One-Sentence Definition

Cerebral autoregulation 是腦血管在一定 perfusion pressure 範圍內維持 cerebral blood flow 的能力；ischemic stroke 時 autoregulation 受損，使 systemic BP、collateral flow and cerebral perfusion pressure 對缺血組織更具決定性。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 ischemic stroke 的 cerebral autoregulation、cerebral blood flow thresholds and acute BP physiology。它不取代 IVT / EVT protocol、ICH / SAH BP management 或 chronic secondary prevention BP target。

相關急性處置見 [[Acute_Stroke_Initial_Assessment與Stabilization]] 與 [[中風急性期處置與時間窗]]；long-term BP secondary prevention 見 [[Stroke_Antihypertensive_Therapy_次發預防]]。

## Why It Matters

急性 ischemic stroke 不能把高 BP 直覺等同「該立刻降到正常」。缺血區域可能已經失去 autoregulatory reserve，perfusion 依賴 systemic pressure；若病人長期 hypertension，autoregulation curve 又可能右移。過快降壓可能讓 penumbra perfusion 更差，反過來高壓超出 autoregulatory upper limit 也可能加劇 edema。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult suspected or confirmed acute ischemic stroke。
- 已先區分 ischemic versus hemorrhagic stroke，因 hemorrhagic stroke 的 BP 目標不同。
- 本頁用於理解 physiology，不直接給藥物劑量或完整 BP protocol。

## Mechanism

```text
cerebral blood flow
-> depends on cerebral vascular resistance and cerebral perfusion pressure
-> autoregulation changes vessel diameter to buffer moderate pressure changes
-> ischemic stroke impairs autoregulation
-> pressure drop beyond compensation lowers CBF
-> oxygen extraction rises, then metabolic thresholds fail
```

### Flow Thresholds from the Source

| Approximate CBF | Physiologic consequence |
|---|---|
| <50 mL/100 g/min | protein synthesis inhibition |
| 35 mL/100 g/min | protein synthesis ceases; glucose use transiently increases |
| 25 mL/100 g/min | glucose utilization drops; anaerobic glycolysis and lactic acidosis begin |
| 16-18 mL/100 g/min | neuronal electrical failure |
| 10-12 mL/100 g/min | membrane ion homeostasis failure; typical infarct threshold |

## Observable Patterns

- Normal autoregulation often works across mean arterial pressure about 60-150 mmHg, but individual limits vary.
- Below the autoregulatory range, CBF falls passively with pressure and ischemia risk rises.
- Above the range, CBF rises passively with pressure and edema risk rises.
- Chronic hypertension shifts the autoregulatory range toward higher pressures.
- During stroke, impaired autoregulation means the usual "stable CBF despite pressure change" assumption becomes unreliable.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Acute BP decisions should be tied to stroke type, reperfusion plan and comorbid emergency indications, not just a chronic outpatient target.
- In ischemic stroke without IVT / EVT, permissive hypertension has a physiologic rationale: maintain perfusion to vulnerable tissue.
- Research using perfusion thresholds must separate physiologic model values from patient-specific imaging or monitoring data.

## Fact

- The source states cerebral blood flow is determined by cerebral vascular resistance and cerebral perfusion pressure.
- The source states autoregulation usually maintains CBF within MAP about 60-150 mmHg, with individual variation.
- The source states ischemic stroke can impair cerebral autoregulation.
- The source states oxygen extraction fraction initially increases as perfusion pressure falls.
- The source gives CBF thresholds for protein synthesis inhibition, anaerobic glycolysis, electrical failure and membrane ion homeostasis failure.
- The source states hypertension adapts autoregulation to higher arterial pressures and BP reduction to normal levels could further decrease cerebral blood flow during stroke.

## Inference

- 急性 ischemic stroke 的 BP 目標應避免把 chronic BP target 直接套用到 first hours。
- Penumbra perfusion may be pressure-dependent when autoregulatory reserve is exhausted.

## Assumption

- CBF thresholds are approximate and derived from physiologic models summarized by the source.
- The page assumes adult ischemic stroke, not pediatric stroke or isolated global hypoxic-ischemic injury.

## Uncertainty

- Autoregulation mechanisms are not fully understood.
- Individual autoregulatory limits are not routinely known at bedside.
- The source does not resolve optimal acute BP target by stroke subtype.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 60-150 mmHg 當成每個病人的固定安全範圍。
- 不可把 CBF threshold table 當作可以直接替代 CT/MRI perfusion imaging。
- 不可把 ischemic stroke 的 permissive hypertension 套到 ICH / SAH。
- 不可把 chronic BP target 直接套到 acute stroke first hours。
