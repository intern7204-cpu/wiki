# SCI 共病 TBI 與運動學習調整

## One-Sentence Definition

Co-occurring TBI in SCI 會把 rehabilitation 從單純 motor / equipment training 改寫成 cognition-aware motor learning problem。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 traumatic SCI 合併 TBI 時，cognition、learning strategy、cueing、environment 與 rehab length of stay 的調整。
- 本頁不是完整 TBI 診斷頁，也不是 general SCI functional training page。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Traumatic_Spinal_Cord_Injury]]。

## Why It Matters

- SCI 病人若合併未辨識 TBI，transfer、bed mobility、dressing、wheelchair skill 等新任務學習會變慢，錯誤可能被誤解為 noncompliance、poor motivation、poor coping 或 bad attitude。
- 這是臨床標籤風險：問題可能不是病人不配合，而是 rehab instruction 與 cognitive status 不匹配。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需主動追問或回查 injury mechanism、loss of consciousness、posttraumatic amnesia、acute documentation 與 early cognitive behavior。
- Motor vehicle collision、fall、cervical spinal injury 等情境要提高 co-occurring TBI 警覺。

## Mechanism

```text
Traumatic SCI + missed TBI
→ processing speed / new learning / attention / executive function / memory impairment
→ task decomposition and carryover failure
→ slower acquisition of transfers, bed mobility, ADL, wheelchair skills
→ need for Rancho-informed learning strategy and cognitive therapy input
```

## Observable Patterns

- 可能看到 decreased processing speed、decreased new learning、decreased verbal fluency、inattention、executive dysfunction、memory impairment。
- 功能上可能表現為 poor motor planning、spatial awareness difficulty、task carryover 差、同一技巧反覆忘記或情境轉移失敗。
- 團隊可能在沒有 TBI diagnosis 的情況下，於紀錄或 team meeting 中觀察到 brain injury-like behaviors。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Rancho VII 或以下、posttraumatic amnesia、memory impairment 或仍在 cognitive stage 的病人，來源建議以 errorless learning 為主：拆步驟、示範後立即重現、限制錯誤暴露。
- Rancho VIII 或以上且進入 associative / autonomous motor learning stage 的病人，可逐步使用 error-based learning，讓病人從錯誤中 problem-solve。
- 若病人已有能力進入 associative / autonomous stage，仍持續使用過度 errorless learning，來源指出可能降低 functional gains。
- 環境與任務調整包含 quiet environment、extra time、external cues、person-centered activity adaptation，以及 cognitive therapist assessment / recommendation。
- 需避免把成人講話幼兒化；來源特別提醒不要用 patronizing language、過度簡化語句、放慢到不自然或大聲說話來損害尊嚴。

## Fact

- Co-occurring traumatic brain injury in spinal injury at any location is estimated at 32.5%，cervical levels increase to estimated 40.4%。
- Certain risk factors such as motor vehicle collision or fall may make incidence approach 60% or more。
- Imaging-only diagnostic methods report lower rates than methods including posttraumatic amnesia。
- Co-occurring TBI is often undiagnosed or undocumented because life-saving SCI or other injury care is prioritized。
- Patients with co-occurring brain and SCI often require longer inpatient rehabilitation length of stay to reach expected outcomes for SCI level。
- Without extra learning time and individualized strategy, short- and long-term functional outcomes may decrease。

## Inference

- SCI outcome prediction by neurological level alone is insufficient when co-occurring TBI is present。
- Rehab team 若沒有調整 teaching strategy，會把 cognitive learning barrier 誤讀為 personality 或 motivation problem。

## Assumption

- Rancho Los Amigos Brain Injury Severity Scale-Revised 或同等 cognitive staging 能被合理取得或由 cognitive therapist 協助判讀。
- 目標任務仍可被拆成可示範、可重複、可回饋的 functional units。

## Uncertainty

- Incidence estimates vary widely because diagnostic criteria differ。
- Chronic SCI 中的 cognitive deficit 不一定全由 unrecognized TBI 解釋，仍需考慮 sleep、depression、medication、pain、hypoxia、fatigue 等其他因素；此點是臨床推論，非本來源直接完整展開。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「SCI 合併 TBI 就不能學。」正確是 learning strategy 要調整，且需要更多時間、環境控制與 cueing。
- 誤讀：「errorless learning 永遠最好。」正確是 Rancho VII 或以下較適合；當病人達 Rancho VIII 或以上，error-based learning 可能更符合進階 motor learning。
- 誤讀：「講更慢更大聲就是認知友善。」來源反而提醒這可能 patronizing，應改為尊重成人身份的清楚結構化教學。
