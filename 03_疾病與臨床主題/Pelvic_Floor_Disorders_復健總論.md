# Pelvic Floor Disorders 復健總論

## One-Sentence Definition

Pelvic floor disorders 是 pelvic floor muscle、viscera、peripheral nerves、lumbopelvic biomechanics、psychological context 與 chronic pain mechanism 交互造成的疼痛與排尿排便功能障礙。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../09_來源摘要/Pelvic_Floor_Disorders]] 建立總論。
- 本頁不取代 gynecology、urology、urogynecology、colorectal surgery 或 gastroenterology 的專科診斷。

## Why It Matters

- 只用 visceral diagnosis 解釋 pelvic pain 會漏掉 pelvic floor myofascial pain、pudendal / border nerve pain、SIJ / hip / lumbar contributors 與 coordination disorder。
- 只教 Kegel 也可能錯，因為 high-tone 或 dyssynergic pelvic floor 可能需要 downtraining and relaxation。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Exam 必須有清楚說明、同意、隱私、chaperone、可隨時停止的權利。
- 若有 infection、malignancy、fracture、acute neurologic deficit、severe visceral red flag、unexplained bleeding 或需 specialist evaluation 的 finding，先轉介。

## Mechanism

```text
PFM increased tone / pain / decreased tone / dyssynergia
-> abnormal contraction-relaxation-support pattern
-> pelvic pain, dyspareunia, urinary or fecal leakage, urgency, constipation
-> avoidance, anxiety, reduced sexual and social participation
-> mechanism-matched PFPT + interdisciplinary care
```

## Observable Patterns

- Increased tone：high resting resistance、impaired relaxation、tender taut bands、pain reproduction。
- Decreased tone：introital / anal gaping、levator or sphincter defect、weak endurance。
- Coordination disorder：voiding / defecation 時 paradoxical contraction 或 failure to relax。
- Pelvic floor myofascial pain：deep / internal pain、sitting intolerance、dyspareunia、dysuria、urgency、dyschezia 或 painful defecation。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- PFPT prescription should state suspected mechanism: downtraining, reverse Kegel, relaxation, biofeedback, myofascial release, dilator work, strengthening, coordination training, or education.
- Chronic pelvic pain should be framed separately from acute pelvic floor pain because central sensitization and overlapping pain syndromes may dominate.
- Neurogenic bowel / bladder patients can try PFPT but expectations differ when underlying neurologic impairment drives symptoms.

## Fact

- 來源指出 pelvic floor disorders occur in both sexes.
- 來源指出 PFM dysfunction categories include increased tone, pain, decreased tone, coordination disorders, and pudendal neuralgia as a separately discussed condition.
- 來源指出 PFPT is first-line treatment for pelvic floor myofascial pain.
- 來源指出 recent RCTs for vaginal diazepam did not show clear benefit for tone or pain and systemic uptake / dependency risk must be considered.

## Inference

- Pelvic floor assessment should be symptom-mechanism based rather than specialty-silo based.
- Generic strengthening may worsen or fail high-tone pelvic floor symptoms if relaxation is the missing function.

## Assumption

- Patient can tolerate consent-based assessment, or the clinician adapts the evaluation without forcing internal exam.

## Uncertainty

- Evidence for injections, botulinum toxin, suppositories, and chronic pelvic pain medication strategies varies and needs current literature review.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「Pelvic floor disorder 只發生在女性」不成立。
- 「Pelvic pain 檢查沒有器官病就是心理問題」不成立；musculoskeletal and neuropathic sources may be present.
- 「所有 pelvic floor 問題都做 Kegel」不成立。
