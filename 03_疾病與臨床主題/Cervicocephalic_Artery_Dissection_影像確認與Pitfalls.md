# Cervicocephalic Artery Dissection 影像確認與 Pitfalls

## One-Sentence Definition

Cervicocephalic artery dissection 的影像確認，是用 urgent head/neck MRI/MRA 或 CT/CTA 尋找 long tapered stenosis / occlusion、pseudoaneurysm、intimal flap、double lumen 或 intramural hematoma，並在高 suspicion 但初始影像陰性時用 complementary 或 repeat imaging 補足盲點。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 dissection 的 imaging confirmation 與 diagnostic pitfalls。

不處理 treatment、antithrombotic choice、revascularization、serial follow-up schedule 或 prognosis。何時懷疑 dissection 見 [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_臨床辨識]]。

## Why It Matters

Dissection 的危險點在於臨床表現可像 migraine、cluster headache、isolated Horner syndrome、posterior circulation stroke 或 SAH，但確診必須靠 neuroimaging。若只看 brain CT、只做 carotid duplex、或只看重建血管圖不看 source images，可能漏掉 skull-base / vertebral artery / wall hematoma lesions。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Clinical suspicion of cervical or intracranial artery dissection.
- Acute ischemic stroke / hemorrhagic stroke candidate needing comprehensive vascular evaluation, or local symptoms suspicious for dissection without infarct / SAH.
- 可取得 head and neck MRI/MRA 或 CT/CTA；必要時可轉介 DSA。

## Mechanism

```text
intramural hematoma / false lumen
-> arterial wall signal and lumen contour abnormalities
-> MRI/MRA or CT/CTA detects wall hematoma, stenosis, occlusion, intimal flap, double lumen or pseudoaneurysm
```

```text
equivocal initial study
-> complementary modality or repeat imaging over time
-> higher diagnostic confidence and treatment-planning baseline
```

## Observable Patterns

### First-Line Confirmation

- Use urgent noninvasive multimodal imaging:
  - brain and neck MRI plus head and neck MRA, or
  - head CT plus head and neck CTA.
- MRI protocol should include standard axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted, FLAIR and DWI.
- Cervical and cranial T1-weighted MRI with fat saturation helps identify small intramural hemorrhage.
- MRA should include contrast-enhanced and time-of-flight MRA.
- CT/CTA is an appropriate alternative based on local availability, contraindications and expertise.

### Diagnostic Imaging Signs

- Long tapered arterial stenosis.
- Tapered or flame-shaped occlusion.
- Dissecting aneurysm / pseudoaneurysm.
- Intimal flap.
- Double lumen.
- Intramural hematoma, including crescent or yin-yang sign.
- String sign.

### Modality Boundaries

- There is no single gold standard; complementary imaging and repeat imaging may be needed.
- DSA is reserved for younger patients when suspicion remains high despite negative noninvasive imaging.
- DSA is not needed when CTA or MRA clearly establishes diagnosis.
- Carotid duplex and TCD can screen or monitor, but they are not sufficient rule-out tests in high-suspicion cases.
- Carotid duplex detection was 68 to 95 percent in cited studies.
- Duplex and TCD have suboptimal yield near the skull base and in vertebral artery dissection within transverse foramina.
- Ultrasound is unreliable in isolated Horner syndrome.

### Pitfalls

- Normal or equivocal ultrasound does not exclude dissection.
- A normal-appearing lumen can miss wall hematoma; source images matter.
- V3 vertebral artery dissection may not show a classic crescent sign because of vessel orientation and vertebral venous plexus signal.
- Reconstructed angiographic images alone can be insufficient if the wall abnormality is subtle.
- Imaging should include head and neck vessels, not only intracranial circulation.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Clinical notes should name the modality, arterial segment and sign: for example, "left ICA long tapered stenosis with mural hematoma on T1 fat-sat MRI."
- If dissection suspicion remains high after negative ultrasound, next step should be MRA or CTA, not reassurance.
- Research reports should specify whether diagnosis was based on luminal signs, wall hematoma, intimal flap/double lumen, pseudoaneurysm, DSA, CTA or MRI/MRA.

## Fact

- The source states clinical features may raise suspicion, but diagnosis is confirmed by neuroimaging findings.
- The source obtains urgent noninvasive multimodal imaging with head/neck MRI/MRA or head CT plus head/neck CTA.
- The source states there is no gold standard and diagnosis often requires complementary imaging modalities, which may need repeating over time.
- The source states DSA is reserved for younger patients when suspicion remains high despite negative noninvasive imaging.
- The source states DSA is not needed if CTA or MRA clearly diagnoses cerebral or cervical artery dissection.
- The source states a 2009 systematic review found MRI/MRA and CT/CTA sensitivity and specificity were relatively similar.
- The source states carotid duplex detects abnormalities in only 68 to 95 percent of cases.
- The source states ultrasound is unreliable for carotid artery dissection in isolated Horner syndrome.
- The source states MRA or CTA should be pursued in ultrasound-negative cases when clinical history suggests dissection.
- The source lists string sign, elongated/tapered stenosis or flame-shaped occlusion, intimal flap, pseudoaneurysm and intramural hematoma as characteristic findings.
- The source states intramural hematoma was observed on MRI in 91 percent of 157 vertebral artery dissections in a prospective European study.

## Inference

- A dissection workup should be designed as brain-plus-vessel-plus-wall evaluation.
- Negative ultrasound is a weak negative test when the clinical pattern is strong.
- Diagnostic certainty can evolve over time; initial equivocal imaging should not be treated as final when clinical suspicion remains high.

## Assumption

- Local imaging quality and neuroradiology experience are adequate for vessel wall and lumen assessment.
- Contrast, MRI safety and renal function have been considered when selecting modality.

## Uncertainty

- The source does not define a universal repeat imaging interval.
- Choice between MRI/MRA and CT/CTA depends on local availability and experience.
- Some subtle dissections remain difficult even with multimodal imaging.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可用 clinical suspicion alone 診斷 dissection。
- 不可只因 carotid duplex / TCD negative 就排除 dissection。
- 不可把 DSA 當所有病人的 routine first-line test。
- 不可只看腦部 infarct pattern 而忽略 head/neck vessel imaging。
- 不可用本頁推論 antithrombotic treatment；本來源不處理 treatment / prognosis。
