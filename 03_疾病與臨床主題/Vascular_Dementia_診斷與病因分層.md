# Vascular Dementia 診斷與病因分層

## One-Sentence Definition

Vascular dementia / vascular cognitive impairment 是由 cerebrovascular disease 或 impaired cerebral blood flow 造成或參與造成的 cognitive impairment syndrome；診斷重點是確認 vascular brain injury 是否「足以」解釋 cognitive impairment，而不是單純確認 MRI 上有 vascular lesion。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 VaD / VCID 的診斷邏輯與病因分層。它不是 VaD treatment、antithrombotic decision、cholinesterase inhibitor trial 或 dementia rehabilitation 頁；相關治療邊界見 [[Vascular_Cognitive_Impairment_血管風險與Antithrombotic邊界]] 與 [[Vascular_Dementia_認知藥物與預後限制]]。

VCID 包含 vascular MCI 與 VaD。VaD 通常在兩種臨床情境被辨識：clinical stroke 後出現 dementia，或沒有 clinical stroke history 但 dementia patient 的 brain imaging 顯示 vascular brain injury。

## Why It Matters

Older adults 常有 asymptomatic cerebrovascular brain injury，也常同時有 Alzheimer disease pathology。若把任何 white matter hyperintensity 或 silent infarct 都直接貼成 VaD，會造成 overdiagnosis；若只承認 clinical stroke 後 dementia，又會漏掉 covert cerebrovascular disease 造成的 progressive cognitive impairment。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with measurable cognitive impairment。
- 需先分類為 vascular MCI 或 VaD；主要差異是 functional independence 是否相對保留。
- 必須有 clinical stroke history 或 neuroimaging evidence of silent / covert cerebrovascular disease。
- 必須判斷 lesion burden、location、severity 與 clinical syndrome 是否足以造成 cognitive impairment。
- 必須考慮 AD、Lewy body disease、NPH、depression 等 alternative or coexisting causes。

## Mechanism

```text
Cerebrovascular / cardiovascular disease
-> ischemia, hemorrhage, hypoperfusion, embolism, or small vessel pathology
-> strategic lesion, cumulative infarct / hemorrhage burden, white matter network disruption, or neurovascular unit dysfunction
-> cognitive impairment
-> classify as vascular MCI or VaD by objective cognition + functional impact + vascular causal sufficiency
```

## Observable Patterns

### Diagnostic Scenarios

- Clinical stroke followed by dementia：vascular timing is usually clearer, but prestroke cognitive decline must still be checked。
- Dementia plus imaging vascular brain injury without clinical stroke history：requires judgment that covert disease is severe and anatomically plausible enough to explain impairment。
- Multiple-etiology dementia：vascular pathology and AD pathology can both contribute; finding AD does not exclude vascular contribution。

### Etiologic Layers

- Ischemic stroke mechanisms：cardioembolism、large artery atherosclerosis、cerebral small vessel disease。
- Hemorrhagic mechanisms：intracerebral hemorrhage、subarachnoid hemorrhage。
- Cerebral small vessel disease has an outsized role in VaD burden。
- Arteriolosclerosis：associated with aging、hypertension and conventional vascular risk factors；often affects subcortical regions with multiple lacunes or extensive confluent white matter lesions。
- Cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA)：beta-amyloid deposition in small leptomeningeal / cortical vessels；classically causes lobar hemorrhage, microbleeds or superficial siderosis patterns。
- Less common causes：CADASIL、small vessel vasculitides、inflammatory disorders、venous collagenosis。

### Causality Threshold

The source reports VAS-COG examples that may be sufficient for vascular MCI or VaD: one to two large vessel infarcts, one large or strategic infarct, more than two lacunar infarcts outside the brainstem, one to two strategic lacunes with or without extensive white matter lesions, or extensive confluent white matter lesions of presumed vascular origin。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Reported vascular lesions should be translated into a causal question: incidental, contributory, or sufficient。
- VaD diagnosis is incomplete until the underlying cerebrovascular or cardiovascular damage is characterized, because secondary prevention depends on mechanism。
- Do not require memory impairment; modern criteria can accept significant impairment in one domain when functional criteria are met。
- Mixed pathology should be documented explicitly instead of forcing "pure AD" or "pure VaD" labels。

## Fact

- VCID refers to syndromes caused in whole or in part by cerebrovascular disease or impaired cerebral blood flow。
- VaD refers to dementia primarily caused by cerebrovascular disease or impaired cerebral blood flow, or in which these are contributing causative factors。
- Neuroimaging detection of cerebrovascular brain injury in asymptomatic older adults means imaging abnormality alone is not sufficient for VaD diagnosis。
- VaD is a syndrome, not a disease。
- The diagnosis of VaD is not complete until the underlying cerebrovascular or cardiovascular damage has been characterized。
- Vascular disease is a cause or contributor in 25 to 50 percent of dementia cases in this source。
- Pure VaD is relatively uncommon in clinicopathologic dementia specialty clinic studies, approximately 10 percent of all dementia cases。
- Multiple-etiology dementia with vascular component, often with AD, accounts for approximately 30 to 40 percent of all dementia cases in those studies。
- Major VaD risk factors overlap with stroke risk factors: advanced age, hypertension, diabetes, elevated total cholesterol, lower physical activity, low or high BMI, smoking, coronary artery disease and atrial fibrillation。
- Poststroke dementia risk factors include prestroke factors, stroke factors, stroke complications and lower brain reserve。
- Consensus criteria share three common elements: classify as vascular MCI or VaD, identify cerebrovascular disease by history or neuroimaging, and judge vascular disease sufficient to cause cognitive impairment。

## Inference

- The most defensible diagnostic habit is to separate "vascular pathology exists" from "vascular pathology explains the cognitive syndrome"。
- A diagnosis of VaD should trigger stroke-mechanism thinking, but the antithrombotic decision still requires treatment-specific evidence and bleeding-risk assessment。
- Covert small vessel disease can produce a dementia pattern even without a recognized stroke event。

## Assumption

- Structural neuroimaging has enough quality to identify infarcts, lacunes, white matter lesions, hemorrhage markers and atrophy。
- Functional history is sufficient to separate vascular MCI from VaD。
- This source is applied to adults。

## Uncertainty

- Vascular pathology is common in asymptomatic aging, so individual-level causality remains partly judgment-based。
- No single infarct-volume threshold reliably predicts poststroke dementia because lesion location and cognitive reserve matter。
- Neurovascular unit dysfunction beyond ischemia / hemorrhage is biologically plausible but unproven as a VaD cause in this source。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRI 有 white matter hyperintensity 就是 VaD。」來源明確反對 imaging finding alone 作為診斷。
- 誤讀：「沒有 clinical stroke history 就不是 VaD。」covert cerebrovascular disease 仍可造成 cognitive impairment。
- 誤讀：「VaD 是單一 disease entity。」來源將 VaD 視為 syndrome，必須追究 underlying vascular / cardiovascular cause。
- 誤讀：「只要有 AD evidence 就排除 vascular contribution。」multiple-etiology dementia 很常見。
