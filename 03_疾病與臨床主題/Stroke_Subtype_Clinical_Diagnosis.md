# Stroke Subtype Clinical Diagnosis

## One-Sentence Definition

Stroke subtype clinical diagnosis 是用 symptom onset/course、associated symptoms、risk factors 與 bedside exam 對 ischemic thrombosis / embolism / hypoperfusion、ICH、SAH 做 presumptive diagnosis，但最後仍要 brain and vascular imaging confirmation。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 bedside subtype recognition，不處理完整 [[Stroke_Etiologic_Classification]]、IVT / EVT、ICH neurocritical care、SAH aneurysm repair 或 secondary prevention；從 presumptive diagnosis 走到 mechanism confirmation 的 workflow 見 [[Stroke_Evaluation_病因確認與Directed_Diagnostic_Testing]]。

它回答的問題是：「眼前這個 acute neurologic deficit 比較像哪一類 stroke mechanism？」  
它不能回答：「影像、vascular imaging、cardiac evaluation and labs 已支持的最終病因與治療計畫是什麼？」TOAST / CCS / ISPS25 的 etiologic label 屬於下一層分類，不是 bedside impression 的同義詞。

## Why It Matters

Ischemic stroke、ICH、SAH 的 BP、antithrombotic、reperfusion、mobilization 與 neurosurgical decision 完全不同。臨床線索能先決定警覺方向，但若把 presumptive subtype 當成 definitive diagnosis，會造成反向治療錯誤。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult suspected acute stroke 或復健病房新發 / 惡化 neurologic deficit。
- 可取得 symptom onset、evolution、witness history、risk factors、general physical examination 與 neurologic examination。
- 仍需 noncontrast CT / MRI 與 vascular imaging 來確認 hemorrhage、infarct pattern 與 vascular lesion。

## Mechanism

```text
stroke pathophysiology
-> symptom onset and evolution pattern
-> associated symptoms and risk-factor context
-> bedside subtype probability
-> imaging confirmation
-> subtype-specific treatment logic
```

### Time Course as the Main Bedside Axis

| Pattern | Favored subtype | Mechanism logic |
|---|---|---|
| Sudden deficit maximal at onset | Embolic ischemic stroke | embolus abruptly blocks an artery |
| Fluctuating / remitting / stuttering progression | Thrombotic ischemic stroke | local arterial lesion evolves over time |
| Minutes-to-hours or stuttering development, often [[Lacunar_Stroke_Syndromes_臨床表現與影像確認]] | Penetrating artery occlusion / lacunar-appearing stroke | small penetrating artery occlusion evolves over a short period, but syndrome still requires imaging and competing-mechanism exclusion |
| Gradual worsening over minutes to hours, no early improvement | ICH | expanding hematoma injures surrounding tissue |
| Instant severe widespread headache, often activity cessation | SAH | arterial blood enters CSF and rapidly raises ICP |
| Diffuse nonfocal dysfunction, often bilateral | Systemic hypoperfusion | global cerebral blood flow problem |

## Observable Patterns

### History Clues

- Same-territory recurrent TIA favors local vascular lesion / thrombosis.
- Multi-territory transient attacks favor embolism from heart or aorta.
- Fever raises suspicion for endocarditis-related embolism.
- Severe headache at neurologic onset favors SAH.
- Headache after onset plus worsening neurologic signs、decreased consciousness、vomiting favors ICH.
- Vomiting can occur with ICH、SAH and posterior circulation large artery ischemia.
- Acute seizure favors lobar ICH or brain embolism more than thrombosis.
- Reduced alertness favors hemorrhage, but large ischemic stroke and posterior circulation stroke can also reduce consciousness.
- Sex or physical activity can precipitate ICH / SAH.
- Trauma before stroke suggests traumatic dissection、arterial occlusion or traumatic hemorrhage.
- Recent minor trauma、sudden neck movement、sports / vigorous physical activity、intense coughing/sneezing or peripartum Valsalva with acute/subacute head or neck pain should raise [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_臨床辨識]] suspicion.
- Acute partial Horner syndrome, cranial/cervical neuropathy, pulsatile tinnitus or unexplained tooth pain are local dissection clues, especially when paired with TIA/stroke symptoms.

### Risk and Exam Clues

- Atrial fibrillation、valvular disease、recent MI、endocarditis、murmur and cardiac enlargement favor cardiac-origin embolism.
- Hypertension is relevant to both thrombotic stroke and ICH; severe uncontrolled hypertension strongly favors ICH.
- Diabetes supports large / small artery ischemic disease.
- Absent pulses or focal high-pitched neck bruit favors atherosclerotic occlusive disease / thrombosis.
- Funduscopic embolic material supports embolic mechanism; subhyaloid hemorrhage suggests sudden brain hemorrhage or SAH.
- Pure motor stroke favors penetrating artery thrombotic stroke or small ICH.
- Classic lacunar syndromes include pure motor hemiparesis、pure sensory stroke、ataxic hemiparesis、sensorimotor stroke and dysarthria-clumsy hand syndrome; absence of cortical signs raises suspicion but does not prove [[Lacunar_Infarcts_診斷與病因邊界]].
- Aphasia suggests anterior circulation disease.
- Vertigo、staggering、diplopia、deafness、crossed signs、bilateral motor / sensory signs and hemianopsia suggest posterior circulation involvement.
- Posterior circulation ischemia is also supported by altered alertness / behavior / memory、visual loss、eye movement or pupillary abnormality、bulbar weakness、somatosensory loss、dizziness / vertigo and limb / gait ataxia; see [[Posterior_Circulation_Stroke_臨床定位與診斷陷阱]].
- Continuous acute vertigo/dizziness with central HINTS signs, severe gait/truncal ataxia or new hearing loss should not be treated as simple peripheral vestibulopathy; see [[HINTS_急性前庭症候群中樞與周邊鑑別]].
- New neck/occipital pain plus posterior circulation signs is a vertebral artery dissection clue, but dizziness/vertigo alone is nonspecific.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Acute care: subtype suspicion helps prioritize CT urgency, vascular imaging, BP frame, reperfusion candidacy and neurosurgical alerting.
- Rehabilitation: new decline during inpatient rehab should be framed as recurrent ischemia、hemorrhagic conversion、ICH / SAH、seizure、infection mimic or edema-related deterioration until evaluated.
- Documentation: write "suspected embolic pattern" or "clinical course favors ICH" rather than treating the bedside impression as final mechanism.
- Research: clinical suspicion, imaging pattern and final etiologic classification should be separated.

## Fact

- Stroke subtypes include ICH、SAH and ischemia due to thrombosis、embolism or systemic hypoperfusion.
- Overview of stroke evaluation source states that history、physical examination and initial CT / MRI can form a presumptive diagnosis, but confirmation requires more extensive directed testing.
- The source states the most important historical item for differentiating stroke subtypes is the pace and course of symptoms and signs.
- Clinical diagnosis based on history and examination requires confirmation by brain and vascular imaging.
- Embolic stroke usually has abrupt onset and deficit maximal at onset.
- Thrombosis-related symptoms often fluctuate, remit or progress in a stuttering fashion.
- Penetrating artery occlusions usually develop over hours or at most a few days.
- ICH usually progresses during minutes or hours and does not improve early.
- Aneurysmal SAH develops in an instant and focal brain dysfunction is less common.
- Neurologic signs usually localize the brain lesion more than they define the stroke subtype.
- Biomarkers such as NT-proBNP and D-dimer do not have enough sensitivity or specificity for routine clinical subtype diagnosis.
- The posterior circulation source states CT is less sensitive than DWI MRI for acute ischemic stroke, especially posterior fossa stroke, but even DWI MRI can miss small acute posterior circulation infarcts.
- The posterior circulation source states HINTS is useful only in continuous acute vertigo/dizziness and is not useful for momentary position-related vertigo or resolved TIA symptoms.
- The lacunar infarcts source states clinical lacunar syndrome recognition in the hyperacute setting may not reflect final lacunar infarction diagnosis.
- The lacunar infarcts source states CT has low sensitivity for small acute lacunar infarcts, while DWI MRI can differentiate acute from chronic lacunes and identify multiple acute lesions suggesting embolism.
- The dissection source states dissection is a common cause of stroke in the young but may occur at any age.
- The dissection source states head and/or neck pain is the most frequent initial symptom and Horner syndrome occurs in approximately 25 percent of cases.
- The dissection source states acute onset Horner syndrome with neck pain and ipsilateral internal carotid territory ischemic stroke or TIA is suggestive of carotid artery dissection.

## Inference

- Subtype diagnosis is a probability model, not a bedside verdict.
- If the time course and risk profile disagree, imaging and mechanism workup should override the first impression.
- Rehab plans should not schedule mobilization, antithrombotic changes or BP targets without checking whether the event is ischemic, ICH or SAH.
- Dissection clues should raise the priority of head/neck vascular imaging rather than replacing the broader stroke subtype workup.

## Assumption

- A usable witness history or functional reconstruction of symptom evolution is available.
- Brain / vascular imaging can be obtained quickly enough to revise the clinical impression.

## Uncertainty

- Some ICH can be abrupt, and some ischemic stroke can worsen later.
- Risk factors overlap; hypertension, age and atherosclerosis do not identify a single mechanism.
- Pediatric, pregnancy-related and unusual stroke causes need separate sources.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 AF 寫成 cardioembolic stroke 的自動證明。
- 不可把 headache / vomiting 簡化成「一定 hemorrhage」；posterior circulation ischemia 也可能 vomiting。
- 不可把 pure motor stroke 寫成一定 lacunar；small ICH 也可能。
- 不可把 lacunar syndrome 當作 final small vessel occlusion diagnosis；仍需 imaging、vascular evaluation and cardiac evaluation。
- 不可用 blood biomarkers 取代 imaging。
- 不可把 covert brain infarct 當成無意義 incidental finding；它可能與 cognitive decline 和 future stroke risk 有關。
- 不可把 TOAST、CCS 或 ISPS25 分類當作 bedside subtype clue；classification system 需要 ancillary studies and diagnostic confidence assignment。
- 不可把 early CT / DWI MRI negative 當成 posterior circulation stroke 排除證據。
- 不可把 isolated dizziness 直接等同 vertebrobasilar TIA；需要 timing、ocular motor signs、gait/limb ataxia、bulbar/sensory/visual signs and vascular context。
- 不可把 headache / Horner / minor trauma 當成 dissection 診斷本身；需要 [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_影像確認與Pitfalls]]。
