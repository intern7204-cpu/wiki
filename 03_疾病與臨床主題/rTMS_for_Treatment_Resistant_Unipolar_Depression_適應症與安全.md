# rTMS for Treatment-Resistant Unipolar Depression 適應症與安全

## One-Sentence Definition

Repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) for treatment-resistant unipolar depression 是一種 noninvasive neuromodulation treatment，主要用於 adult unipolar major depression 在至少一種 antidepressant response 不足後，透過 focal cortical stimulation 改變 prefrontal-limbic mood-regulation networks。

## Definition and Boundary

### What It Is

- 一個 adult unipolar major depression 的 treatment option。
- 常見臨床位置是 antidepressant medication response inadequate 之後。
- 主要 protocol 類型包括 surface cortical TMS、theta burst TMS 與 deep TMS。
- 臨床價值在於 noninvasive、通常不需 general anesthesia、不需刻意誘發 seizure，且 systemic adverse effects 少。

### What It Is Not

- 不是所有 depression 的 default first-line treatment。
- 不是 ECT 的等效替代品； severe major depression 中 ECT evidence suggests greater efficacy。
- 不是無風險的 wellness procedure；需要 contraindication screen、motor threshold calibration、hearing protection 與 seizure response plan。
- 不是可把 aphasia、stroke motor recovery、pain 或 tinnitus 的 TMS evidence 直接外推到 unipolar depression 的總稱。

## Why It Matters

Treatment-resistant unipolar depression 可能造成長期 disability、functional decline、suicidality risk 與 rehabilitation participation 下降。rTMS 提供一個介於 pharmacotherapy / psychotherapy 與 ECT 之間的 noninvasive somatic option，但若忽略 indication、contraindication 與 ECT 比較，容易把它過度神化或過度貶低。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with confirmed unipolar major depressive episode。
- 通常已有至少一種 antidepressant medication adequate trial response 不足。
- 需排除或另外處理 bipolar depression、delirium、substance-induced mood syndrome、psychotic depression requiring rapid / higher-efficacy intervention、catatonia、imminent suicidality。
- 需確認是否已有 prior TMS response；relapse 後 repeated course 可能有幫助，但主要 evidence 來自 observational data。
- 若 patient 拒絕 pharmacotherapy 或無法取得 psychotherapy，source 認為某些情境可考慮 TMS，但這不等於 routine first-line。

## Mechanism

```text
Scalp coil
-> rapidly alternating magnetic field
-> induced focal cortical electric current
-> cortical neuron depolarization / excitability modulation
-> altered prefrontal and connected anterior cingulate / meso-cortico-limbic network activity
-> mood symptom improvement in a subset of patients
```

Protocol-level mechanism is usually framed as:

- High-frequency left prefrontal surface cortical stimulation：tends to increase target-region excitability。
- Low-frequency right prefrontal surface cortical stimulation：tends to inhibit target-region activity。
- Theta burst TMS：shorter high-frequency burst protocol intended to mimic endogenous theta rhythms。
- Deep TMS：uses coil designs intended to stimulate deeper structures than conventional surface cortical approaches。

These are mechanism models, not guaranteed individual-response predictors.

## Observable Patterns

### Indication Pattern

- Adult unipolar major depression after at least one failed antidepressant medication trial。
- Prior TMS responder with relapse may be considered for another course using similar parameters。
- Patients with systemic medication tolerability issues may be reasonable candidates because TMS has few systemic adverse effects。

### Efficacy Pattern

- Surface cortical TMS has the largest evidence base.
- High-frequency left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex TMS and low-frequency right dorsolateral prefrontal cortex TMS are each superior to sham in randomized evidence summarized by the source.
- Theta burst TMS and deep TMS also show efficacy against sham and appear broadly comparable to surface cortical TMS in network meta-analyses, but direct head-to-head evidence is limited.
- rTMS is typically delivered with ongoing antidepressant pharmacotherapy in many studies.
- For severe major depression, best evidence in this source indicates ECT is more efficacious than rTMS.
- Acute responders may maintain benefit short-term; persistence to one year is possible but less frequent over time.
- Maintenance TMS after acute response remains uncertain.

### Safety Screen

Contraindications / major cautions:

- Seizure disorder。
- Intracranial metallic clips, electrodes, plates, stimulators, shrapnel or other metal in the magnetic field。
- Cochlear implants。
- MRI-unsafe implanted electrical devices, including pacemakers, implantable cardioverter-defibrillators, intracardiac lines and medication pumps。
- Ferromagnetic-containing tattoo ink in head or neck。
- Vagus nerve stimulator requires expert consultation; it may not be absolute if coil position avoids device components。
- Psychotic features are not an absolute contraindication, but most trials excluded psychotic depression and ECT may be favored.

Seizure-risk amplifiers:

- Personal or first-degree family history of epilepsy。
- Preexisting neurologic disorder, including prior head injury with loss of consciousness, prior brain surgery or congenital brain malformation。
- Proconvulsant medications such as bupropion, stimulants, tricyclic antidepressants, antipsychotics and theophylline。
- Recent discontinuation of alcohol, benzodiazepines or anticonvulsants。
- Stimulant use disorder or sleep deprivation。
- Higher stimulation frequency, higher intensity or shorter intertrain interval。

### Common Adverse Effects

- Headache and scalp pain。
- Transient auditory threshold increase；hearing protection is needed。
- Vasovagal syncope。
- Rare generalized tonic-clonic seizure。
- Treatment-emergent mania / hypomania is reported, but pooled trial data summarized by the source did not show a clear active-versus-sham excess.
- Randomized evidence summarized by the source does not show cognitive impairment from rTMS in major depression.

## Clinical / Research Implication

### Clinical Use

- rTMS referral should state diagnosis, episode severity, failed medication / psychotherapy history, current medications, seizure-risk factors, implant / metal status, pregnancy/postpartum or older adult considerations, and ECT rationale.
- Do not describe TMS as "safer than medication" without specifying seizure screen, implant/device contraindications, hearing protection and emergency response.
- If scalp pain is handled by lowering intensity, document that this may reduce efficacy.
- In poststroke depression, this source reports possible benefit but also substantial heterogeneity and limited sham-controlled structure in many studies; do not treat it as definitive stroke guideline evidence.

### Research Use

- Better head-to-head trials are needed among surface cortical TMS, theta burst TMS and deep TMS.
- Maintenance schedule, relapse-prevention role and retreatment strategy remain open questions.
- Connectivity-guided targeting, EEG, blood-flow markers and genetic polymorphisms are research-level predictors, not routine clinical selection criteria.
- Benzodiazepine association with poorer response may reflect residual confounding by anxiety.

## Fact

- rTMS is indicated for adult unipolar major depression after at least one antidepressant failure according to this source.
- Surface cortical, theta burst and deep TMS all have randomized evidence suggesting efficacy and general tolerability.
- ECT is more efficacious than rTMS for major depression in the evidence summarized by this source.
- TMS requires screening for seizure disorder, intracranial metal / shrapnel, cochlear implant, MRI-unsafe implanted devices and ferromagnetic head/neck tattoo ink.
- Seizure is rare under safety-guideline conditions but is the most serious adverse effect.
- Headache, scalp pain and transient auditory threshold increase are common adverse effects.

## Inference

- rTMS is most useful as a structured noninvasive treatment option when medication response is inadequate and ECT is not preferred, not as a casual add-on.
- The main clinical risk is not only the magnetic stimulation itself, but failure to screen seizure threshold and implanted-device risk.
- Protocol selection should be individualized by trained clinicians rather than inferred from one protocol name.

## Assumption

- The patient has adult unipolar depression rather than bipolar depression or another psychiatric / neurologic mimic.
- The treatment site can accurately determine motor threshold and respond to seizure or syncope.
- Local payer and device criteria are compatible with the evidence-based indication.

## Uncertainty

- Maintenance TMS benefit after acute response is not established.
- Clinically meaningful safety differences among standard TMS protocols are not clearly known.
- Long-term occupational safety for clinicians administering TMS over many years is not established in this source.
- Biomarker-guided prediction remains investigational.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not infer that "noninvasive" means no serious adverse events.
- Do not infer that a favorable odds ratio implies high absolute remission for every treatment-resistant patient.
- Do not apply this adult unipolar depression evidence to aphasia, stroke motor recovery, chronic pain, tinnitus or OCD without separate sources.
- Do not present poststroke depression TMS evidence as guideline-level routine care from this source alone.
- Do not use this page as an administration protocol; coil placement, motor threshold, pulse dose and device-specific parameters require dedicated protocol sources.
