# Poststroke Neuropsychiatric Disorders 辨識與轉介

## One-Sentence Definition

Poststroke neuropsychiatric disorders 辨識與轉介，是在 stroke recovery 中系統評估 mood、motivation、anxiety、trauma response、emotional expression 與 unsafe behavior，並用 functional impairment 與 safety risk 決定是否轉介 mental health clinician。

## Definition and Boundary

### What It Is

- 一個 adult poststroke neuropsychiatric surveillance framework。
- 處理範圍包括 adjustment disorder、poststroke depression、apathy、poststroke anxiety、ASD / PTSD、pseudobulbar affect (PBA)、poststroke anger or aggression (PSAA)。
- 核心問題不是「病人配不配合」，而是症狀是否造成 functional impairment、rehabilitation interruption、self-care decline、adherence problem、social withdrawal 或 caregiver safety risk。

### What It Is Not

- 不是 psychiatric DSM 診斷手冊。
- 不是所有 poststroke sadness / worry 都病理化。
- 不是把低 motivation 直接等同 depression。
- 不是用 PHQ-9、GAD-7 或 HADS total score 直接取代臨床判斷。
- 不是 disorder-specific pharmacologic guideline。

## Why It Matters

Poststroke psychiatric and neurobehavioral symptoms 會降低 rehabilitation engagement、secondary prevention adherence、community participation 與 caregiver safety。若只把症狀寫成「不配合」「情緒差」「家屬壓力大」，會錯過可處理的 clinical syndrome，也會讓 therapy dose 與安全計畫失真。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 適用：adult ischemic stroke、ICH、SAH 或 TIA 後出現 psychiatric / behavioral symptoms。
- 先確認：delirium、new neurologic deterioration、infection、pain、sleep disorder、medication effect、aphasia、cognitive impairment、hearing/vision impairment、motor disability。
- 症狀必須放回 functional context：self-care、therapy participation、appointment adherence、medication adherence、family relationship、driving/work/community participation。
- 若有 suicidal ideation、psychosis、dangerous behavior、aggression、poor judgment with imminent harm，優先處理 safety and referral。

## Mechanism

```text
Stroke
-> neural injury + neuroinflammation + disrupted networks
-> disability, identity change, fear of recurrence, financial / relationship stress
-> depression / apathy / anxiety / PTSD / PBA / anger-aggression
-> reduced initiation, avoidance, poor adherence, social withdrawal, caregiver burden
-> lower rehabilitation dose + worse participation + safety risk
```

## Observable Patterns

### Referral triggers

- Psychiatric symptoms causing functional impairment。
- Lack of response to initial treatment。
- Suicidal ideation or behavior。
- Psychotic features such as delusions or hallucinations。
- Impulsive, dangerous or aggressive behavior。
- Need for adjunctive psychotherapy。
- Poor judgment that places patient or others at imminent risk。
- Patient interest in referral。

### Depression pattern

- Poststroke depression 是最常見 poststroke neuropsychiatric disorder。
- PHQ-2 / PHQ-9 可用於 non-aphasic patients；PHQ-2 >= 3 應進一步評估。
- Aphasia 可考慮 ADRS 這類 behavioral observation-based scale。
- 必須問 suicidal ideation / behavior。
- 診斷時重點是 persistent depressed mood、anhedonia、worthlessness、guilt、hopelessness、suicidality，而不是只看 fatigue、sleep、appetite 或 slowed movement。

### Apathy pattern

- 核心是 reduction or loss of motivation、initiation、goal-directed activity。
- 可表現為需要反覆 prompting 才完成 ADL、對健康或外觀缺乏興趣、social engagement 下降。
- 與 depression 的關鍵差異：apathy 不一定有 worthlessness、hopelessness、guilt、shame、negative self-evaluation 或 suicidal ideation。
- 目前沒有 proven therapy；傳統 antidepressant 對純 apathy 可能有限。

### Anxiety / trauma pattern

- Poststroke anxiety 可表現為 persistent worry、panic、fear of recurrence、fear of falling、avoidance、sleep difficulty、somatic hypervigilance。
- GAD-7、GAI、HADS 可協助篩檢，但 cognitive load 與 stroke symptom overlap 會影響解讀。
- ASD 發生於 event 後 days to weeks；PTSD 通常需症狀超過 one month。
- PTSD / anxiety 可能透過 avoidance、medication nonadherence 與 reduced participation 影響 stroke recovery。

### PBA pattern

- PBA 是 involuntary or inappropriate laughing / crying，外在 affect 與內在 mood 不一致或過度。
- Crying-only PBA 容易被誤診為 depression。
- 評估時問 episode onset、trigger、proportionality、suppressibility、internal mood、social embarrassment。

### Anger / aggression pattern

- Acute/subacute aggression 先考慮 delirium、pain、infection、medication、sleep deprivation 或環境刺激。
- Persistent PSAA 可有 irritability、reduced frustration tolerance、disinhibition、hostility、rage、verbal or physical aggression。
- 若有 caregiver safety concern，需依 local protocol 處理 imminent harm、weapons at home 或 domestic safety issue。

## Clinical / Research Implication

### 臨床應用

- Therapy participation 下降時，不要只寫 motivation poor；至少篩查 depression、apathy、anxiety、delirium、pain、sleep、aphasia/cognition 與 PBA。
- Depression/anxiety questionnaire 是入口，不是結論；item-level review 比 total score 更重要。
- Poststroke crying 要分 depression、PBA、adjustment disorder 與 normal grief。
- Apathy 應被列為 rehabilitation dose 下降與 caregiver burden 的可能原因。
- Safety referral threshold 應低於一般門診：suicidality、psychosis、dangerous behavior、aggression、poor judgment with imminent risk 都不能只放在復健追蹤。

### 研究應用

- Poststroke mental health outcome 需按 diagnosis 分層，避免把 depression、apathy、PBA 與 PTSD 混成 mood problem。
- 報告 prevalence / outcome 時需標明 time since stroke、stroke subtype、aphasia/cognitive impairment、screening tool 與 functional impairment criteria。

## Fact

- Poststroke psychiatric disorders are common and under-recognized in the source。
- Functional impairment is central to diagnosing clinically meaningful psychiatric disorder。
- Referral is indicated for functional impairment, suicidality, psychosis, dangerous/aggressive behavior, poor judgment with imminent risk, lack of response to initial treatment, adjunctive psychotherapy need or patient request。
- Poststroke depression prevalence is reported around 25-50 percent across studies, with methodologic variation。
- PHQ-2 / PHQ-9 are widely used for non-aphasic poststroke depression screening; ADRS is validated for aphasic patients。
- Apathy can occur independently from depression and has no proven therapy in this source。
- PBA may be misread as depression because crying can occur in both。

## Inference

- Neuropsychiatric surveillance should be a rehab readiness domain alongside dysphagia, cardiopulmonary stability, VTE/infection surveillance and fall risk。
- Low initiation and therapy refusal should be treated as clinical data requiring differential diagnosis。
- Caregiver safety is part of stroke rehabilitation risk management when PSAA is present。

## Assumption

- The care setting can access psychiatry, psychology, social work or mental health referral when indicated。
- Screening tools are administered in a language, sensory and cognitive context that permits meaningful interpretation。

## Uncertainty

- This source does not establish a universal screening interval for all poststroke neuropsychiatric disorders。
- Treatment evidence is uneven: depression has more evidence than apathy, PTSD, PBA and PSAA。
- Pediatric stroke, neonatal stroke and severe communication impairment need separate source support。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「病人不練」不等於「不想好」。
- 「中風後難過」不一定是 major depression；但 functional impairment、suicidality、persistent depressed mood 或 anhedonia 不可忽略。
- 「哭」不等於 depression；PBA 需看 episode pattern and mood congruence。
- 「問卷分數高」不等於診斷；fatigue、sleep、appetite、concentration 與 psychomotor slowing 可由 stroke 本身造成。
- 「SSRI 預防 depression 有資料」不等於所有 acute stroke patient 都應 routine prophylaxis，尤其 hemorrhagic stroke 或 bleeding / fall / fracture risk 需謹慎。
