# MS 步態與 Spasticity 復健框架

## One-Sentence Definition

MS mobility care 要把 walking speed、endurance、spasticity、weakness、fatigue、heat intolerance、fall risk 與 assistive technology 放在同一個復健決策框架。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 MS gait impairment 與 spasticity management。
- 本頁不是完整 DMT 或 neuro-urology 管理頁。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Multiple_Sclerosis]]。

## Why It Matters

- Mobility impairment 是 MS 最常見且最影響 independence / quality of life 的功能問題之一。
- Spasticity 若只在坐姿評估 tone，會漏掉 gait、cocontraction、night spasm、skin、bowel / bladder、sleep 與 pain 影響。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先評估 relapse / pseudo-relapse、fatigue、heat exposure、infection、medication sedation、weakness、sensory / cerebellar issue、spasticity、contracture、foot drop、assistive device fit。
- Gait outcome 至少可追蹤 T25FW；20% walking speed change 在來源中被視為 clinically meaningful。

## Mechanism

```text
CNS demyelination / neurodegeneration
→ weakness + spasticity + sensory / cerebellar impairment + fatigue
→ slower walking, wider steps, altered knee / ankle / hip kinematics
→ falls, deconditioning, reduced independence
→ measured gait rehab + tone management + equipment / medication / injection options
```

## Observable Patterns

- PwMS gait 可見 decreased walking speed and step length、increased step width、reduced hip extension in stance、reduced knee flexion in swing、reduced ankle dorsiflexion at initial contact。
- Lower limb spasticity 比 upper limb spasticity 更常見。
- Spasticity may worsen sleep and mobility and should be assessed dynamically。
- T25FW is feasible for clinic / research；2MWT / 6MWT add endurance and fatigue information but take longer。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Exercise improves fitness / quality of life without evidence of increasing relapse in cited reviews, but dose must account for heat intolerance, fall risk, strength, endurance, and fatigue。
- Oral baclofen is the usual first-line oral antispasticity agent in this source；tizanidine may be limited by sedation。
- BoNT is useful when focal spasticity or oral medication intolerance limits function。
- ITB should be considered when oral / injectable treatments become ineffective and before severe walking impairment or weakness makes benefit harder to capture。
- Dalfampridine can be trialed for walking impairment but only 30-40% are responders in the source and seizure history is an absolute contraindication。

## Fact

- Approximately 75% of PwMS have mobility challenges in the source。
- Spasticity affects up to 85% of PwMS。
- Lower limb spasticity is reported as 97% vs upper limb 50% among those with spasticity in the source。
- T25FW 20% change is clinically meaningful。
- Canadian physical activity guideline in the source recommends aerobic activity twice weekly and strength training twice weekly with gradual progression。
- ITB studies cited show improvements in spasticity, quality of life, sleep quality, bowel / bladder performance, skin integrity, and ambulation。

## Inference

- AFO / cane / walker / wheelchair decisions should be linked to measured gait pattern, not only patient age or EDSS。
- Tone reduction may improve walking or may unmask weakness; treatment targets must be functional and measurable。

## Assumption

- Patient is not in an acute relapse or untreated infection when long-term gait baseline is measured。

## Uncertainty

- Individual response to dalfampridine, BoNT, cannabis products, and ITB varies; current safety and regulatory rules require separate verification。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MS 有 spasticity 就只能吃 baclofen。」正確是 PT/OT、BoNT、ITB、focal procedures 與 gait-specific goals都可能需要。
- 誤讀：「exercise 會惡化 MS。」正確是來源整理的 evidence 不支持 exercise 增加 relapse / adverse events。
- 誤讀：「T25FW 正常就沒有 mobility problem。」正確是 endurance、fatigue sensitivity、falls、heat intolerance 與 community ambulation 仍需評估。
