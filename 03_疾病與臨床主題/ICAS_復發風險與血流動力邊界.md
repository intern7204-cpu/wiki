# ICAS 復發風險與血流動力邊界

## One-Sentence Definition

ICAS recurrent risk stratification 是用 stenosis severity、recent symptoms、borderzone infarct / impaired collateral flow、hemodynamic symptoms、sex and statin exposure 等特徵辨識 symptomatic ICAS 的高復發風險，但目前 hemodynamic markers 尚不足以單獨決定介入治療。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 symptomatic ICAS 的 recurrence risk and hemodynamic boundary。

不處理：

- ICAS DAPT / statin / BP 處方細節；見 [[Symptomatic_ICAS_次發預防與DAPT邊界]]
- ICAS stenting / angioplasty trial 細節；見 [[ICAS_Stenting與介入治療限制]]
- posterior circulation syndrome localization；見 [[Posterior_Circulation_Stroke_臨床定位與診斷陷阱]]

## Why It Matters

ICAS 的復發風險不是平均風險。Severe stenosis、recent symptoms、borderzone pattern、poor collateral flow and low-flow physiology 會把病人推向高風險端；但這些 marker 目前不能簡化成「低流量就該 stent」。真正的臨床用途是調整 monitoring、BP titration caution、risk communication and specialist follow-up urgency。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Stenosis is symptomatic and anatomically tied to the qualifying event territory。
- Brain and vascular imaging are available enough to assess territory、stenosis severity、infarct pattern and competing mechanisms。
- Patient is being managed for secondary prevention, not only acute reperfusion。

## Mechanism

```text
intracranial stenosis
-> embolic plaque activity and/or reduced distal perfusion reserve
-> recurrence risk rises when stenosis is severe, symptoms are recent, collaterals are impaired, or BP/position/exertion reveals low-flow vulnerability
-> intensive medical therapy and cautious BP management
-> hemodynamic markers remain risk signals, not validated treatment-selection rules
```

## Observable Patterns

| Risk signal | Source-supported meaning | Clinical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Severe stenosis >=70% | higher same-territory stroke risk than <70% in WASID | flags high recurrence risk and longer DAPT window |
| Recent ischemic symptoms | symptoms within prior 17 days had higher same-territory risk in WASID | early follow-up and recurrence vigilance |
| Borderzone infarct + impaired collateral flow | highest recurrent risk in SAMMPRIS MCA subgroup analysis | suggests perfusion vulnerability |
| Symptoms with position / exertion / antihypertensive change | GESICA clinical hemodynamic definition | caution with BP lowering; not a stent indication by itself |
| Low posterior circulation flow on QMRA | VERITAS associated with later vertebrobasilar stroke | risk marker, but evidence not uniform |
| MyRIAD low-flow measures | no association with recurrent infarcts | limits confidence in universal hemodynamic rule |
| Female sex in WASID | higher combined stroke / vascular death and recurrent ischemic stroke | risk marker, not a separate treatment algorithm |
| No statin at SAMMPRIS entry | high-risk feature in medical arm | supports urgent lipid therapy |

## Clinical / Research Implication

Clinical implication：在 ward / rehab handoff 中，ICAS 病人若有 fluctuating deficits、position-related symptoms、exertional symptoms、borderzone infarct or low-flow imaging，應明確標記為 recurrence/perfusion surveillance group，並避免把 BP 快速推到一般 long-term target。

Research implication：需要更可靠的 hemodynamic biomarker，才能判斷哪些病人只是 high risk，哪些病人可能從 intervention 或特殊 BP strategy 得益。

## Fact

- ICAS is associated with high recurrent stroke risk。
- WASID reported territory ischemic stroke rate at one year of 11-12% in symptomatic 50-99% stenosis under aspirin or warfarin-era treatment。
- Modern intensive medical therapy appears to lower recurrence compared with older aspirin-alone cohorts。
- Severe stenosis（>=70%）was associated with higher same-territory stroke risk than stenosis <70% in WASID。
- Symptoms within prior 17 days were associated with higher same-territory stroke risk in WASID。
- Borderzone infarct pattern plus impaired collateral flow had high recurrence risk in a SAMMPRIS MCA subgroup analysis。
- Women in WASID had higher combined stroke / vascular death and recurrent ischemic stroke than men。
- In SAMMPRIS medical arm，old infarct in the stenosis territory、presentation with stroke and absence of statin at entry were high-risk features。
- GESICA defined hemodynamic stenosis clinically by symptoms with positional change、effort/exertion or antihypertensive initiation / increase。
- VERITAS found low posterior circulation flow by QMRA associated with subsequent vertebrobasilar stroke。
- MyRIAD did not show association between low flow by QMRA / perfusion MRI and recurrent infarcts。
- Source concludes hemodynamic stability measures inconsistently predict ischemic risk and need further research。

## Inference

- Hemodynamic warning signs should change how clinicians titrate BP and monitor recurrence, even if they do not authorize intervention。
- Recent symptomatic severe ICAS should be treated as a high-risk recurrence state during transitions of care。
- If a patient's deficits worsen after antihypertensive escalation, ICAS low-flow physiology should be considered rather than assuming ordinary stroke fluctuation。

## Assumption

- The event territory matches the stenotic intracranial artery。
- Perfusion imaging, when available, is interpreted with clinical context and not as a stand-alone decision rule。
- Other recurrence mechanisms, especially AF and dissection, have been considered。

## Uncertainty

- No single clinical or imaging hemodynamic marker is validated enough to select ICAS patients for intervention。
- Sex-associated recurrence differences may reflect biological, treatment or sampling factors; the source does not give a separate treatment pathway。
- Optimal BP target below SBP <140 remains unresolved in low-flow or unstable symptomatic ICAS。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Misreading：hemodynamic symptoms prove the patient needs stenting。  
  Correction：source treats this as a risk marker with inconsistent predictive evidence, not a proven intervention indication。

- Misreading：normal perfusion imaging eliminates recurrence risk。  
  Correction：ICAS recurrence also includes plaque activity, embolism and recent symptoms。

- Misreading：all ICAS patients should undergo aggressive BP lowering to <130/80 immediately。  
  Correction：source advises SBP <140 for symptomatic 50-99% ICAS and caution with lower targets in selected patients。
