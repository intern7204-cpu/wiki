# CRPS 預後復發與預防

## One-Sentence Definition

Adult [[CRPS_臨床辨識與治療限制|CRPS]] 的 prognosis variable：六個月內 symptomatic improvement 常見，但 prolonged disability 與 recurrence 也不少見；prevention 主要是 fracture nutrition、避免過久 immobilization、prior-CRPS surgery risk planning，以及選擇性 vitamin C supplementation，而非 universal prophylaxis。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 adult CRPS 的 long-term disability、recurrence risk 與 prevention strategy。它不是 CRPS diagnosis page，也不是 [[CRPS_多模式治療與升階流程]] 的治療細節頁，更不是 pediatric CRPS recurrence guide。

## Why It Matters

CRPS 預後若被說成「會自然好」會低估 long-term work disability；若被說成「一定終身失能」則會放大 fear-avoidance 與 disability identity。正確表述要同時保留 improvement、persistent symptoms、work limitation 與 recurrence risk。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult CRPS 已臨床診斷。
- 已排除可逆 mimic 或持續傷害來源。
- 若討論 prevention，情境需區分：
  - acute fracture / limb injury 後初級預防。
  - prior CRPS patient 面對 elective surgery / new trauma 的 recurrence prevention。

## Mechanism

```text
Limb trauma / fracture / surgery
-> pain, edema, disuse and immobilization risk
-> CRPS onset or recurrence in susceptible patients
-> nutrition and fracture-healing support
-> safe mobilization when healing allows
-> avoid elective surgery during CRPS exacerbation
-> perioperative planning if prior CRPS history exists
```

## Observable Patterns

### Long-Term Outcome Range

- Symptom improvement within six months is common。
- A substantial proportion still develops prolonged disability。
- Work outcome can range from complete return to work to work with adjustments or inability to work。
- Litigation and work-related compensation issues are common in tertiary pain management populations, but this does not mean CRPS is psychogenic。

### Recurrence Pattern

- Recurrence estimates range approximately 10 to 30 percent。
- Recurrence can be spontaneous, triggered by cold exposure, trauma, new surgery, or emotional trauma。
- Recurrence may occur in a different limb more often than in the originally affected limb in one large study。

### Prevention Pattern

- Vitamin C evidence after distal radius fracture is mixed。
- The source does not support routine vitamin C for all distal fractures or all high-risk injuries。
- The source supports adequate whole-food protein, vitamin C, calcium and vitamin D intake for six to eight weeks after fracture。
- Vitamin C 500 mg daily for six to eight weeks is reasonable in distal radius fracture patients with poor baseline nutritional status or inability to maintain whole-food intake。
- Early mobilization after limb injury may reduce risk, but high-quality evidence is lacking and fracture healing must be protected。
- For patients with prior CRPS, elective surgery should wait until previous CRPS features improve and the patient is stable；avoid surgery during exacerbation。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Prognosis counseling should name both early improvement and prolonged disability risk。
- Work participation should be monitored as an outcome, not only pain score。
- Recurrence counseling should include new limb involvement, not only relapse at the original site。
- Vitamin C should be framed as low-risk but limited-evidence selective supplementation, not a guaranteed CRPS prevention strategy。
- Surgery in a prior-CRPS limb should include explicit perioperative planning rather than reflex avoidance of all future surgery。

## Fact

- Symptomatic improvement within six months is common in this source。
- A population-based Dutch report of 102 patients followed for a mean 5.8 years found ongoing CRPS fulfilling diagnostic criteria in 64 percent。
- In the same Dutch report, patients considered themselves recovered, stable or worse in 30, 54 and 16 percent of cases。
- In the same Dutch report, previous work was resumed completely in 41 percent, resumed with adjustments in 28 percent, and not resumed in 31 percent。
- A retrospective population-based study of 74 cases found resolution of symptoms in 74 percent, sometimes spontaneously；symptom duration ranged 1-60 months, median 7 months。
- Recurrence estimates range approximately 10 to 30 percent。
- A study of 1183 consecutive patients found recurrence in 10 percent and estimated recurrence incidence 1.8 percent per patient-year。
- In that study, recurrence occurred more often in a different limb than in the originally affected limb that had become largely asymptomatic。
- A 416-patient trial in older females with distal wrist fracture found lower one-year CRPS prevalence with any vitamin C dose versus placebo。
- A later 336-adult distal radius fracture trial found no difference in CRPS rate, disability scores, or other functional outcomes with vitamin C 500 mg daily versus placebo。
- A 2021 systematic review of approximately 2000 patients found prophylactic vitamin C 500-1000 mg daily up to 50 days after orthopedic injury or trauma was associated with reduced CRPS rates。
- A meta-analysis of three trials found a nonsignificant trend toward vitamin C benefit, with risk ratio 0.45 and 95 percent CI 0.18-1.13；overall evidence quality was low。
- The source does not find vitamin C evidence compelling enough to warrant routine use in all distal fractures or other high-risk injuries。
- For prior CRPS, the source says elective surgery should be performed when previous CRPS features have improved and the patient is stable, and should be avoided during exacerbations。
- Perioperative strategies proposed for prior CRPS include optimal limb perfusion, avoidance of tourniquet hemostasis, and perioperative intravenous mannitol, but evidence is limited。

## Inference

- Prognosis should be documented as probabilistic, not deterministic。
- Prevention is mostly risk reduction and care planning, not a validated universal prophylaxis bundle。
- Long-term follow-up should track function, work, recurrence, and medication/procedure burden。

## Assumption

- The patient is an adult。
- Fracture care is coordinated with orthopedic healing constraints；mobilization recommendations must not compromise fracture stability。
- Vitamin C supplementation is considered in the context of nutrition, renal stone risk, medication/supplement history and local practice。

## Uncertainty

- Recurrence-prevention interventions have limited evidence and are often based on small case series。
- It is uncertain whether results from children generalize to adult CRPS。
- Vitamin C trial results are inconsistent, and meta-analytic certainty is low。
- The best perioperative strategy for prior CRPS remains unresolved。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「多數六個月內改善，所以 CRPS 不嚴重。」來源同時指出 prolonged disability 仍常見。
- 誤讀：「CRPS 一旦復發只會在原本 limb。」來源中的大型 study 顯示 recurrence 常在不同 limb。
- 誤讀：「Vitamin C 可以預防 CRPS，所有 fracture 都該吃。」來源不支持 routine universal use。
- 誤讀：「prior CRPS 絕對不能再手術。」來源支持穩定後、避免 exacerbation 時、加上 perioperative planning 的選擇性處理。
- 誤讀：「work compensation presence 代表症狀不真實。」來源只是描述 tertiary clinic population 的關聯，不能推論疼痛是裝出來。
