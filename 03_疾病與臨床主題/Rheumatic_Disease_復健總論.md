# Rheumatic Disease 復健總論

## One-Sentence Definition

Rheumatic disease rehabilitation 是把 disease activity、joint biomechanics、pain、ROM、strength、deformity、ADL、work 與 participation 放在 ICF 架構下處理的復健。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../10_來源摘要/Rehabilitation_of_Common_Rheumatological_Disorders]] 建立 common rheumatologic disorders 的復健骨架。
- 本頁不提供 current DMARD / biologic / gout / septic arthritis 藥物 guideline。

## Why It Matters

- Rheumatic disease 會造成 pain、stiffness、joint destruction、deformity、weakness、fatigue、ADL limitation、work disability。
- 只看 x-ray 或只做 modality 會錯過 disease activity、joint protection、orthoses、exercise dose、assistive device 與 environmental modification。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先分清 inflammatory、degenerative、crystal-related、infectious、connective tissue disease。
- Fever、acutely swollen hot joint、marked inflammatory marker elevation、immunosuppression、acute neurologic deficit 或 suspected septic arthritis 不應當 routine musculoskeletal pain 處理。

## Mechanism

```text
Joint inflammation / cartilage degeneration / crystal deposition / infection
-> pain, effusion, stiffness, instability, weakness, deformity
-> impaired gait, hand function, ADL, work, participation
-> medical disease control + rehab evaluation + exercise + joint protection + orthoses
-> improved activity and reduced disability
```

## Observable Patterns

- OA：activity-related pain、limited morning stiffness、crepitus、restricted movement、bony enlargement、function loss。
- RA：small-joint inflammatory pattern、morning stiffness > 1 hour in active stage、tenosynovitis、deformity、extraarticular risk。
- AS：inflammatory back pain、spinal stiffness、sacroiliitis pattern，需注意 posture and mobility。
- Gout / crystal disease：episodic inflammatory arthritis but diagnosis and urate management require medical care.
- Septic arthritis：不能延誤；rehab is secondary to diagnosis and infection control.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- OA imaging abnormality must be matched to clinical findings; asymptomatic radiographic OA is common.
- Exercise should include strengthening, stretching, aerobic and recreational components when safe.
- Joint protection includes load distribution, pacing, assistive device, orthoses, energy conservation, work / home modification.

## Fact

- 來源指出 symptomatic knee OA diagnosis should mainly be based on clinical findings.
- 來源指出 radiographs in older adults commonly show OA changes and symptoms may precede x-ray changes.
- 來源指出 RA pharmacologic treatment includes NSAIDs, low-dose corticosteroid, and DMARDs, but rehab still addresses function.
- 來源列出 patient education、exercise、orthoses、physical modalities as rehabilitation management components.

## Inference

- Rheumatic disease rehab should explicitly document disease stage/activity; otherwise exercise and modality choice may be unsafe or ineffective.

## Assumption

- Disease diagnosis and medical management are coordinated with rheumatology or relevant specialty when inflammatory disease is suspected.

## Uncertainty

- Current biologic / targeted therapy, injection procedures, and emerging OA interventions need updated guideline review.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「X-ray 有 OA，所以痛一定是 OA」不成立。
- 「RA 只要藥物控制，不需要復健」不成立。
- 「關節炎痛就休息」不成立；acute inflammation、chronic disease、joint protection and exercise dose must be separated.
