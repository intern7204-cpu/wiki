# 周邊多發神經病變：典型與非典型型態

## One-Sentence Definition

Typical polyneuropathy 多為 chronic、length-dependent、symmetric、axonal、sensory-motor process；任何偏離此型態的表現都應重新尋找可治療或高風險病因。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 generalized peripheral polyneuropathy 的 pattern recognition、complication 與 rehabilitation implications。
- 本頁不是完整 neuropathy lab workup 或 GBS / CIDP guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Peripheral_Nerve_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- 「腳麻」可以是慢性 metabolic neuropathy，也可以是 inflammatory、toxic、nutritional、infectious、vasculitic、hereditary 或 small-fiber predominant process。
- 對 rehabilitation 來說，neuropathy 的核心後果常是 falls、foot breakdown、pain、deconditioning 與 participation loss。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 評估至少要描述：fiber type、length dependence、symmetry、time course、axonal vs demyelinating、motor / sensory / autonomic involvement。
- 若有 rapid progression、marked asymmetry、proximal involvement、motor-predominant pattern、nonlength-dependent pattern 或 demyelinating-range EDX findings，不應直接視為 typical diabetic neuropathy。

## Mechanism

```text
Length-dependent axonal neuropathy
→ distal sensory loss and impaired ankle proprioception
→ delayed perception of center-of-mass displacement
→ delayed corrective ankle torque
→ dynamic postural instability, falls, avoidance, deconditioning
```

```text
Loss of protective sensation + motor neuropathy + autonomic dysfunction
→ unrecognized trauma, abnormal pressure distribution, dry cracked skin
→ ulceration / infection / Charcot neuroarthropathy
→ amputation risk and mobility loss
```

## Observable Patterns

- Typical neuropathy：gradual toe / forefoot onset，months-to-years distal-to-proximal progression，paresthesia / dysesthesia / sensory loss。
- Balance problem may appear during uneven terrain or darkness; repeated falls can be the initial complaint。
- Early motor exam may be normal；longstanding disease 可見 intrinsic foot / hand atrophy、claw toes、hammertoes、steppage gait。
- Reflexes usually decline length-dependently, often Achilles reflex first。
- Small fiber neuropathy may cause burning pain or temperature perception change, but standard EDX does not adequately assess small fibers。
- GBS：progressive weakness with nadir in 2-4 weeks, early reflex loss, possible respiratory / autonomic / bulbar involvement。
- CIDP：progression over at least 2 months, proximal and distal involvement, demyelinating EDX pattern across multiple nerves。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Falls prevention in neuropathy should include visual correction, lighting, footwear, cane / orthosis strategy, hip / trunk strengthening, and balance training。
- Foot protection requires daily inspection, offloading footwear / orthoses, skin care, temperature caution, podiatry / diabetic foot clinic when needed, and rapid treatment of skin breakdown。
- Neuropathic pain medication choice must include cognition, dizziness, orthostatic symptoms, gait instability, and fall risk。
- Atypical pattern should prompt diagnostic escalation instead of only symptomatic treatment。

## Fact

- Common causes of typical axonal neuropathy in the source include diabetes mellitus, alcohol toxicity, hypothyroidism, renal failure, and metabolic syndrome。
- Impaired glucose tolerance, severity / duration of hyperglycemia, hypertension, elevated triglycerides, tobacco use, obesity, microvascular disease, and cardiovascular disease are listed risk factors。
- The source states strict glycemic control reduces polyneuropathy prevalence by almost 70% and autonomic dysfunction by over 50% in cited data。
- Foot ulcer lifetime risk in diabetic polyneuropathy is cited as 15%。
- Neuropathy is associated with 8- to 18-fold increased foot ulcer risk and 2- to 15-fold greater amputation risk。
- Patients with polyneuropathy are about 20 times more likely to fall than those without neuropathy in the cited source。
- Oral neuropathic pain medication options listed include gabapentinoids, tricyclic antidepressants, and duloxetine；topical capsaicin and lidocaine patches may be considered in selected pain distributions。
- Opioids are described as improving pain and sleep but not function or mood, with cognitive side effects, physiologic dependency, and addiction risk。

## Inference

- Neuropathy management cannot be considered complete unless it includes skin, footwear, balance, environment, assistive device, medication safety, and activity participation。
- In older adults, medication adverse effects and sensory loss may compound each other; a "pain improved" outcome may still be unsafe if falls increase。

## Assumption

- Diabetes or metabolic syndrome is not used to close the diagnostic workup when presentation is atypical.

## Uncertainty

- This source does not replace current guideline-level GBS / CIDP treatment criteria, neuropathy lab panels, or diabetic foot protocols。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「老人腳麻是正常老化。」正確是 aging 會造成 distal decline，但 recurrent falls、Achilles reflex loss、vibration / proprioception loss 仍需 neuropathy evaluation。
- 誤讀：「standard EDX 正常就排除 small fiber neuropathy。」正確是 standard EDX 不足以評估 small fibers。
- 誤讀：「GBS 一定只有運動麻痺。」正確是可有 sensory、cranial nerve、bulbar、respiratory、autonomic、pain 與 fatigue 問題。
- 誤讀：「CIDP 只要看到一個 entrapment site demyelination 就可診斷。」正確是需多神經、多段、符合 clinical / EDX criteria 的 systemic demyelinating pattern。
