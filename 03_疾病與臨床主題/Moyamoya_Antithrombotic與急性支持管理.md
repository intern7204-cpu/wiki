# Moyamoya Antithrombotic與急性支持管理

## One-Sentence Definition

Moyamoya antithrombotic 與急性支持管理，是在 ischemic and hemorrhagic risks coexist 的狀態下，區分 antiplatelet、avoidance of long-term anticoagulation、pregnancy / pediatric considerations and acute perfusion-supportive care。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 antiplatelet / anticoagulation boundary、MMS underlying-condition treatment、pregnancy、acute ischemic stroke supportive management、intracranial hemorrhage acute management and perfusion physiology precautions。Surgical referral、surveillance and revascularization 見 [[Moyamoya_治療決策與Revascularization]]。

## Why It Matters

Moyamoya 同時有 ischemic stroke risk 與 hemorrhagic collateral risk；把它直接套用一般 ischemic stroke antithrombotic algorithm 會出錯。急性期也不能只追求標準降壓或常規止痛，因為 hypocapnia、hypovolemia and hypotension 可能惡化 already impaired cerebral perfusion。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 已確認或高度懷疑 moyamoya vasculopathy。
- 已區分 ischemic-type、hemorrhagic-type、postoperative state or pregnancy。
- 正在處理 acute stroke / TIA、perioperative period、secondary prevention or surveillance。
- Medication decisions 必須納入 bleeding risk、heart failure、age、pregnancy、surgical plan and specialist input。

## Mechanism

```text
Stenotic intracranial arteries + fragile collateral network
-> ischemia risk and hemorrhage risk coexist
-> antiplatelet may support ischemic prevention or bypass patency
-> anticoagulation lacks proven moyamoya-specific benefit and raises hemorrhage concern
-> hyperventilation lowers CO2 and can provoke vasoconstriction
-> hypovolemia or hypotension reduces flow through limited perfusion reserve
-> acute management aims to maintain stable cerebral perfusion physiology
```

## Observable Patterns

- Adult ischemic-type moyamoya：source favors cilostazol unless contraindicated。
- Pediatric ischemic-type moyamoya：source favors aspirin; pediatric cilostazol / clopidogrel evidence limited。
- Hemorrhagic-type moyamoya：avoid antiplatelet acutely and during recovery in most cases。
- Post-revascularization：antiplatelet therapy is typically used to support bypass graft patency。
- Pregnancy：hydration, low-dose aspirin and individualized delivery planning are emphasized。
- Acute ischemic presentation：avoid hyperventilation, pain/agitation, hypovolemia and hypotension。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Antithrombotic choice should document presentation type and surgical status。
- Acute rehab and inpatient nursing plans should include hydration and avoidance of hyperventilation triggers, not just neurologic checks。
- Research should separate bypass patency, ischemic stroke, hemorrhagic stroke, mortality and functional independence outcomes。

## Fact

- Antithrombotic medications are complex in moyamoya because both ischemic and hemorrhagic cerebrovascular complications occur。
- Antiplatelet therapy is frequently used for primary and secondary stroke prevention in moyamoya。
- For most adult patients with asymptomatic or symptomatic ischemic-type moyamoya, this source suggests cilostazol 100 mg twice daily over other agents。
- Adult alternatives when cilostazol is not tolerated or is contraindicated include aspirin 50 to 100 mg daily or clopidogrel 75 mg daily。
- Low-dose aspirin monotherapy is used by this source for pregnant individuals with moyamoya。
- For children with asymptomatic or symptomatic ischemic-type moyamoya, this source suggests aspirin 2 to 5 mg/kg daily。
- Pediatric cilostazol or clopidogrel may be alternatives, but safety and efficacy data are limited。
- For most hemorrhagic-type moyamoya presentations, antiplatelet therapy is avoided acutely and during recovery。
- Postoperatively, antiplatelet therapy is typically started for ischemic or hemorrhagic moyamoya after surgical revascularization to help maintain bypass graft patency。
- Antiplatelet evidence suggests improved bypass patency, reduced hemorrhagic stroke and lower mortality in some analyses, but not clearly reduced ischemic stroke or improved functional independence。
- Long-term anticoagulation is generally contraindicated for children and adults with moyamoya。
- High-quality evidence for benefit of anticoagulation in moyamoya is unavailable。
- Short-term anticoagulation safety is not established and must be individualized when alternatives are unavailable。
- MMS may require treatment of the underlying associated condition; sickle cell disease-associated MMS typically requires transfusion therapy for primary and secondary stroke prevention。
- Pregnant patients should maintain adequate oral hydration。
- Cesarean delivery is commonly selected but available case-series evidence did not prove outcome benefit over individualized planning。
- Revascularization for selected pregnant patients is typically delayed until after delivery and recovery。
- Acute ischemic stroke treatment is mainly supportive: minimize ischemic injury, reduce cerebral edema and control secondary complications such as seizures。
- Hyperventilation from pain, excitement or crying can lower carbon dioxide and worsen cerebral ischemia via vasoconstriction。
- Euvolemia should be maintained with oral or IV fluids; isotonic IV fluids may be given at 1.25 to 1.5 times normal maintenance rate in this source。
- Hypertension may need treatment to reduce future ischemic and hemorrhagic complications, but hypotension and hypovolemia should be avoided。
- IV thrombolysis in moyamoya lacks randomized-trial evidence; it may be considered individually for selected disabling ischemic stroke without brain hemorrhage history after risk-benefit discussion。
- Acute intracranial hemorrhage treatment is similar to non-moyamoya patients, with ventricular drainage, hematoma removal or aneurysm repair when indicated。

## Inference

- A moyamoya patient with dehydration, orthostatic symptoms or repeated hyperventilation-triggered episodes may have modifiable perfusion stressors。
- Long-term anticoagulation should require a separate compelling indication and specialist discussion rather than being used as default stroke prevention。
- In hemorrhagic-type moyamoya, the timing of any antiplatelet restart should be tied to recovery phase and surgical plan rather than automatic secondary prevention。

## Assumption

- Medication dosing is reviewed against patient-specific contraindications, especially heart failure for cilostazol and bleeding risk for any antithrombotic。
- Pregnancy management is coordinated among obstetrics, neurology, anesthesia and neurosurgery。
- Acute treatment occurs in a setting capable of monitoring neurologic status and perfusion risk。

## Uncertainty

- Antiplatelet comparative effectiveness is mainly observational。
- Pediatric cilostazol / clopidogrel evidence remains limited。
- Thrombolysis practice is uncertain because randomized trial evidence is lacking and collateral hemorrhage concern persists。
- Pregnancy delivery-mode evidence is limited and does not support one universal approach。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「moyamoya ischemic risk 高，所以 long-term anticoagulation 更強。」來源相反：long-term anticoagulation generally contraindicated。
- 誤讀：「所有 moyamoya 都吃 aspirin 就好。」Adult ischemic-type 在此來源偏好 cilostazol；hemorrhagic-type acute/recovery 多數避免 antiplatelet。
- 誤讀：「急性中風處理只看 BP target。」Moyamoya 還要避免 hyperventilation、hypovolemia and hypotension。
- 誤讀：「孕婦 moyamoya 一律 Cesarean。」來源指出 delivery plan 應個別化，Cesarean 未證明 outcome benefit。
