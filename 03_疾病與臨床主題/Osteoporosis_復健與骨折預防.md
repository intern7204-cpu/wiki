# Osteoporosis 復健與骨折預防

## One-Sentence Definition

Osteoporosis rehabilitation 是以 BMD / fracture risk、姿勢、fall prevention、bone-specific loading 與 spinal safety 為核心的功能維持策略。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../09_來源摘要/Osteoporosis]] 建立 rehabilitation frame。
- 本頁不作藥物處方 guideline；bisphosphonates、denosumab、PTH analog、hormone therapy 等需另查 current guideline。

## Why It Matters

- Osteoporosis 的臨床後果不是低 T-score 本身，而是 fragility fracture、pain、kyphosis、fear of falling、immobility、loss of independence。
- 不當的 spinal flexion、vertical compression 或高風險跌倒活動可能讓「運動」變成 fracture trigger。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先確認 T-score / Z-score、fragility fracture、vertebral fracture、hip fracture risk、fall history、secondary causes、pain、kyphosis、neurologic / vestibular / vision contributors。
- Acute vertebral fracture、new neurologic deficit、suspected hip fracture、unexplained severe pain 或 malignancy red flag 需先升級。

## Mechanism

```text
Bone resorption > formation + low peak bone mass / aging / secondary cause
-> fragile bone and reduced structural reserve
-> fall or excessive spinal strain
-> vertebral / hip / distal forearm fracture
-> pain, kyphosis, weakness, inactivity
-> further fall and fracture risk
```

```text
Individualized resistance / impact / balance / posture program
-> muscle strength, back extensor support, balance, confidence, mechanical stimulus
-> lower fall and vertebral compression risk
-> preserved function
```

## Observable Patterns

- Vertebral fracture：acute back pain may occur after minor trauma; chronic kyphosis and postural pain may follow.
- Hip fracture：highest mortality and functional consequence among common osteoporotic fractures.
- Fall risk：environmental hazards、weakness、balance disorder、visual impairment、postural hypotension、medication effects。
- High-risk movement：excessive spinal flexion、high vertical compression、poor lifting mechanics。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Exercise prescription should include what to strengthen, what to avoid, how to progress, and what symptoms require stopping.
- Back extensor strengthening and posture training are not cosmetic; they are part of vertebral fracture-risk management.
- Fall prevention should be paired with bone treatment because low BMD and falls interact.

## Fact

- 來源指出 BMD T-score -2.5 or less defines osteoporosis by WHO criteria.
- 來源指出 peak adult bone mass occurs between ages 30 and 35.
- 來源指出 moderate-intensity resistance training and impact exercise can reduce vertebral fracture risk in osteoporosis / osteopenia, with individualized recommendations.
- 來源指出 strong back muscles support posture and skeletal alignment.

## Inference

- Rehabilitation notes should not simply write "strengthening"; in osteoporosis they should specify spine-sparing mechanics and fracture precautions.

## Assumption

- Patient can participate in exercise with monitoring and has no unstable fracture or urgent neurologic / oncologic cause of pain.

## Uncertainty

- Best exercise dose differs by baseline BMD, fracture history, pain, frailty, and comorbidity; current guideline may refine thresholds.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「T-score 不好就只能休息」不成立；immobility can worsen bone and muscle.
- 「任何 resistance training 都安全」不成立；loading direction and fracture risk matter.
- 「沒有痛就沒有 vertebral fracture」不成立；部分 vertebral fractures can be subclinical.
