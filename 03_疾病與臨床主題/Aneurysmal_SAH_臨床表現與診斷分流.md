# Aneurysmal SAH 臨床表現與診斷分流

## One-Sentence Definition

Aneurysmal SAH 臨床表現與診斷分流是 adult nontraumatic sudden or rapid onset severe headache 的 high-stakes emergency workflow：先以 noncontrast head CT 評估 subarachnoid blood，CT negative 但 suspicion 持續時用 lumbar puncture 補上 CT 盲點，確認 SAH 後再以 CTA / DSA 找出 ruptured aneurysm。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 **aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage 的 clinical manifestations and diagnosis**。

不處理：

- Aneurysmal SAH epidemiology / pathogenesis / risk factors。
- Aneurysmal SAH treatment and prognosis；見 [[Aneurysmal_SAH_急性治療與預後管理]]。
- Unruptured intracranial aneurysm。
- Nonaneurysmal SAH / perimesencephalic nonaneurysmal SAH 的完整診斷與處置。
- Mycotic aneurysm from infective endocarditis。

## Why It Matters

SAH 的關鍵風險是漏診，而不是記不住典型教科書圖像。最危險的錯誤是：

- 以為 SAH 一定 coma 或 neurologic deficit。
- 以為 migraine patient 的 severe headache 不需要 SAH workup。
- 以為 CT negative 就一定排除 SAH。
- 以為 CTA 可以無代價取代 LP。

這會延誤 aneurysm securement，增加 rebleeding 與 poor outcome 風險。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with acute nontraumatic severe headache。
- Headache sudden onset or rapidly reaches maximal intensity, especially within one hour。
- Associated vomiting、brief loss of consciousness、neck pain/stiffness、meningismus、seizure、hypertension、Terson syndrome 或 pupil-involving third nerve palsy 會提高 suspicion。
- 但沒有上述特徵不等於排除 SAH。

## Mechanism

```text
Ruptured saccular aneurysm
→ bleeding into subarachnoid space
→ abrupt meningeal irritation and possible ICP surge
→ thunderclap headache ± vomiting/LOC/neck stiffness/seizure
→ patient may still be awake and neurologically intact
→ noncontrast CT detects acute subarachnoid blood best early
→ LP detects CSF blood/xanthochromia when CT is negative but suspicion persists
→ CTA/DSA identifies aneurysm source for definitive management planning
```

### Step 1: Suspect It Early

- Sudden or rapid onset severe headache should always raise SAH suspicion。
- Headache may be isolated。
- In patients with migraine or tension-type headache, SAH headache is usually qualitatively different and often more severe。
- Headache location is not useful for diagnosis。
- Sentinel headache days to weeks earlier may be reported, but the warning-leak concept is uncertain and retrospective data may be biased。

### Step 2: Use Clinical Rules Correctly

Ottawa SAH Rule applies to a narrow population: neurologically intact, acute nontraumatic headache, maximal intensity within one hour。

High-risk features include:

- Age >=40 years。
- Neck pain or stiffness。
- Limited neck flexion。
- Witnessed loss of consciousness。
- Onset during exertion。
- Thunderclap headache。

Interpretation:

- The rule is designed for sensitivity, not specificity。
- It may reduce missed SAH risk, but it eliminates testing in only a minority。
- It should not be used as a broad reassurance tool outside its population。

### Step 3: Noncontrast Head CT First

- All suspected SAH patients require noncontrast head CT。
- CT sensitivity is highest within the first six hours and declines over time。
- CT within 24 hours detects >90% of cases in source summary wording。
- CT sensitivity may drop with low-volume bleeding, delayed imaging or significant anemia。
- Radiologist should be told that SAH is the target diagnosis so subtle cisternal or ventricular blood is actively sought。

### Step 4: Know When LP Is Still Needed

Lumbar puncture is typically required if CT is normal but suspicion remains。

Selected early CT exception requires all core conditions:

- Isolated headache。
- Normal neurologic examination。
- CT performed within six hours of headache onset。
- Modern scanner, technically adequate images。
- Expert interpretation。
- No important caveats such as meningismus, atypical symptoms, significant anemia or unclear onset time。

LP should include:

- Opening pressure。
- CSF RBC and WBC count。
- Tube-to-tube RBC pattern。
- Visual inspection for xanthochromia。

Classic SAH LP pattern:

- Elevated opening pressure。
- RBC count that does not meaningfully diminish from tube 1 to tube 4。
- Xanthochromia。

Pitfall:

- Clearing of blood across tubes is not reliably a traumatic tap unless the final tube normalizes in the right context。
- Xanthochromia may be absent very early；absence before two hours is not useful to exclude SAH。

### Step 5: Identify the Bleeding Source

Once SAH is diagnosed, vascular imaging is required。

- CTA is fast and often available immediately after CT。
- DSA has highest resolution and remains the gold standard for aneurysm detection and anatomy。
- CTA/MRA can miss very small aneurysms；DSA is needed if CTA is negative but SAH is confirmed。
- If initial angiography is negative, repeat angiography in 4-14 days is critical for most patients unless the pattern is perimesencephalic and judged lower risk。

## Observable Patterns

- Thunderclap headache, worst headache of life, or severe headache peaking rapidly。
- Vomiting, brief LOC, neck stiffness, meningismus or lower back pain developing hours later。
- Terson syndrome suggests abrupt ICP rise and poorer prognosis。
- Pupil-involving third nerve palsy mandates aneurysm workup, even though absence of this sign does not lower SAH probability。
- CT blood pattern:
  - basal cisterns / sylvian fissures / interhemispheric fissure can support SAH。
  - perimesencephalic pattern suggests nonaneurysmal SAH。
  - convexal SAH suggests RCVS in younger patients or CAA in older patients。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- ED workflow for severe acute headache should prioritize ruling out SAH with a time-sensitive diagnostic pathway。
- Acute stroke pages should place SAH before treatment details: diagnosis and aneurysm-source identification come before vasospasm prevention.
- Research comparing CT-LP and CT-CTA must account for incidental aneurysm discovery, downstream procedures and missed small aneurysms。
- Rehabilitation handoff after SAH should note whether aneurysm source was identified and secured, because unsecured aneurysm changes activity and transfer safety.

## Fact

- 本來源明確指出 sudden or rapid onset severe headache should always trigger SAH evaluation。
- 本來源指出 all patients with this complaint should undergo immediate evaluation beginning with head CT, even if alert and neurologically intact。
- 本來源指出 LP is typically required for most patients with normal head CT, except selected early-CT negative patients meeting strict conditions。
- 本來源指出 DSA has the highest resolution and remains the gold standard for aneurysm detection/anatomic definition。
- 本來源指出 repeat angiography is necessary if initial study is negative, except selected perimesencephalic pattern where repeat may be optional。

## Inference

- SAH diagnosis should be documented as a pathway status: suspected, CT positive/negative, LP indicated/performed, CSF interpretation, vascular imaging result and whether repeat angiography is needed。
- A patient with sudden severe headache and normal exam should still be treated as potentially high risk until SAH is excluded by the correct pathway。
- CT-CTA strategy may be reasonable in some systems but should be framed as an alternative with downstream consequences, not as a universal LP replacement。

## Assumption

- Adult nontraumatic headache setting。
- CT is available quickly；LP and vascular imaging can be obtained or transferred for。
- Radiology interpretation quality matters, especially for six-hour CT rule-out decisions。

## Uncertainty

- Sentinel headache frequency and mechanism are uncertain。
- The optimal CT-LP vs CT-CTA pathway remains debated across experts and systems。
- Spectrophotometry for xanthochromia is more sensitive but often unavailable and has lower specificity。
- Angiography-negative SAH is heterogeneous; perimesencephalic and nonperimesencephalic patterns should not be pooled casually。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not use “looks well” to rule out SAH。
- Do not use headache location to rule in/out SAH。
- Do not treat a negative CT after more than six hours as definitive rule-out。
- Do not use Ottawa SAH Rule outside the population it was designed for。
- Do not assume negative first angiography rules out aneurysm in diffuse SAH pattern。
