# Poststroke Sleep-Related Breathing Disorders 篩檢與診斷

## One-Sentence Definition

Poststroke sleep-related breathing disorder screening 是在 stroke / TIA 後以高懷疑度、overnight oximetry、polysomnography 或 HSAT 辨識 OSA、CSA and Cheyne-Stokes breathing 的流程，而不是只問 snoring 或 daytime sleepiness。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 adult stroke / TIA 後 sleep-related breathing disorders 的辨識與診斷。治療見 [[Poststroke_Sleep_Apnea_PAP治療與預後邊界]]；stroke risk 機制見 [[OSA_as_Stroke_Risk_Factor_機制與證據邊界]]。

## Why It Matters

Poststroke sleep apnea 很常見，且可能和 early neurologic deterioration、rehabilitation outcome、90-day cognition / function and vascular cognitive impairment 相關。最危險的錯誤是用「沒有嗜睡」或「問卷低分」排除，因為中風病人的典型症狀與自我報告常不可靠。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult acute stroke、subacute stroke、chronic stroke 或 TIA。
- 可取得或安排 overnight oximetry、HSAT type III / IV 或 in-laboratory polysomnography。
- 測試時機需考慮 medical stability、cognition、language、neglect、motor limitation and ability to comply。

## Mechanism

```text
acute stroke or preexisting undiagnosed sleep apnea
-> impaired consciousness, sedating medication, bulbar dysfunction, infratentorial or bilateral lesion effects
-> obstructive or central respiratory events during sleep
-> nocturnal desaturation and sleep fragmentation
-> neurologic deterioration / lower rehab tolerance / worse outcome signal
```

## Observable Patterns

### Prevalence and type

- In acute stroke / TIA, pooled prevalence was 67 percent for AHI >=5, 50 percent for AHI >=15 and 32 percent for AHI >=30 in the source-cited meta-analysis。
- Severe sleep apnea in chronic poststroke / TIA phase was pooled at 25 percent in seven studies。
- OSA is more common than CSA after stroke; CSA or Cheyne-Stokes breathing was predominant in 7 percent in a source-cited analysis。
- Sleep-related breathing disorders may be detectable within 24 hours after stroke。

### Higher-risk clinical cues

- Increased BMI。
- Male sex。
- Systolic hypertension。
- Early neurologic deterioration。
- Nocturnal oxygen desaturation。
- Increased stroke severity。
- Hemorrhagic stroke。
- Prior stroke。
- Atrial fibrillation。
- Dysphagia or dysphonia in acute stroke。

### Screening tools and diagnostic tests

- Berlin questionnaire had only moderate utility in poststroke patients。
- Modified STOP-Bang without neck circumference had high sensitivity but low specificity。
- Formal diagnosis requires polysomnography or HSAT type III / IV because clinical features and questionnaires lack high predictive value。
- At minimum, review overnight oximetry during the first five days poststroke when feasible。
- Frequent nocturnal desaturations, such as 15 or more episodes per hour of >=3 percent SpO2 decrease for >=10 seconds, are highly suggestive of moderate to severe sleep apnea in the source。
- Overnight oximetry can identify inpatient hypoxemia and guide short-term management, but is generally not considered adequate diagnostic testing for long-term PAP prescription。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Acute and rehab stroke workflows should include sleep breathing surveillance, especially when neurologic recovery is worse than expected, nocturnal desaturation appears, or dysphagia / dysphonia is present。
- If inpatient testing is impractical, discharge planning should include sleep testing referral when suspicion remains。
- Repeat testing may be needed because AHI / RDI can change during the first year of recovery。

## Fact

- The source states sleep-related breathing disorders are common in stroke and TIA。
- The source states typical clinical features may be lacking after stroke。
- The source states diagnosis requires formal sleep testing with polysomnography or HSAT type III or IV。
- The source states OSA diagnosis in stroke patients does not require additional symptoms beyond respiratory-event criteria。
- The source states clinicians should at least review overnight oximetry during the first five days poststroke。
- The source states decisions about universal diagnostic testing remain debated and should consider patient preference, PAP adherence potential, functional status, quality of life and goals of care。

## Inference

- In stroke rehab, unexplained fatigue, cognitive fluctuation or poor therapy tolerance should trigger sleep breathing review, not only mood or motivation assessment。
- Dysphagia / dysphonia may be a practical clue because swallowing, phonation and sleep inspiration share oropharyngeal muscle control。

## Assumption

- Testing results are interpreted by clinicians familiar with OSA / CSA distinction and stroke-related limitations。

## Uncertainty

- Universal sleep testing for all stroke patients is not settled。
- Sleep apnea severity may improve or change subtype as stroke recovery progresses。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可用 Berlin、STOP-Bang 或 STOP-Bag 單獨診斷或排除。
- 不可把 normal oximetry 當成所有睡眠呼吸問題的永久排除，尤其症狀或風險仍高時。
- 不可把 OSA / CSA subtype 當成可靠 lesion localization tool。
