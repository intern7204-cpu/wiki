# Ischemic Stroke 預後判讀與恢復時間軸

## One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic stroke 預後判讀是把 acute neurologic severity、age、infarct size/location、stroke mechanism、comorbidities、complications、treatment response and domain-specific recovery 放進同一條時間軸，估計 survival、disability、ADL independence、walking、communication、swallowing、return to work and participation。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 adult ischemic stroke 的總體 prognosis framing，重點在「如何判讀與更新預後」，不是 reperfusion eligibility、secondary prevention regimen、pediatric stroke、ICH / SAH prognosis 或單一 impairment 的完整治療頁。

常見錯誤是把 prognosis 縮成一個分數：NIHSS、mRS、Barthel Index 都有用，但各自只看部分 outcome。對 rehabilitation 而言，hand dexterity、balance、cognition、aphasia、swallowing、neglect、return to work and social participation 必須另外評估。

## Why It Matters

預後判讀會直接影響家屬諮詢、rehabilitation intensity、discharge destination、time-limited trial、goals of care and payer communication。若過早悲觀，會錯失 recovery chance；若過度樂觀，會低估 long-term disability、caregiver burden and secondary prevention urgency。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 適用：adult acute ischemic stroke 到 early rehabilitation phase。
- 需要：serial neurologic examination、NIHSS or similar impairment scale、CT / MRI infarct burden and location、mechanism workup、complication surveillance and rehab-domain assessment。
- 外推限制：posterior circulation / infratentorial stroke、pediatric stroke、neonatal stroke、ICH / SAH 需 dedicated source；小體積 posterior fossa lesion 可造成嚴重 disability，不能直接套用 supratentorial volume-outcome relation。

## Mechanism

```text
ischemic event
-> infarct volume, location and collateral / reperfusion status
-> initial NIHSS and clinical deficits
-> age, prestroke dependency, comorbidity and stroke mechanism modify baseline risk
-> complications and intervention response change early trajectory
-> domain-specific recovery determines ADL, mobility, communication, swallowing, work and participation
```

## Observable Patterns

### Morbidity and mortality

- Source states 30-day case fatality after first ischemic stroke is roughly 13.5-23 percent, with summary range 16-23 percent。
- Minor ischemic stroke still carries long-term risk；one 10-year cohort reported 32 percent cumulative mortality。
- At six months, hemiparesis and cognitive deficits may persist in roughly 40-50 percent range, and hemianopia / aphasia / sensory deficit in roughly 15-20 percent range in the cited older community sample。
- Depression symptoms、unassisted walking limitation、social disability and institutionalization are common six-month outcomes, so prognosis cannot be motor-only。

### Major predictors

- Strongest acute predictors: stroke severity and age。
- Severity should integrate clinical deficit and CT / MRI infarct size and location。
- NIHSS is useful, but it can miss hand function, fine motor coordination, cognition and balance。
- Early NIHSS should be updated after reperfusion therapy and after the first days because deficits may be unstable。
- Large artery occlusion, large infarct volume, poor collateral flow, diffusion-perfusion mismatch, cerebral edema, cardioembolic / large artery mechanism and medical complications generally worsen prognosis。

### Recovery timing

- Many uncomplicated patients improve from 12 hours to 7 days after onset。
- Greatest recovery proportion occurs in the first three to six months。
- Some patients continue to improve up to 18 months。
- Functional outcome at three to six months has long-term survival implication, but this is population-level risk, not individual certainty。

### Domain-specific predictors

- Arm and hand: early active finger extension、grasp release、shoulder shrug、shoulder abduction and active range of motion are favorable signs。
- Walking: early sitting balance, paretic leg contraction/strength, intact corticospinal tract, ADL independence, no cognitive impairment, no neglect and continence support better ambulation prognosis。
- Aphasia: milder initial aphasia has better full recovery chance; recovery time course resembles motor recovery。
- Dysphagia: common early after stroke and often improves; persistent severe dysphagia risk is higher with high NIHSS and bihemispheric lesions。
- Hemianopia: partial homonymous hemianopia has better early full recovery chance than complete homonymous hemianopia；driving requires ophthalmology clearance or formal driver rehabilitation process。

## Clinical / Research Implication

### Clinical

- Prognosis should be phrased as a range plus modifiable next steps: acute treatment response, complication prevention, rehabilitation dose and secondary prevention。
- Use NIHSS for initial severity, mRS / Barthel for global function, and domain-specific tests for motor, balance, language, swallowing, cognition and participation。
- Reassess prognosis after reperfusion, after 24-72 hours, near discharge planning, and at early outpatient follow-up。
- Do not use poor early prognosis language as a substitute for goals-of-care discussion or time-limited trial。

### Research

- Outcome studies should not report only global mRS when the question concerns hand dexterity, walking, language, swallowing or participation。
- Prognostic model papers should state assessment timing, stroke territory, intervention exposure, complication status and rehab access。

## Fact

- The source identifies stroke severity and patient age as the strongest acute predictors。
- NIHSS predicts outcome but misses several impairment domains。
- Neuroimaging size and location are important adjuncts because clinical examination alone can mislead early after stroke。
- Most recovery occurs in the first three to six months, with some patients improving later。
- Reperfusion therapy, stroke unit care, rehabilitation and secondary prevention can improve outcomes when appropriate。

## Inference

- The best bedside prognosis is iterative rather than static。
- Rehabilitation prognosis should be written as domain-specific hypotheses, not one global statement。
- A low NIHSS patient with large vessel occlusion or large perfusion deficit may still have high progression risk。

## Assumption

- The clinician has access to serial examination, imaging and interdisciplinary rehabilitation assessment。
- Prognosis is being used to guide care planning, not to deny indicated therapy。

## Uncertainty

- Socioeconomic and race/ethnicity outcome associations may partly reflect access, comorbidity, stroke severity and rehabilitation dose rather than independent biology。
- Corticospinal tract integrity markers are promising for motor prognosis but not yet routine clinical tools。
- Specific recovery estimates may differ across health systems, rehabilitation availability and stroke territory。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 NIHSS <=6 解讀為一定能回到原工作與社會參與。
- 不可把 NIHSS >=16 解讀為治療無效或不應進入 rehabilitation。
- 不可把 mRS / Barthel 當成完整 rehab outcome；它們不足以代表 hand dexterity、aphasia、cognition、balance、return to work and participation。
- 不可因「最大恢復在 3-6 個月」就否定 chronic phase rehabilitation trials or meaningful late gains。
- 不可把 population-level percentage 直接變成個案確定預後。
