# 慢性肺病 Pulmonary Rehabilitation

## One-Sentence Definition

Pulmonary rehabilitation 是針對 chronic lung disease 的 multidisciplinary program，結合 exercise、breathing retraining、airway clearance、oxygen planning、education、nutrition 與 psychosocial support。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../10_來源摘要/Chronic_Medical_Conditions_Pulmonary_Disease_Organ_Transplantation_and_Diabetes]] 建立慢性肺病 PR 骨架。
- 本頁不替代 current GOLD、ATS / ERS、transplant 或 oxygen coverage policy。

## Why It Matters

- Dyspnea 會造成 activity avoidance and deconditioning；deconditioning 又會降低 exercise tolerance，形成惡性循環。
- PR 的價值是把 physiology、exercise、airway clearance、oxygen logistics 與 self-management 接起來。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需先分 obstruction、restriction、pulmonary vascular disease、hypoventilation 或 mixed pattern。
- Training 前要看 medical stability、SpO2 response、cardiac risk、PFT / CPET / 6MWT、oxygen needs、secretion burden、cognition、motivation。

## Mechanism

```text
Chronic lung disease
-> airflow limitation / restriction / pulmonary vascular limitation / hypoventilation
-> dyspnea + muscle deconditioning + anxiety + secretion burden
-> reduced ADL and community ambulation
-> PR with exercise, breathing, airway clearance, oxygen, education, nutrition
-> improved exercise tolerance and symptom self-management
```

## Observable Patterns

- Obstructive disease：prolonged expiration、air trapping、dynamic airway collapse、dyspnea。
- Restrictive disease：reduced lung volumes、lower compliance、weakness or chest wall limitation。
- Pulmonary vascular disease：exertional limitation, hypoxemia, right-heart strain risk。
- Secretion problem：ineffective cough、bronchiectasis / CF / neuromuscular weakness、atelectasis or infection risk。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Exercise prescription should include aerobic, resistance, flexibility, posture, breathing strategy, monitoring target, and oxygen plan.
- Diaphragmatic breathing should not be reflexively prescribed to all COPD patients.
- Airway clearance techniques need indication; cough suppressants can impair secretion clearance if overused.

## Fact

- 來源定義 PR 為 evidence-based, multidisciplinary, comprehensive intervention。
- 來源指出 COPD 是 PR 最常見 referral reason。
- 來源指出 oxygen therapy 在適當 hypoxemic COPD patients 可降低 mortality。
- 來源指出 diaphragmatic breathing in COPD may increase work of breathing and dyspnea compared with natural breathing pattern.
- 來源列出 airway clearance indications：abnormal cough mechanics、altered mucus rheology、structural airway defects、altered mucociliary clearance。

## Inference

- A PR note should not simply say "increase endurance"; it should document pulmonary physiology, dyspnea trigger, SpO2 response, secretion strategy, and progression logic.

## Assumption

- Patient can participate safely in progressive cardiorespiratory endurance training or receives preparatory basic mobility / strengthening first.

## Uncertainty

- Long COVID pulmonary rehabilitation mechanisms and best protocols remain evolving.
- Oxygen policy and device availability vary by system.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「COPD 就教 diaphragmatic breathing」不一定正確。
- 「PR 只有 aerobic exercise」不成立。
- 「休息避免喘就是安全」不成立；activity avoidance can worsen deconditioning.
