# Vascular Dementia 認知藥物與預後限制

## One-Sentence Definition

Vascular dementia 的 cognitive medication treatment 只有有限角色：donepezil 或 galantamine 可考慮用於 progressive cognitive decline 且不能直接歸因於 clinical stroke 的 VaD，但效益小且臨床意義不確定；memantine 不建議 routine 使用，預後則因 vascular burden、stroke recovery 和 mixed Alzheimer disease pathology 而高度異質。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 VaD cognitive medication、nonpharmacologic evidence boundary 和 prognosis。它不處理完整 Alzheimer disease medication protocol，不處理 behavioral symptom medication，也不取代 [[Dementia_復健與Caregiver支持框架]]。本頁假設 VaD / VCID diagnosis 已依 [[Vascular_Dementia_診斷與病因分層]] 建立，而不是只因 MRI 有 vascular lesion。

血管風險與 antithrombotic 邊界見 [[Vascular_Cognitive_Impairment_血管風險與Antithrombotic邊界]]。

## Why It Matters

VaD 很容易被誤解成「另一種 Alzheimer disease，所以直接套 cholinesterase inhibitor / memantine」。來源的立場較窄：cholinesterase inhibitors 只在特定 progressive decline 情境下建議，且效果 slight；memantine evidence 不夠 compelling；nonpharmacologic care 重要，但對 cognition 的 high-quality evidence 不足。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with vascular dementia / vascular cognitive impairment。
- 需判斷 cognitive trajectory：
  - acute stroke recovery phase。
  - stable cognitive impairment consistent with stroke syndrome。
  - ongoing progressive cognitive decline not directly attributable to a clinical stroke。
- 需承認 mixed Alzheimer disease pathology 常見且難以排除。

## Mechanism

```text
Vascular brain injury +/- mixed Alzheimer disease pathology
-> cognitive impairment and functional limitation
-> selected progressive cases may have small symptomatic response to cholinesterase inhibitors
-> no robust disease-reversing drug effect established for pure VaD
-> prognosis depends on recurrent vascular injury, white matter burden, medial temporal atrophy and baseline cognition
```

## Observable Patterns

### Cholinesterase Inhibitor Use

- Consider donepezil or galantamine when progressive cognitive decline cannot be directly attributed to a clinical stroke。
- Do not initiate when dementia is diagnosed after a stroke but there is no progressive cognitive decline。
- Expected benefit is slight and of uncertain clinical significance。
- It is reasonable for some patients to forego this treatment。

### Memantine Boundary

- Source suggests against memantine in VaD because benefit evidence is not compelling。
- Memantine trials showed cognitive-scale benefit but not clinical global impression or ADL benefit。
- Some clinicians may use it when AD cannot be ruled out and is comorbid, but this is not the source's preferred VaD-specific recommendation。

### Nonpharmacologic Boundary

- Exercise and social interactions are suggested。
- High-quality evidence for benefit of exercise, nutrition or lifestyle changes on cognition in established VaD is lacking。
- A small aerobic exercise trial showed short-term cognitive performance improvement, but benefit did not persist after retesting six months later。
- Poststroke rehabilitation programs may include cognitive and physical rehabilitation, but evidence is limited by inconsistent study quality and nonstandardized interventions。

### Prognosis Pattern

- VaD is heterogeneous, so consistent progression is not defined。
- After acute stroke, some cognitive recovery is expected。
- After initial recovery, some patients remain stable while others continue to decline。
- White matter change severity, medial temporal lobe atrophy, recurrent strokes and baseline impaired cognition before stroke are poor prognostic factors in this source。
- Population-based studies suggest increased mortality in vascular cognitive impairment, VaD and dementia after stroke。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Medication trial should define what will be monitored: cognition, global function, ADL, caregiver report, adverse effects and stop criteria。
- Cholinesterase inhibitor use is not a substitute for vascular risk modification and recurrent stroke prevention。
- A stable poststroke cognitive syndrome should not automatically receive cholinesterase inhibitor solely because dementia is present。
- Nonpharmacologic rehabilitation should target function, safety, caregiver support and participation even when cognition-specific evidence is weak。

## Fact

- The source suggests cholinesterase inhibitor therapy for VaD patients with progressive cognitive decline not directly attributable to clinical stroke。
- This is graded Grade 2B in the source summary。
- The source does not initiate cholinesterase inhibitor therapy in dementia diagnosed after stroke without progressive cognitive decline。
- Many VaD patients have concomitant Alzheimer disease。
- A meta-analysis of six cholinesterase inhibitor trials found statistically significant benefit on ADAS-Cog, but the effect was about two points and of uncertain clinical significance。
- Donepezil or galantamine has somewhat better evidence than rivastigmine in this source。
- The source suggests against memantine use in VaD because evidence is not compelling。
- Two memantine studies showed benefit on cognitive scales but not on clinical global impression or activities of daily living。
- Investigational agents have mostly inconclusive evidence。
- Findings for isosorbide mononitrate alone or with cilostazol after recent lacunar infarction require confirmation before recommendation。
- Lifestyle interventions such as exercise and social interactions are suggested, but high-quality evidence in established VaD is lacking。
- Prognosis is not predictable because VaD is heterogeneous。
- Life expectancy appears somewhat shortened in VaD in this source。

## Inference

- Cholinesterase inhibitor in VaD should be treated as a modest symptomatic trial, not a strong disease-specific treatment。
- If functional decline is driven mainly by recurrent stroke risk, undertreated BP, AF, lipid, diabetes or inactivity, dementia medication alone addresses the wrong layer。
- Rehabilitation remains rational because function and caregiver burden can improve or stabilize even when cognitive test gains are limited。

## Assumption

- Clinician can distinguish progressive decline from fixed poststroke deficit well enough for medication decision-making。
- Drug adverse-effect risk and patient/caregiver goals are included in the decision。
- This source is applied to adults, not pediatric or congenital vascular cognitive syndromes。

## Uncertainty

- Mixed AD pathology makes "pure VaD" treatment response hard to estimate。
- ADAS-Cog change may not translate into meaningful daily function。
- Nonpharmacologic intervention dose, content and target population remain uncertain for established VaD。
- Prognosis cannot be predicted accurately from this source alone。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「VaD 就照 AD 開 donepezil / memantine。」來源只支持 selected cholinesterase inhibitor trial，且不建議 routine memantine。
- 誤讀：「Cholinesterase inhibitor 對 VaD 效果明確。」來源描述為 slight benefit with uncertain clinical significance。
- 誤讀：「沒有認知改善證據就不用復健。」復健仍可針對 function、safety、caregiver burden 和 participation。
- 誤讀：「poststroke cognitive impairment 一定會惡化。」來源指出有些病人在 initial recovery 後可能 stable，有些才繼續 decline。
