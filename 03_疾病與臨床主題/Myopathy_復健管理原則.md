# Myopathy 復健管理原則

## One-Sentence Definition

Myopathy rehabilitation 是長期 interdisciplinary care，核心是反覆評估 strength / mobility / ROM / ADL，並以疾病特異的 exercise safety、contracture prevention、equipment matching、pain management 與 participation 為目標。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 myopathic disorders 的通用復健管理原則。
- 本頁不是 DMD、FSHD、IBM、Pompe disease 或 inflammatory myopathy 的 disease-specific protocol。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Myopathic_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- 肌病復健不是單純「多練肌力」；不適合的 eccentric / high-resistance exercise 可能加重 muscle damage。
- Mobility aid、orthosis、power wheelchair、seating、transfer equipment 與 home modification 是功能保存，不是治療失敗。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 每次追蹤都應評估 strength、mobility、ROM、falls、transfer safety、ADL assistance、pain、equipment fit、respiratory / bulbar symptoms。
- Exercise plan 需依 diagnosis、disease stage、fall risk、rhabdomyolysis risk、cardiac / respiratory status 與 post-exertional symptoms 調整。

## Mechanism

```text
Myopathic weakness
→ altered gait, falls, fatigue, contracture, pain, ADL limitation
→ staged reassessment and individualized supports
→ mobility aid / orthosis / seating / home adaptation / exercise modification
→ safer participation and reduced secondary disability
```

## Observable Patterns

- Progressive proximal weakness 可造成 waddling gait、lumbar lordosis、compensated Trendelenburg gait、difficulty rising from chair。
- Loss of active movement at a joint increases contracture risk and shifts care toward passive ROM / splinting。
- Upper-limb weakness may require adaptive utensils、universal cuffs、forearm orthoses、voice-activated technology、communication devices。
- Seating / cushion / positioning problems may present as pain、poor posture、pressure risk、transfer difficulty。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Strength and mobility assessment should occur at each visit, ideally with PT input。
- Low-intensity exercise appears safe and beneficial in FSHD according to cited AAN guidance, but this cannot be generalized to all myopathies。
- Eccentric high-resistance exercise should be avoided in diseases such as DMD。
- Exercise causing delayed-onset muscle soreness is generally not recommended by many neuromuscular physicians。
- Passive ROM should be used when active movement is substantially reduced。

## Fact

- The source lists interdisciplinary team members including PT, OT, SLP, rehabilitation psychology, nutrition, social work, assistive technology, orthotics, wheelchair vendors, physiatry, neurology, rheumatology, orthopedics, endocrinology, pulmonology, and cardiology。
- Transfer boards, transfer benches, and lifts can facilitate safer transfers。
- Contractures can worsen function, skin breakdown, posture, positioning, pain, hygiene access, and comfort。
- Contracture prevention includes regular ROM assessment, passive ROM, stretching, bracing, custom nocturnal foot splints, and resting hand splints。
- Pain is common; one DMD study cited in the source reported pain in 73% of participants。
- Pain contributors include cramps, altered biomechanics, posture strain, contractures, osteoporosis / fracture risk, and regional musculoskeletal pathology。

## Inference

- Rehabilitation goals should be stage-specific: preserve ambulation when possible, transition to wheelchair before unsafe overexertion, maintain upper-limb function, and reduce caregiver burden。
- Pain treatment should not assume muscle inflammation; musculoskeletal overload and seating / positioning problems may be the driver。

## Assumption

- The rehabilitation team knows the likely natural history of the specific myopathy or has neuromuscular specialist input。

## Uncertainty

- The source explicitly notes sparse literature for exercise guidance across muscle disease；many prescriptions remain individualized rather than evidence-fixed。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「肌病就是不能運動。」正確是 exercise 可能可行，但需 disease-specific、low-risk、gradual、symptom-monitored。
- 誤讀：「power wheelchair 會讓病人退化。」正確是當 manual propulsion 不安全或不可行時，power mobility 可維持 independence、減少疲勞與壓力傷風險。
- 誤讀：「contracture 出現再處理即可。」正確是 contracture difficult to treat，預防比補救重要。
