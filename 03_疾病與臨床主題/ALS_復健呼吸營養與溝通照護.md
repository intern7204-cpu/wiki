# ALS 復健呼吸營養與溝通照護

## One-Sentence Definition

ALS care 是 anticipatory multidisciplinary care：用 stage-based rehabilitation、routine respiratory monitoring、nutrition / PEG planning、AAC、mood / cognition care 與 early palliative care 維持 function、survival 與 quality of life。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 ALS supportive / rehabilitation care，不是 disease-modifying medication protocol。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Motor_Neuron_Diseases]]。

## Why It Matters

- ALS 功能狀態快速改變，等病人主訴再處理 respiratory、nutrition 或 communication，常已錯過低風險介入窗口。
- 來源明確指出 multidisciplinary clinics、NIV、PEG、riluzole 可影響 survival。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 每次追蹤需評估 mobility、falls、orthosis / wheelchair need、ROM、spasticity、bulbar symptoms、weight、meal duration、FVC / MIP、secretion、speech intelligibility、mood、cognition、goals of care。
- Exercise plan 必須避免 fatigue、dyspnea、eccentric exercise、cramping 與 overuse weakness。

## Mechanism

```text
Progressive UMN / LMN loss
→ weakness, spasticity, dysphagia, hypoventilation, secretion burden, communication loss
→ staged equipment + exercise modification + NIV / cough assist + PEG / RIG + AAC
→ fewer crises, improved survival, better quality of life, clearer end-of-life decisions
```

## Observable Patterns

- Stage 1-2：ambulatory and independent；可需要 AFO、wrist-hand orthosis、opponens splint、OT work simplification。
- Stage 3：still ambulatory but severe selected weakness；lift chair、elevated toilet seat、walker、manual wheelchair for distance may become relevant。
- Stage 4-6：power wheelchair、hospital bed、Hoyer lift、shower chair、pressure relief、positioning、caregiver training、palliative focus progressively become central。
- Dysphagia often starts with thin liquids；later solids, prolonged meals, weight loss, choking, dehydration become triggers for PEG / RIG discussion。
- Respiratory symptoms include fatigue、dyspnea、orthopnea、morning headaches, but subclinical impairment is common。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Respiratory measurements should be performed every visit regardless of symptoms。
- NIV should be discussed before crisis; the source describes improved quality of life, survival, and slower respiratory decline。
- PEG / RIG indications include weight loss >10%, dehydration, meals limited by dysphagia, choking, or meals taking >30 minutes。
- PEG / RIG should be discussed before FVC drops below 50% predicted when possible。
- PEG does not reduce aspiration pneumonia and should not be sold as aspiration prevention。
- AAC and voice banking should be discussed early while the patient can still record a strong voice。
- Palliative care should be involved early and wishes should be revisited repeatedly。

## Fact

- ALSFRS-R is the most commonly used longitudinal functional tool but identical total scores can reflect different stage / prognosis because subdomains differ。
- Riluzole is described as prolonging survival by around 4 months and should be started early in the source。
- Edaravone benefit is described as subgroup-limited; randomized survival data are lacking in the source。
- Tofersen is for SOD1-ALS and uses intrathecal dosing in the source。
- NIV prolongs survival by 205 days in cited data and improves energy, dyspnea, sleep quality, depression, concentration, and fatigue measures。
- Use of NIV >4 hours/day offers additional survival advantage in cited data。
- Oxygen therapy can worsen CO2 retention and cause hypercapnic coma or respiratory arrest。
- Mechanical insufflation-exsufflation can be used when peak cough flow is below 300-350 L/min in the source。
- Pseudobulbar affect is not a mood disorder and may occur in over half of ALS patients in cited data。
- Cognitive difficulties affect up to half of ALS patients；about 15% meet FTD criteria in the source。

## Inference

- ALS clinic workflow should be checklist-driven because respiratory, nutrition, communication, and cognition issues are commonly underreported。
- AFO or dorsiflexion assist can be harmful if plantarflexion spasticity triggers clonus; orthosis choice must match tone pattern, not only weakness。

## Assumption

- Patient preferences and caregiver capacity are reassessed as disease stage changes。

## Uncertainty

- NIV thresholds, medication indications, and coverage rules are policy-sensitive and need current verification before clinical orders。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「ALS 沒藥可治，所以復健沒用。」正確是來源指出 supportive / rehab measures 可 improve function、quality of life，且部分措施延長 survival。
- 誤讀：「給 oxygen 可治 ALS dyspnea。」正確是 oxygen 可抑制 respiratory drive 並加重 hypercapnia，除 terminal symptomatic hypoxia palliation 外不應常規使用。
- 誤讀：「PEG 可預防 aspiration pneumonia。」正確是 PEG 支持 nutrition / survival，但不降低 aspiration pneumonia。
