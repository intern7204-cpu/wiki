# Brainstem Ocular Motor Syndromes

## One-Sentence Definition

Brainstem ocular motor syndromes 是以 gaze limitation、saccade / pursuit / VOR / convergence pattern、pupil 與 nystagmus 特徵定位 midbrain、pons 或 extraaxial mimic 的臨床框架。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 vertical gaze palsy、dorsal midbrain syndrome、INO、horizontal gaze paresis、global gaze paralysis 與 one-and-a-half syndrome。
- 本頁不處理完整 neuro-ophthalmology differential 或 treatment guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Brainstem_Syndromes]]。

## Why It Matters

- Eye movement exam 可以在 altered mental status 或 history 不完整時提供客觀 brainstem localization。
- MG、Miller Fisher syndrome、GBS、thyroid eye disease、cavernous sinus disease 與 chronic progressive external ophthalmoplegia 可模擬 central ocular motor syndrome。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 評估需包含 spontaneous gaze、saccade、pursuit、VOR、convergence、pupil、ptosis、nystagmus、fatigability 與 associated brainstem signs。
- 時間軸與年齡會改變 differential：acute older adult 偏 stroke；gradual older adult with downgaze problem 需考慮 PSP；young adult 需考慮 MS 或 other inflammatory / structural causes。

## Mechanism

```text
Specific ocular motor network lesion
-> selective abnormality in vertical gaze, horizontal gaze, internuclear coordination, or convergence
-> pattern separates riMLF, posterior commissure, MLF, PPRF / CN VI nucleus, and extraaxial disorders
```

## Observable Patterns

- Combined vertical gaze ophthalmoplegia：upgaze and downgaze paresis，常指向 riMLF pathway。
- Dorsal midbrain / Parinaud pattern：upgaze palsy、light-near dissociation、convergence-retraction nystagmus、lid retraction。
- INO：MLF lesion；horizontal gaze adduction impairment with internuclear pattern；isolated MLF lesion usually preserves convergence。
- Horizontal gaze paresis：可來自 frontopontine tract、mesencephalic reticular formation、PPRF 或 CN VI nucleus。
- One-and-a-half syndrome：PPRF or CN VI nucleus lesion plus internuclear fibers。
- Global ophthalmoplegia isolated：來源提醒多半是 extraaxial，而非 focal brainstem lesion。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- INO in older adult often suggests stroke; in young adult often suggests MS in the source.
- Intact convergence helps distinguish isolated MLF lesion from CN III, medial rectus, or neuromuscular junction disorders.
- MRI with appropriate cuts is important when INO or focal brainstem localization is suspected.
- When isolated ocular motor findings lack obligatory brainstem signs, evaluate extraaxial mimics.

## Fact

- Dorsal midbrain syndrome can be caused by pineal tumor, stroke, trauma, MS, AVM, PSP, and other etiologies depending on age and course.
- Pineal tumor is described as the most common cause of isolated upgaze paresis in the source.
- INO can be a false localizing sign from brainstem compression.
- MG, GBS, and Miller Fisher syndrome can simulate INO or one-and-a-half-like patterns.

## Inference

- Ocular motor localization should use pattern recognition plus mimic exclusion, not one sign alone.
- VOR and convergence testing are clinically useful because they separate supranuclear, internuclear, nuclear, nerve, NMJ, and muscle problems.

## Assumption

- Patient cooperation, vision, attention, and arousal are sufficient to interpret the exam, or the limitation is documented.

## Uncertainty

- Some syndromes overlap clinically, and imaging / laboratory evaluation is required when stroke, demyelination, infection, tumor, MG, or GBS / Miller Fisher is plausible.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「眼睛動不了就是 brainstem stroke」不成立；isolated complete ophthalmoplegia 多半先想 extraaxial。
- 「有 INO 就一定是 MS」不成立；年齡與急性時間軸會使 stroke 更可能。
- 「VOR preserved / impaired 不重要」不成立；它是 localization 的關鍵。
