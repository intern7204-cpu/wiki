# AF Related Stroke 病因歸屬與診斷評估

## One-Sentence Definition

AF related stroke attribution 是在 ischemic stroke / TIA patient with atrial fibrillation 中，判斷 LAA / cardiac embolism 是否為最可能機制，同時保留 large artery disease、small vessel disease、aortic embolism 或其他 competing mechanisms 的診斷空間。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 AF patient 發生 ischemic stroke / TIA 時的病因歸屬與 diagnostic evaluation。

不處理：

- acute IVT / EVT 操作細節。
- anticoagulation restart timing 與長期藥物選擇；見 [[AF_Stroke_Anticoagulation_時機與長期策略]]。
- anticoagulation failure；見 [[AF_Stroke_Anticoagulation_Failure]]。
- 完整 stroke etiology classification；見 [[Stroke_Etiologic_Classification]]。

## Why It Matters

看到 AF 後停止診斷，是 stroke care 的常見錯誤。AF patient 的 stroke 很可能是 cardioembolic，但 older AF patients 同時常有 carotid disease、intracranial atherosclerosis、hypertension-related small vessel disease、aortic plaque 或 malignancy / hypercoagulability。病因歸屬錯誤會造成錯誤的 carotid intervention、antithrombotic selection、recurrent stroke workup 與 rehabilitation risk framing。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult ischemic stroke 或 TIA。
- Known AF、newly recognized AF，或 prolonged monitoring 後偵測到 AF。
- 已取得或可取得 brain imaging、neurovascular imaging、ECG / rhythm monitoring、TTE，並能在 selected scenario 做 TEE。

## Mechanism

```text
atrial fibrillation
-> left atrial mechanical dysfunction and stasis
-> left atrial appendage thrombus risk
-> embolus to cerebral artery
-> large cortical, multi-territory, or wedge-shaped infarct pattern
```

但同一病人也可能：

```text
age / hypertension / diabetes / atherosclerosis
-> large artery stenosis or small vessel disease
-> ischemic stroke in a patient who happens to have AF
```

所以診斷不是「AF yes/no」，而是「AF 是否最能解釋 infarct pattern、vascular imaging、cardiac findings and competing-risk profile」。

## Observable Patterns

### Suggestive of AF cardioembolism

- Deficit often more severe than carotid-source embolism.
- Infarct may involve one vascular territory or multiple vascular territories.
- Imaging may show wedge-shaped infarcts involving cortex and adjacent subcortical white matter.
- Other patterns include striatocapsular infarction from MCA stem occlusion or borderzone infarcts.
- AF-related events are more often hemispheric than retinal compared with carotid disease in the source-cited trial comparison.
- "Longer" TIAs in AF patients may show abnormal DWI and meet revised AHA tissue-based stroke definition.

### Does not prove AF causality

- AF is common in older adults.
- High-grade ipsilateral cervical / intracranial stenosis may be an independent or competing cause.
- Lacunar-appearing small subcortical infarct may reflect small artery disease.
- Competing mechanisms can still coexist even when long-term anticoagulation remains indicated for AF.

### Silent cerebral infarction

- The source cites a 2014 systematic review/meta-analysis showing silent cerebral infarction prevalence among AF patients of 40 percent by MRI and 22 percent by CT.
- AF was associated with more than twofold increased odds of silent cerebral infarction in patients without symptomatic stroke history.
- Most pooled studies were cross-sectional, so direct causality remains uncertain.

## Diagnostic Evaluation

- Brain imaging and neurovascular imaging are required to define infarct pattern, hemorrhage exclusion, LVO, stenosis, dissection or other vascular cause.
- Acute rhythm monitoring remains part of evaluation, but known AF does not remove the need for mechanism workup.
- TTE is recommended for most ischemic stroke patients, mainly to investigate structural conditions associated with AF and other cardiac findings.
- TEE may identify LAA thrombus and other embolic sources such as complex aortic plaque.
- TEE is not used to exclude AF causality because residual atrial thrombus may or may not remain visible after embolization.
- For cryptogenic stroke / TIA without AF on short-term ECG / telemetry, prolonged monitoring for two to four weeks is suggested by the source.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Clinical note should write "AF-associated cardioembolic stroke favored" only when imaging and evaluation support it.
- If AF and high-grade ipsilateral carotid stenosis coexist, both mechanisms must be considered; treatment may require anticoagulation plus carotid-specific management.
- Research cohorts should separate known AF before stroke, AF detected during admission, and AFDAS detected later by prolonged monitoring.

## Fact

- The source states that ischemic stroke in AF is commonly caused by cardiac embolus, most commonly LAA thrombus.
- The source states that AF cardioembolic stroke is generally associated with increased clinical severity compared with embolic stroke from carotid disease.
- The source states that AF cardioembolic stroke may affect any or multiple vascular territories and often appears as wedge-shaped cortical/subcortical infarcts.
- The source states that AF is associated with silent cerebral infarction, but the cited meta-analysis was largely cross-sectional.
- The source states that AF presence does not always mean causal relationship.
- The source states all stroke patients, even with AF, may benefit from complete evaluation for other causes.
- The source states the evaluation generally includes brain and neurovascular imaging, acute rhythm monitoring and TTE.
- The source states TEE can identify LAA thrombus and other embolic sources, but is not used to exclude AF as cause.

## Inference

- AF should be documented as a powerful mechanism hypothesis, not as self-proving etiology.
- In post-stroke rehabilitation, anticoagulation need and stroke mechanism attribution should be checked separately.
- A silent infarct in AF supports vascular brain injury burden but should not by itself specify embolic mechanism without pattern review.

## Assumption

- The patient is an adult with confirmed ischemic stroke / TIA or radiographic infarction.
- Imaging and cardiac testing are technically interpretable.

## Uncertainty

- AFDAS may include neurogenic and cardiogenic subtypes; source states anticoagulation management does not currently distinguish them.
- Silent cerebral infarction causality remains limited by cross-sectional data.
- Attributable fraction when AF and ipsilateral high-grade stenosis coexist remains clinically difficult.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 AF 當成停止 vascular imaging 的理由。
- 不可把 TEE 沒看到 LAA thrombus 解讀成 AF 不可能是原因。
- 不可把 multi-territory infarcts 全部歸因 AF；aortic, malignancy and other embolic sources 仍需考慮。
- 不可把 AFDAS 當成與 known AF 完全相同的 biology；目前 treatment 邏輯相近，但 mechanism 仍未定。
