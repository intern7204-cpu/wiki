# Parkinson-Plus Syndrome 紅旗

## One-Sentence Definition

Parkinson-plus syndrome 是帶有 parkinsonism 但同時有 early falls / gaze palsy、autonomic failure / cerebellar signs、或 asymmetric cortical signs 的 atypical neurodegenerative syndrome，不能用 idiopathic PD 模板硬套。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理來源中的 PSP、MSA、CBGD clinical red flags。
- 本頁不是完整 atypical parkinsonism guideline 或 neuropathology review。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Degenerative_Movement_Disorders_of_the_CNS]]。

## Why It Matters

- PSP、MSA、CBGD 會和 PD 重疊，但 levodopa response、falls、autonomic crisis、dysphagia、cognitive / cortical signs、survival 與 caregiver planning 都不同。
- 早期辨識會改變安全目標、吞嚥監測、跌倒預防、autonomic management 與 prognosis counseling。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 對 parkinsonism 病人要固定追問：falls onset timing、gaze / saccade 問題、dysphagia、autonomic failure、cerebellar ataxia、pyramidal signs、apraxia、cortical sensory loss、alien limb、levodopa response。
- Poor levodopa response 需確認 dose、duration、adherence 與 side effect 後再判讀。

## Mechanism

```text
Non-idiopathic PD neurodegenerative pathology
-> parkinsonism plus gaze / autonomic / cerebellar / cortical features
-> faster disability accumulation and poor / atypical levodopa response
-> prognosis-aware rehabilitation and supportive planning
```

## Observable Patterns

- PSP：axial rigidity、bradykinesia、postural instability with falls、cognitive deficits、supranuclear vertical gaze palsy。
- PSP common initial feature：gait disturbance and history of falling, typically within 12 months of onset。
- PSP clues：stuttering、palilalia、early dysphagia、personality change、sleep disturbance、apraxia、dementia、perpetual astonishment facial expression、extensor neck posturing、pseudobulbar palsy、hyperreflexia、Babinski signs。
- MSA：autonomic failure、poor levodopa-responsive parkinsonism、cerebellar ataxia、pyramidal signs。
- MSA autonomic symptoms：orthostatic lightheadedness、syncope、urinary retention / incontinence、sexual dysfunction、fecal incontinence、sweating abnormality。
- CBGD：dystonia、postural instability、athetosis、orofacial dyskinesia、apraxia、cortical sensory loss、alien limb phenomenon、dementia、frontal lobe reflexes；最突出特徵是 asymmetry。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- PSP classic syndrome differs from PD by lack of tremor, relatively spared limb movement except writing, greater neck rigidity than limb rigidity, and early freezing gait, but atypical signs may still occur.
- MSA central diagnostic elements are autonomic dysfunction plus cerebellar ataxia or poorly levodopa-responsive parkinsonism; no definitive diagnostic test exists in the source.
- MSA management is largely symptomatic because disease-modifying treatment is lacking; autonomic symptoms are often more treatable than cerebellar manifestations.
- CBGD MRI may show asymmetric cortical atrophy greater contralateral to the more involved side and can help exclude other processes.

## Fact

- PSP is an adult-onset rapidly progressive neurodegenerative syndrome and a common cause of levodopa-nonresponsive parkinsonism.
- Definite PSP is neuropathologic postmortem diagnosis in the source.
- MSA is rare, adult-onset, rapidly progressive, and fatal; mean onset is approximately 56 years in the source.
- MSA median survival is 9 years, with variation up to 15 years in the source.
- Only one-third of MSA patients show any levodopa response, and response is almost always atypical in the source.
- CBGD mean life expectancy from onset is 6.6 years in the source, and treatment is purely symptomatic.

## Inference

- Early falls or dysphagia in a patient labeled PD should immediately raise PSP-level concern.
- Early severe orthostatic hypotension, urinary retention / incontinence, or cerebellar ataxia should raise MSA-level concern.
- Marked asymmetry with apraxia, cortical sensory loss, or alien limb should raise CBGD-level concern.

## Assumption

- Patient has been assessed longitudinally, because early PD and Parkinson-plus syndromes can overlap.

## Uncertainty

- The source warns that relying strictly on supranuclear vertical gaze palsy may reduce PSP sensitivity or delay diagnosis.
- MSA can mimic PD more closely than PSP, so early distinction may remain uncertain.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「沒有 vertical gaze palsy 就不是 PSP。」正確是來源明確提醒這樣會延誤診斷。
- 誤讀：「有 levodopa response 就不是 MSA。」正確是 MSA 有時可有 atypical response。
- 誤讀：「Parkinson-plus 沒有治療所以不用早診斷。」正確是安全、autonomic、吞嚥、caregiver 與 prognosis planning 都會改變。
