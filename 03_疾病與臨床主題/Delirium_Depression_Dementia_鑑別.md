# Delirium Depression Dementia 鑑別

## One-Sentence Definition

Delirium 是急性、波動、以 attention / awareness disturbance 為核心的 medical syndrome；depression 可模擬 cognitive decline；dementia 則是逐漸進展並影響獨立性的 major neurocognitive disorder。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理單一來源中 delirium、depression、MCI / minor neurocognitive disorder 與 dementia / major neurocognitive disorder 的鑑別框架。
- 本頁不是 DSM 診斷手冊，也不是完整 geriatric psychiatry guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Aging_Dementia_and_Disorders_of_Cognition]]。

## Why It Matters

- Delirium 是需要即時找原因的 medical signal；若誤判為 dementia，可能延誤 infection、metabolic problem、urinary retention、constipation、pain 或 medication toxicity 的處理。
- Depression 可讓年長者看起來像 dementia；若只看 cognitive screening，可能錯過可治療的 mood disorder。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先確認 onset、time course、fluctuation、attention、sleep-wake cycle、awareness、functional baseline 與 medication changes。
- 必須詢問 family / caregiver 對 baseline cognition、ADL / IADL、behavior 與近期變化的觀察。
- Screening tool 只能篩檢，不能單獨下診斷。

## Mechanism

```text
Medical / medication / sensory / pain / retention trigger
-> arousal and attention network disruption
-> acute fluctuating awareness and cognition
-> delirium pattern requiring medical evaluation
```

```text
Depressive syndrome in older adult
-> fatigue, slowed behavior, poor concentration, sleep / appetite change, irritability, pain complaints
-> dementia-like functional presentation
-> requires depression screening and clinical evaluation
```

## Observable Patterns

- Delirium：sudden or rapid onset、attention disturbance、altered awareness、cognitive disturbance、sleep-wake change、emotional change、fluctuating severity。
- Dementia：progressive cognitive decline with increasing IADL / ADL dependence。
- Depression in older adults：pain、weakness、headache、agitation、fatigue、appetite / weight change、constipation、insomnia / hypersomnia、irritability。
- Major vs minor neurocognitive disorder：主要差異是認知下降是否明顯干擾日常獨立性。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Rehabilitation participation 突然下降時，應先找 delirium trigger，而不是只記錄「不配合」。
- Dementia diagnosis 應整合 cognitive domain、functional independence、time course 與 reversible contributors。
- Positive cognitive screen should lead to formal assessment, not premature dementia labeling.

## Fact

- Delirium requires early medical assessment and treatment in the source.
- Geriatric Depression Scale cutoffs in the source are more than 11 on the 30-item version and more than 5 on the 15-item version for possible depression.
- MMSE cutoff 24 is reported in the source with sensitivity 87.6% and specificity 81.6%, but score is affected by education and may be low in depression / delirium.
- The source lists MIS, GPCOG, and Mini-Cog as brief primary-care screening tools cited by an Alzheimer’s Association workgroup.
- AAN MCI guidance cited in the source states that memory / cognition concern from patient or close contact should be assessed and not assumed to be normal aging.

## Inference

- A low cognitive score during acute illness is not sufficient to diagnose dementia.
- A person with preserved independence but measurable cognitive decline may fit minor neurocognitive disorder / MCI rather than dementia.

## Assumption

- Screening is performed in a language, sensory, and cultural context where the tool is reasonably interpretable.

## Uncertainty

- No single screening tool is optimal across all dementia severities, education levels, languages, and care settings in this source.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「老人住院後變混亂就是 dementia」不成立；急性波動先想 delirium。
- 「MMSE 低於 cut-off 就能直接診斷 dementia」不成立。
- 「病人說記性差一定只是正常老化」不成立；來源引用 AAN guidance 反對這種假設。
