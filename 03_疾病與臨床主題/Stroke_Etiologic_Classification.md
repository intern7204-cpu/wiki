# Stroke Etiologic Classification

## One-Sentence Definition

Stroke etiologic classification 是把 stroke 從 broad pathophysiology 分成 ischemia versus hemorrhage，再把 ischemic stroke 依 large artery atherosclerosis / thrombosis、cardioaortic or artery-to-artery embolism、small vessel disease、systemic hypoperfusion、other determined causes 或 undetermined causes 進行機制歸類。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 etiologic / pathophysiologic classification。它不處理 first-hours IVT / EVT、ICH acute treatment、SAH aneurysm repair、完整 secondary prevention drug choice 或 rehab dose。

和 [[Stroke_Subtype_Clinical_Diagnosis]] 的差異是：bedside subtype diagnosis 用 symptom course、risk factors、associated symptoms 與 exam 形成 presumptive probability；etiologic classification 需要 brain imaging、intracranial / extracranial vascular imaging、ECG / rhythm monitoring、echo and selected labs 來支持或排除 competing mechanisms。從疑似 stroke 走到這一層的流程見 [[Stroke_Evaluation_病因確認與Directed_Diagnostic_Testing]]。

## Why It Matters

Secondary prevention 不是「中風後都給同一組藥」。Noncardioembolic atherosclerotic stroke、cardioembolic stroke、lacunar stroke、dissection、PFO-related stroke、APS-related stroke、ICH and SAH 的 recurrence mechanism 和 treatment harm 完全不同。若病因分類錯誤，常見後果是漏掉 anticoagulation、錯用 long-term DAPT、把 PFO / AF / carotid plaque 誤當必然病因，或把 cryptogenic stroke 當成沒有病因。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult stroke or TIA context.
- 已完成 acute safety triage，包含 hemorrhage exclusion / confirmation 與 reperfusion eligibility 判斷。
- 至少有 brain imaging、intracranial / extracranial vascular imaging、12-lead ECG、rhythm monitoring、必要時 echocardiography and selective laboratory testing。

## Mechanism

```text
acute neurologic injury
-> broad process: ischemia vs hemorrhage
-> ischemia mechanism: local arterial disease vs embolism vs hypoperfusion vs blood disorder / other cause
-> diagnostic evidence: imaging + vascular + rhythm + echo + selected labs
-> etiologic label with confidence
-> mechanism-specific secondary prevention and prognosis framing
```

## Observable Patterns

### Broad Categories

- Brain ischemia：thrombosis、embolism 或 systemic hypoperfusion 造成 oxygen / nutrient delivery 不足。
- Brain hemorrhage：ICH 或 SAH 使血液進入 brain parenchyma 或 subarachnoid space。
- Cerebral venous thrombosis 較難放進單純 arterial ischemia / hemorrhage 二分法，因為 venous occlusion 可同時造成 edema、ischemia 和 hemorrhage。

### Ischemic Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Core idea | Practical implication |
|---|---|---|
| Large artery atherosclerosis / thrombosis | extracranial or intracranial large artery lesion causes low flow, artery-to-artery embolism, or both | 要找 lesion location、severity、plaque / thrombus features and intervention relevance |
| Small vessel disease / [[Lacunar_Infarcts_診斷與病因邊界]] | penetrating artery pathology, often lipohyalinosis or branch atheromatous disease | 不可只用 pure motor syndrome 或 small deep lesion 診斷；仍需排除 parent artery disease、embolic pattern and other etiologies |
| Cardioaortic embolism | embolus from heart or aorta reaches brain artery | definite vs possible source 必須分清；possible source 可能只是 incidental |
| Artery-to-artery embolism | local arterial lesion sheds embolic material downstream | vascular imaging is central |
| Systemic hypoperfusion | global circulatory failure injures vulnerable regions, especially watershed zones | 常合併 hypotension or circulatory compromise，症狀較 diffuse / bilateral |
| Blood disorders / hypercoagulability | uncommon primary arterial cause; can promote thrombosis when endothelial lesion exists | selected testing only, especially young / cryptogenic / clotting history |
| Other determined causes | [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_臨床辨識]]、vasculitis、moyamoya、genetic / cancer / procedure-related causes | 需要 source-specific workup；dissection 還需 [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_影像確認與Pitfalls]] |

Pathophysiology source 補上 classification 的生理底層：ischemic strokes arise from reduced or blocked blood flow due to decreased systemic perfusion, severe stenosis or vessel occlusion；main causes include thrombosis, embolization and lacunar infarction from small vessel disease. Systemic hypotension 特別容易傷害 borderzone / watershed regions，且可能因 preexisting vascular lesions 而呈現不對稱。

### Classification Systems

- TOAST：large artery atherosclerosis、cardioembolism、small vessel occlusion、other determined etiology、undetermined etiology。
- Undetermined in TOAST includes three different states: more than one plausible cause, negative evaluation, and incomplete evaluation.
- SSS-TOAST / CCS：在 TOAST-like categories 內加入 evident、probable、possible confidence levels。
- CCS 是 SSS-TOAST 的 computerized algorithm version；source states inter-rater reliability is good but TOAST-versus-CCS overall agreement is only moderate at best。
- ISPS25：保留 cardioembolism、large artery atherosclerosis、small vessel disease、other determined causes、undetermined cause，但更強調 expanded minimum diagnostic evaluation。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Clinical documentation：不要只寫 "ischemic stroke"；至少標示 working mechanism、supporting evidence、competing mechanisms and remaining uncertainty。
- Rehab / ward handoff：antithrombotic、BP、mobilization safety and recurrence surveillance 都依機制不同而改變。
- Research：必須註明 TOAST、CCS 或 ISPS25，不能把不同系統產生的 label 視為等價。

## Fact

- The source classifies stroke into brain ischemia and brain hemorrhage.
- The source classifies brain ischemia into thrombosis, embolism, and systemic hypoperfusion.
- The source classifies brain hemorrhage into ICH and SAH.
- The source states approximately 80 percent of strokes are ischemic brain infarction and 20 percent are brain hemorrhage in its definitions section.
- Thrombotic strokes can be divided into large and small vessel disease.
- Large extracranial and intracranial arterial lesions can cause reduced distal blood flow and intra-arterial emboli.
- Atherosclerosis is described as the most common cause of local large artery disease supplying the brain.
- Small vessel stroke commonly involves penetrating arteries supplying basal ganglia, internal capsule, thalamus and pons.
- Embolic strokes may be cardiac, possible cardiac / aortic, arterial source, or truly unknown source after negative embolic-source testing.
- Blood and coagulation disorders are uncommon primary causes of stroke and TIA but should be considered in selected younger, cryptogenic, or clotting-history patients.
- TOAST is widely used and has good interobserver agreement in the source.
- SSS-TOAST and CCS add evident / probable / possible subcategories.
- Original TOAST and CCS agreement is described as moderate at best.
- ISPS25 minimum diagnostic evaluation includes history / physical examination, brain and vascular imaging, ECG and at least 24 hours rhythm monitoring if AF is not seen initially, selected echocardiography, and laboratory testing.
- The lacunar infarcts source states lacunar infarction diagnosis requires exclusion of other ischemic stroke etiologies.
- The lacunar infarcts source describes hypertensive microangiopathy and branch atheromatous disease as pathologically proven mechanisms, while embolism and endothelial dysfunction remain important but more contested mechanisms.
- The dissection source states subintimal dissection can cause luminal stenosis or occlusion with cerebral ischemia due to thromboembolism, hypoperfusion or both, with thromboembolism considered the major cause of ischemic symptoms.
- The dissection source states clinical features may raise suspicion but diagnosis is confirmed by neuroimaging.
- The pathophysiology source states ischemic strokes are due to reduced or complete blockage of blood flow from decreased systemic perfusion, severe stenosis or vessel occlusion.
- The pathophysiology source states TOAST categories include large artery atherosclerosis, cardioembolism, small vessel occlusion, other determined etiology and undetermined etiology.
- The pathophysiology source states systemic hypotension may cause generalized brain ischemia, especially in borderzone / watershed areas.
- The pathophysiology source states no clear cause is found in approximately 25 percent of ischemic stroke patients at hospital discharge despite extensive testing.

## Inference

- Etiologic classification is a confidence statement, not a single-test output.
- Mechanism-specific prevention should not start from a single positive finding without checking whether another mechanism better explains the event.
- "Cryptogenic" should mean adequate standard evaluation did not reveal a determined cause; it should not mean evaluation was incomplete.
- Dissection-related stroke should be classified as a source-supported mechanism, not as a default label for young stroke or neck pain.

## Assumption

- The patient is an adult with suspected or confirmed stroke / TIA.
- The classification is being used to guide diagnosis, prognosis or prevention, not to replace acute stabilization.

## Uncertainty

- Some cardiac / aortic findings have uncertain causal strength.
- Hypercoagulable markers may be venous-predominant and not directly causal for arterial ischemic stroke.
- Rare mechanisms require separate disease-specific sources.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 AF 自動等同 cardioembolic stroke；AF 是 high-risk source，但仍需看 infarct pattern and competing mechanisms。
- 不可把 PFO 或 atrial septal aneurysm 自動寫成病因；source 將這些放在 potential source。
- 不可把 lacunar syndrome 自動等同 small vessel occlusion；parent artery disease can mimic。
- 不可把 small deep infarct 自動等同 small vessel occlusion；DWI pattern、vascular imaging and cardiac evaluation can redirect the mechanism.
- 不可把 TOAST 與 CCS 的 label 直接合併分析。
- 不可把 "undetermined" 和 "not evaluated" 混用。
- 不可把 dissection clue 當成 final etiology；需要 vessel-wall / lumen imaging evidence。
