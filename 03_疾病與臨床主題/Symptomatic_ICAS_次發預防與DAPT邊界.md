# Symptomatic ICAS 次發預防與DAPT邊界

## One-Sentence Definition

Symptomatic ICAS 次發預防，是對近期 TIA / ischemic stroke 歸因於 intracranial large artery stenosis 的病人，以 stenosis severity、event risk and phase 決定 short-term DAPT duration，並同時執行 SBP、LDL-C、physical activity and lifestyle risk factor control 的 intensive medical therapy。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 symptomatic intracranial large artery atherosclerosis（ICAS）的 secondary prevention 與 DAPT 邊界。

不處理：

- ICAS epidemiology / diagnosis 的完整 workup
- acute IV thrombolysis / mechanical thrombectomy eligibility 細節
- stenting / angioplasty trial 細節；見 [[ICAS_Stenting與介入治療限制]]
- recurrent risk / hemodynamic risk stratification；見 [[ICAS_復發風險與血流動力邊界]]

## Why It Matters

ICAS 是 post-stroke secondary prevention 中最容易被兩種錯誤夾擊的場景：一邊是把 severe stenosis 當成 stenting indication，另一邊是把所有 noncardioembolic stroke 都塞進相同的 single antiplatelet / BP <130/80 routine。此來源的重點是：severe recent symptomatic ICAS 有更長 DAPT window，但仍以 medical therapy 為主，且 BP 下修需考慮 low-flow risk。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with recent ischemic stroke or TIA。
- Index event is attributed to atherosclerotic intracranial large artery stenosis。
- Hemorrhage 已排除，且若接受 IV thrombolysis，前 24 小時不能給 antithrombotic。
- 已檢查或正在排除 AF、dissection、moyamoya、vasculitis、lacunar mimic and other competing mechanisms。

## Mechanism

```text
intracranial atherosclerotic stenosis
-> artery-to-artery embolism / local thrombosis +/- low-flow vulnerability
-> high early territory recurrence risk
-> short-term DAPT when event and stenosis severity justify it
-> long-term single antiplatelet plus vascular risk factor control
```

## Observable Patterns

| ICAS scenario | Antiplatelet approach in source | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recent stroke / TIA within 30 days + 70-99% symptomatic ICAS | aspirin + clopidogrel DAPT up to 90 days, then aspirin monotherapy | UpToDate Grade 2C suggestion; not indefinite DAPT |
| 50-69% stenosis + low-risk TIA（ABCD2 <4） | aspirin alone | DAPT not automatic |
| 50-69% stenosis + moderate-to-major ischemic stroke（NIHSS >5） | aspirin alone | bleeding / infarct burden matters |
| 50-69% stenosis + high-risk TIA（ABCD2 >=4）or minor stroke（NIHSS <=5） | aspirin + clopidogrel DAPT for 21 days | follows high-risk TIA / minor stroke logic |
| Beyond 21 or 90 days | aspirin monotherapy; clopidogrel or aspirin-ER dipyridamole are alternatives | alternatives not specifically studied in ICAS |
| Routine oral anticoagulation for ICAS | no routine role | WASID found warfarin harmful compared with aspirin |

## Clinical / Research Implication

臨床上要把「DAPT 是否需要」拆成三個問題：

- stenosis 是 50-69% 還是 70-99%？
- index event 是 low-risk TIA、high-risk TIA、minor stroke 還是 moderate / major stroke？
- 現在是在 21-day / 90-day window 內，還是已進入 long-term phase？

研究上，ICAS 是 DAPT duration、BP lower target、hemodynamic selection and rescue intervention 仍未完全穩定的族群；不能把一般 noncardioembolic stroke trial 結論直接無條件外推。

## Fact

- Source 為 UpToDate topic review；literature review current through 2026-04，topic last updated 2026-01-14。
- Acute stroke / TIA due to ICAS 的 initial treatment 與其他 acute ischemic stroke / TIA 類似，重點仍是快速判斷 IV thrombolysis and mechanical thrombectomy eligibility。
- Recent stroke / TIA attributed to 70-99% atherosclerotic intracranial stenosis：source suggests aspirin plus clopidogrel DAPT up to 90 days rather than aspirin monotherapy。
- 50-69% stenosis with low-risk TIA or moderate-to-major stroke：source starts aspirin alone。
- 50-69% stenosis with high-risk TIA or minor stroke：source uses aspirin plus clopidogrel DAPT for 21 days。
- Beyond DAPT duration，source recommends aspirin monotherapy for long-term prevention。
- Clopidogrel monotherapy or aspirin-extended-release dipyridamole are reasonable alternatives to aspirin but have not been specifically studied in ICAS。
- WASID trial found aspirin treatment had lower death rate than warfarin, with similar composite ischemic stroke / brain hemorrhage / vascular death。
- Source states oral anticoagulation with warfarin is harmful for TIA / stroke due to ICAS。
- Risk factor control includes antihypertensive therapy、LDL-C lowering therapy、physical activity、smoking cessation、weight control、salt restriction and healthy diet。
- For patients with hypertension and stroke / TIA attributed to 50-99% intracranial stenosis，source suggests SBP target <140 mmHg rather than a lower target。
- Source uses high-intensity statin and LDL-C target <70 mg/dL for atherosclerotic ischemic stroke / TIA including symptomatic ICAS。
- Physical activity was independently associated with lower recurrent stroke、MI and vascular death in medically treated SAMMPRIS patients。

## Inference

- Severe symptomatic ICAS is a specific exception to the simpler "minor stroke / high-risk TIA gets 21-day DAPT" teaching rule。
- In a rehabilitation unit, ICAS handoff should include DAPT stop date, stenosis percentage, qualifying event date, SBP target rationale and LDL-C plan。
- If neurologic symptoms fluctuate with exertion, position or BP lowering, BP intensification should be treated as a perfusion-risk decision rather than routine chronic BP cleanup。

## Assumption

- ICAS attribution is adequately supported by vascular imaging and event-territory correlation。
- The patient has no separate anticoagulation indication, such as AF, mechanical valve or LV thrombus。
- Antiplatelet choice and dose are adjusted for bleeding risk, allergy, procedures and local specialist judgment。

## Uncertainty

- The optimal lower SBP boundary in symptomatic ICAS is not well defined。
- The source does not settle CYP2C19 genotype-guided antiplatelet selection。
- Clopidogrel alternatives have less ICAS-specific evidence than aspirin-based strategies。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Misreading：70-99% ICAS means stent.  
  Correction：source makes intensive medical therapy first-line and recommends against routine stenting。

- Misreading：ICAS DAPT can continue indefinitely because recurrence risk is high。  
  Correction：source uses time-limited DAPT, then long-term single antiplatelet。

- Misreading：post-stroke BP target is always <130/80, so ICAS should be lowered aggressively。  
  Correction：source suggests SBP <140 for 50-99% symptomatic ICAS and advises caution with lower targets in selected low-flow or unstable patients。

- Misreading：warfarin is stronger than aspirin for intracranial stenosis。  
  Correction：WASID found harm signal with warfarin and no efficacy advantage。
