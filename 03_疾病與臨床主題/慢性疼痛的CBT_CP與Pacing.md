# 慢性疼痛的 CBT-CP 與 Pacing

## One-Sentence Definition

CBT-CP 是針對 chronic pain 的 structured behavioral treatment，透過 physical activity、time-based pacing、relaxation、sleep education、pleasant activity scheduling 與 cognitive coping 改善 function、mood 與 pain interference。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 Chronic Pain 來源中的 CBT-CP 與 pacing 邏輯。
- 本頁不是完整心理治療手冊，也不取代 pain psychologist 評估。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Chronic_Pain]]。

## Why It Matters

- Chronic pain 常形成 pain flare → avoidance 或 overpush-crash → deconditioning → lower tolerance → more pain interference。
- CBT-CP 把 treatment target 從「先完全不痛才活動」改成「在可承受範圍內重建 activity and control」。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先確認 red flags 與 medical instability 不存在。
- 若有 severe depression、suicidality、active substance-use crisis、severe trauma response，需 psychiatric / psychological support。

## Mechanism

```text
Pain
→ thoughts, fear, catastrophizing
→ emotional arousal and avoidance or overpush
→ deconditioning, poor sleep, low self-efficacy
→ higher pain interference
→ CBT-CP skills and time-based pacing
→ function and mood improve despite residual pain
```

## Observable Patterns

- Fear avoidance：因擔心活動傷害身體而避免活動。
- Catastrophizing：對 pain threat 的負向放大。
- Overpush-crash：疼痛較輕時過度活動，flare 後長時間休息。
- Time-based pacing：依時間 / plan 停止或進展，而不是完全被 pain intensity 支配。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Walking log、activity schedule、relaxation practice、sleep plan、pleasant activity scheduling、cognitive coping homework 都是 treatment components。
- Rehab prescription 可與 CBT-CP 合併：graded walking、strengthening、pacing rule、flare plan、sleep routine。
- Treatment success 可用 pain interference、physical function、mood、activity consistency、sleep，而不只 pain intensity。

## Fact

- The source states CBT-CP improves function, mood, and pain interference。
- CBT-CP targets the relation among thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and pain。
- Sessions may include increased physical activity, time-based pacing, relaxation, pleasant activity scheduling, sleep education, and cognitive coping。
- Time-based pacing aims to maintain enough activity without overpushing into pain flares。
- CBT-CP targets fear avoidance and catastrophizing。
- ACT and EAET are also discussed as related behavioral approaches。
- Chronic pain disrupts sleep; CBT-I is first-line for chronic insomnia in the source。

## Inference

- CBT-CP 是 function restoration treatment，不是說服病人「不要痛」。
- Pacing 太保守會變成 avoidance；pacing 太激進會變成 overpush-crash。

## Assumption

- Patient can engage in education and home practice; if cognition, language, family context, or psychiatric symptoms limit this, intervention must be adapted。

## Uncertainty

- 本來源沒有提供標準 session number、dose-response 或不同 diagnosis 的最佳 CBT-CP protocol。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「CBT 代表疼痛是想太多。」正確是 CBT-CP 處理 pain-maintaining loop。
- 誤讀：「Pacing 就是少做一點。」正確是 time-based pacing 讓活動穩定且可進展。
- 誤讀：「心理介入會取代醫療評估。」正確是 red flags and medical drivers must still be evaluated。
