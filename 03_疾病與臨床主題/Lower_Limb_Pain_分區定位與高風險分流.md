# Lower Limb Pain 分區定位與高風險分流

## One-Sentence Definition

Lower limb pain 評估應先把症狀定位到 hip / groin、thigh / knee、lower leg、ankle、foot，再用 history、physical exam 與高風險結構清單判斷是否能保守處理、需要影像，或需要 urgent referral。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁是 lower limb pain 的 differential triage concept。
- 本頁不提供每個疾病的完整 treatment protocol。
- 本頁不取代單病種 guideline、Ottawa ankle / foot rules、pediatric hip protocol、stress fracture guideline 或 return-to-sport protocol。
- 本頁重點是「先分區、再篩高風險、最後才命名」。

## Why It Matters

- lower limb pain 的 referred pain 與 mimic 很多；groin、lateral hip、low back、thigh、knee、ankle、foot 之間常互相偽裝。
- 大多數 lower limb sports / MSK problems 可保守處理，但某些 diagnosis 不能延誤：SCFE、high-risk stress fracture、Achilles rupture、quadriceps / patellar tendon rupture、unstable osteochondral lesion、neurovascular pathology。
- 若先貼上常見診斷，容易漏掉 pediatric hip disease、bone stress injury、tendon rupture、intraarticular locking 或 persistent post-sprain pathology。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 已取得 pain map、onset、trauma / nontrauma、training or load change、weight-bearing tolerance、night / rest pain、systemic symptom、neurovascular symptom、prior injury 與 medication / bone-health context。
- 已做 region-specific physical exam，包括 ROM、strength、palpation、gait / single-leg loading、neurovascular screen、joint stability 或 special tests。
- 若懷疑 fracture、stress injury、rupture、osteochondral lesion、pediatric hip pathology 或 unstable intraarticular injury，需設定影像或轉介問題。

## Mechanism

```text
Pain complaint
-> regional localization
-> load / trauma / referral / pediatric / systemic / neurovascular context
-> physical exam tests whether the region label fits the tissue behavior
-> high-risk screen separates routine overuse from diagnoses where delay changes prognosis
-> selective imaging or referral answers a specific clinical question
```

## Observable Patterns

### Hip / groin

- anterior hip / groin pain：保留 AVNFH、hip dislocation、labral pathology、FAI、hip OA、pediatric hip pathology。
- posterior hip / buttock：保留 spine referral、hamstring source、deep gluteal / pelvic referral。
- lateral hip：保留 GTPS、gluteal tendinopathy、abductor tendon pathology、ITB thickening、lumbar mimic。
- medial groin：保留 athletic pubalgia / sports groin。
- immature patient 的 knee pain 或 limp 不能只當 knee problem；SCFE / LCPD 需要納入 differential。

### Thigh / knee

- anterior loaded flexed-knee pain 可符合 PFP，但 swelling after dislocation、locking、traumatic pop、rapid effusion、extensor lag、palpable tendon defect 都不是單純 PFP。
- femoral bone stress injury 可像 vague muscle strain；progressive activity pain、groin / thigh pain、single-leg hop pain 或 fulcrum concern 應升級。
- quadriceps / patellar tendon rupture：突然機制、palpable defect、無法主動伸膝時要 immobilize、avoid weight bearing、refer。

### Lower leg

- diffuse posteromedial tibial pain 可符合 MTSS，但 focal tibial pain、weight-bearing worsening、rest / night pain 或 failed load modification 需往 stress injury 想。
- anterior tibial cortex middle third 是高風險位置，不能和常見 posteromedial tibial stress pattern 等量齊觀。
- persistent exertional pain with neurologic / vascular symptoms 要保留 CECS、PAES 或 entrapment neuropathy。

### Ankle / Achilles

- common lateral ankle sprain 多涉及 ATFL，許多 grade 1-2 injury 可早期 ROM、balance / proprioception、strength progression。
- 若 persistent pain、locking、stiffness、recurrent sprain、peroneal snapping、syndesmotic features 或 bony tenderness，需重新評估。
- Achilles rupture 可在仍有 active plantarflexion 時被漏掉；Thompson test 與 palpable defect 很重要。

### Foot

- forefoot pain：metatarsal stress fracture、metatarsalgia、Morton neuroma 等需要依 focal tenderness、burning / sensory symptom、footwear / load pattern 分流。
- midfoot pain：navicular、Lisfranc、cuboid、base of fifth metatarsal 不應被簡化成 generic arch strain。
- hindfoot / heel pain：plantar fasciitis 常見，但 calcaneal stress fracture、Achilles insertional pathology、retrocalcaneal bursitis、fat-pad syndrome、tarsal tunnel 需保留。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 寫 lower limb pain note 時，先寫 region map，再寫 high-risk screen，再寫最可能 diagnosis。
- 對 athlete / runner，不要只問疼痛位置；需問 load spike、hill / downhill、surface、footwear、RED-S / bone-health clue、prior stress injury。
- 對 persistent post-ankle-sprain pain，不要反覆只寫 sprain；需重查 osteochondral lesion、peroneal pathology、syndesmosis、fracture 或 chronic instability。
- 對 pediatric limp、hip / groin pain 或 unexplained knee pain，要把 hip disease 放在前面。

## Fact

- Caldwell, Hamner, and Hupe state that the chapter begins with history and clinical examination and focuses on common lower-limb disorders in musculoskeletal, sports medicine, and general physiatry practice.
- Hip pain may refer to groin, buttock, lateral hip / greater trochanteric region, low back, and thigh.
- The chapter divides hip pain into anterior, lateral, posterior, and medial regions.
- SCFE is described as the most common adolescent hip pathology and an orthopedic emergency requiring prompt referral for internal fixation.
- LCPD is described as idiopathic osteonecrosis of the femoral epiphysis in preadolescent children and may lead to premature OA.
- GTPS is described as a collection of peritrochanteric pathologies; bursal inflammation plays a limited role.
- PFP is described as common in active populations and associated with pathologic patellar tracking related to quadriceps and hip abductor dysfunction.
- Femoral neck tension-side stress injury is listed as high risk.
- Middle anterior tibial cortex stress fracture with a “dreaded black line” is listed as high risk.
- Navicular bone, fifth metatarsal Jones fracture, and talar dome fracture are listed as high-risk stress fracture sites.
- Lateral ankle sprains are described as most common and often initially involving the ATFL.
- Achilles rupture can be missed if active plantarflexion is used alone because other plantarflexors may still produce motion.

## Inference

- A common diagnosis should be considered provisional until the time course, functional behavior, and exam match it.
- High-risk stress fracture locations should lower the threshold for protection, imaging, and referral compared with lower-risk overuse pain.
- Region-based triage is useful because it preserves a broad differential without forcing the clinician to perform every possible test.

## Assumption

- The patient is medically stable enough for outpatient-style MSK evaluation unless red flags indicate otherwise.
- The clinician can repeat exam or escalate if symptoms do not follow the expected course.
- Conservative management is reasonable only after high-risk diagnoses have been sufficiently excluded.

## Uncertainty

- The exact treatment dose, progression, and return-to-sport criteria for each condition require condition-specific sources.
- Some procedures and regenerative treatments mentioned in the source require updated evidence before routine use.
- Textbook organization supports differential reasoning, but local imaging / referral thresholds may vary by setting and access.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：「分區定位」等於痛哪裡就是哪個 tissue。錯；分區只是建立 differential 的入口。
- 誤讀 2：lateral hip pain 就是 bursitis。錯；GTPS 常與 gluteal tendinopathy、ITB thickening、abductor tendon pathology 有關。
- 誤讀 3：ankle sprain 常見，所以慢性不穩或持續痛不用重查。錯；persistent symptoms 需重新分流。
- 誤讀 4：shin splints、plantar fasciitis、PFP 這些常見名詞可以先寫再說。錯；若有 focal bone pain、rupture sign、locking、neurovascular symptom 或 pediatric hip clue，要先處理高風險問題。
