# Ischemic Core 與 Penumbra

## One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic core 是已死亡或注定死亡的 central infarct tissue；penumbra 是周邊仍有 partial oxygen / glucose supply、若及時恢復 blood flow 可能存活的 tissue。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 focal ischemic stroke 的 spatial and temporal injury model。它不是 CT perfusion / MR perfusion mismatch 操作頁，也不直接決定 IVT / EVT eligibility。

急性治療時間窗見 [[中風急性期處置與時間窗]]；初始評估與穩定化見 [[Acute_Stroke_Initial_Assessment與Stabilization]]。

## Why It Matters

"Time is brain" 的生理本質不是所有腦組織以同一速度死亡，而是 penumbra 隨著 duration of ischemia、collateral adequacy and ATP depletion 逐步變成 irreversible infarct。這解釋為何早期 reperfusion 有最大機會保留功能，也解釋為何 late-window treatment 需要 imaging selection。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Focal ischemic stroke caused by arterial flow reduction or occlusion。
- 存在中心到周邊的 perfusion gradient。
- Collateral flow 或 diffusion 仍能部分供應周邊 tissue。

## Mechanism

```text
arterial occlusion or severe flow reduction
-> central territory receives least oxygen / glucose
-> severe ATP depletion
-> necrosis-dominant irreversible core
-> peripheral tissue receives partial collateral / diffusion support
-> penumbra remains viable for a limited time
-> delayed reperfusion allows ATP depletion and conversion to infarct
```

## Observable Patterns

- The brain has high metabolic demand and little or no energy stores.
- Focal ischemia typically involves a vessel and its downstream branches.
- Central tissue near the affected vessel is most severely affected.
- Peripheral tissue can survive temporarily if partial oxygen and glucose delivery persists.
- Core ATP may fall much lower than penumbral ATP in focal ischemia models.
- Penumbra is a continuum, not a fixed ring with sharp clinical borders.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Reperfusion therapy is biologically aimed at saving penumbra, not reviving already necrotic core.
- Imaging mismatch concepts attempt to estimate core / penumbra physiology, but this source does not set mismatch thresholds.
- Prognosis after delayed presentation depends heavily on collateral status and remaining viable tissue.

## Fact

- The source states central core tissue becomes irreversibly damaged and dies by necrosis if ischemia lasts long enough.
- The source defines the infarct as central tissue destined to die or already dead.
- The source defines penumbra as potentially salvageable tissue.
- The source states some peripheral cells may receive oxygen and glucose from collateral vessels and recover if blood flow is restored in a timely manner.
- The source states ATP levels may drop to about 25 percent of normal in focal ischemic core models and about 50-70 percent in penumbra.
- The source states longer ischemia increases the proportion of necrosis and decreases apoptosis.

## Inference

- Penumbra preservation is the physiologic reason not to delay indicated reperfusion.
- A patient with better collateral flow may retain viable penumbra longer than another patient with the same clock time.

## Assumption

- This page assumes focal arterial ischemia rather than global hypoxic injury.
- Imaging confirmation of core / penumbra requires separate acute stroke imaging protocols.

## Uncertainty

- Exact salvageability varies by collateral flow, metabolic demand, temperature, glucose, BP and reperfusion timing.
- Symptoms alone cannot quantify core or penumbra.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 penumbra 寫成一定可救回。
- 不可把 large deficit 自動等同 large core，或 small deficit 自動等同 small core。
- 不可把本頁當作 thrombectomy late-window imaging selection rule。
