# Myopathy 呼吸心臟吞嚥風險

## One-Sentence Definition

Myopathy 的臨床風險不能只看 limb weakness；respiratory muscle weakness、cardiomyopathy / conduction disease 與 oropharyngeal weakness 會直接決定安全性、死亡率與復健可行性。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 myopathic disorders 的 respiratory、cardiac、bulbar / dysphagia risk frame。
- 本頁不是 DMD、Pompe、IBM、OPMD、myotonic dystrophy 等疾病的完整監測 guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Myopathic_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- 一個病人可以 limb function 看似穩定，但因 nocturnal hypoventilation、weak cough、cardiomyopathy、arrhythmia 或 aspiration risk 而變成高風險。
- Enteral feeding、NIV、cough-assist、cardiology referral 若太晚處理，會被 respiratory decline 反過來限制選項。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 追蹤時需主動問 dyspnea on exertion、orthopnea、sleep quality、morning headache、daytime sleepiness、fatigue、impaired concentration、nighttime arousals、cough strength、respiratory infection、choking、sialorrhea、palpitations、chest pain、edema。
- 高風險 subtype 應早期連結 pulmonology、respiratory therapy、cardiology、SLP、nutrition。

## Mechanism

```text
Myopathic involvement of diaphragm / respiratory muscles
→ restrictive lung disease + impaired cough
→ nocturnal hypoventilation, secretion retention, atelectasis, pneumonia
→ reduced therapy tolerance and survival risk
```

```text
Myopathic involvement of cardiac or bulbar muscles
→ cardiomyopathy / conduction abnormality or dysphagia
→ arrhythmia, heart failure, aspiration, nutrition compromise
→ rehabilitation interruption and mortality risk
```

## Observable Patterns

- Respiratory muscle weakness：dyspnea on exertion、orthopnea、nocturnal hypoventilation、weak cough、frequent respiratory infection。
- Bulbar dysfunction：coughing / choking on food、aspiration events、sialorrhea、diet texture issues。
- Cardiac involvement：chest pain、palpitations、edema、cardiomyopathy、conduction abnormality or rhythm disturbance depending on subtype。
- DMD：loss of ambulation is associated with decreasing FVC and pulmonary dysfunction。
- IBM：dysphagia-related aspiration pneumonia is described as a leading cause of mortality。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Routine PFTs should be obtained in those at risk, with pulmonology referral early in the course。
- Source lists FVC、maximum inspiratory pressure、maximum expiratory pressure as DMD pulmonary monitoring measures。
- Symptoms of nocturnal hypoventilation should prompt polysomnography evaluation in the DMD discussion。
- Mechanical insufflation-exsufflation can support secretion clearance when cough is weak。
- NIV at night is described as improving survival and nocturnal hypoventilation in DMD。
- Swallowing symptoms should trigger SLP evaluation and VFSS when indicated。
- Severe respiratory impairment can preclude enteral tube placement; feeding discussions should occur early enough。

## Fact

- Respiratory muscle weakness can lead to restrictive lung disease and is one of the most serious manifestations in myopathic disorders。
- Late-onset Pompe disease can present with significant diaphragm weakness。
- Cardiomyopathy and conduction abnormalities are frequently found in myotonic muscular dystrophy, DMD, EDMD, and select LGMD。
- Dysphagia can be seen in IBM and OPMD；DMD dysphagia typically appears late。
- DMD patients may develop cardiomyopathy due to dystrophin dysfunction in cardiac muscle fibers。
- Female DMD carriers are at increased cardiomyopathy risk and should receive regular screening according to the source。
- Sialorrhea may be managed with anticholinergic medication such as glycopyrrolate, with dry mouth as a tradeoff；botulinum toxin injection into salivary glands can reduce saliva production case by case。

## Inference

- Respiratory and swallowing assessment should be paired because dysphagia and weak cough together increase aspiration consequence。
- Myopathy follow-up without cardiac and pulmonary risk review is incomplete in diagnoses known for multisystem involvement。

## Assumption

- The responsible clinician knows which myopathy subtype carries cardiac, respiratory, or bulbar risk; if not, neuromuscular referral is needed。

## Uncertainty

- Surveillance intervals, device thresholds, and disease-specific screening rules require current guideline-level sources, not this single chapter alone。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「還能走路就沒有呼吸風險。」正確是 diaphragm weakness 或 nocturnal hypoventilation 可早於明顯 limb-stage impression。
- 誤讀：「吞嚥問題只要改食物就好。」正確是需評估 aspiration、nutrition、respiratory reserve 與 enteral feeding timing。
- 誤讀：「cardiac screening 只在有症狀時做。」正確是多種 muscular dystrophy 可有 cardiomyopathy / conduction disease，需要 subtype-based surveillance。
