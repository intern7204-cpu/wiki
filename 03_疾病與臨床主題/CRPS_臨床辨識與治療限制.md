# CRPS 臨床辨識與治療限制

## One-Sentence Definition

CRPS 是 trauma 後出現的 regional、disproportionate、continuous pain syndrome，合併 sensory、autonomic、sudomotor、motor / trophic changes；診斷以臨床型態與排除 mimics 為主。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁依 Bradley & Daroff 來源整理 CRPS 的臨床辨識與治療限制。
- 本頁另以 UpToDate CRPS pathogenesis / diagnosis topic review 補強 Budapest criteria、diagnostic testing boundary 與 mimic exclusion。
- 本頁再以 UpToDate CRPS treatment / prognosis / prevention topic review 補強 treatment escalation、prognosis、recurrence and prevention boundary。
- 本頁是 hub page；具體 treatment flow 見 [[CRPS_多模式治療與升階流程]]，prognosis / recurrence / prevention 見 [[CRPS_預後復發與預防]]。

## Why It Matters

- CRPS 若被當作一般 sprain pain、單一 nerve injury 或純心理問題，會延誤早期 function-preserving rehabilitation。
- 反過來，未排除 infection、DVT、fracture、vascular disease 等 mimic 就貼上 CRPS，也會造成危險漏診。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 常有 trauma 後 pain，但 pain pattern 應 disproportionate、regional、非單一 nerve territory。
- 必須先排除 infection、DVT、fracture、vascular disorder、neuropathy 等替代診斷。

## Mechanism

```text
Trauma or nerve lesion
→ neurogenic inflammation and CGRP / substance P activity
→ immune and sympathetic coupling
→ peripheral and central sensitization
→ cortical reorganization and motor/autonomic dysfunction
→ regional pain, edema, color/temperature/sweat/motor/trophic changes
```

## Observable Patterns

- Regional continuous pain, often disproportionate to injury.
- Edema, temperature asymmetry, color change, sweating change.
- Motor dysfunction, stiffness, weakness, tremor, dystonia, decreased use.
- Pain distribution does not respect one peripheral nerve.
- Psychological distress may coexist but does not define the syndrome.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Early PT / OT aims to preserve movement, function, desensitization, and use of the limb.
- Psychological evaluation is useful when symptoms persist, not because CRPS is psychogenic, but because chronic pain, fear, and disability require behavioral support.
- Pain control 的主要目的，是讓病人能參與 active rehabilitation，而不是用 procedure 或 medication 取代 limb use。
- Sympathetic block, epidural / intrathecal treatments, ketamine, bisphosphonate, SCS 等治療 evidence 多有限或不一致，需個案化並注意 safety / expertise。

## Fact

- CRPS type I occurs without identifiable nerve lesion；CRPS type II occurs with definable nerve lesion。
- 來源指出 CRPS pain is regional and not in a nerve distribution。
- EMG / NCS are not sensitive for CRPS and can be painful。
- Diagnosis is clinical after excluding alternatives such as infection and DVT。
- Budapest clinical criteria require continuing disproportionate pain, at least one symptom in three of four domains, at least one sign at evaluation in two of four domains, and no better alternative diagnosis。
- The four Budapest domains are sensory、vasomotor、sudomotor/edema、motor/trophic。
- No gold-standard confirmatory test exists for CRPS。
- Bone scintigraphy, radiography and autonomic testing are adjunctive in atypical cases; negative bone scan does not rule out CRPS。
- MRI may help exclude mimics but does not confirm CRPS；CT is not suggested as a diagnostic test。
- Positive sympathetic block response is not diagnostic of CRPS。
- Treatment goals include pain relief, function, and psychological care。
- 來源提到 bisphosphonates 在 review 中較有 clear benefit；ketamine promising but requires more evidence。
- 來源對 SCS 長期效益描述保留，指出 5-year follow-up 未顯示比 PT 額外有利。
- UpToDate treatment source suggests multidisciplinary management for adult CRPS。
- Patient education、PT/OT、psychosocial assessment and symptomatic pain management are appropriate initial components for all patients with CRPS in that source。
- PT/OT are first-line treatments, though evidence quality for specific modalities is limited。
- Early medication options in the source include NSAID, neuropathic pain adjuvant, selected bisphosphonate course, and topical lidocaine / capsaicin; the recommendation grade is Grade 2C。
- Pain management specialist referral is appropriate for unsatisfactory initial response, progressive symptoms/signs, severe CRPS, or chronic CRPS。
- Prognosis is variable；symptomatic improvement within six months is common, but prolonged disability and recurrence occur。
- Vitamin C prophylaxis evidence after distal radius fracture is inconsistent and not compelling enough for routine universal use in this source。

## Inference

- CRPS rehabilitation 不應等待完全止痛才開始；過度保護可能加劇 disuse 與 cortical / motor changes。
- 單一 procedure 很難取代 multidisciplinary care。

## Assumption

- 本頁假設病人已完成危險 mimic screen；若有 fever、progressive swelling、vascular compromise、new neurologic deficit，應先轉醫療診斷。

## Uncertainty

- UpToDate treatment source 仍不是 procedure-specific protocol；ketamine、sympathetic block、SCS、DRG stimulation、opioid 與 perioperative prevention 的精確 patient selection 仍需各自 procedure-specific or guideline-level source。
- Vitamin C prevention evidence is low quality and inconsistent。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「CRPS type II 就是一般 peripheral neuropathy。」正確是 type II 有 definable nerve lesion，但 syndrome 仍是 regional CRPS pattern。
- 誤讀：「EMG 正常排除 CRPS。」正確是 EMG / NCS 對 CRPS 不敏感。
- 誤讀：「心理介入表示疼痛是心理造成。」正確是心理介入處理 chronic pain disability、fear 與 coping。
