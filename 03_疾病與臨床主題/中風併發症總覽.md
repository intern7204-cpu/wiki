# 中風併發症總覽

## One-Sentence Definition

中風併發症總覽是把 acute stroke 與 early rehabilitation period 可能出現的 medical、pulmonary、cardiac、GI、urinary、fall、psychiatric 與 neurologic complications 放進同一個 surveillance framework，避免把中風復健誤縮成單純 motor training。

## Definition and Boundary

### What It Is

- 一個 poststroke complication surveillance framework。
- 目標是辨識哪些併發症會改變：
  - mortality / morbidity
  - 30-day readmission
  - rehabilitation readiness
  - therapy tolerance
  - swallowing / nutrition route
  - cardiopulmonary safety
  - fall and fracture risk

### What It Is Not

- 不是各併發症的完整治療 guideline。
- 不是所有病人都要 routine 使用 antibiotics、stress ulcer prophylaxis、catheter、tube feeding 或 imaging。
- 不是用單一 incidence number 預測個別病人風險。

### Commonly Confused Concepts

- complication surveillance ≠ routine prophylaxis for everything。
- dysphagia screening ≠ definitive swallowing diagnosis。
- troponin elevation after stroke ≠ 一定是 acute coronary thrombotic occlusion。
- early mobilization ≠ 不顧 neurologic / cardiopulmonary stability 的越早越好。

## Why It Matters

中風後併發症會直接決定病人能不能安全進入復健、能承受多少訓練量，以及是否會因 infection、cardiac instability、aspiration、fall 或 neurologic deterioration 而中斷療程。

## Preconditions or Conditions

此框架主要適用於：

- adult acute stroke。
- ischemic stroke、intracerebral hemorrhage、subarachnoid hemorrhage 的總覽層級。
- acute hospital、stroke unit、early inpatient rehabilitation transition。

外推限制：

- pediatric stroke / neonatal stroke 不能直接套用。
- SAH、ICH、ischemic stroke 的 neurologic complication 與 cardiopulmonary risk profile 不同，實務上需分 subtype。

## Mechanism

```text
Acute stroke
→ immobility + dysphagia + impaired consciousness + autonomic dysregulation + bladder dysfunction + cognitive-perceptual deficits
→ pneumonia / UTI / VTE / MI-arrhythmia / neurogenic cardiopulmonary complication / GI bleeding / urinary incontinence / falls / depression / neurologic deterioration
→ therapy interruption + lower exercise tolerance + higher morbidity/mortality + worse functional outcome
```

## Observable Patterns

### Medical complications

- Potentially serious complications include pneumonia、UTI、GI bleeding、MI、DVT、PE。
- Any in-hospital medical complication 與 30-day readmission risk 上升相關。
- Serious medical complication 在 RANTTAS placebo-group prospective data 中約 24%。

### Dysphagia and aspiration pneumonia

- Dysphagia increases pneumonia risk；aspiration increases it even more。
- Poststroke dysphagia incidence 會因偵測方法而變：screening method 低於 trained clinician，VFSS 最高。
- Awake and alert acute stroke patients 應在 oral medication / food 前做 swallowing screen。

### Infection

- Pneumonia and UTI 是兩個常見 infectious complications。
- Catheter duration 是 UTI risk factor；indwelling urinary catheter 應盡量避免。
- General prophylactic antibiotics may reduce infection but do not improve mortality or functional outcome；intubated acute brain injury population 的 single-dose ceftriaxone 是較特定情境。

### Cardiac / pulmonary complications

- Acute stroke patients need ECG、troponin、至少 24 小時 cardiac monitoring。
- Troponin elevation may reflect ACS, but also stroke-induced autonomic dysfunction or nonischemic myocardial injury。
- Neurogenic pulmonary edema、abnormal respiratory patterns、intubation / mechanical ventilation need 都會改變 rehab readiness。

### Sleep-related breathing disorders

- [[Poststroke_Sleep_Related_Breathing_Disorders_篩檢與診斷]] 補上 poststroke OSA、CSA and Cheyne-Stokes breathing 的 surveillance frame。
- Poststroke sleep-related breathing disorders 很常見；acute phase AHI >=5 的 pooled prevalence 可接近 67%。
- OSA 是最常見型態；CSA / Cheyne-Stokes breathing 可在 early acute phase 更明顯並影響 auto-titrating CPAP tolerance。
- 典型 OSA 症狀如 snoring、daytime sleepiness 在 stroke patient 可能缺乏；不能用症狀 absence 排除。
- First five days 可先 review overnight oximetry；nocturnal desaturation、dysphagia 或 dysphonia 應提高 formal polysomnography / HSAT 門檻。
- [[Poststroke_Sleep_Apnea_PAP治療與預後邊界]] 指出 PAP and behavioral modification 是治療主軸，但 stroke-specific outcome benefit 證據 mixed，需看 adherence、tolerance and recovery trajectory。

### Falls and fractures

- Falls and serious injury are important poststroke complications。
- Hemiparesis、cognitive impairment、neglect、anosognosia、polypharmacy raise fall risk。
- Paretic-side fracture risk is clinically important because protective response may be impaired。

### Neuropsychiatric complications

- [[Poststroke_Neuropsychiatric_Disorders_辨識與轉介]] 補上 depression 以外的 surveillance frame：adjustment disorder、apathy、anxiety、ASD / PTSD、PBA、anger/aggression 都可能降低 rehabilitation engagement。
- Functional impairment 是 poststroke psychiatric symptom 進入 clinical disorder / referral frame 的核心門檻。
- Therapy participation 下降時，不能只寫 motivation poor；需回查 depression、apathy、anxiety、delirium、pain、sleep、aphasia/cognition、PBA 與 unsafe behavior。
- Suicidal ideation、psychosis、dangerous/aggressive behavior、poor judgment with imminent risk 或 lack of response to initial treatment 應觸發 mental health referral。

### Palliative and caregiver needs

- [[Stroke_Neuropalliative_Care與Goals_of_Care]] 把 pain、dysphagia、depression、anxiety、prognostic uncertainty、caregiver distress 與 care transition 視為 stroke complication workflow 的一部分。
- Palliative care 可以和 acute treatment 並行，不等於 hospice 或 withdrawal。
- Dysphagia 若進入 long-term artificial nutrition decision，不只是 swallowing issue，也是 goals-of-care issue。
- Severe stroke 的 mechanical ventilation、predicted major dependency 或 long-term artificial nutrition 需求應觸發 serious illness conversation。

## Clinical / Research Implication

### 臨床應用

- 每日 rehab plan 應先確認：swallowing safety、infection signs、oxygenation、hemodynamics、cardiac rhythm、urinary catheter need、fall risk、mental status 與 neurologic stability。
- 若病人 therapy tolerance 突然下降，不應只解釋為 motivation poor；要回查 fever、pneumonia、UTI、cardiac event、GI bleeding、AKI、neurologic deterioration 或 depression。
- Complication prevention 的核心是 risk-specific action，而不是 blanket prophylaxis。

### 研究應用

- 比較 poststroke complication incidence 時必須標明：stroke subtype、care setting、screening method、follow-up duration。
- Dysphagia incidence、UTI incidence、fall rate 等都高度依賴偵測方法與族群。

## Fact

- Acute stroke complications are common and outcome-relevant。
- Dysphagia is a major risk factor for aspiration pneumonia。
- Formal dysphagia screening protocol was associated with lower aspiration pneumonia risk in the source summary。
- VTE prophylaxis is indicated for acute stroke patients with restricted mobility。
- ECG、troponin、continuous cardiac monitoring at least first 24 hours are part of acute stroke cardiac surveillance in the source。
- Sleep-related breathing disorders are common after stroke / TIA and may be associated with early neurologic worsening and poorer long-term cognitive / functional outcomes in the sleep-stroke source。
- Formal poststroke sleep apnea diagnosis requires polysomnography or HSAT type III / IV; overnight oximetry is a triage tool, not a complete diagnostic substitute。
- Poststroke psychiatric symptoms causing functional impairment warrant mental health evaluation in the poststroke neuropsychiatric source。
- Neuropalliative source states palliative care can begin at stroke presentation and can be offered concurrently with acute treatment。

## Inference

- Rehabilitation readiness should include complication surveillance, not only strength、balance、mobility assessment。
- Stroke rehab pages should explicitly link motor recovery to medical stability because complications can limit practice dose and safety。

## Assumption

- Adult UpToDate topic review is being used as a clinical overview source for adult stroke rehab workflow。
- Local rehab setting has access to nursing surveillance、SLP swallow screening、medical evaluation、cardiac monitoring escalation when indicated。

## Uncertainty

- Exact complication incidence in a specific hospital or rehab unit may differ from the cited data。
- Stroke-specific evidence for some fall-prevention and aspiration-prevention measures remains limited。
- Pediatric stroke and neonatal stroke require separate sources。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not read “complications are common” as justification for treating asymptomatic bacteriuria or giving prophylactic antibiotics to all stroke patients。
- Do not read “water swallow test” as a complete swallowing evaluation when clinical risk remains high。
- Do not treat troponin elevation after stroke as automatically ACS or automatically benign。
- Do not apply the same monitoring urgency to all stroke subtypes without considering ischemic stroke vs ICH vs SAH.
- Do not label reduced therapy participation as poor motivation before evaluating depression、apathy、anxiety、delirium、pain、sleep and communication/cognitive barriers.
- Do not treat palliative care referral as equivalent to hospice or withdrawal of disease-directed treatment.
- Do not exclude poststroke sleep apnea simply because the patient denies sleepiness or snoring.
- Do not treat CPAP prescription as proof of effective therapy without adherence and tolerance follow-up.
