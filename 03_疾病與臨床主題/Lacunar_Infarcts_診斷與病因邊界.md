# Lacunar Infarcts 診斷與病因邊界

## One-Sentence Definition

Lacunar infarcts 是 small noncortical infarcts caused by occlusion of a single penetrating branch，但臨床診斷必須同時看 compatible lacunar syndrome、small noncortical imaging lesion and exclusion of other ischemic stroke etiologies。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 lacunar infarct 的 definition、anatomy、etiologic mechanisms and diagnostic boundary。Classic lacunar syndromes 與 CT/MRI confirmation 的操作細節見 [[Lacunar_Stroke_Syndromes_臨床表現與影像確認]]。

它不等於完整 [[Stroke_Etiologic_Classification]]，也不取代 [[Stroke_Evaluation_病因確認與Directed_Diagnostic_Testing]]、[[中風急性期處置與時間窗]] 或 [[中風次發預防]]。

## Why It Matters

最危險的誤讀是把「small deep infarct」或「pure motor syndrome」直接寫成 small vessel disease。這會漏掉 parent artery atheroma、large artery occlusive disease、cardiac/aortic embolism，並可能錯過 reperfusion triage、vascular intervention、anticoagulation indication 或 recurrence-prevention pathway。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult suspected ischemic stroke or TIA。
- 有 acute neurologic syndrome、brain CT/MRI、neurovascular imaging and cardiac evaluation。
- 已先排除 hemorrhage、stroke mimic and immediately treatable large artery occlusion。

## Mechanism

```text
penetrating artery anatomy
-> limited collateral and sharp origin from large artery
-> hypertensive microangiopathy or branch atheroma
-> small deep noncortical infarct
-> lacunar syndrome without cortical signs
-> diagnosis still requires competing-mechanism exclusion
```

### Main Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Source-supported role | Diagnostic implication |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertensive microangiopathy | usual cause; arteriolosclerosis / lipohyalinosis / fibrinoid necrosis can narrow penetrating arteries | supports small vessel disease, especially with chronic hypertension |
| Branch atheromatous disease | microatheroma at perforator origin; pathologically proven for basilar artery | parent artery disease can look like lacunar infarct |
| Embolism | possible from cardiac, aortic or large artery sources; causality may be difficult to prove | multiple acute DWI lesions or cortical/small cortical lesions should raise embolic suspicion |
| Endothelial dysfunction / BBB disruption | proposed mechanism; unconfirmed in source | keep as emerging mechanism, not clinical certainty |

## Observable Patterns

- Typical locations: basal ganglia, thalamus, internal capsule, corona radiata and pons。
- Traditional chronic lacune size: 2-15 mm。
- Acute DWI size may be larger, often up to 20-25 mm in studies, because infarct volume can shrink over time。
- Lacunar infarcts account for approximately 15-26 percent of ischemic strokes in the source。
- Hypertension is the main risk factor/mechanism; diabetes, smoking, age, LDL cholesterol and selected genetic factors may contribute。
- Hypertension and diabetes are associated with stroke generally, but whether they are more specific to lacunar stroke than other subtypes is conflicting in the source。
- CADASIL and other monogenic cerebral small vessel diseases can produce lacunar-like events, but require separate disease-specific evaluation.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- In clinical notes, write "lacunar-appearing infarct" or "lacunar syndrome, mechanism under evaluation" until vascular/cardiac workup is complete.
- In research datasets, separate clinical syndrome, lesion topography, lesion size and final mechanism.
- In secondary prevention, lacunar stroke usually follows noncardioembolic single-antiplatelet logic unless another mechanism is found, but long-term DAPT is not supported by this source.

## Fact

- The source defines lacunar infarcts as small noncortical infarcts caused by occlusion of a single penetrating branch of a large cerebral artery.
- The source states not all small deep infarcts are lacunar, and diagnosis requires excluding other ischemic stroke etiologies.
- Most lacunes occur in basal ganglia, thalamus, internal capsule / corona radiata and pons.
- Mechanisms described are hypertensive microangiopathy, branch atheromatous disease, embolism and endothelial dysfunction with BBB disruption.
- Hypertensive microangiopathy and branch atheromatous disease are pathologically proven in the source.
- Endothelial dysfunction theory remains unconfirmed.
- Lacunar infarcts account for 15-26 percent of ischemic strokes.
- A systematic review cited by the source found lacunar index events were more likely to recur as lacunar than nonlacunar stroke.
- The source states carotid stenosis and cardiac sources are found in some radiologic lacunes, but causal relationship can be difficult to prove.
- Multiple acute DWI lesions in a patient presenting with lacunar syndrome suggest a proximal embolic source.
- Confirmed lacunar infarction is not a mechanical thrombectomy target, but acute vascular imaging is still warranted because small deep infarcts are not always lacunar.

## Inference

- The safest diagnostic posture is mechanism-probability framing rather than binary labeling.
- A single small lesion in a classic location raises lacunar probability; multiple acute lesions or parent artery stenosis should reduce confidence in pure small vessel occlusion.

## Assumption

- The source applies primarily to adult ischemic stroke / TIA.
- Local stroke workflow can obtain vascular imaging and at least basic cardiac evaluation.

## Uncertainty

- Imaging cannot directly show occlusion of most penetrating arteries.
- The true contribution of embolism in lacunar infarction remains debated.
- Whether embolic or parent-artery lacunar-appearing infarcts have different prognosis or treatment response remains unknown.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 pure motor stroke 自動寫成 lacunar infarct。
- 不可把 small deep infarct 自動寫成 small vessel disease。
- 不可因為懷疑 lacunar 就省略 acute vascular imaging。
- 不可把 hypertension / diabetes 當作單獨證明病因。
- 不可把 endothelial dysfunction 寫成已確定臨床機制。
