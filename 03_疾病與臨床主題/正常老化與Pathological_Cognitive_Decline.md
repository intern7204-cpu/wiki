# 正常老化與 Pathological Cognitive Decline

## One-Sentence Definition

正常老化可伴隨 brain structure、network organization 與 executive function 變化，但 pathological cognitive decline 指超出預期老化、造成日常獨立性或 participation 受損的認知下降。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 normal brain aging、reserve、functional cognition 與 pathological decline 的邊界。
- 本頁不處理完整 dementia subtype diagnosis，也不替代 delirium / depression workup。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Aging_Dementia_and_Disorders_of_Cognition]]。

## Why It Matters

- 把所有 cognitive complaint 歸因於「老化」會漏掉 delirium、depression、MCI、dementia、medication effect 或 metabolic / infectious trigger。
- 把所有年長者的 processing slowness 視為 dementia 也會造成過度診斷，並低估 pacing、environmental support 與 task simplification 的效果。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 判讀 normal aging 前，需確認沒有 acute fluctuation、attention disturbance、major mood syndrome、infection、metabolic abnormality、pain、constipation、urinary retention、sedating / anticholinergic medication effect 或 sensory deprivation。
- 評估應同時看 cognition、daily function、task context、pace、environment 與 caregiver observation。

## Mechanism

```text
Age-related cortical / network changes
-> slower or less efficient executive processing under high demand
-> performance worsens in fast, unfamiliar, complex tasks
-> reserve, meaningful activity, enriched environment, and pacing modify observed function
```

## Observable Patterns

- Healthy aging may preserve memory, language, and visuospatial function relatively well.
- Executive function is more vulnerable, especially when task pace is fast, unfamiliar, or complex.
- Functional cognition may improve when the person can control pace and engage in meaningful, familiar activities.
- Cognitive and neural reserve can make two people with similar age or brain changes show different real-world function.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Cognitive screening should not be interpreted without education, language, sensory status, affect, medication exposure, and functional history.
- Rehabilitation should test whether environmental cues, reduced complexity, slower pacing, and meaningful context improve performance.
- Prevention claims should distinguish observational association from randomized trial evidence.

## Fact

- Healthy aging is associated with decreased cortical thickness / volume and network reorganization in the source.
- The source states these changes do not necessarily cause overt cognitive impairment with activity limitation or participation restriction.
- Neuroplastic changes continue through old age.
- Enriched environments may improve brain volume, connectivity, and cognition.
- Physical activity / exercise and cognitively stimulating activities may enhance reserve in the source’s framework.

## Inference

- Functional assessment is necessary because structural aging markers alone cannot decide whether a person has clinically meaningful cognitive disability.
- A pace-controlled, familiar task may reveal preserved ability that is hidden during rushed testing.

## Assumption

- The observed task difficulty is not primarily caused by visual impairment, hearing impairment, aphasia, pain, medication sedation, delirium, or depression.

## Uncertainty

- Reserve explains variability but does not provide an individual-level deterministic prediction.
- The exact dose and combination of physical, cognitive, and social activity needed to prevent dementia remain uncertain in this single source.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「年紀大所以忘東忘西一定正常」不成立。
- 「任何認知篩檢低分都等於 dementia」不成立。
- 「MRI 有萎縮就能直接推論 functional incapacity」不成立；功能表現需實測。
