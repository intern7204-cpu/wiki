# Ischemic Stroke Cerebral Edema 與 Hemorrhagic Conversion

## One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic cerebral edema and hemorrhagic conversion arise when ischemia disrupts ATP-dependent ion transport, endothelial barrier function and extracellular matrix integrity, producing cytotoxic / ionic / vasogenic edema and, in severe vascular failure, blood-product extravasation into infarcted tissue.

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 ischemic stroke 後 edema、BBB failure and hemorrhagic conversion 的病理生理。它不處理 malignant MCA infarction surgery criteria、post-thrombolysis hemorrhage protocol 或 anticoagulation restart timing。

相關急性處置見 [[中風急性期處置與時間窗]]；ICH 急性處理見 [[Spontaneous_ICH_急性抗擴大與Neurocritical_Stabilization]]。

## Why It Matters

Large ischemic stroke 的臨床惡化常不只來自 initial occlusion，還可能來自 edema、ICP rise、mass effect、herniation or hemorrhagic conversion。這些 secondary injury mechanisms 需要獨立監測，不能因為已完成 reperfusion triage 就停止警覺。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Ischemic brain tissue with ATP depletion and cell swelling.
- Neurovascular unit injury involving endothelial cells, basal lamina and BBB.
- Infarct burden large enough or location vulnerable enough to produce clinically significant edema / mass effect.

## Mechanism

```text
ischemic ATP depletion
-> sodium and calcium transport failure
-> intracellular water accumulation
-> cytotoxic edema
-> endothelial ion-water transport across BBB
-> ionic / interstitial edema
-> BBB endothelial breakdown
-> protein and macromolecule leakage
-> vasogenic edema
-> increased ICP, lower CBF, mass effect and possible herniation
```

```text
ischemia and infarction
-> protease and MMP activity
-> basal lamina collagen / laminin degradation
-> vascular integrity loss and BBB disruption
-> possible blood constituent extravasation
-> hemorrhagic conversion
```

## Observable Patterns

- Cytotoxic edema: failure of ATP-dependent sodium and calcium transport causes swelling of neurons, glia and endothelial cells.
- Ionic / interstitial edema: transitional edema before BBB breakdown, driven by ion gradients and endothelial transport.
- Vasogenic edema: BBB endothelial breakdown allows proteins and macromolecules into extracellular space.
- Malignant or massive ischemic stroke accounts for roughly 10 percent of ischemic strokes in this source and is defined by space-occupying edema severe enough to cause elevated ICP and herniation risk.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Monitoring after large infarct must include mental status, signs of ICP, mass effect risk and posterior fossa / hemispheric compression concerns.
- Hemorrhagic conversion risk should be framed as ischemic vascular injury plus clinical modifiers, not only as "antithrombotic complication."
- Mechanistic edema categories help organize injury progression, but bedside management requires dedicated malignant infarction / neurocritical care sources.

## Fact

- The source states ischemia and infarction cause loss of structural integrity of affected brain tissue and blood vessels.
- The source states matrix metalloproteases degrade basal lamina collagens and laminins.
- The source states vascular integrity loss causes BBB breakdown and cerebral edema.
- The source states catastrophic vascular integrity failure is postulated to cause hemorrhagic conversion by allowing blood constituents to enter brain parenchyma.
- The source states cerebral edema can increase ICP, reduce CBF, cause mass effect and lead to herniation.
- The source describes cytotoxic, ionic / interstitial and vasogenic edema after ischemic stroke.
- The source states roughly 10 percent of ischemic strokes are malignant or massive due to space-occupying edema severe enough to cause elevated ICP and herniation.

## Inference

- Secondary neurologic decline after ischemic stroke should trigger evaluation for edema, hemorrhagic conversion and other complications, not only recurrent ischemia.
- BBB failure links edema and hemorrhagic conversion in a shared neurovascular injury model.

## Assumption

- This page addresses adult ischemic stroke and does not include pediatric-specific edema physiology.
- Management details require additional source-specific pages.

## Uncertainty

- The source describes hemorrhagic conversion mechanism as postulated when vascular integrity catastrophically fails.
- The source does not specify timing, imaging grading or treatment thresholds.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 edema 當作 purely radiologic bystander。
- 不可用本頁決定 decompressive hemicraniectomy timing。
- 不可用本頁決定 thrombolysis、antiplatelet 或 anticoagulation timing。
- 不可把 hemorrhagic conversion 全部歸因於藥物。
