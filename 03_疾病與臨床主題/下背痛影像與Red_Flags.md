# 下背痛影像與 Red Flags

## One-Sentence Definition

LBP imaging 應有明確臨床意圖：辨識 serious pathology、解釋持續神經症狀或支援治療決策；acute uncomplicated LBP 不建議 routine early MRI。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 LBP red-flag triage 與 imaging intent。
- 本頁不是完整 radiology interpretation manual，也不是 cauda equina guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Low_Back_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- MRI 對許多退化變化敏感，但 specificity 低；asymptomatic abnormalities common。
- 早期 MRI 可能增加 surgery、cost、opioid use 與 nocebo，而不一定改善 outcome。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Imaging 前需先問：結果會改變什麼管理？是否有 red flag？是否有 progressive neurologic deficit？是否準備介入或手術？
- Cauda equina symptoms、progressive motor weakness、systemic red flags 應優先 escalated evaluation。

## Mechanism

```text
LBP presentation
→ red-flag and neurologic triage
→ decide whether imaging result is actionable
→ choose x-ray, MRI, CT, CT myelography, EDX, or labs by clinical question
→ interpret results against symptoms and exam
```

## Observable Patterns

- Red flags：new severe pain <18 or >55, violent trauma, constant night pain, cancer history, systemic steroids, drug abuse, HIV / immunocompromised status, weight loss, fever / night sweats, severe ROM restriction, deformity, bladder / bowel dysfunction, saddle anesthesia, progressive motor weakness, gait disturbance, inflammatory features。
- Cauda equina：bowel、bladder、sexual dysfunction、saddle anesthesia、severe neurologic deficit。
- Inflammatory clues：morning stiffness、peripheral joint symptoms、iritis、rash、colitis、urethral discharge、family rheumatic history。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Red flags must be interpreted in context because many primary-care LBP patients endorse at least one red flag。
- Absence of red flags does not fully exclude serious disease; clinical judgment and follow-up matter。
- Imaging report language should avoid nocebic overinterpretation of common degenerative findings。

## Fact

- Guidelines cited in the chapter recommend against MRI for acute uncomplicated LBP。
- MRI has high sensitivity but low specificity in LBP because asymptomatic abnormalities are common。
- Early MRI within six weeks was associated in cited studies with more surgery, cost, opioid use, and worse pain。
- One cited study reported surgery 13 times more likely in early MRI group。
- Plain radiographs are used after trauma or red-flag context but are low-sensitivity / low-specificity screening tools。
- MRI with contrast is useful for tumor, infection, or scar vs recurrent herniation contexts。
- EMG helps distinguish radiculopathy from mononeuropathy or polyneuropathy, but does not evaluate radicular pain or sensory-root dysfunction well。

## Inference

- The most important imaging question is not "Is there degeneration?" but "Is there actionable pathology matching the clinical syndrome?"
- Red-flag lists should be used to structure triage, not to replace clinical reasoning.

## Assumption

- Clinician has access to follow-up; if follow-up is unreliable and red-flag uncertainty is high, threshold for escalation may be lower。

## Uncertainty

- Red-flag sensitivity / specificity differs by sign and setting; this source gives caution but not a full predictive model。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「沒有 red flags 就絕對沒有 cancer。」正確是 absence reduces suspicion but does not eliminate risk。
- 誤讀：「MRI 看到 disc bulge 就是痛源。」正確是 asymptomatic disc findings common。
- 誤讀：「不做早期 MRI 表示忽略病人。」正確是避免無意圖影像造成傷害，同時保留 red-flag escalation。
