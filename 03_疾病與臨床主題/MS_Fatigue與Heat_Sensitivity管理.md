# MS Fatigue 與 Heat Sensitivity 管理

## One-Sentence Definition

MS fatigue and heat sensitivity management 要先區分 central / peripheral fatigue 與 secondary causes，再用 cooling、energy conservation、sleep / mood treatment、trigger avoidance 與 graded exercise 維持 participation。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 MS fatigue、Uhthoff phenomenon、heat sensitivity 與 exercise dosing。
- 本頁不是 pharmacologic fatigue prescribing guide。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Multiple_Sclerosis]]。

## Why It Matters

- Fatigue 常是 PwMS 最 disabling 的症狀，但外觀可能「看起來很好」，導致家庭、職場與醫療端低估。
- Heat sensitivity 會造成 transient neurologic worsening；若錯誤理解成「不能運動」，會加重 deconditioning。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Fatigue 變差時先排除 relapse / pseudo-relapse、infection、heat exposure、sleep disorder、depression / anxiety、thyroid dysfunction、anemia、medication side effects、overexertion。
- Exercise prescription 需納入 heat intolerance、fall risk、strength、endurance、fatigue recovery time。

## Mechanism

```text
CNS demyelination and reduced conduction reserve
→ heat / infection / dehydration / exertion worsens conduction
→ transient motor, visual, cognitive worsening
→ fear of activity and deconditioning
→ cooling + pacing + trigger management + graded exercise
```

## Observable Patterns

- Peripheral fatigue：activity after a few minutes causes exhaustion and improves with rest。
- Central fatigue：constant exhaustion / lassitude with arousal and attention dysfunction；rest may not help。
- Uhthoff phenomenon：heat exposure、physical exhaustion、infection、dehydration 造成 transient neurologic worsening。
- Heat-induced fatigue affects physical and cognitive function。
- Sleep disorders, nocturia, pain / paresthesia, spasticity, DMT side effects, depression and anxiety can mimic or worsen fatigue。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Fatigue Severity Scale、Fatigue Impact Scale、Modified Fatigue Impact Scale can quantify fatigue。
- Energy conservation and family / caregiver education are part of treatment because fatigue may appear suddenly。
- Cooling strategies include avoiding warm baths / heated pools, water temperature caution, precooling with ice water or cool bath, head cooling, and evaporative cooling garments。
- Exercise should be encouraged in moderation for strength, fitness, and health, with cooling and pacing rather than avoidance。

## Fact

- Fatigue is described as one of the most common and often most disabling MS symptoms。
- Secondary fatigue factors in the source include thermosensitivity、mood disorder、anxiety、sleep disturbance、infection、thyroid dysfunction、anemia、DMTs, antidepressants, antispasmodics, narcotics, sedatives。
- Depression lifetime prevalence in PwMS is described as up to 50% in the source, and depression treatment is associated with fatigue improvement。
- Insomnia is reported by about 40-50% of PwMS vs about 10% of the general population。
- Heat-induced fatigue affects approximately 58-93% of PwMS in the source。
- Source advises water temperatures no greater than 84°F for swimming / water exposure。
- Exercise improves quality of life in PwMS with no evidence of deleterious effects in the cited review。

## Inference

- A fatigue plan that skips sleep, mood, nocturia, medication, and infection review is incomplete。
- The safest exercise plan for heat-sensitive MS is not zero exercise but temperature-aware graded activity。

## Assumption

- Patient has access to cooling strategies and can monitor exertional symptoms during and after exercise。

## Uncertainty

- Pharmacologic fatigue agents have variable response; the source encourages trying another agent when response is subtherapeutic, but current prescribing details require updated review。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MS fatigue 是心理問題。」正確是可能有 CNS fatigue，但 mood / sleep / medication / infection 等 secondary factors 也需處理。
- 誤讀：「Uhthoff 就是 relapse。」正確是 heat-related transient worsening，不等於新 inflammatory relapse。
- 誤讀：「怕熱所以不能運動。」正確是需 cooling、pacing、moderate intensity 與安全監測。
