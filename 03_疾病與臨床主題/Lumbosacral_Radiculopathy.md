# Lumbosacral Radiculopathy

## One-Sentence Definition

Lumbosacral radiculopathy / radicular pain 是 lumbosacral nerve root irritation or dysfunction，常由 disc pathology 引起，但 pain 可能來自 inflammation and chemical irritation rather than mechanical compression alone。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 lumbosacral radicular pain / radiculopathy 的 source-specific framework。
- 本頁不涵蓋 peripheral neuropathy、hip pathology、vascular claudication 或 full surgical decision-making。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Low_Back_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- Treating radicular pain as purely mechanical compression can overemphasize imaging and underemphasize natural history, inflammation, function, and conservative care。
- Cauda equina syndrome 與 progressive neurologic deficit 必須從一般 sciatica 分開。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 必須篩 fever、weight loss、night pain、cancer、osteoporosis risk、bowel / bladder / sexual dysfunction、saddle anesthesia、progressive weakness。
- 若 cauda equina 疑似，需 urgent evaluation；來源指出 decompression within 48 hours yields best neurologic recovery。

## Mechanism

```text
Disc protrusion / extrusion or foraminal pathology
→ nerve-root chemical inflammation ± mechanical compression
→ radicular pain, paresthesia, weakness, reflex change
→ natural regression or persistent deficit
→ conservative care, ESI, or surgery depending on course and red flags
```

## Observable Patterns

- Lancinating、shock-like、electric pain。
- Narrow band leg pain, often below knee, but not always dermatomal。
- May be sclerotomal rather than neat dermatome。
- Neurologic findings can include myotomal weakness, sensory change, depressed reflex。
- Cauda equina：bowel、bladder、sexual dysfunction、saddle anesthesia。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Many disc protrusions / extrusions regress spontaneously; conservative management is preferred in many cases even with some neurologic injury when not progressive。
- Surgery is mainly for neurologic progression, cauda equina, or persistent radicular symptoms after maximal conservative care for 6-8 weeks in the source。
- ESI may provide short-term pain benefit and should connect to active rehabilitation。

## Fact

- Radicular symptoms may arise from mechanical compression or chemical inflammation。
- Normal nerve roots may not produce pain with mechanical stimulation, but inflamed roots can。
- Disc protrusion is the most common cause in the source。
- Serious causes are less than 1% but must be evaluated when systemic red flags exist。
- Cauda equina syndrome occurs in up to 1% of disc herniations in the source。
- NSAIDs are not better than placebo or other drugs for sciatica in cited evidence。
- Oral steroids have mostly no significant pain / surgery benefit, with possible small function role。
- SPORT data summarized in the source show both surgery and nonoperative care improve; leg pain relief is more predictable than back pain relief。

## Inference

- A negative or nonconcordant MRI does not automatically invalidate radicular symptoms; inflammation and clinical course matter。
- Back-pain-dominant cases should have cautious surgical expectations。

## Assumption

- Patient is stable without cauda equina or progressive motor deficit; otherwise this page's conservative framing does not apply。

## Uncertainty

- Optimal ESI timing, approach, and steroid selection require procedure-specific evidence beyond this source。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Sciatica 一定是神經被壓到。」正確是 inflammation / chemical irritation can drive pain。
- 誤讀：「Leg pain 不照 dermatome 就不是 radicular。」正確是 distribution can be sclerotomal or imperfect。
- 誤讀：「保守治療就是放著不管。」正確是 education、activity、pain control、monitoring deficit、rehab and escalation criteria。
