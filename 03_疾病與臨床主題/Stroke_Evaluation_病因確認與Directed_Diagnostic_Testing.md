# Stroke Evaluation 病因確認與 Directed Diagnostic Testing

## One-Sentence Definition

Stroke evaluation 是從急性穩定與 hemorrhage / reperfusion triage 開始，先用 history、physical examination 與初始 brain imaging 形成病因假說，再用 directed neurovascular imaging、cardiac evaluation、blood tests 與 hemorrhage etiology workup 確認 pathophysiologic process。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 stroke diagnostic workflow：如何從「疑似 stroke」走到「較可信的 mechanism / etiology」。

不處理 IVT / EVT eligibility 細節、ICH / SAH acute treatment、secondary prevention drug choice 或 rehabilitation dose。這些應連到 [[Acute_Stroke_Initial_Assessment與Stabilization]]、[[中風急性期處置與時間窗]]、[[Spontaneous_ICH_診斷與病因分層]]、[[Aneurysmal_SAH_臨床表現與診斷分流]]、[[中風次發預防]]。

## Why It Matters

Stroke care 的危險錯誤是把「看起來像」當成「已經確認」。CT 可以快速排除 hemorrhage，但 CT normal 不排除 ischemia；AF、carotid stenosis、lacunar-like syndrome 都只能改變機率，不能自動證明病因。病因確認會改變 reperfusion、carotid procedure、anticoagulation、antiplatelet、ICH etiology workup 與 long-term prevention。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult suspected acute stroke 或 TIA。
- 已完成 first-hours safety triage：vital stability、glucose/oxygen、noncontrast CT 或 MRI、reperfusion candidacy 判斷。
- 有能力取得 brain imaging、vascular imaging、cardiac monitoring / ECG、echocardiography 與基本 blood tests。

## Mechanism

```text
acute neurologic symptom
-> stabilize + exclude immediately dangerous mimic / hemorrhage
-> classify broad pathophysiology
-> form presumptive subtype from history, exam, CT/MRI
-> choose directed tests based on hypothesis
-> confirm mechanism enough to guide treatment and secondary prevention
```

### Four Diagnostic Layers

1. Safety layer: airway / breathing / circulation、glucose、oxygenation、noncontrast CT / MRI、IVT / EVT decision。
2. Classification layer: intrinsic vessel abnormality、embolism、inadequate blood flow、vessel rupture。
3. Presumptive mechanism layer: [[Stroke_Subtype_Clinical_Diagnosis]] 用 onset/course、risk factors、associated symptoms、exam 與 infarct pattern 形成 working diagnosis。
4. Etiologic classification layer: [[Stroke_Etiologic_Classification]] 用 TOAST / CCS / ISPS25-like categories 把 supported mechanism 寫成 large artery atherosclerosis、cardioembolism、small vessel disease、other determined cause 或 undetermined cause。
5. Confirmation layer: neurovascular imaging、cardiac monitoring、echocardiography、selected blood tests、ICH-focused etiologic testing。

### Minimum Etiologic Dataset

From the etiology / classification source, ISPS25 frames minimum ischemic stroke mechanism evaluation as:

- history and physical examination。
- brain imaging plus intracranial and extracranial vascular imaging。
- 12-lead ECG and at least 24 hours continuous cardiac rhythm monitoring when initial ECG does not show AF。
- TTE with agitated saline contrast when PFO closure might be relevant, and/or ultrasound enhancing agent when LV disease is a concern。
- laboratory testing including CBC、basic metabolic panel、D-dimer、troponin、lipid panel、HbA1c、coagulation panel, with HIV and syphilis testing when indicated。

This minimum dataset does not mean every patient needs every rare-cause test; additional testing follows if the minimum evaluation is unrevealing or if age / comorbid factors raise suspicion.

## Observable Patterns

### Brain and Neurovascular Imaging

- Noncontrast CT 是 suspected stroke 常用第一線，優點是快速、可近性高、對急性 hemorrhage 敏感。
- MRI 對早期 brain infarction 較敏感；DWI / FLAIR 在 early ischemia 有用。
- Ischemia 尚未形成 infarction 時，CT 和 MRI 都可能正常。
- 多數 suspected acute ischemic stroke / TIA 應取得 brain imaging 加 comprehensive neurovascular evaluation。
- Anterior circulation event 需聚焦 extracranial / intracranial carotid、MCA / ACA branches。
- Posterior circulation event 需聚焦 vertebral arteries、basilar artery、PCA territory。
- Posterior fossa ischemia 是 early imaging pitfall：CT sensitivity lower than DWI MRI，且 small acute posterior circulation infarct 仍可 DWI false negative。
- CTA / MRA 可和 CT / MRI 同步做，用於排除 parent artery occlusion、找 LVO、stenosis、dissection、vascular malformation 或 aneurysm。

### Dissection-Specific Vascular Imaging

- [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_臨床辨識]] 的 suspicion 由 acute/subacute head or neck pain、stroke symptoms、Horner syndrome、cranial/cervical neuropathy、pulsatile tinnitus、minor trigger 或 vascular predisposition 啟動。
- [[Cervicocephalic_Artery_Dissection_影像確認與Pitfalls]] 需要 urgent head/neck MRI/MRA 或 CT/CTA；確認依據是 long tapered stenosis、tapered occlusion、pseudoaneurysm、intimal flap、double lumen 或 intramural hematoma。
- Carotid duplex / TCD 可 screen 或 monitor，但 skull base、vertebral artery transverse foramina and isolated Horner syndrome 都是 ultrasound pitfall；clinical history suggestive 時 ultrasound negative 仍應追 MRA 或 CTA。

### Acute Vestibular Syndrome / HINTS

- Continuous acute vertigo/dizziness 可用 [[HINTS_急性前庭症候群中樞與周邊鑑別]] 協助區分 brainstem / cerebellar ischemia 與 peripheral vestibular disease。
- Central HINTS pattern：normal head impulse bilaterally、direction-changing nystagmus 或 skew deviation。
- Peripheral-leaning HINTS pattern：abnormal unilateral head impulse、unidirectional horizontal-torsional nystagmus and absent skew 必須三者同時成立。
- HINTS 不適用於 momentary position-related transient vertigo，也不適用於檢查時已不 dizziness 的 TIA。
- New ipsilateral hearing loss 是 AICA / internal auditory artery infarction 的危險線索，即使眼動表現像 peripheral lesion。

### Embolic Stroke Workup

- Embolic mechanism 較可能見於 sudden maximal onset、large deficit/infarct、known cardiac or large artery lesion、hemorrhagic infarct、multiple vascular territories、rapid improvement。
- Extracranial / intracranial arteries 可能是 artery-to-artery embolic source，不能只找心臟。
- Artery-to-artery vs cardioaortic embolism 常需結合 vascular imaging、TTE / TEE、rhythm monitoring 與 infarct pattern。

### Lacunar and Large-Vessel Atherothrombotic Workup

- [[Lacunar_Stroke_Syndromes_臨床表現與影像確認]] 加 hypertension / diabetes risk factors and small subcortical infarct 時，small vessel disease 機率上升。
- 但 [[Lacunar_Infarcts_診斷與病因邊界]] 不能只由 syndrome 或 small deep lesion 決定；parent artery atheroma、large artery occlusive disease、cardiac/aortic embolism or multiple acute DWI lesions 都可改變 mechanism。
- Confirmed lacunar infarction is not a mechanical thrombectomy target, but acute vascular imaging is warranted because small deep infarcts are not always lacunar.
- CT 對 acute lacunar infarct sensitivity low；若 CT nondiagnostic 或 lesions of different ages coexist，DWI MRI 有助於確認 acute culprit lesion and embolic pattern。
- Suspected large-vessel atherothrombotic stroke 需 intracranial and extracranial vascular testing。
- Carotid duplex、CTA、MRA、TCD 各有角色；conventional angiography 多留給 noninvasive test 不清楚、結果不一致、或懷疑 dissection / fibromuscular dysplasia 等 nonatherosclerotic disease。Dissection source 進一步把 DSA 保留給 younger patients with high suspicion despite negative noninvasive imaging；若 CTA / MRA 已清楚診斷，通常不需要 DSA。

### Cardiac Evaluation

- 多數 brain ischemia 病人需要 cardiac evaluation：history for ischemia/arrhythmia、cardiac exam、ECG。
- 所有 ischemic stroke 應至少在 stroke onset 後前 24 hours 做 cardiac monitoring 以找 subclinical AF。
- Cryptogenic ischemic stroke / TIA 若 ECG 與 24-hour monitoring 無 AF，來源建議 several weeks ambulatory monitoring。
- Suspected embolic stroke 應做 echocardiogram。
- TTE 常作多數人的 initial test；TEE 在年輕無已知心血管病、高 cardiac embolic pretest probability、AF 且結果會改變 management、prosthetic valve、suspected aortic pathology 等情境較適合作 initial test。
- TEE 較適合看 left atrial appendage clot、atria、atrial septum、aorta、PFO / ASD / atrial septal aneurysm；TTE 較適合看 left ventricular apical thrombus。
- [[AF_Related_Stroke_病因歸屬與診斷評估]] 補上 AF-specific boundary：AF patient 的 stroke 可能是 LAA / cardiac embolism，但 AF common in older adults，不能因為有 AF 就省略 large artery disease、small vessel disease、aortic source 或 other cause evaluation。
- TEE 可找 LAA thrombus and complex aortic plaque，但 TEE negative 不能排除 prior AF embolism，因為 embolization 後 residual thrombus 不一定仍可見。
- 如果 AF 與 high-grade ipsilateral large artery stenosis 或 convincing lacunar pattern 共存，應把 AF anticoagulation indication 和 competing stroke mechanism management 分開處理。

### Blood Tests and Hypercoagulable Testing

- 基本 blood tests 包含 glucose、CBC / platelet count、PT / INR / aPTT、疑似 direct anticoagulant 時 thrombin time 或 ecarin clotting time、lipid profile。
- D-dimer / fibrinogen 有時可提示 cancer 或 inflammatory disease 相關 brain ischemia，但不是 routine mechanism classifier。
- Hypercoagulable testing 不應 routine 套用所有 ischemic stroke；適用於 systemic thrombosis 個人/家族史、cardiac/vascular imaging 後仍 unexplained，特別是 young stroke，或臨床提示 SLE / APS。
- aPL 在 ischemic stroke 中的解讀有衝突；急性 stroke 可有 transient elevation。

### ICH Etiology Workup

- CT 可定義 ICH size、location、IVH、mass effect、midline shift、herniation、hydrocephalus。
- Severe hypertension 加典型位置 well-circumscribed homogeneous hematoma 時，hypertensive ICH 可相對有把握。
- 所有 intracranial hemorrhage 都需 bleeding-disorder evaluation，尤其原因不明時。
- Lobar / atypical hemorrhage 需考慮 cerebral amyloid angiopathy、tumor、vascular malformation。
- Cocaine-associated ICH 需 vascular imaging，因 underlying aneurysm / vascular malformation incidence 較高。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 臨床紀錄應分成 working diagnosis、tests supporting mechanism、remaining uncertainty。
- 轉入 rehab 或 ward 後的新 neurologic decline 仍需先回到 stroke evaluation workflow，而不是直接標為「舊中風波動」。
- Research 上，stroke mechanism adjudication 應明確寫出 clinical pattern、brain imaging、vascular imaging、cardiac monitoring、echo 與 lab 的角色。

## Fact

- UpToDate source states that symptoms do not accurately reflect infarction presence and symptom tempo does not indicate ischemia cause.
- The source states that initial evaluation includes rapid stabilization, hemorrhage detection, reperfusion decision, presumptive etiology formation, and directed diagnostic confirmation.
- The source states that noncontrast CT is typically the first diagnostic study in suspected stroke.
- The source states that MRI is more sensitive than CT for early brain infarction.
- The source states that presumptive subtype diagnosis requires more extensive testing for confirmation.
- The source states that brain imaging and comprehensive neurovascular evaluation should be obtained for most suspected acute ischemic stroke or TIA patients.
- The source states that cardiac monitoring for at least the first 24 hours after ischemic stroke onset is useful to look for arrhythmias.
- The source suggests several weeks of ambulatory cardiac monitoring for cryptogenic ischemic stroke / TIA when ECG and 24-hour monitoring do not show AF.
- The source states that all suspected embolic stroke patients should have an echocardiogram.
- The source states that hypercoagulable testing indications are limited and inherited thrombophilia causality for arterial stroke remains unclear.
- The source states that every intracranial hemorrhage patient requires bleeding-disorder evaluation, especially if cause is not immediately apparent.
- The etiology / classification source states that TOAST classifies ischemic stroke into large artery atherosclerosis, cardioembolism, small vessel occlusion, other determined etiology, and undetermined etiology.
- The etiology / classification source states that SSS-TOAST / CCS add evident, probable, and possible confidence levels.
- The etiology / classification source states that original TOAST and CCS agreement is only moderate at best.
- The posterior circulation source states CT has lower sensitivity than DWI MRI for acute ischemic stroke, particularly in the posterior fossa.
- The posterior circulation source states false-negative DWI acute ischemic stroke is strongly associated with posterior circulation localization.
- The posterior circulation source states HINTS is most helpful in continuous vertigo or dizziness, not momentary positional vertigo or TIA when the patient is not dizzy at examination.
- The AF stroke source states that all stroke patients, even in the setting of AF, may benefit from complete evaluation for other causes of stroke.
- The AF stroke source states TTE is recommended for most ischemic stroke patients and TEE may identify LAA thrombus or other embolic sources, but TEE is not used to exclude AF as the cause.
- The lacunar infarcts source states standard evaluation for suspected lacunar stroke includes history/physical, brain imaging, neurovascular imaging, cardiac monitoring and echocardiography.
- The lacunar infarcts source states not all small deep infarcts are lacunar and diagnosing lacunar infarction requires excluding other ischemic stroke etiologies.
- The dissection source states diagnosis is confirmed by neuroimaging, particularly long tapered stenosis, tapered occlusion, pseudoaneurysm, intimal flap, double lumen or intramural hematoma.
- The dissection source states urgent noninvasive multimodal imaging with MRI/MRA or CT/CTA of the head and neck is used to confirm initial diagnosis and guide serial treatment decisions.
- The dissection source states ultrasound-negative cases with suggestive clinical history should be confirmed with MRA or CTA.

## Inference

- A stroke mechanism label should be treated as provisional until supported by appropriate imaging / cardiac / laboratory evidence.
- A single abnormality such as AF, carotid stenosis, or positive aPL is not automatically the causal mechanism.
- In AF patients, mechanism attribution and anticoagulation indication should be documented separately because AF may remain an anticoagulation indication even when the index stroke has a competing cause.
- In a rehab setting, diagnostic certainty matters because anticoagulation, BP target, mobilization timing, swallowing plan, and recurrence-prevention strategy can diverge sharply.
- "Undetermined" should be separated into negative evaluation, incomplete evaluation, and multiple plausible causes.
- Dissection should be documented as suspected artery + clinical trigger + imaging sign, not merely "young stroke."

## Assumption

- The workflow assumes adult patients and access to acute stroke imaging.
- It assumes local systems can perform at least ECG / telemetry, vascular imaging, and echocardiography when indicated.

## Uncertainty

- Optimal cardiac monitoring method and duration remain uncertain.
- Whether anticoagulation of subclinical AF improves outcomes after initially cryptogenic stroke lacks rigorous evidence in this source.
- aPL interpretation in ischemic stroke is conflicting.
- Rare stroke causes require source-specific workup beyond this overview.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 CT normal 解讀成沒有 ischemia。
- 不可把 bedside subtype clues 當 final etiology。
- 不可把 AF / carotid stenosis / lacunar syndrome 自動等同 causal mechanism。
- 不可因為臨床像 lacunar syndrome 就省略 vascular imaging 或 cardiac evaluation。
- 不可用 TEE negative for LAA thrombus 排除 AF-related embolism。
- 不可 routine 做所有 hypercoagulable panels。
- 不可把 lobar / atypical ICH 不加區分地歸因於 hypertension。
- 不可把 early CT / DWI MRI negative 視為 posterior circulation stroke 已排除。
- 不可把 HINTS 用在不符合 continuous acute vestibular syndrome 的 dizziness。
- 不可把 carotid duplex / TCD negative 當成 dissection rule-out，尤其是 isolated Horner syndrome、skull-base lesion 或 vertebral artery segment。
