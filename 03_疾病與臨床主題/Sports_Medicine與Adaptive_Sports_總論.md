# Sports Medicine 與 Adaptive Sports 總論

## One-Sentence Definition

Sports medicine in PM&R 是以 athlete health、event safety、training load、kinetic chain、tissue healing、RTP 與 adaptive participation 為核心的功能醫學。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../09_來源摘要/Sports_Medicine_and_Adaptive_Sports]] 建立 sports medicine / adaptive sports 主框架。
- 本頁不取代 current concussion consensus、anti-doping list、transgender athlete policy、PPE cardiovascular screening guideline。

## Why It Matters

- 運動傷害不是只看 painful tissue；還要看 sport demand、training load、kinetic chain、equipment、environment、心理準備與 RTP risk。
- Adaptive athlete 不是把一般 athlete protocol 直接套用；classification、equipment、skin / shoulder load、autonomic / thermoregulation、prosthesis or wheelchair interface 都會改變風險。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需確認 sport、position、level、season timing、training load、mechanism、tissue diagnosis、biomechanical diagnosis、medical risk、rules、RTP criteria。
- Event coverage 需先有 emergency action plan、chain of command、equipment、AED、environmental monitoring、communication。

## Mechanism

```text
Training load + sport-specific mechanics + tissue capacity + recovery
-> adaptation if load is appropriate
-> injury if load exceeds capacity or kinetic chain fails
-> phase-based rehab + sport-specific progression
-> RTP when symptoms, function, mechanics, strength, proprioception, and risk are acceptable
```

## Observable Patterns

- Acute phase：symptom control, protection, education, maintain ROM.
- Recovery phase：progressive load, flexibility, strength, endurance, kinetic-chain correction.
- Functional phase：sport-specific drills, neuromuscular control, practice progression, RTP decision.
- Overtraining：performance decrement, fatigue, mood disturbance, poor sleep, higher illness / injury.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- RTP requires documented recovery, completed rehab, risk discussion, and appropriate consultant input when outside scope.
- Kinetic chain assessment should test multiplanar sport-specific dynamics.
- Adaptive sports require injury surveillance by disability cause and equipment interface.

## Fact

- 來源列出 team physician responsibilities: eligibility, injury care, RTP, emergency preparedness, training oversight, supervision, liability protection.
- 來源描述 periodization, overload, specificity, individuality, tapering.
- 來源描述 PEACE / LOVE approach for acute and recovery phases.
- 來源指出 full RTP requires absence of pain, normal flexibility / strength / proprioception, appropriate sport-specific mechanics and skills.
- 來源包含 adaptive sports classification, equipment, and injury patterns.

## Inference

- A sports rehab plan should include tissue diagnosis plus biomechanical diagnosis; otherwise return may fail even if pain improves.

## Assumption

- Athlete and team can follow staged progression and reporting; sport context is known enough to test relevant tasks.

## Uncertainty

- Concussion, eligibility, anti-doping, and transgender athlete policies are time-sensitive and must be updated from current primary sources.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「痛消失就 RTP」不成立。
- 「RTP 由 therapist 單方決定」不成立；team physician has final clearance responsibility in many contexts.
- 「adaptive sports 只是運動醫學加上 disability label」不成立。
