# Parkinson Disease 復健與非運動症狀管理

## One-Sentence Definition

PD care 要把 levodopa-centered motor symptom control、DBS / device-aided options、physical therapy、ADL adaptation、nonmotor symptom treatment 與 caregiver support 串成同一個功能維持策略。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 PD management 中與 rehabilitation、nonmotor burden、caregiver role 相關的內容。
- 本頁不是完整 pharmacologic dosing 或 surgical candidacy guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Degenerative_Movement_Disorders_of_the_CNS]]。

## Why It Matters

- PD 的 major disability 不只來自 tremor / rigidity；depression、executive dysfunction、orthostatic hypotension、sleep disorder、dysphagia、falls 與 caregiver strain 都會決定生活品質與安置風險。
- Levodopa 有效不代表復健可以省略；來源直接指出 interdisciplinary team care 是 overall most effective approach。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Management 應先明確定義目前主要問題：ADL interference、gait / fall risk、wearing-off、dyskinesia、dysphagia、cognition、mood、autonomic symptoms、sleep、caregiver burden。
- Motor training 應盡量和 medication on/off 狀態、fatigue、orthostatic symptoms、cognitive load 對齊。

## Mechanism

```text
Progressive dopaminergic and extra-nigral alpha-synuclein pathology
-> motor fluctuation + gait / axial dysfunction + nonmotor symptoms
-> ADL loss, falls, swallowing risk, cognitive / mood burden, caregiver load
-> medication optimization + rehabilitation + environmental adaptation + caregiver support
```

## Observable Patterns

- Wearing-off：症狀在 levodopa dose interval 末段惡化，下一次 dose 後改善。
- Levodopa-induced dyskinesia：full efficacy / on state 時出現 choreatic movements。
- Nonmotor symptoms：apathy、anhedonia、anxiety、depression、visual hallucinations、delusions、cognitive impairment、orthostatic hypotension、constipation、urinary urgency / retention、sexual dysfunction、sialorrhea、insomnia、RLS、periodic limb movement、excessive daytime sleepiness、REM sleep behavior disorder、hyposmia、visual processing problems。
- Cognitive domains：executive dysfunction 常最早、最嚴重；memory 與 visuospatial function 也常受影響。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Levodopa 是來源中最有效的 motor symptom drug；開始治療時需用 lowest effective dose。
- 需監測 dyskinesia、nausea、impulse control disorder、hallucination、excessive daytime sleepiness、orthostatic hypotension worsening。
- High-protein meals can reduce levodopa absorption and efficacy。
- DBS 可用於 selected patients with disabling drug-related movements and motor fluctuations after medical management exhausted，且需 levodopa challenge response、非 Parkinson-plus syndrome、無重大干擾 medical condition。
- Physical therapy 在來源引用的 MDS-EBM update 中為 likely efficacious / clinically useful for PD motor symptoms。
- OT、specific exercise format、technology-based movement strategy、video-assisted swallowing therapy 在來源中 evidence 較不足或 clinical implication 較保留。

## Fact

- Nonmotor symptoms worsen over time and are major determinants of quality of life, nursing home placement, and progression to overall disability。
- Depression may add disability beyond motor dysfunction alone。
- PD patients in a large prospective cohort had approximately four times higher dementia risk than matched controls in the cited study。
- REM sleep behavior disorder may be an early presenting sign in some PD patients。
- Caregivers support ADL、safety awareness、medication management、organization、transportation、social involvement、history taking、treatment observation。

## Inference

- PD rehabilitation should include fall prevention, cueing / gait strategy, ADL simplification, swallowing surveillance, cognitive compensation, mood / sleep screening, autonomic safety, and caregiver education.
- A caregiver who manages medication timing and observes on/off response is part of the treatment system, not just a bystander.

## Assumption

- Medication changes, DBS decisions, psychosis treatment, and orthostatic hypotension pharmacotherapy are coordinated with neurology / movement disorder specialists.

## Uncertainty

- Disease-specific exercise dosing, OT protocols, technology cueing, swallowing therapy, and palliative timing require more targeted sources before stronger wiki synthesis.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「PD treatment 就是調 levodopa。」正確是 motor、nonmotor、rehab、caregiver 與 safety 都要處理。
- 誤讀：「Dyskinesia 一律代表治療失敗。」正確是它是 levodopa complication，需在 symptom control 與 dyskinesia 間平衡。
- 誤讀：「DBS 適合所有 advanced PD。」正確是需嚴格 selection，Parkinson-plus syndrome 與 major interfering conditions 不適合。
