# ICAS Stenting與介入治療限制

## One-Sentence Definition

ICAS stenting 與介入治療限制，是指 symptomatic intracranial large artery atherosclerosis 的 stroke prevention 不應把 angioplasty / stenting 當 initial treatment，因 randomized trials 顯示 early harm 或 no benefit；介入治療只在 recurrent stroke despite maximal medical therapy 時作為未證實的 last-resort / research-context option。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 ICAS 的 endovascular / surgical intervention 邊界。

不處理：

- acute mechanical thrombectomy for acute large vessel occlusion
- carotid stenosis 的 extracranial CEA / CAS
- moyamoya revascularization
- dissection aneurysm intervention
- medical therapy 細節；見 [[Symptomatic_ICAS_次發預防與DAPT邊界]]

## Why It Matters

「血管狹窄很嚴重，所以打開它」在 ICAS 是危險直覺。Intracranial artery 比 extracranial carotid 更靠近 perforator branches，stenting / angioplasty 會帶來 perforator occlusion、vessel injury、hemorrhage、reperfusion injury and restenosis。此來源的核心結論是：routine intervention 的 early harm 足以抵消理論上的 flow benefit。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Symptomatic ICAS 已被合理歸因為 index stroke / TIA mechanism。
- 已完成或正在執行 intensive medical therapy。
- 問題是 secondary prevention，不是急性 thrombectomy。
- 若討論 rescue intervention，需確認 recurrent events despite maximal medical therapy，而非單次 index event。

## Mechanism

```text
severe intracranial stenosis
-> angioplasty / stenting attempts lumen expansion
-> perforator occlusion, arterial injury, restenosis, hemorrhage or reperfusion injury
-> higher early stroke/death risk in trials
-> default strategy remains intensive medical therapy
```

## Observable Patterns

| Evidence / context | Finding in source | Clinical boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAMMPRIS | 70-99% stenosis with recent TIA / stroke；30-day stroke/death higher with stenting than medical therapy | stenting harm drove recommendation against routine use |
| VISSIT | higher 30-day safety outcome and higher 12-month same-territory stroke/TIA with stent group | no support for routine stenting |
| CASSISS | lower-risk, later-enrolled Chinese trial still found no benefit of stenting over medical therapy | better selection did not prove benefit |
| 2023 meta-analysis cited by source | endovascular therapy plus medical treatment had higher death/stroke at 30 days and one year | routine endovascular therapy remains unfavorable |
| WEAVE registry | apparent short-term safety only in highly selected patients meeting strict criteria | uncontrolled and short follow-up; not proof of benefit |
| BASIS submaximal balloon angioplasty | lower one-year primary outcome but more early stroke/death and major generalizability limits | promising but not ready for wide routine use |

## Clinical / Research Implication

Clinical implication：看到 70-99% symptomatic ICAS，不應直接問「要不要 stent？」；應先問：

- 是否已完成 DAPT / risk factor control？
- 是否為 first event 還是 recurrent events despite maximal medical therapy？
- 是否有 perforator-territory infarct、recent DWI lesion、hemorrhage risk or unstable symptoms？
- 是否有 trial / expert center context？

Research implication：未來試驗需要更清楚定義 high-risk medical failure、hemodynamic biomarker、procedure timing、operator experience and long-term outcome。

## Fact

- Source states intensive medical therapy is superior to intracranial arterial stenting for patients with recent stroke or TIA attributed to severe ICAS。
- Intracranial arterial stenting and other interventional procedures are not recommended for patients with first stroke or TIA attributable to severe intracranial stenosis。
- SAMMPRIS was stopped early because 30-day stroke or death was higher for angioplasty/stenting than medical therapy alone（14.7% vs 5.8%）。
- SAMMPRIS final follow-up showed higher stroke/death in stenting group at one and three years，largely driven by 30-day outcomes。
- VISSIT found worse early and 12-month outcomes in the stent group compared with medical therapy。
- CASSISS found no benefit of stenting compared with medical therapy despite excluding higher-risk early/perforator cases。
- Source-cited 2023 meta-analysis found endovascular therapy plus medical treatment led to higher death or stroke at 30 days and one year。
- Source recommends against intracranial stenting for recent stroke / TIA attributed to ICAS。
- AHA/ASA recommendations summarized by the source state angioplasty or stenting should not be initial treatment for severe 70-99% symptomatic stenosis, even if the patient was taking antithrombotic therapy at the time。
- For medical failures, usefulness of angioplasty alone or stent placement is unknown。
- NICE position cited by the source states endovascular stent insertion for intracranial atherosclerotic disease should only be used in research context。
- BASIS suggested potential benefit of submaximal balloon angioplasty but source notes delayed enrollment, open-label design, center concentration and procedural-experience generalizability concerns。

## Inference

- The default interventional answer for symptomatic ICAS is "no routine stent," not "refer for stent because stenosis is severe."
- A recurrent event on antithrombotic therapy alone is insufficient to label "failed maximal medical therapy" unless BP, LDL-C, physical activity, smoking and DAPT timing were also addressed。
- Rescue intervention should require explicit documentation of uncertainty and risk。

## Assumption

- The stenosis is atherosclerotic, not moyamoya、vasculitis、dissection or another intracranial stenosis mimic。
- The patient is being considered for stroke prevention intervention, not emergent thrombectomy。
- Local procedural outcomes and patient anatomy do not override the trial-level evidence boundary without a specialized rationale。

## Uncertainty

- Whether submaximal balloon angioplasty can generalize beyond experienced centers and selected Chinese trial populations remains uncertain。
- Which medically refractory ICAS subgroup, if any, benefits from stenting or angioplasty is not established。
- Hemodynamic markers do not yet provide a validated intervention-selection rule。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Misreading：medical therapy failure proves stenting benefit。  
  Correction：source says therapies are last resort and unproven; randomized data do not demonstrate benefit。

- Misreading：CASSISS makes stenting safe enough to use routinely。  
  Correction：CASSISS still found no benefit over medical therapy。

- Misreading：BASIS means submaximal balloon angioplasty should replace medical therapy。  
  Correction：source explicitly calls for more research before wide use。

- Misreading：acute thrombectomy evidence applies to chronic ICAS prevention stenting。  
  Correction：these are different clinical questions。
