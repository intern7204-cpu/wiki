# Stroke Movement Dysfunction Impairment Model

## One-Sentence Definition

Poststroke movement dysfunction 應拆成 primary neurologic impairments、secondary impairments 與 composite task-level movement problems，而不是只用 hemiparesis 或 spasticity 概括。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 Movement Dysfunction in Stroke 來源中的 movement analysis 與 impairment model。
- 本頁不處理 acute stroke reperfusion、secondary prevention 或完整 gait intervention guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Movement_Dysfunction_in_Stroke]]。

## Why It Matters

- 不同 impairment 需要不同 intervention；weakness、sensory loss、timing problem、postural control、pain、edema、contracture、deconditioning 與 compensation 不能混成一個字。
- Stroke rehab 應以 activity limitation and movement performance 指導，而不是只以影像診斷或病灶名稱指導。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需觀察實際 task，包括 bed mobility、sitting、standing、transfer、walking、reach、grasp、self-care 或其他 relevant activity。
- 若 arousal、attention、aphasia、neglect、depression 或 pain 影響表現，需先標記，不可直接歸因為 motor deficit。

## Mechanism

```text
Stroke lesion
-> neural drive, sensation, cognition, communication, and motor timing disruption
-> primary impairments
-> inactivity, malalignment, shortening, pain, edema, deconditioning
-> secondary impairments
-> movement deficit, atypical movement, compensation during tasks
```

## Observable Patterns

- Primary impairments：neurologic weakness、altered muscle activation、poor initiation / cessation、impaired sequencing / timing / force、somatosensory impairment、tone change。
- Secondary impairments：cardiopulmonary deconditioning、alignment / mobility restriction、muscle / soft tissue length change、pain、edema。
- Composite movement problems：movement deficits、atypical movements、undesirable compensations。
- Postural control includes anticipatory and compensatory adjustments; sequence may persist but timing can be impaired.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Movement examination should identify initiation, transition / execution, completion / termination, posture, trunk-extremity coordination, and skilled extremity movement.
- Assisted movement can test hypotheses by correcting alignment, limiting degrees of freedom, assisting weak muscle, blocking or stabilizing joints, and observing whether performance improves.
- Outcome selection should match the impairment and activity target, not only global disability.

## Fact

- The source states movement intervention should be directed by movement and activity limitations rather than stroke diagnosis alone.
- 75-80% of people after stroke are described as having insufficient force production.
- Proprioception is described as a poor prognostic sign for functional movement recovery when impaired.
- CRS-R is recommended in the source for standardized assessment of disorders of consciousness.

## Inference

- If assisted movement improves task quality, then part of the problem may be alignment, degrees of freedom, weak muscle assistance, or joint stabilization rather than fixed inability.
- Composite task failure may persist even when a single impairment measure improves.

## Assumption

- Acute medical instability, severe cardiopulmonary limitation, and activity restrictions have been screened before active task testing.

## Uncertainty

- A single impairment model does not predict individual recovery without time course, lesion severity, early return, comorbidities, and therapy exposure.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「看到 abnormal synergy 就等於 spasticity caused it」不成立。
- 「會用健側代償就是完成任務，沒有問題」不成立；需看安全、learned nonuse、secondary impairment 與 future recovery。
- 「坐站走都差就是單純肌力差」不成立；postural control、sensory processing、attention、timing 與 fear / pain 也會影響。
