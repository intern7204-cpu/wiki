# Myopathy 診斷框架

## One-Sentence Definition

Myopathy diagnosis 是從 motor-predominant weakness pattern 出發，結合 onset、progression、family history、extramuscular signs、labs、EDX、imaging、biopsy、exercise testing 與 genetic testing 來分出 hereditary vs acquired muscle disease。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 suspected myopathy 的 diagnostic formulation。
- 本頁不是各 myopathy subtype 的完整診斷標準。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Myopathic_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- Myopathy 常被誤認成 neuropathy、deconditioning、aging、pain inhibition 或 psychiatric fatigue。
- 但某些 acquired myopathies 有 disease-modifying treatment；某些 hereditary myopathies 需要 cardiac / respiratory screening 與 genetic counseling。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先確認是否為 true weakness，而非 pain-limited movement、contracture、spasticity、ataxia 或 effort inconsistency。
- History 至少要問 age of onset、progression speed、exercise trigger、rhabdomyolysis / cola-colored urine、family history、developmental history、medications、autoimmune / endocrine / infectious / toxic exposure。

## Mechanism

```text
Muscle fiber structural / metabolic / inflammatory dysfunction
→ weakness, cramps, rhabdomyolysis, exercise intolerance
→ pattern by onset, progression, distribution, family history, extramuscular signs
→ CK / labs + EDX + imaging + biopsy + exercise testing + genetic testing
→ subtype diagnosis and organ-risk screening
```

## Observable Patterns

- Common pattern：symmetric proximal weakness affecting shoulder and pelvic girdle。
- FSHD：asymmetric face / scapular / upper limb / hip flexor / dorsiflexor involvement。
- IBM：older adults，long finger flexor and knee extensor weakness，often mixed EMG features。
- Metabolic myopathy：exercise intolerance may be immediate with high-intensity activity in glycogen storage disorders, or low-intensity long-duration in fatty acid oxidation disorders。
- Respiratory, cardiac, bulbar, cognitive / neuropsychiatric features may point to specific subtypes.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Sensory NCS usually normal in myopathy；conduction block、temporal dispersion、marked slowing should raise neuropathy / demyelinating process concern。
- EMG myopathic motor units are typically low amplitude、short duration、polyphasic with early recruitment，但 steroid myopathy may be normal。
- MRI / ultrasound can identify disease pattern, severity, and biopsy target。
- Genetic testing can avoid biopsy in some cases and guide prognosis, family counseling, organ screening, and targeted therapy eligibility。

## Fact

- Myopathies are diseases characterized by muscle-fiber dysfunction and symptoms such as weakness, cramps, rhabdomyolysis, and exercise intolerance。
- Hereditary myopathies include muscular dystrophies, metabolic myopathies, channelopathies, mitochondrial myopathies, congenital myopathies, and distal myopathies。
- Acquired myopathies may be autoimmune, toxic, endocrine, infectious, medication-related, or critical illness-related。
- CK often correlates with muscle damage but is nonspecific and can rise after strenuous exercise。
- AST / ALT / LDH / aldolase may rise in muscle disease and hepatic dysfunction；GGT can help distinguish liver source。
- Nonischemic forearm exercise testing can support glycogen storage disease when ammonia rises without lactate rise。
- Ischemic forearm exercise testing is not preferred in the source because of rhabdomyolysis and compartment syndrome risk。

## Inference

- A normal sensory study plus proximal weakness supports myopathy only when clinical pattern fits; it does not replace subtype workup。
- Family history should ask about gait, braces, wheelchair use, and young cardiac problems, because relatives may not know the term "muscular dystrophy."

## Assumption

- Neuromuscular specialist access is available for unclear, progressive, multisystem, or genetically suspicious cases。

## Uncertainty

- This source gives a broad diagnostic framework, not disease-specific testing algorithms, variant interpretation standards, or payer authorization pathways。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「CK 高就是 inflammatory myopathy。」正確是 CK 高度 nonspecific，可見於 genetic、toxic、endocrine、exercise、rhabdomyolysis 等情境。
- 誤讀：「沒有 sensory loss 就不用 EDX。」正確是 EDX 可協助區分 myopathy vs neuropathy / NMJ / motor neuron disease。
- 誤讀：「EMG 正常排除 myopathy。」正確是 steroid myopathy 等狀況可能 EMG 正常。
