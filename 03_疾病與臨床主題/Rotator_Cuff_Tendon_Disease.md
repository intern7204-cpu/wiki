# Rotator Cuff Tendon Disease

## One-Sentence Definition

Rotator cuff tendon disease 是從 tendinopathy、partial tear、chronic full-thickness tear 到 acute traumatic full-thickness tear 的 spectrum，其中 acute traumatic full-thickness tear 需要早期 MRI 與 surgical consult。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 rotator cuff spectrum 與 rehab / referral decision。
- 本頁不涵蓋 adhesive capsulitis、glenohumeral OA、labral tear、instability 或 cervical referred pain 的完整鑑別。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Upper_Limb_Pain_and_Dysfunction]]。

## Why It Matters

- 同樣被稱為「rotator cuff pain」，tendinopathy 與 acute traumatic full-thickness tear 的處置完全不同。
- 錯過急性 full-thickness tear 可能因 tendon retraction、fatty infiltration、fibrosis 造成後續修補與功能結果變差。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先問 traumatic event、sudden severe pain、functional decline、inability to use shoulder。
- 檢查需同時看 scapular dyskinesis、impingement signs、active / passive ROM、strength、painful arc 與 cervical / neurologic clues。

## Mechanism

```text
Overload or trauma
→ cuff tendon irritability, partial tear, chronic tear, or acute full-thickness tear
→ altered humeral head control and scapular mechanics
→ subacromial pain, weakness, overhead limitation
→ rehab if stable spectrum; urgent imaging/referral if acute traumatic full-thickness tear suspected
```

## Observable Patterns

- Anterolateral shoulder or lateral arm pain。
- Pain with overhead activity、behind-back motion、lifting、side lying。
- Stiffness、weakness、clicking may occur。
- Inability to abduct >90 degrees with restricted external rotation suggests adhesive capsulitis or OA more than isolated cuff disease。
- Acute traumatic full-thickness tear often has sudden severe pain and marked functional loss after significant injury.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Tendinopathy、partial tear、chronic full-thickness tear 多數先以 nonsurgical rehabilitation 處理。
- Rehabilitation 應修正 scapular force couple、ROM、strength、posterior shoulder / pectoral tightness、kinetic chain 與 task load。
- Acute traumatic full-thickness tear 應 prompt MRI and surgical consultation。

## Fact

- 來源將 rotator cuff pathology 分成 tendinopathy、partial tears、chronic full-thickness tears、acute traumatic full-thickness tears。
- Tendinopathy、partial tears、chronic full-thickness tears usually receive initial nonsurgical rehabilitation。
- Acute traumatic full-thickness tear warrants prompt MRI and surgical consult。
- Delay as little as six weeks may allow fatty infiltration and reduce outcome in the source。
- Full can test is preferred over empty can in the chapter because empty can can provoke impingement and may not isolate supraspinatus。
- External / subacromial impingement can result from poor scapulothoracic mechanics。

## Inference

- "Rotator cuff disease" should not be a final plan label; plan must specify suspected spectrum location and referral urgency.
- Chronic tear in older adults does not automatically require surgery if function can be restored and tear is chronic / degenerative.

## Assumption

- The patient has no acute fracture, dislocation, infection, tumor, or cervical myelopathy / radiculopathy driving shoulder symptoms.

## Uncertainty

- 本來源未提供完整 imaging criteria 或 surgical outcome stratification；tear size、retraction、fatty infiltration、age、demand 需 orthopedic source 補強。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Shoulder impingement 就是骨頭夾住，所以只要止痛或手術。」正確是 scapulothoracic mechanics 與 cuff control 常是 rehab target。
- 誤讀：「所有 full-thickness tears 都急著開刀。」正確是 acute traumatic full-thickness tear 的 referral urgency 特別高，chronic tears 要看功能、可修補性與需求。
- 誤讀：「Empty can test 陽性就等於 supraspinatus tear。」正確是單一 test 不足，且來源偏好 full can。
