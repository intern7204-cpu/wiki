# ALS 診斷框架

## One-Sentence Definition

ALS diagnosis 是 progressive motor impairment 加上正確分布的 UMN / LMN dysfunction，並透過 EDX、imaging、lab 與臨床脈絡排除 mimic。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只整理 Motor Neuron Diseases 來源中的 ALS diagnostic frame。
- 本頁不是 ALS treatment guideline，也不處理所有 ALS genetic subtypes。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Motor_Neuron_Diseases]]。

## Why It Matters

- ALS 誤診代價高：false positive 會錯過可治療疾病；false negative 會延誤 disease-modifying therapy、NIV、nutrition、AAC 與 palliative planning。
- 老年族群常有 cervical spondylosis 或 nonspecific MRI change；影像常見不等於病因成立。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先判斷 weakness 是否 painless、progressive、motor-predominant，且是否缺乏 sensory / sphincter / autonomic symptoms。
- 需分 region：bulbar、cervical、thoracic、lumbosacral。
- 需同時找 UMN signs 與 LMN signs，並確認是否有 mimic red flags。

## Mechanism

```text
Motor neuron degeneration
→ LMN signs: weakness, atrophy, fasciculations, denervation on EMG
→ UMN signs: spasticity, brisk reflexes, pathologic reflexes, slowed coordinated movement
→ progressive regional spread
→ ALS suspicion rises when UMN + LMN patterns cannot be explained by a treatable mimic
```

## Observable Patterns

- Focal painless weakness without sensory complaints is suspicious for ALS。
- ALS onset roughly divides into bulbar、upper limb、lower limb regions。
- Limb weakness often starts asymmetrically and distally。
- Bulbar involvement may be underreported unless specifically asked。
- Fasciculations alone are nonspecific；with weakness and EMG denervation they become much more concerning。
- Normal reflex in an atrophic limb can still be pathologically brisk in context。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- EDX should be performed in nearly all suspected ALS cases to identify LMN involvement and exclude mimics。
- NCS are needed to evaluate MMN, neuropathy, radiculopathy, and other peripheral mimics；needle EMG is central for LMN loss。
- Conduction block outside entrapment sites should raise MMN concern。
- RNS / SFEMG may be needed when fatigability or bulbar-predominant presentation raises NMJ disorder concern。
- Genetic counseling / testing should be considered in newly diagnosed ALS, especially with ALS / FTD family history, but the source notes evolving use even without family history。

## Fact

- Gold Coast criteria include progressive motor impairment after normal motor function, UMN and LMN dysfunction in at least one body region or LMN dysfunction in at least two body regions, and exclusion of mimics。
- EMG evidence of LMN dysfunction must include chronic neurogenic change plus ongoing denervation。
- To classify a limb region as LMN-involved, two muscles innervated by different roots and peripheral nerves must be abnormal。
- No laboratory or imaging test can make ALS diagnosis in isolation。
- In a patient with clinical ALS features, no diagnostic test can rule out ALS。
- CK may be elevated in ALS, typically less than 10 times the upper limit of normal in the source。
- Anti-GM1 antibody can support MMN but can also be positive in up to 10% of ALS patients in cited data。
- False-positive ALS diagnosis is estimated at 5-10% in the source；about half had treatable diagnoses, usually MMN or cervical spondylotic myelopathy。

## Inference

- A diagnosis of ALS should be communicated only after sufficient confidence that treatable mimics are not being missed。
- If weakness and denervation extend beyond the level expected from a cervical lesion, incidental cervical spondylosis should not close the case。

## Assumption

- Clinician has enough neuromuscular expertise or referral access to interpret mixed UMN / LMN findings。

## Uncertainty

- Early focal ALS may not yet show enough regional spread for diagnostic confidence。
- Objective UMN biomarkers remain an area needing improvement。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRI 有 stenosis 就不是 ALS。」正確是 stenosis 很常見，需看 sensory signs、myotomal pattern、UMN/LMN distribution 與 EDX。
- 誤讀：「fasciculation 就是 ALS。」正確是 isolated fasciculation 通常 nonspecific。
- 誤讀：「EMG 正常就排除 ALS。」正確是 early focal disease 可能尚未顯示足夠分布；需 clinical follow-up。
