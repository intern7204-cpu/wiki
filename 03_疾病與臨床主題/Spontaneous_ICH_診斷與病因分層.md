# Spontaneous ICH 診斷與病因分層

## One-Sentence Definition

Spontaneous ICH 診斷與病因分層是 adult atraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage 的 initial diagnostic framework：用 CT/MRI mandatory confirmation 排除 ischemic stroke mimic，接著用 location、volume、IVH/edema/herniation、labs、CTA/MRI etiology clues 與 expansion-risk markers 決定急性監測、治療強度與後續影像路徑。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 **spontaneous atraumatic ICH 的 diagnosis、clinical localization、pathogenesis / etiology clues、hematoma volume and expansion-risk imaging**。

不處理：

- Acute treatment protocol：見 [[Spontaneous_ICH_急性抗擴大與Neurocritical_Stabilization]]。
- Secondary prevention / recurrence prevention。
- SAH、traumatic ICH、pediatric hemorrhagic stroke、newborn stroke。
- CAA、AVM、RCVS、[[Moyamoya_臨床辨識與診斷影像|moyamoya]]、cerebral venous thrombosis 的完整專題。

## Why It Matters

ICH 的 early decision 不只是「有沒有出血」。影像和病因分層會決定：

- 是否要立刻做 anticoagulation reversal。
- BP lowering 要多密集。
- 是否需要 ICU / neurosurgery / ventriculostomy / posterior fossa vigilance。
- Deterioration 是 expansion、edema、hydrocephalus、seizure、metabolic problem 還是 new ischemia。
- 後續是否要查 vascular lesion、tumor、venous thrombosis、CAA 或其他 recurrence risk。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult suspected spontaneous atraumatic ICH。
- Acute focal neurologic deficit、progressive neurologic worsening、severe headache/vomiting、severe hypertension 或 altered consciousness 可提高 suspicion。
- 但 clinical suspicion 不能取代 imaging；CT or MRI is mandatory。

## Mechanism

```text
Small-vessel disease, CAA, vascular lesion, venous thrombosis, coagulopathy, tumor, or hemorrhagic transformation
→ intracerebral blood collection
→ hematoma expansion + perihematomal edema
→ mass effect, ICP increase, hydrocephalus, herniation risk
→ neurologic signs depend on location and size
→ CT/MRI confirms hemorrhage and maps severity
→ CTA/MRI/labs identify etiology and expansion-risk clues
→ acute monitoring/treatment and follow-up imaging are selected
```

### Pathogenesis and Etiology Clues

- Primary mechanical injury comes from hematoma expansion and perihematomal edema。
- Secondary brain injury follows BBB breakdown with excitotoxic / inflammatory processes；mechanism remains uncertain。
- Hypertensive vasculopathy commonly affects penetrating artery territories：putamen、thalamus、pons/midbrain、basal ganglia、cerebellar nuclei。
- CAA weakens small- to medium-sized cortical/leptomeningeal vessels and is an important cause of lobar ICH in older adults。
- CMB distribution helps: deep CMBs favor hypertensive vasculopathy；lobar CMBs favor CAA。
- Less common causes include AVM/cavernous malformation、cerebral venous thrombosis、hemorrhagic infarction、RCVS、tumor、CNS infection、[[Moyamoya_臨床辨識與診斷影像|moyamoya]]、vasculitis、coagulopathy and sympathomimetic drug exposure。

### Clinical Localization

- Progressive worsening over minutes to hours supports ICH but is not diagnostic。
- Headache, vomiting and decreased consciousness occur more often with large hemorrhage; headache may be absent。
- Putaminal hemorrhage: hemiplegia、hemisensory loss、homonymous hemianopsia、gaze palsy。
- Caudate hemorrhage: acute confusion、personality / memory change、transient contralateral weakness or numbness。
- Internal capsule hemorrhage: dysarthria、contralateral hemiparesis and sensory deficit。
- Cerebellar hemorrhage: inability to walk due to imbalance、vomiting、occipital headache；often no hemiparesis；can deteriorate through obstructive hydrocephalus or brainstem compression。
- Thalamic hemorrhage: hemiparesis、hemisensory loss、gaze/pupil abnormalities；dominant side may cause aphasia, nondominant side may cause neglect。
- Lobar hemorrhage: signs vary by lobe；parietal/occipital common；seizures more common because cortical tissue is involved。
- Pontine hemorrhage: rapid deep coma, bilateral paralysis, pinpoint pupils and horizontal gaze impairment。

### Imaging and Volume

- Noncontrast head CT accurately identifies acute ICH and separates it from ischemic stroke。
- MRI can diagnose acute ICH with high sensitivity in experienced hands and is more accurate for chronic hemorrhage, but emergency availability and contraindications limit use。
- CTA can be paired with CT to identify underlying vascular cause and expansion propensity。
- ABC/2 estimates ICH volume:
  - A: greatest hemorrhage diameter。
  - B: largest diameter perpendicular to A。
  - C: number of slices with hemorrhage adjusted by slice thickness。
  - ABC/2 = approximate volume in cm3。

### Expansion Risk and Follow-Up Imaging

- Hemorrhage enlargement is associated with neurologic deterioration and worse outcomes。
- Major predictors include shorter time from onset to initial imaging, larger ICH volume, antiplatelet/anticoagulant use, and contrast extravasation such as CTA spot sign。
- Spot sign is contrast enhancement within hematoma on CTA source images and is associated with expansion and poor outcome。
- Intraventricular extension is common and associated with neurologic complications and worse outcome。
- Follow-up imaging is warranted when clinical deterioration suggests expansion or rebleeding。
- Follow-up imaging is also needed when initial clinical/imaging findings suggest an underlying cause or when hematoma/edema may hide structural pathology。
- MRI with contrast is preferred for many underlying-cause evaluations; CT with contrast is less sensitive when MRI cannot be done；CTA/MRA/DSA are used when vascular lesion is suspected。

## Observable Patterns

- Clinical suspicion pattern: acute focal deficit plus minutes-to-hours progression, severe hypertension, headache/vomiting or reduced consciousness。
- Deep ICH pattern: longstanding hypertension, putamen/thalamus/pons/cerebellar nuclei, deep CMBs。
- Lobar ICH pattern: older adult, cortical/subcortical hemorrhage, lobar CMBs or superficial siderosis, seizure tendency。
- Posterior fossa danger pattern: cerebellar hemorrhage with gait inability/vomiting/occipital headache and no hemiparesis。
- Expansion-risk pattern: early imaging, larger volume, antithrombotic exposure, CTA spot sign, IVH or marked heterogeneity/irregularity。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- ED stroke workflow should treat ICH imaging as diagnostic plus triage information, not just a yes/no exclusion before ischemic reperfusion。
- Rehab teams should not assume a fixed deficit early after ICH; expansion, edema, hydrocephalus and seizure can change neurologic status over hours to days。
- Etiology clues guide later recurrence prevention, but recurrence-prevention recommendations require separate source processing。
- Research should separate imaging biomarkers that predict expansion from interventions that improve functional outcome。

## Fact

- 本來源明確指出 CT or MRI is mandatory to confirm ICH and exclude ischemic stroke / mimics。
- 本來源明確指出 clinical characteristics alone cannot distinguish brain hemorrhage and ischemia。
- 本來源指出 CT/MRI acute imaging also provides severity, expansion risk and underlying cause information。
- 本來源指出 ICH expansion predictors include shorter onset-to-imaging time, ICH volume, antiplatelet/anticoagulant use and CTA spot sign。
- 本來源指出 seizures in the first days after ICH occur in approximately 15% and are more common in lobar hemorrhage。

## Inference

- ICH diagnosis should be documented as a structured imaging interpretation: location, volume, IVH, edema, herniation, vascular imaging, expansion-risk markers and suspected etiology。
- Deep vs lobar location is a useful first branch but should not be treated as definitive etiology without imaging/lab context。
- A patient with unexplained encephalopathy after ICH location/size is accounted for should trigger seizure / NCSE consideration and EEG, not just be attributed to “stroke severity”。

## Assumption

- 本頁使用 adult spontaneous ICH as default。
- ABC/2 is used as an approximation for adult parenchymal hematoma severity, not exact volumetry。
- Source assumes access to emergency neuroimaging and basic laboratory testing。

## Uncertainty

- BBB breakdown and secondary inflammatory injury mechanisms remain incompletely defined。
- DWI lesions remote from the hematoma occur in primary ICH, but mechanism and prognostic meaning remain undefined。
- Spot sign and CT markers predict expansion, but exact treatment escalation thresholds depend on institution and treatment evidence。
- MRI is diagnostically strong but may be impractical in unstable emergency patients。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not use absence of headache to rule out ICH。
- Do not assume every lobar ICH is CAA or every deep ICH is hypertension。
- Do not treat ABC/2 as definitive prognosis or a withdrawal-of-care tool。
- Do not delay acute stabilization while pursuing complete etiology workup；diagnosis and acute interventions may need to proceed in parallel。
