# 急性醫療虛弱與 Early Mobilization

## One-Sentence Definition

急性醫療虛弱與 early mobilization 是在 hemodynamic、ventilatory、renal、delirium 與 frailty 風險監測下，主動預防 hospitalization-related deconditioning 的復健策略。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../10_來源摘要/Acute_Medical_Conditions_Cardiopulmonary_Disease_Medical_Frailty_and_Renal_Failure]] 建立 acute medical rehab frame。
- 本頁不提供 ICU mobilization numeric stop criteria 或 dialysis prescription；需依 local protocol。

## Why It Matters

- Bedrest 不是中性處置；它會造成 muscle loss、orthostasis、DVT risk、atelectasis、delirium、insulin resistance、functional decline。
- Frail patient reserve low，若不主動保護 mobility，出院後 disability risk 會被放大。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需明確設定 vital sign、oxygen / ventilator、orthostasis、arrhythmia、ischemia、renal / electrolyte、dialysis timing、delirium、line / device safety、fall risk。
- Hemodynamic instability、uncontrolled ischemia / arrhythmia、unsafe oxygenation、severe electrolyte disturbance、unstable fracture 或 unsafe lines 需先處理。

## Mechanism

```text
Acute illness + bedrest
-> inactivity physiology
-> sarcopenia, deconditioning, orthostasis, delirium, thrombosis, pulmonary complication
-> lower rehab tolerance and discharge function
-> early mobility with monitoring and graded dose
-> preserved reserve and safer transition
```

## Observable Patterns

- Frailty：slow gait、weakness、exhaustion、low activity、weight loss 或 deficit accumulation。
- ICU-acquired weakness：diffuse weakness after critical illness, often compounded by immobility, inflammation, medications, and organ failure.
- Renal failure rehab limitation：uremia、electrolyte shift、anemia、bone disease、post-dialysis fatigue、vascular access precautions。
- Cardiopulmonary limitation：dyspnea、chest pain、palpitation、syncope、edema、fatigue、cough。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Early mobilization must be dose-adjusted, not all-or-none.
- Cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation principles can be embedded even when the admission diagnosis is stroke, amputation, vascular disease, renal failure, or frailty.
- Transition planning should preserve mobility gains across ICU, ward, inpatient rehab, home, and outpatient care.

## Fact

- 來源指出 frailty results in decreased mobility and functional decline and clinicians should prioritize mobility.
- 來源指出 cardiac rehabilitation is evidence-based secondary prevention that reduces morbidity and mortality.
- 來源指出 aerobic prescription includes intensity, duration, frequency, specificity.
- 來源整理 immobility effects and ICU / renal failure rehabilitation considerations.

## Inference

- In acute care, "not ready for rehab" should be replaced by "what level of mobility is safe today under current constraints?"

## Assumption

- Medical team can provide current stability criteria and device / line restrictions.

## Uncertainty

- Exact thresholds for early mobilization depend on ICU protocol, diagnosis, hemodynamics, ventilatory support, and dialysis timing.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「越虛弱越該臥床」不成立。
- 「cardiac rehab 只是運動課」不成立；它是 secondary prevention program。
- 「腎衰竭只要避開透析時間就好」不成立；electrolyte、uremia、anemia、bone、access、BP 都會影響安全。
