# Burn Rehabilitation 總論

## One-Sentence Definition

Burn rehabilitation 是從 acute burn admission 到 long-term survivorship 的 interdisciplinary prevention-and-function program。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../10_來源摘要/Burns]] 建立 burn rehabilitation 主框架。
- 本頁不替代 burn center resuscitation、surgical debridement、grafting 或 wound protocol。

## Why It Matters

- Burn 傷害不只在皮膚；它會造成 pain、edema、scar、contracture、hypermetabolism、dysphagia、neuropathy、cognition、body image、sexuality、return-to-work 與 community reintegration 問題。
- 若等到 scar mature 才開始處理，contracture and function loss 可能已經形成。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 必須確認 TBSA、burn depth、location、etiology、inhalation injury、electrical injury、fracture / brain injury、surgery / graft plan、ROM / weight-bearing restrictions、splinting / positioning order、infection risk。

## Mechanism

```text
Thermal / chemical / electrical / radiation burn
-> tissue injury + inflammatory response + surgery / grafting + hypermetabolism
-> pain, edema, weakness, scar, contracture, dysphagia, cognitive / psychological burden
-> reduced ROM, ADL, mobility, work and participation
-> early PM&R + PT/OT/SLP + splinting + positioning + pain/nutrition/exercise/scar care
-> preserved function and reintegration
```

## Observable Patterns

- Deep partial-thickness / full-thickness burn：poor reepithelialization, grafting need, scar / contracture risk.
- Inhalation injury：pulmonary diffusion limitation, respiratory weakness, dysphagia risk.
- Postburn pain：background, episodic, procedural, donor site, anticipatory anxiety, neuropathic component.
- Hypermetabolism：high caloric/protein needs, lean mass loss, prolonged recovery burden.
- Scar / contracture：joint-crossing burns, hypertrophic scarring, ROM loss, splinting need.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Physiatry consult should occur early enough to influence positioning, splinting, ROM, pain, IPR candidacy, and discharge planning.
- Pain control must support therapy participation and should include nonpharmacologic adjuncts.
- Rehabilitation outcomes should extend to body image, sexuality, return to work, and community participation.

## Fact

- 來源指出 burn rehabilitation should begin at admission and continue through inpatient, outpatient, and long-term follow-up.
- 來源指出 burn size and depth define severity and treatment planning.
- 來源列出 burn center transfer criteria.
- 來源指出 inhalation injury is a major predictor of mortality and an independent risk factor for dysphagia.
- 來源指出 burn survivors may have prolonged hypermetabolic state and nutrition needs.

## Inference

- Burn rehab plan should be updated after every surgical / grafting milestone because ROM, splinting, compression, and mobility precautions may change.

## Assumption

- Burn surgical team provides graft status and tissue protection constraints.

## Uncertainty

- Optimal pharmacologic management of neuropathic pain, pruritus, hypermetabolism, and scar varies and requires current burn literature.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「Burn wound 還沒好所以不能復健」不成立。
- 「Full-thickness burn 比 partial-thickness burn 一定更痛」不一定；nerve destruction can reduce surface pain, while deep injury remains severe.
- 「止痛只靠 opioid」不成立；來源支持 multimodal analgesia and nonpharmacologic adjuncts.
