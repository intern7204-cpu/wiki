# Stroke Neuropalliative Care 與 Goals of Care

## One-Sentence Definition

Stroke neuropalliative care 是在 acute 到 postacute stroke care 中，同步處理 symptom burden、functional status、prognostic uncertainty、goals of care、advance care planning、shared decision-making 與 caregiver support 的照護框架。

## Definition and Boundary

### What It Is

- 一個可以從 stroke presentation 開始的 palliative approach。
- 可與 reperfusion therapy、neurocritical care、surgery、rehabilitation 同時存在。
- 重點是減少 suffering、優化 function、讓 treatment intensity 與 patient values 對齊。
- 包括 primary palliative care 與 specialist palliative care：
  - primary：stroke team 具備的基本 symptom management、serious illness communication、care coordination。
  - specialist：refractory symptoms、complex grief/depression/anxiety/existential distress、conflict around goals or potentially inappropriate treatment。

### What It Is Not

- 不是 hospice 的同義詞。
- 不是撤治療的代名詞。
- 不是只在「沒救了」才開始。
- 不是 code status 討論。
- 不是用 prognostic score 直接決定 withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment。

## Why It Matters

Stroke 的 illness trajectory 很特殊：突然發生、病人常無法參與早期決策、家屬需在時間壓力下作 substituted judgment，而 severe stroke 的 outcome 又可能從死亡到可接受的功能恢復都有。若沒有 neuropalliative care frame，臨床容易在兩端出錯：過早撤治療，或在不清楚 patient values 的情況下延長侵入性治療。

## Preconditions or Conditions

### General serious illness triggers

- Age 80 years or older and hospitalized。
- Patient、family 或 caregiver 主動要求 conversation。
- 對 surprise question 回答 yes：若病人在一年內死亡不會令人意外。

### Stroke-specific triggers

- Requiring mechanical ventilation。
- Predicted major functional and/or cognitive deficits requiring long-term assistance。
- Requiring long-term artificial nutrition。

### Higher-need situations

- Marked functional or cognitive challenges。
- Decisions around PEG、tracheostomy、ventilation、decompressive surgery、EVD / shunt、long-term institutional care。
- Family conflict、high caregiver distress、complex depression/anxiety/grief/existential distress。

## Mechanism

```text
Severe stroke
-> sudden crisis + impaired decision capacity + uncertain prognosis
-> surrogate decision-making under time pressure
-> risk of overuse or underuse of life-sustaining treatment
-> early values clarification + transparent prognosis + symptom control
-> time-limited trials when uncertainty is high
-> reassessment as recovery trajectory becomes clearer
-> goal-concordant acute care, rehabilitation, transition or end-of-life care
```

## Observable Patterns

### Acute setting

- Clinician must often discuss serious news before a stable relationship exists.
- Family may be in shock and simultaneously asked to decide about mechanical ventilation、thrombectomy、decompressive surgery、EVD、PEG/tracheostomy or care limitations。
- Goals-of-care conversations should ask who the patient is, what matters, and what outcomes would or would not be acceptable.
- Goals of care are not the same as code status; code status is one downstream decision.

### Prognostic uncertainty

- Clinical uncertainty：physical and cognitive recovery over time is uncertain。
- Affective uncertainty：future self may adapt to disability differently than the current healthy self imagines。
- 2024 neurocritical care guidance cited by the source recommends avoiding premature prognostication after severe stroke。
- Prognostic models may bracket estimates but should not be the primary basis for end-of-life decisions。

### Time-limited trials

- 適用於 recovery potential 或 patient values 尚不清楚，但 treatment may preserve meaningful recovery chance 的情境。
- 必須明確寫出：
  - trial 的 intervention。
  - reassessment time point。
  - outcome markers。
  - who will reassess。
  - transition plan if goals are not met。
- 若 time-limited trial 從 acute hospital 延伸到 postacute setting，handoff 必須保留原始決策理由與重新評估計畫。

### Symptom and caregiver needs

- Poststroke palliative needs 包含 pain、dysphagia、depression、anxiety、caregiver distress，不只 end-of-life symptoms。
- Pain treatment must balance analgesia with sedation、confusion、falls and therapy participation。
- Dysphagia / artificial nutrition decisions should include recovery expectation、aspiration risk、rehab transition constraints and patient values。
- Caregiver needs include physical/emotional/psychological support、information and decision support、instrumental support。

## Clinical / Research Implication

### 臨床應用

- 對 severe stroke，不要等到「決定不治療」才啟動 palliative conversation。
- 若病人需要 ventilation、long-term artificial nutrition 或預期長期嚴重 dependency，應啟動 serious illness conversation。
- 對 ICH / SAH / large ischemic stroke，prognosis discussion 應明確說出「目前知道什麼、還不知道什麼、何時再評估」。
- PEG / tracheostomy 不應只由出院去向決定；要連回 goals of care 與 time-limited trial。
- Caregiver support 是 stroke care 的一部分，尤其是 aphasia、cognitive impairment 或 patient lacks capacity 時。

### 研究應用

- Stroke palliative care intervention 需明確描述 components：communication、symptom management、caregiver support、transition planning、advance care planning。
- Outcome 不應只看 hospice referral；應納入 goal-concordant care、decisional conflict、caregiver burden、symptom burden、transition quality and function。

## Fact

- Source states palliative care can begin at stroke presentation and can be offered concurrently with acute treatment。
- Source states neuropalliative care focuses on symptom management, functional status, goals of care, advance care planning and shared decision-making。
- Source distinguishes palliative care from hospice。
- Source lists stroke-specific serious illness conversation triggers: mechanical ventilation, predicted major functional/cognitive dependency and long-term artificial nutrition。
- Source states most patients who present with stroke do not have applicable advance directives。
- Source states many severe acute stroke patients cannot participate in early treatment decisions。
- Source states 2024 neurocritical care guidelines recommend avoiding premature prognostication after severe stroke。
- Source states time-limited trials can help reduce uncertainty but require a clear follow-up strategy。

## Inference

- Stroke rehabilitation planning should include goals-of-care and caregiver-support status, not only impairment and discharge destination。
- Severe stroke pages should link prognosis caution to communication practice, not only to statistical score limitations。
- A palliative care consult request should be framed as support for complex decisions and symptoms, not as a decision to stop treatment.

## Assumption

- Adult stroke population。
- Care team can hold family meetings and document goals, surrogate decision-maker, time-limited trial endpoints and reassessment plans。
- Local legal / institutional rules govern surrogate decision-making, hospice eligibility and end-of-life orders。

## Uncertainty

- Stroke-specific palliative intervention evidence is less mature than cancer palliative care evidence。
- Hospice eligibility criteria may differ by jurisdiction and payer.
- Prognosis after severe stroke remains vulnerable to bias, withdrawal-of-care effects and individual adaptation uncertainty。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「palliative care = hospice」不成立。
- 「goals of care = DNR」不成立。
- 「家屬說病人以前不能接受失能，所以一定該撤治療」過度簡化；disability paradox and future-self adaptation 必須納入。
- 「prognostic score 高，所以可以直接撤治療」不成立；score 只能輔助估計，不應作為 sole basis。
- 「PEG 是轉院門票」不成立；artificial nutrition 是 preference-sensitive decision。
