# TBI ICF照護連續體與Task Analysis

## One-Sentence Definition

Moderate to severe TBI rehabilitation 應用 ICF 把 health condition、body functions、activity、participation、environmental / personal factors 串成 phase-specific plan，再用 task analysis 決定真正限制功能的 impairment 與需要練習的 whole task。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 moderate to severe TBI 的 rehabilitation workflow。
- 本頁不處理 concussion / mTBI return-to-play 的完整 guideline。
- 本頁不是 neurosurgical ICP protocol，也不取代 acute trauma / ICU guideline。
- 本頁的核心是 evaluation-to-intervention logic，不是列出所有 TBI impairments。

## Why It Matters

- TBI outcome 不是 injury severity 單獨決定，也受 prior function、support system、secondary insults、access to rehabilitation、care timing、care quality 與 long-term support 影響。
- 只治療 impairment 可能無法改變 activity；skill learning 需要 whole-task practice。
- 高功能 TBI 可能在 straight-line gait 或 basic ADL 看似良好，但在 dual-task、community mobility、school / work、family role 或 social interaction 中失敗。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 已確認目前 phase of recovery：coma / VS、MCS、PTCS / RLA IV-VI、postconfusional stage、inpatient rehab、outpatient / community。
- 已確認 medical stability、activity orders、precautions、devices、vital sign / ICP limits、pain、seizure risk、behavior risk、staffing / equipment。
- 已有病人與 caregiver 的生活角色與 discharge / participation target。

## Mechanism

```text
TBI health condition
-> primary / secondary brain injury + complications
-> current phase of consciousness and recovery
-> ICF profile: body functions + activity + participation + environment + personal factors
-> task analysis of priority activity
-> hypothesis: which impairment actually limits the task?
-> phase-specific intervention: safety, arousal, mobility, task practice, dual-task / environmental demands, participation support
-> repeated outcome measurement and plan adjustment
```

## Observable Patterns

- RLA I-II / coma or VS：examination focuses on body functions, joint mobility, tone, stimulus-induced response, positioning tolerance, secondary complication risk。
- RLA III / emerging MCS：begin looking for consistent but limited command-following or reproducible responses。
- RLA IV-VI / agitation-confusion：assessment and treatment are shaped by agitation, confusion, attention, memory, orientation, fluctuation, sleep-wake disturbance, and safety。
- Postconfusional / higher recovery：focus shifts toward activity, participation, dual-task demands, school / work / community reintegration, mood, cognition, endurance, vestibular symptoms, and role-specific practice。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Choose outcome measures by phase and question: CRS-R / RLA / ABS / Moss for consciousness-behavior-attention; FIM / FAM / Barthel for basic function; 6MWT / 10MWT / Berg / CB&M / HiMAT / Walking and Remembering Test for mobility and higher-level demands; DRS / CIQ / QOLIBRI-like participation measures when the question is life role.
- Write goals at the activity / participation level, then document the body-function hypothesis and task practice needed to achieve them.
- Use dual-task and environmental challenge when the target participation requires it; gains should not be assumed to generalize outside trained tasks.

## Fact

- Reina-Guerra organizes moderate to severe TBI rehabilitation using the WHO ICF framework.
- ICF captures function as dynamic interaction between health condition, body structures / functions, activity, participation, environmental factors, and personal factors.
- TBI examination and evaluation should use injury mechanism, initial GCS, injury date, age, medical interventions, duration of unconsciousness, consciousness stage, and recovery progress.
- Family support, education, living circumstances, complications, coexisting disease, and coexisting injuries inform prognosis and discharge planning.
- In coma / VS / RLA I-II, examination is focused on body functions.
- In RLA III, consistency of response to simple instructed tasks may emerge.
- In RLA IV-VI, agitation and confusion guide examination and interventions.
- With improving consciousness, the focus progresses toward activities and participation.
- Task analysis links activity limitation to underlying body-function hypotheses.
- The source explicitly warns that improving abnormal movement components may not improve activity limitation.
- The source explicitly warns that treating impairments does not necessarily teach a skill.
- Whole-task practice is needed for skill learning.
- Across settings, secondary complication prevention includes pneumonia, pressure sores, adaptive shortening, disuse atrophy, HO, joint contractures, and DVT.
- Acute-stage priorities include preservation of life and neural tissue, secondary-complication prevention, early mobility, and arousal / awareness support in DoC.
- Long-term care focuses on community reintegration, return to school / work / family responsibilities, independent function, and lifelong support.

## Inference

- A useful TBI rehab note should show the chain from task problem to impairment hypothesis to intervention, not only list strength, tone, balance, and cognition scores.
- For a patient walking independently but failing in the community, dual-task gait, environmental scanning, vestibular function, attention, fatigue, anxiety, and transportation barriers may be more relevant than isolated gait speed.
- Pediatric TBI needs repeated reassessment because later school and social demands may expose deficits not visible during early physical recovery.

## Assumption

- The clinician can observe real or simulated functional tasks rather than relying only on impairment testing.
- The team can repeat outcome measures over time and adjust goals as the recovery phase changes.
- The plan of care includes caregiver training and environmental context, not only clinic-based exercise.

## Uncertainty

- Optimal dose, intensity, and timing for many interventions after moderate to severe TBI remain incompletely established.
- VR, robotic gait, NBS, and some sensory stimulation approaches have limited or heterogeneous evidence.
- Prognostic models inform risk but cannot fully capture treatment response, family support, rehabilitation access, and individual participation priorities.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：ICF 是表格，不是臨床 reasoning。錯；ICF 是把 impairment、activity、participation 和 context 接起來的決策框架。
- 誤讀 2：task analysis 只是觀察動作好不好看。錯；它的重點是建立哪個 impairment 限制哪個 task 的假說。
- 誤讀 3：高強度訓練一定越早越好。錯；acute TBI 需先看 metabolic vulnerability、medical stability、precautions、vitals、ICP、pain、seizure risk。
- 誤讀 4：兒童 TBI 因 neuroplasticity 所以自然恢復較好。錯；受傷可干擾後續 brain development 與未來 skill acquisition。
