# 下背痛評估總論

## One-Sentence Definition

Low back pain 評估先分 acute / subacute / chronic、red flags、yellow flags、radiculopathy、nonspecific LBP 與 specific pain source，而不是先用 MRI 尋找所有退化。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 LBP 初始臨床分類與 rehabilitation 問題表述。
- 本頁不涵蓋所有 spinal pathology、surgical technique 或 interventional pain procedure。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Low_Back_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- LBP 很常見且多數會改善；不加選擇地影像化會造成 overdiagnosis、nocebo、cost 與不必要手術。
- 慢性失能常由 pain intensity、psychological distress、多部位疼痛、fear avoidance、work context 與 deconditioning 共同維持。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先排除 red flags：cancer、infection、fracture、cauda equina、progressive neurologic deficit、inflammatory disease 等。
- 同時辨識 yellow flags：catastrophizing、fear avoidance、poor sleep、anxiety / depression、poor job satisfaction、compensation context、low recovery expectation。

## Mechanism

```text
Low back pain presentation
→ time course and red-flag triage
→ neurologic pattern and radicular screen
→ yellow-flag and work/function assessment
→ nonspecific vs specific source formulation
→ education, activity, PT, medication, imaging/referral if actionable
```

## Observable Patterns

- Acute LBP：0-4 weeks。
- Subacute LBP：4-12 weeks。
- Chronic LBP：more than 12 weeks。
- Nonspecific LBP：no clear specific diagnosis despite clinical assessment。
- Chronic disabling LBP：often persistent pain plus psychosocial, work, sleep, and activity factors。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 初始 treatment 應強調 education、stay active、return to normal activities、PT、movement therapies、psychological program when persistent symptoms。
- 問病史需包括 belief、fear、expectation、goals、sleep、exercise attitude、work and compensation context。
- 長期 outcome 應追蹤 pain interference、function、work participation、activity tolerance、mood、sleep，而非只看 pain score。

## Fact

- 來源指出 LBP affects at least 80% of people and is a leading cause of disability。
- Acute LBP prognosis is favorable；75-90% recover or return to work within 3-4 weeks。
- Nonspecific LBP accounts for about 85% of care-seeking cases in the source。
- 80-90% of cost comes from roughly 10% of chronic / disabled cases in the source。
- Fear avoidance and catastrophizing predict disability and work absence better than pain or diagnosis in the chapter framing。
- Multifidi / paraspinal atrophy and motor-control changes may persist after pain resolution。

## Inference

- LBP 的核心問題常不是「影像有沒有退化」，而是退化、負荷、motor control、belief、sleep、work demand 與 nervous-system modulation 的交互作用。
- Early reassurance must be accurate: reassuring without red-flag screen is unsafe; imaging everyone is also unsafe.

## Assumption

- Patient has no emergent neurologic deficit or systemic red flag requiring immediate medical escalation。

## Uncertainty

- 本來源是 textbook chapter；特定 guideline recommendations 需用最新 guideline 補強。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Nonspecific LBP 等於沒有問題。」正確是目前沒有可單一定位的 specific pathology，但 pain / disability 仍真實。
- 誤讀：「Yellow flags 表示心理作祟。」正確是 disability risk factors and treatment targets。
- 誤讀：「Stay active 等於不管疼痛硬做。」正確是避免 bedrest，並以 graded、safe、goal-directed activity 進行。
