# Vascular Dementia 臨床表現與評估路徑

## One-Sentence Definition

Suspected vascular dementia 的評估路徑是整合 cognitive trajectory、functional impact、stroke timing、vascular risk factors、neurologic signs、cognitive screening / neuropsychological pattern、MRI evidence 與 differential diagnosis，以判斷 vascular contribution 是否臨床上成立。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 VaD / VCID 的 clinical syndromes、evaluation elements、neuroimaging patterns 與 differential diagnosis。VaD 的 etiologic classification 與 diagnostic sufficiency 詳見 [[Vascular_Dementia_診斷與病因分層]]。

本頁不是 MoCA 教學、Hachinski Ischemic Score 原始量表頁、MRI report template，也不是 dementia medication 或 secondary stroke prevention protocol。

## Why It Matters

VaD 可表現為 poststroke stepwise decline，也可因 covert small vessel disease 呈現 smooth progressive decline 而模仿 AD。相反地，AD、Lewy body disease、NPH 與 late-life depression 也可模仿 VaD 的 executive slowing、gait slowing 或 apathy。單靠一個 cognitive screen 或 MRI finding 容易誤分類。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with cognitive concern, objective impairment or functional decline。
- 需要 baseline cognition / function 與 caregiver history。
- 需要確認 cognitive symptom 與 clinical stroke 的時間關係。
- 若有 stroke sequelae，需把 cognitive functional loss 與 motor、sensory、aphasic limitations 分開判讀。
- 需要 brain imaging，通常 MRI 優先。

## Mechanism

```text
Clinical stroke, covert cerebrovascular disease, cerebral small vessel disease or CAA
-> lesion-location effect + white matter network disruption + mixed pathology
-> executive / processing speed impairment, cortical signs, gait / urinary / affective features, or heterogeneous cognitive profile
-> structured evaluation to decide vascular contribution and exclude / identify coexisting mimics
```

## Observable Patterns

### Poststroke Dementia

- Typical pattern：stepwise cognitive decline following clinically diagnosed stroke。
- Cognitive profile often has prominent executive dysfunction with relative episodic memory sparing, but this is not universal。
- Stroke location can dominate the cognitive profile; anterior thalamic infarction may cause isolated memory impairment and mimic AD。
- Cortical signs such as aphasia or apraxia, and motor / sensory stroke signs, may coexist。
- Course can improve during stroke recovery, then remain static if vascular risk is treated and there are no recurrent strokes。
- Systematic reviews cited by the source found approximately 10 to 30 percent develop new dementia one to five years after stroke。

### VaD Without Recent Stroke

- May be progressive or stepwise without symptomatic stroke history, with imaging evidence of covert cerebrovascular disease。
- Arteriolosclerotic cerebral small vessel disease often causes executive dysfunction and processing-speed impairment。
- VCID patients on average tend to have better verbal learning / recall than AD and worse executive function, but overlap is substantial。
- Associated features may include depression、abulia、apathy、psychosis、pseudobulbar affect、gait slowing / lower-body parkinsonism and urinary frequency。
- CAA-related cognitive impairment can occur without hemorrhagic stroke and often resembles other small vessel disease profiles。

### Evaluation Elements

- History：time course, ADL / IADL impact, stroke history and timing。
- Hachinski Ischemic Score：score 7 or greater indicates vascular contribution is likely in this source。
- Cognitive screening：objective impairment is required; MoCA appears more sensitive than MMSE for VCID, but cutoff interpretation is variable and cannot replace diagnosis。
- Neuropsychological testing：use when severity or pattern remains unclear after screening and neurologic exam。
- Physical examination：look for gait impairment, bradykinesia, rigidity, hyperreflexia and focal signs。
- Laboratory evaluation：electrolytes, creatinine, calcium, liver enzymes, CBC, vitamin B12, TSH; syphilis testing for high-risk travel / endemic exposure context。
- Neuroimaging：structural imaging should be performed; MRI is preferred over CT for small vessel disease and microbleeds unless cost or contraindication prevents MRI。
- STRIVE-type findings：nonlacunar infarcts, lacunes, white matter hyperintensities / hypodensities, microbleeds, superficial siderosis, perivascular spaces and atrophy。
- Vascular cause assessment：history of smoking / diet / obesity, blood pressure, pulse for atrial fibrillation; embolic-appearing silent infarcts may justify rhythm monitoring, and carotid-territory embolic patterns may justify carotid imaging when revascularization is relevant。

### Differential Diagnosis

- AD：typically more prominent episodic memory impairment, but progressive small vessel disease can mimic AD。
- Lewy body disease / Parkinson disease dementia：look for REM sleep behavior disorder, alertness fluctuation and prominent visuospatial dysfunction; vascular parkinsonism tends toward lower-body parkinsonism without resting tremor。
- NPH：cognition + gait + urinary features overlap; classic NPH gait is apraxic, wide-based and magnetic。
- Depression：can cause apathy, psychomotor slowing and executive dysfunction; late-life depression can also be an early manifestation or risk marker rather than a clean alternative diagnosis。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- MoCA, Hachinski score, neuropsychological pattern and MRI should be treated as converging evidence, not standalone diagnostic proof。
- A "vascular" cognitive profile is probabilistic; lesion anatomy and functional trajectory decide plausibility。
- In poststroke patients, document prestroke baseline to avoid mislabeling preexisting dementia as new poststroke dementia。
- MRI reports should be translated into clinically meaningful categories: infarct, lacune, white matter disease, microbleed, superficial siderosis, atrophy and CAA clues。

## Fact

- The essential feature of VaD is disabling cognitive decline clinically attributed to a vascular cause。
- The two main VaD syndromes are poststroke dementia and VaD without recent stroke。
- Poststroke dementia often features executive dysfunction with relative episodic memory sparing, but interindividual variation is wide。
- Multi-infarct / multi-hemorrhage dementia reflects cumulative disruption from multiple brain regions。
- Strategic infarction or hemorrhage can produce dementia from a single lesion。
- Cognitive impairment may improve during stroke recovery。
- New dementia one to five years after stroke occurs in approximately 10 to 30 percent of patients in systematic reviews cited by this source。
- Covert cerebrovascular disease is preferred over "silent" because it is associated with lower cognitive performance and elevated dementia risk。
- Arteriolosclerotic small vessel disease is marked by executive function and processing-speed impairment, but memory and other domains may also be involved。
- MoCA scores of 25 and below are generally considered abnormal in this source, but cutoffs vary and do not substitute for integrated diagnosis。
- MRI is more sensitive than CT for small vessel disease signs and microbleeds。
- Alternative or accompanying diagnoses should be considered because many dementia cases have multiple causes。

## Inference

- Executive dysfunction plus gait slowing should raise suspicion for small vessel disease but still requires imaging and differential diagnosis。
- Smooth progression does not rule out VaD, especially when cerebral small vessel disease is progressive。
- A normal or nonspecific single screening result should not end the evaluation when caregiver history and function suggest decline。

## Assumption

- Cognitive testing is interpretable despite language, education, vision, hearing, aphasia, mood and delirium considerations。
- MRI findings are reviewed with attention to location and burden rather than only counting whether lesions exist。
- This page applies to adults。

## Uncertainty

- Neuropsychological studies distinguishing VaD from AD often lack AD biomarker confirmation。
- Sensitivity / specificity thresholds for many cognitive patterns remain underdeveloped。
- Late-life depression can be mimic, risk marker or early manifestation, so directionality may remain uncertain。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「VaD 一定是 stair-step decline。」來源指出 covert small vessel disease 也可 smooth progressive。
- 誤讀：「VaD 一定保留 memory。」poststroke strategic lesions 或 mixed pathology 可造成 prominent memory impairment。
- 誤讀：「MoCA abnormal 就能診斷 VaD。」MoCA 只是 objective cognitive impairment 的篩檢證據。
- 誤讀：「MRI 比 CT 好，所以一定能找到 treatable cause。」來源指出 MRI 較敏感，但不太可能找到 CT 看不到的 treatable dementia cause。
