# Brainstem Stroke 交叉徵象與 Locked-In

## One-Sentence Definition

Brainstem stroke 常以 cranial nerve deficit 加 long tract sign 呈現；當 bilateral ventral pontine long tract 受損而 reticular activating system preserved 時，可形成 locked-in syndrome。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 brainstem ischemic stroke 的 crossed signs、posterior circulation pattern、locked-in syndrome 與 pseudobulbar / bulbar implication。
- 本頁不處理 reperfusion eligibility、血壓目標或 antithrombotic prescription。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Brainstem_Syndromes]]。

## Why It Matters

- Brainstem stroke 的生命與功能風險可被低估，尤其當病人不能說話或不能動時。
- Locked-in syndrome 若被誤判成 coma，會直接阻斷溝通、決策參與與復健目標設定。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需要評估 consciousness、vertical / horizontal eye movement、blink response、cranial nerve lower motor signs、corticospinal signs、corticobulbar signs、sensory tract、coordination、swallowing 與 respiratory risk。
- 急性情境仍需依 stroke workflow 進行 imaging and vascular evaluation。

## Mechanism

```text
Vertebrobasilar ischemia
-> patchy / rostrocaudal brainstem tract involvement
-> ipsilateral cranial nerve sign + contralateral long tract deficit
-> crossed brainstem syndrome
```

```text
Bilateral basis pontis lesion
-> corticospinal and corticobulbar tract interruption
-> quadriplegia + anarthria / severe speech loss
-> reticular activating system spared
-> preserved consciousness with eye movement / blinking as possible voluntary output
```

## Observable Patterns

- Crossed sign：ipsilateral cranial nerve palsy with contralateral motor / sensory long tract deficit。
- Posterior circulation clues：coma、ataxia、vertigo、INO、unreactive pupils、LMN cranial nerve impairment、skew deviation。
- Locked-in syndrome：quadriplegia、corticobulbar involvement、loss of speech、preserved consciousness、eye movement or blinking as voluntary communication。
- Pseudobulbar palsy / supranuclear bulbar palsy：dysarthria、dysphagia、bilateral facial weakness、extremity weakness、emotional lability。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Communication testing should be attempted using eye movement / blinking when locked-in syndrome is possible.
- Dysphagia screening and instrumental assessment pathway should be considered when bulbar signs are present.
- Rehabilitation planning should separate consciousness, cognition, motor output, communication channel, swallowing safety, respiratory safety, and mobility prognosis.

## Fact

- Vertebrobasilar lesions often do not form neat transverse syndromes in the source.
- Bilateral long tract lesions can cause locked-in syndrome or pseudobulbar palsy depending on distribution.
- The reticular activating system is spared in classic locked-in syndrome.
- Eye movements / blinking may be the only voluntary control in locked-in syndrome.

## Inference

- Severe motor paralysis with preserved alertness demands an explicit communication access plan.
- Brainstem stroke documentation should describe tract and cranial nerve findings rather than rely on a single eponym.

## Assumption

- Apparent unresponsiveness is not interpreted until arousal, awareness, eye movement, and motor output channels are separately assessed.

## Uncertainty

- Prognosis depends on lesion size, level, vascular status, complications, communication access, respiratory status, dysphagia, and rehabilitation intensity; this source does not provide a complete prognostic model.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「不能說話不能動就是 coma」不成立。
- 「Brainstem stroke 一定能套進 classic eponym」不成立。
- 「眼球或眨眼反應只是 reflex」不一定；可能是唯一 voluntary communication channel。
