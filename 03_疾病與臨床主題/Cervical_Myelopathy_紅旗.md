# Cervical Myelopathy 紅旗

## One-Sentence Definition

Cervical myelopathy 是 cervical spinal cord dysfunction；gait disturbance、UMN signs、hand dysfunction、proprioceptive deficit 或 bladder change 代表不能當一般 neck pain 處理。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 cervical myelopathy 的臨床紅旗與轉介邏輯。
- 本頁不是手術術式選擇頁，也不取代 neurosurgery / spine guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Common_Neck_Problems]]。

## Why It Matters

- Myelopathy 可被 neck pain、radicular pain、hand numbness 或 gait issue 掩蓋。
- 延誤辨識可能造成不可逆 spinal cord dysfunction。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Neck pain、upper limb paresthesia、hand clumsiness、falls、gait change 或 bladder symptom 都應觸發 cord screen。
- 需排除 MS、motor neuron disease、vasculitis、neurosyphilis、B12 deficiency、syringomyelia、tumor 等 differential diagnosis。

## Mechanism

```text
Cervical canal stenosis or cord compression
→ spinal cord dysfunction
→ upper motor neuron signs, proprioceptive loss, hand dysfunction, gait disturbance
→ MRI correlation and surgical threshold assessment
```

## Observable Patterns

- Insidious distal limb numbness or paresthesia。
- Weakness, often lower limb more than upper limb。
- Intrinsic hand wasting or dexterity problem。
- Brisk reflexes、Hoffmann sign、Babinski sign。
- Proprioception / vibration deficits, especially lower limbs。
- Bladder disturbance suggests severe cord injury。
- Radicular pain can coexist as myeloradiculopathy。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Myelopathy signs should override routine mechanical neck pain treatment.
- Nonoperative care is limited to mild, static cases without gait disturbance or pathologic reflexes in the source.
- Severe or progressive symptoms, or failed conservative care, require surgical evaluation.

## Fact

- The source describes cervical myelopathy as the most common cervical cord lesion after middle age。
- Onset often occurs after age 50 and has male predominance。
- Axial neck pain can occur in up to 70% in the source。
- Bladder disturbance occurs in about one-third and suggests severe cord injury。
- Clinical level may differ from maximal radiologic compression by 1-2 segments。
- Central canal less than 10 mm supports symptomatic stenosis, but imaging must show objective cord compromise and match symptoms。
- Asymptomatic central stenosis exists, so imaging alone is insufficient。

## Inference

- A patient with neck pain and gait change should not be treated with manipulation or traction before cord pathology is excluded。
- Bladder symptoms in cervical disease are late/high-risk features rather than routine pain-associated complaints。

## Assumption

- Clinician has ability to perform basic UMN exam; if not, referral is necessary before high-force cervical treatment。

## Uncertainty

- The source describes surgical approach broadly; exact anterior vs posterior surgical planning requires spine surgery evaluation and updated evidence。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「只有脖子痛嚴重才會 myelopathy。」正確是 pain severity 不等於 cord risk；gait / UMN / hand / bladder features 更關鍵。
- 誤讀：「MRI 有 stenosis 就一定要手術。」正確是 asymptomatic stenosis exists；症狀與 objective cord changes 才能決定。
- 誤讀：「Myelopathy 可以先做牽引看看。」正確是 myelopathy 是 traction contraindication in the source。
