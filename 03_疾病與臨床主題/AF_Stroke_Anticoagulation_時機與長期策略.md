# AF Stroke Anticoagulation 時機與長期策略

## One-Sentence Definition

AF stroke anticoagulation strategy 是在 acute ischemic stroke / TIA with atrial fibrillation 中，先依 infarct size and hemorrhagic transformation risk 決定何時重啟 anticoagulation，再為長期 prevention 選擇 DOAC、VKA 或 LAA occlusion pathway。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 AF patient 發生 ischemic stroke / TIA 後的 anticoagulation timing and long-term strategy。

不處理：

- AF primary prevention anticoagulation threshold。
- DOAC detailed dosing table。
- full IVT / EVT protocol。
- anticoagulation failure after breakthrough stroke；見 [[AF_Stroke_Anticoagulation_Failure]]。
- AF causality attribution；見 [[AF_Related_Stroke_病因歸屬與診斷評估]]。

## Why It Matters

臨床錯誤常來自把 phase 混在一起：acute infarct 還有 hemorrhagic transformation risk，所以 anticoagulation 常要先暫停；但 AF 的 long-term embolic risk 又很高，所以長期多數必須 anticoagulate。這不是矛盾，而是時間軸問題。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult ischemic stroke / TIA with known or newly detected AF。
- 已完成急性 brain imaging，排除 active intracranial hemorrhage。
- 已評估 infarct size、hemorrhagic transformation、BP control、renal function、drug interactions、valve disease and bleeding risk。

## Mechanism

```text
acute infarct
-> blood-brain barrier injury and fragile infarct bed
-> early anticoagulation can worsen hemorrhagic transformation
```

```text
atrial fibrillation
-> LAA / intracardiac thrombus risk
-> recurrent embolic stroke risk
-> long-term anticoagulation reduces thromboembolism
```

因此決策分成兩層：

- Acute timing：何時從 hemorrhage-risk window 走到 anticoagulation benefit 超過 risk。
- Long-term agent：DOAC vs VKA vs LAA occlusion or alternative when anticoagulation impossible。

## Acute Phase

### Reperfusion eligibility boundary

AF 本身不是 IVT contraindication，但 anticoagulant exposure 會改變 IV thrombolysis eligibility：

- Current VKA use with anticoagulant effect, such as INR >1.7 or PT >15 seconds, is relevant contraindication.
- Recent DOAC use is a contraindication unless no DOAC dose for more than 48 hours with normal kidney function, or relevant labs are normal.
- Evidence of intracranial hemorrhage on neuroimaging excludes thrombolysis.
- Mechanical thrombectomy remains an option for selected LVO patients within appropriate time and imaging windows.

### Acute antithrombotic handling

- For most patients on anticoagulation at stroke onset, anticoagulation is temporarily withheld during the acute ischemic stroke phase.
- Acute antiplatelet therapy may be warranted while anticoagulation is held; source cites early recurrent stroke risk 3 to 5 percent in the first two weeks.
- Early heparin treatment for acute cardioembolic stroke should generally be avoided because studies showed more harm than benefit.

## Restart Timing

| Clinical situation | Source-based timing frame | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Medically stable, small/moderate infarct, no intracranial bleeding | warfarin can be started soon, such as 24 hours after admission | source prefers waiting 48 hours for DOAC because effect is faster |
| Large ischemic stroke, symptomatic hemorrhagic transformation, poorly controlled hypertension | withholding anticoagulation for one week generally recommended | some newer studies suggest selected early DOAC 4 to 7 days may be safe; uncertainty remains |
| Large infarcts in algorithm note | >1/3 MCA territory or >1/2 PCA territory by CT/MRI; less reliably NIHSS >15 | document imaging-based size when possible |
| Active acute intracranial hemorrhage | anticoagulation contraindicated acutely | reversal / ICH-specific management applies |

## Long-Term Strategy

### DOAC preferred in most patients

- For most patients with ischemic stroke or TIA and AF, the source recommends DOAC rather than VKA.
- DOACs prevent recurrent stroke at least as well as VKA in AF trials and have lower major hemorrhage rates in the source summary.
- Long-term anticoagulation is recommended independent of whether AF was detected before or after stroke presentation.

### VKA-specific settings

VKA rather than DOAC is indicated or preferred in selected settings:

- mechanical heart valve of any type.
- clinically significant rheumatic mitral stenosis.
- relevant drug interactions that reduce or increase DOAC exposure.
- selected severe kidney dysfunction where local specialist judgment favors VKA or selected agent choice.

For warfarin in AF, source describes INR 2 to 3 generally, except special mechanical valve scenarios.

### When long-term anticoagulation is not possible

- Prior ICH does not automatically exclude anticoagulation; risk of recurrent ischemic stroke and intracranial bleeding must be balanced.
- If long-term anticoagulation cannot be used but short-term antithrombotic therapy is acceptable, LAA occlusion should be considered.
- LAA occlusion still requires short-term antithrombotic therapy after implantation in the source.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Stroke transfer notes should include anticoagulation status at onset, reason held, planned restart date or reassessment trigger, and intended long-term agent.
- "Fall risk" should not automatically cancel anticoagulation; the source states absolute anticoagulation-attributable intracranial bleeding risk from falls may be small relative to AF stroke risk.
- Trials and registries should report infarct size, hemorrhagic transformation, timing, and DOAC/VKA exposure separately.

## Fact

- The source states anticoagulation is usually temporarily withheld immediately after ischemic stroke due to hemorrhagic transformation risk.
- The source states long-term anticoagulation is started or resumed once hemorrhagic transformation risk has diminished, usually within first days to two weeks.
- The source states infarct size mainly guides restart timing.
- The source states early heparin after acute cardioembolic stroke should generally be avoided.
- The source recommends DOAC rather than VKA for most ischemic stroke / TIA patients with AF.
- The source states VKA is indicated for mechanical valves and clinically significant rheumatic mitral stenosis.
- The source states LAA occlusion should be considered when long-term anticoagulation is not an option but short-term antithrombotic therapy can be tolerated.

## Inference

- The safest anticoagulation decision is a dated plan, not a vague "hold anticoagulation."
- In inpatient rehabilitation, anticoagulation restart is part of medical management and cannot be left as a passive outpatient issue if the patient remains in the restart window.
- DOAC preference should not override mechanical valve, mitral stenosis, renal function or interaction constraints.

## Assumption

- No active intracranial hemorrhage unless explicitly stated.
- Renal function and medication interaction review are available before DOAC selection.
- Local stroke / cardiology / hematology guidance is used for high-risk edge cases.

## Uncertainty

- Best timing for early DOAC after larger nonhemorrhagic infarcts is evolving.
- Prior ICH plus AF remains individualized and may require dedicated ICH / CAA source review.
- LAA occlusion patient selection after stroke requires procedure-specific evidence not fully covered here.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 "AF needs anticoagulation" 誤讀成 acute first hours 應立刻 heparin。
- 不可把 DOAC preference 套到 mechanical valve 或 clinically significant rheumatic mitral stenosis。
- 不可把 fall risk 當成自動禁忌；需比較 embolic stroke risk and bleeding risk。
- 不可在沒有 infarct size / hemorrhagic transformation review 時設定 restart date。
- 不可把 antiplatelet 當成 AF anticoagulation 的等效替代品。
