# Ischemic Stroke Cell Injury Cascade

## One-Sentence Definition

Ischemic stroke cell injury cascade 是 oxygen / glucose deprivation 引發 ATP depletion、ion pump failure、glutamate excitotoxicity、calcium influx、oxidative / nitric oxide injury、mitochondrial failure、inflammation and cell death 的連鎖病理過程。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 cellular and molecular injury sequence。它不建立 routine neuroprotective medication recommendation，也不取代 reperfusion therapy、BP management 或 malignant edema management。

## Why It Matters

Ischemic stroke 不是單純「血管塞住所以細胞死掉」。Flow reduction 先造成 energy failure，再透過 ion gradients、glutamate receptors、mitochondria、free radicals、inflammation and proteases 放大損傷。這解釋為何單一 neuroprotective target 很難穩定轉譯到臨床，也解釋早期恢復 perfusion 為何仍是核心。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Brain tissue oxygen and glucose delivery insufficient。
- ATP production declines。
- Membrane ion gradients and synaptic neurotransmitter regulation begin to fail。

## Mechanism

```text
oxygen / glucose deprivation
-> ATP depletion
-> sodium, potassium and calcium gradient failure
-> electrical failure and glutamate release
-> NMDA / AMPA / metabotropic receptor activation
-> calcium and sodium influx
-> water influx, mitochondrial failure, nitric oxide and ROS injury
-> DNA / membrane / protein damage
-> inflammation and protease activation
-> necrosis or apoptosis depending on energy state and duration
```

### Necrosis Versus Apoptosis

```text
severe ATP depletion in core
-> apoptosis cannot proceed efficiently
-> necrosis predominates
```

```text
partial ATP preservation in penumbra
-> apoptosis pathways can operate
-> prolonged ischemia eventually depletes ATP
-> necrosis proportion increases
```

## Observable Patterns

- ATP depletion is accompanied by lactate accumulation and acidosis.
- Sodium influx draws water into cells and contributes to edema.
- Astrocyte glutamate uptake can reverse, increasing extracellular glutamate.
- NMDA receptor activation can increase nitric oxide production.
- Reactive oxygen species and peroxynitrite injure DNA, membranes and mitochondria.
- Necrotic debris activates inflammation; inflammation may both support flow and worsen calcium / cytokine-mediated injury.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Reperfusion remains central because it attacks the upstream energy-delivery problem.
- Neuroprotection research must account for redundant pathways and timing.
- Clinical notes should avoid implying that excitotoxicity alone explains all ischemic injury.

## Fact

- The source states ischemia initiates ATP depletion, ion concentration changes, lactate increase, acidosis, oxygen free radicals, intracellular water accumulation and proteolytic activation.
- The source states ischemic electrical failure increases glutamate release.
- The source identifies NMDA receptors as the primary glutamate receptor subtype involved in ischemic damage, with AMPA and metabotropic receptors also thought to contribute.
- The source states sodium influx contributes to edema and reverses astrocyte glutamate uptake, increasing glutamate release.
- The source states nitric oxide can be beneficial at physiologic levels but neuronal and inducible nitric oxide synthase may produce injurious levels.
- The source states reactive oxygen species increase during ischemia and can damage cellular components.
- The source states cell death after cerebral ischemia can occur by necrosis or apoptosis.
- The source states low ATP in core infarct is insufficient to support apoptosis, while penumbra ATP can support apoptosis until prolonged ischemia depletes ATP.

## Inference

- The injury cascade supports early reperfusion and physiologic stabilization over late single-target molecular rescue.
- Penumbra is vulnerable because it retains enough energy for regulated death pathways but not enough stability for indefinite survival.

## Assumption

- Cellular mechanisms summarized here combine animal, cell and limited human pathology data as reviewed by UpToDate.

## Uncertainty

- The contribution of apoptosis in human stroke is less certain than in experimental models.
- The source does not identify a proven routine neuroprotective therapy based on this cascade.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 glutamate excitotoxicity 當作唯一機制。
- 不可把 apoptosis 誤解成一定比較可逆。
- 不可用本頁支持未證實的 neuroprotective treatment。
- 不可把 cellular mechanism 直接外推成 bedside prognosis without imaging and clinical course.
