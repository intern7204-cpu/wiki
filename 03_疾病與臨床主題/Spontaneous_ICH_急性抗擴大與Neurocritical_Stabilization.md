# Spontaneous ICH 急性抗擴大與 Neurocritical Stabilization

## One-Sentence Definition

Spontaneous ICH 急性抗擴大與 neurocritical stabilization 是 adult spontaneous atraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage 發生後，最初數小時至數天內以 hemostasis、BP control、ICP prevention、surgical triage、seizure treatment only when present 與 complication prevention 降低 hematoma expansion 與 secondary brain injury 的流程。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 **spontaneous atraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage 的 acute treatment and prognosis boundary**。

不處理：

- ICH pathogenesis / clinical features / diagnosis：見 [[Spontaneous_ICH_診斷與病因分層]]。
- Secondary prevention 與 long-term prognosis。
- Subarachnoid hemorrhage。
- Pediatric hemorrhagic stroke / newborn stroke。
- Traumatic intracranial hemorrhage。

secondary prevention、SAH、pediatric / newborn stroke 與 traumatic ICH 仍需另行單一來源 ingest；本頁的 treatment/prognosis 內容只連回 [[10_來源摘要/UpToDate_spontaneous_intracerebral_hemorrhage_acute_treatment_prognosis]]。

## Why It Matters

ICH 不是 ischemic stroke 的「另一種 presentation」而已，而是相反的 first-hours physiology：

- Ischemic stroke：目標是 reperfusion 與維持 penumbra perfusion。
- Spontaneous ICH：目標是止血、避免 hematoma expansion、避免 ICP-related secondary injury。

因此 [[中風急性期處置與時間窗]] 必須先用 noncontrast CT 把 ICH 與 ischemic stroke 分開；否則 BP、antithrombotic、mobilization 與 prognosis decision 都可能套錯。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult spontaneous atraumatic intracerebral hemorrhage。
- 已以 CT / MRI 排除或確認 acute hemorrhage pattern。
- 可取得 ICU / stroke unit monitoring、IV antihypertensive therapy、reversal product 與 neurosurgical consultation。
- 若在資源不足醫院，先 stabilization，再考慮轉往有 neurology / neurosurgery / neuroradiology / critical care expertise 的 tertiary care。

## Mechanism

```text
Spontaneous ICH
→ active bleeding + hematoma expansion risk
→ antithrombotic effect and uncontrolled BP delay hemostasis
→ stop antithrombotics + reverse anticoagulation + controlled BP lowering
→ lower expansion risk without excessive hypoperfusion
→ hematoma mass effect, edema, hydrocephalus and rebleeding can raise ICP
→ HOB/venous outflow/isotonic fluids/Na target + serial neuro exams/imaging
→ selected neurosurgery, CSF drainage or osmotic therapy when indicated
→ safer stabilization and delayed prognosis
```

### Step 1: Disposition and Initial Care

- Admit to ICU or dedicated stroke unit with neurology, neurosurgery, neuroradiology and critical care expertise when possible。
- Acute ICH deterioration risk is highest in the first several days because of hematoma expansion, elevated ICP, hydrocephalus, seizure and herniation。
- Source supports initial aggressive care for most acute ICH patients and delaying new care limitations / prognostication for at least the first day。
- Exception: preexisting DNAR or catastrophic ICH with minimal brainstem function。
- 若需要 mechanical ventilation、預期 major functional/cognitive dependency 或 long-term artificial nutrition，應連到 [[Stroke_Neuropalliative_Care與Goals_of_Care]] 啟動 serious illness conversation；這不等於停止 acute ICH treatment。

### Step 2: Stop Bleeding Drivers

- Stop all anticoagulant and antiplatelet drugs initially。
- Reverse anticoagulants immediately and by drug class。
- Warfarin-associated ICH: 4-factor PCC is preferred; IV vitamin K is needed to sustain reversal after PCC / FFP effect wanes。
- Heparin / LMWH-associated ICH: protamine sulfate, with dose based on drug, dose and timing。
- Severe coagulation factor deficiency or severe thrombocytopenia: appropriate factor replacement or platelet transfusion。
- Most antiplatelet-associated ICH: no routine platelet transfusion; selected emergency surgery is the main exception。
- rFVIIa and tranexamic acid are not routine outside clinical trials for noncoagulopathic ICH without antithrombotic exposure because functional benefit is unproven and thrombotic risk matters。

### Step 3: BP Control Without Overshoot

Acute ICH BP control is not a single target for everyone.

- Presenting SBP >220 mmHg:
  - rapidly lower to <220 mmHg。
  - if clinically stable, gradually lower over hours to 140-160 mmHg。
  - if neurologic deterioration occurs, reassess antihypertensive intensity and ICP / perfusion risk。
- Presenting SBP 150-220 mmHg:
  - if clinically stable, lower to target 140 mmHg as soon as possible, ideally within the first hour。
- First hours:
  - avoid SBP <130 mmHg because clear death/disability benefit is absent and adverse events such as cerebral hypoperfusion / kidney injury are plausible。

Medication selection:

- Initial SBP >=160 mmHg: source prefers nicardipine because it is rapid and titratable。
- Initial SBP <160 mmHg: labetalol can be useful because it is easier and longer acting。
- Avoid nitroprusside / nitroglycerin if possible because they may increase ICP。

### Step 4: Prevent and Detect Elevated ICP

Preventive measures for most acute ICH patients:

- HOB 30 degrees。
- Mild sedation for agitation, usually RASS 0 to -2。
- Treat fever。
- Position head / neck and devices to facilitate cerebral venous outflow；avoid neck rotation, internal jugular central line placement, tight ties, tight endotracheal tube holders and constrictive dressings。
- Use isotonic fluids such as normal saline；avoid hypotonic fluids。
- Maintain serum sodium >135 mEq/L。

Monitoring:

- Serial neurologic exams, often hourly in high-risk first days。
- Repeat urgent CT if neurologic deterioration or suspected ICP elevation。
- Consider invasive ICP monitoring only in selected deteriorating patients when exam is unreliable, such as low GCS, herniation signs, midline lesions or obstructive hydrocephalus risk。
- Source notes no high-quality data proving ICP monitoring / elevated ICP management improves ICH outcomes; invasive monitors carry infection and bleeding risks。

Severe/progressive ICP elevation:

- Hypertonic saline or mannitol can lower ICP, but outcome benefit in acute ICH is not established。
- Glucocorticoids are not used for most ICH ICP control because benefit is lacking and infection / hyperglycemia risk increases。
- Hyperventilation is a short temporizing maneuver for acute herniation, not durable therapy。

### Step 5: Surgical Triage

Immediate neurosurgical consultation is indicated when imaging / clinical status suggests emergency surgery:

- Cerebellar hemorrhage >3 cm, or cerebellar hemorrhage with acute neurologic deterioration, brainstem compression or hydrocephalus。
- Intraventricular hemorrhage with ventricular enlargement and acute neurologic deterioration。
- Supratentorial hemorrhage with acute deterioration and life-threatening brain compression or hydrocephalus；decision must be individualized。

Surgery evidence is location- and selection-dependent:

- Cerebellar hematoma evacuation is supported in selected high-risk cases because posterior fossa deterioration can be rapid and fatal。
- Supratentorial surgery is generally reserved for selected large lobar hematomas or severe compression / deterioration。
- ENRICH suggested benefit for early minimally invasive removal, especially lobar hematomas；basal ganglia signal was less clear。
- SWITCH suggested decompressive craniectomy for deep ICH may reduce severe disability/death modestly, but good outcome did not clearly increase。

### Step 6: Seizure and Medical Complication Management

- Acute ICH patients are at risk for early seizures and late poststroke seizures。
- If clinical or electrographic seizure occurs, treat promptly with IV antiseizure medication。
- If no seizure, do not start prophylactic antiseizure medication。
- Prevent aspiration and screen swallowing when clinically appropriate。
- Prevent VTE, pressure injury, fever, hypo/hyperglycemia and volume derangement。
- Avoid routine gastric acid suppression unless there is another indication。

## Observable Patterns

- Progressive neurologic worsening, very high SBP and anticoagulant use increase suspicion for ICH over ischemic stroke, but imaging is required。
- Deterioration in the first 48 hours may reflect hematoma expansion, hydrocephalus, edema, seizure or herniation。
- Imaging features such as hematoma volume, intraventricular extension, infratentorial location and expansion-risk markers affect prognosis。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- ED / ICU order sets should separate ischemic permissive hypertension logic from ICH anti-expansion logic。
- Rehabilitation readiness after ICH should check BP stability, ICP risk, swallowing / aspiration risk, seizure status, VTE prevention and neurosurgical restrictions before therapy dose escalation。
- BP trials should be read by achieved BP, speed, baseline SBP and harm signal, not only by assigned target。
- Hemostatic and surgery trials need functional outcomes, not only hematoma growth or mortality。

## Fact

- 本來源明確指出 acute ICH treatment goals are preventing hemorrhage extension, managing ICP and managing neurologic / medical complications。
- 本來源明確指出 all anticoagulant and antiplatelet drugs should be discontinued initially in acute ICH。
- 本來源支持 SBP 150-220 mmHg and clinically stable patients lowering to target 140 mmHg, ideally within the first hour。
- 本來源支持 SBP >220 mmHg patients first rapidly lowering to <220 mmHg, then gradually to 140-160 mmHg if clinically stable。
- 本來源明確不建議 most antiplatelet-associated ICH routine platelet transfusion。
- 本來源明確不建議 no-seizure acute ICH patients prophylactic antiseizure medication。

## Inference

- 「ICH 反過來快降到 130-150」是過度簡化；比較符合本來源的教學句應是「SBP 150-220 目標 140；>220 先 <220 再 140-160；first hours 避免 <130」。
- Acute ICH stabilization 應獨立於 [[Acute_Stroke_Initial_Assessment與Stabilization]]，否則 acute ischemic stroke 的 first-hours workflow 容易被誤套。
- Prognosis discussion 應延後到 first-day stabilization 之後，並納入 withdrawal-of-care bias。

## Assumption

- 本頁目前依 UpToDate 2026 topic review 整理，未直接檢核 AHA/ASA guideline 原文。
- 本頁假設成人 acute care setting；不可直接外推 pediatric stroke、pregnancy、trauma 或 resource-limited prehospital care。

## Uncertainty

- Acute ICH BP lowering 的 optimal achieved BP 仍不完全確定；over-lowering harm 與 expansion prevention 必須平衡。
- Osmotic therapy / ICP monitoring 的 physiologic effect 明確，但 patient-centered outcome benefit 不確定。
- Supratentorial ICH surgery 的 benefit 依 location、technique、timing 與 selection 差異很大。
- Prediction scores 仍受 early care limitation 的 bias 影響。
- Severe stroke prognosis communication 需同時處理 clinical uncertainty 與 future adaptation / disability paradox，prognostic models 只能輔助 bracket outcome range。

## Clinical Use Version

- 「ICH 的 first-hours 目標不是 reperfusion，是 anti-expansion。」
- 「先 CT 分流；ICH 就 stop antithrombotics、reverse anticoagulation、分層降 BP。」
- 「SBP 150-220 目標 140；>220 先 <220 再 140-160；first hours 不要壓到 <130。」
- 「Antiplatelet-associated ICH 不 routine platelet transfusion。」
- 「No seizure, no prophylactic antiseizure medication。」
- 「ICH score 可以估風險，但不要第一天拿來當撤治療理由。」
- 「Palliative care 可以和 ICU anti-expansion care 並行；goals of care 不是 code status。」
