# Cervical Radiculopathy

## One-Sentence Definition

Cervical radiculopathy 是 cervical nerve root 的 neurophysiologic dysfunction；它可伴隨 radicular pain，但 radicular pain alone 不等於 radiculopathy。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 cervical radicular pain / radiculopathy 的辨識、檢查與保守治療框架。
- 本頁不涵蓋 cervical myelopathy、brachial plexopathy、peripheral entrapment 或 surgical technique 的完整內容。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Common_Neck_Problems]]。

## Why It Matters

- 把 pain-only radicular symptoms 當成確定 radiculopathy 會高估 nerve root dysfunction。
- 反過來，忽略 progressive motor deficit 或 myelopathy signs 會延誤 surgical referral。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 必須先做 myelopathy screen：Hoffmann、Babinski、reflexes、gait、hand dexterity、bladder symptoms。
- Severe weakness <3/5 若不符合單一 root，需考慮 multilevel disease、radiculomyelopathy、ALS、plexopathy、entrapment。

## Mechanism

```text
Cervical disc herniation, spondylosis, or inflammation
→ nerve root irritation
→ radicular pain
± neurophysiologic dysfunction
→ myotomal weakness, sensory disturbance, paresthesia, depressed reflex
→ clinical correlation with MRI / EDX
```

## Observable Patterns

- Acute disc herniation 可先有 axial neck pain，後接 explosive upper limb pain。
- Spondylotic radicular pain 較常 gradual。
- Cough、sneeze、Valsalva、extension 可誘發。
- Shoulder abduction relief sign 可支持 radicular pattern。
- Spurling test highly specific but not sensitive。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- MRI 是 gold standard，但 asymptomatic abnormalities common，因此要對齊 history、exam、EDX。
- EDX 可釐清 root vs plexus vs peripheral nerve；也可提供 prognosis。
- Conservative care 包含 education、activity modification、NSAID、selected adjuvants、posture / flexibility / stabilization、cervical / thoracic / scapulothoracic / rotator cuff restoration。
- Progressive neurologic deficit 是 definitive surgical indication in the source。

## Fact

- Radiculopathy requires neurophysiologic dysfunction such as myotomal weakness、paresthesia、sensory disturbance、depressed reflex。
- Common causes include cervical intervertebral disc herniation and spondylosis。
- Inflammation may trigger radicular symptoms；compression alone does not necessarily cause pain。
- AANEM-style EDX logic in the source requires abnormalities in at least two muscles from same root and different peripheral nerves, with adjacent roots normal。
- Six upper limb muscles plus cervical paraspinals identify 94-99% cervical radiculopathies according to the chapter。
- Surgery is considered for intractable pain, severe myotomal deficit, progressive/stable severe deficit, or progression to myelopathy。

## Inference

- Pain distribution alone is insufficient because somatic referred pain can mimic upper limb radicular symptoms。
- If first whiplash-related radicular injection gives no improvement, repeated injections may be less useful per source framing。

## Assumption

- The patient has no cord compression emergency or systemic cause requiring urgent escalation.

## Uncertainty

- Injection outcomes and surgery timing in the source need current guideline / systematic review confirmation before applied to individual care。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Spurling 陰性排除 radiculopathy。」正確是它 sensitivity 不高。
- 誤讀：「MRI 壓到神經就是症狀來源。」正確是 imaging must match symptoms, exam, and EDX when needed。
- 誤讀：「Radicular pain 一定要馬上手術。」正確是 many cases have natural resolution unless deficit progresses or pain is intractable。
