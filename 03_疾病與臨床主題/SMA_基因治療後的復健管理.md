# SMA 基因治療後的復健管理

## One-Sentence Definition

SMA 是 SMN1-related LMN disorder；SMN-restoring therapies 改變 motor trajectory，但 rehabilitation 仍需長期處理 respiratory、nutrition、gastrointestinal、scoliosis、contracture 與 mobility needs。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 Motor Neuron Diseases 來源中的 proximal SMA 與 treatment-era rehab needs。
- 本頁不是 SMA medication prescribing guide，也不涵蓋完整 newborn screening policy。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Motor_Neuron_Diseases]]。

## Why It Matters

- SMA 已從高度致死的 infantile genetic MND，變成需要早期診斷、gene-based therapy 與長期復健追蹤的新族群。
- 治療改善 milestone 不等於照護結束；musculoskeletal、pulmonary、nutrition、orthopedic burden 仍可累積。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需確認 subtype、age of onset、SMN1 testing result、SMN2 copy number、treatment status、motor milestone、respiratory function、feeding safety、scoliosis / contracture、family goals。
- 若 genetic test for homozygous SMN1 deletion 陰性但 phenotype 仍典型，需考慮 compound heterozygote 或非 SMN1-related SMA。

## Mechanism

```text
SMN1 loss or dysfunction
→ reduced functional SMN protein despite retained SMN2 copies
→ LMN dysfunction and proximal weakness
→ early SMN-restoring therapy improves motor outcomes
→ residual weakness and multisystem burden require longitudinal rehab surveillance
```

## Observable Patterns

- Predominantly proximal weakness and hypotonia。
- Reflexes absent or reduced；sensory exam usually normal。
- Type 1：onset before 6 months，without treatment cannot sit independently。
- Type 2：onset 6-18 months，can sit independently but not stand untreated。
- Type 3：onset after 18 months，may stand or walk at least temporarily。
- Type 4：adult onset with relatively mild proximal weakness。
- Fine tremor / polyminimyoclonus、tongue fasciculations、paradoxic abdominal movement can occur。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Genetic diagnosis should be pursued quickly because earlier SMN-restoring treatment gives better motor benefit。
- Rehabilitation still needs tailored PT, ROM, stretching, moderate aerobic activity as tolerated, mobility support, contracture prevention, and scoliosis surveillance。
- Pulmonology relationship should be established at diagnosis because restrictive lung disease and secretion management drive mortality risk。
- Nutrition should avoid both underfeeding and overfeeding while reducing aspiration risk。
- Corset brace does not halt scoliosis progression but may improve seating stability and delay surgery。

## Fact

- Proximal SMA frequency is about 1/11,000 births; carrier frequency about 1/50 in the source。
- Untreated proximal SMA was historically the most common genetic cause of infant death。
- About 95% of proximal SMA cases have homozygous SMN1 deletion；about 5% are compound heterozygotes。
- SMN2 copy number generally inversely correlates with severity but cannot precisely predict individual severity。
- Nusinersen corrects SMN2 mRNA splicing and is administered intrathecally。
- Onasemnogene abeparvovec is a one-time intravenous gene replacement therapy approved in the source for patients under 2 years with biallelic SMN1 mutations。
- Risdiplam is a daily oral SMN2 pre-mRNA splicing modifier approved in the source for SMA age 2 months and older。
- Earlier treatment initiation leads to better stabilization / improvement of motor function。
- Despite disease-modifying treatment, many SMA patients still require medical and rehabilitation management of musculoskeletal, pulmonary, nutritional, and gastrointestinal complications。

## Inference

- SMA outcomes should not be judged only by survival; sitting, standing, ambulation, respiratory support, feeding safety, scoliosis, family burden, and participation matter.
- Treatment-era SMA may require new transition planning as children survive into older developmental stages and adulthood.

## Assumption

- Medication eligibility and safety are managed by neuromuscular specialists; rehabilitation team focuses on function, respiratory / nutrition risk, and equipment needs.

## Uncertainty

- Long-term effectiveness and adult outcome data remain incomplete for newer therapies in the source。
- SMN2 copy number is useful for population-level severity expectation but uncertain for individual prediction。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「有 gene therapy 就不用復健。」正確是來源明確指出多數病人仍需 medical and rehabilitation management。
- 誤讀：「SMN2 copy number 可精準預測個案嚴重度。」正確是只能大致相關，不能精準預測。
- 誤讀：「SMA 只看 motor milestone。」正確是 pulmonary、nutrition、gastrointestinal、scoliosis 與 family goals 同樣重要。
