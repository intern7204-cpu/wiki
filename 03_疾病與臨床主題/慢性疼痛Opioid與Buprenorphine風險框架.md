# 慢性疼痛 Opioid 與 Buprenorphine 風險框架

## One-Sentence Definition

Chronic noncancer pain 的 opioid decision 需同時評估 function benefit、long-term harm、mental health、substance-use risk、taper harm、OUD ambiguity 與 buprenorphine as selected risk-management option。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 Chronic Pain 來源中的 long-term opioid therapy 與 buprenorphine 風險邏輯。
- 本頁不是 opioid prescribing protocol，不提供劑量、轉換比或 taper schedule。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Chronic_Pain]]。

## Why It Matters

- Long-term opioid therapy 可造成多系統風險，而 chronic pain 的長期功能改善證據有限。
- 但 abrupt discontinuation / rapid taper 也可能造成 harm；因此不能簡化成「一律加量」或「一律立刻停」。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 必須評估 diagnosis、pain mechanism、function goal、previous treatments、mental health、SUD history、concurrent CNS depressants、PDMP、toxicology、overdose risk、naloxone need。
- 若有 OUD，detox alone is not recommended；需要 evidence-based OUD medication。

## Mechanism

```text
Chronic pain and opioid exposure
→ analgesia vs tolerance / dependence / opioid-induced hyperalgesia
→ endocrine, respiratory, bowel, bone, immune, cognition, overdose risks
→ risk-benefit review and shared functional goals
→ nonopioid care, careful continuation/taper, or buprenorphine transition in selected cases
```

## Observable Patterns

- Function benefit unclear despite dose escalation。
- Worsening pain sensitivity or diffuse pain suggesting opioid-induced hyperalgesia。
- Sedation, cognitive dysfunction, falls, constipation, endocrine symptoms。
- Concurrent benzodiazepines、alcohol、cannabis / CBD 或 other CNS depressants increase safety concern。
- Withdrawal or tolerance alone should not automatically be counted as OUD when opioids are medically supervised.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Opioid follow-up should track pain interference, function, mood, sleep, adverse effects, misuse behaviors, PDMP, toxicology, and naloxone planning。
- Nonopioid and nonpharmacologic care remains preferred for subacute / chronic pain in the CDC 2022 concepts summarized by the source。
- Buprenorphine may be considered when full agonist opioids are suboptimal or risk is high, but it is not universal first-line chronic pain therapy。

## Fact

- Long-term opioid harms listed in the source include adrenal insufficiency, respiratory depression, sex hormone disruption, osteoporosis, bowel dysfunction, opioid-induced hyperalgesia, immunosuppression, misuse, dependence, and OUD。
- CDC 2022 concepts summarized include nonopioid preference for subacute / chronic pain, immediate-release opioids when starting, lowest effective dose, no abrupt discontinuation / rapid taper unless life-threatening concern, follow-up within 1-4 weeks, PDMP, toxicology, naloxone risk mitigation, benzodiazepine caution, and evidence-based OUD meds。
- Evidence for long-term opioid benefit in chronic low back pain, osteoarthritis, and diabetic polyneuropathic pain is described as very low quality。
- Buprenorphine is a partial agonist with ceiling effect and lower overdose risk。
- Belbuca and Butrans are FDA-approved for chronic pain；Suboxone / Subutex / Sublocade are OUD formulations but may be used off-label for pain in some contexts。

## Inference

- Opioid plan should be function-centered and safety-centered, not pain-number-centered。
- Buprenorphine is most defensible as a selected risk-reduction or complex-pain option, especially when full agonists interact with SUD or CNS depressant risks。

## Assumption

- Prescriber has authority and infrastructure for opioid monitoring; if not, referral to pain / addiction-informed care is necessary。

## Uncertainty

- This source does not define exact taper rates, equianalgesic conversions, or low-dose buprenorphine initiation protocols；those require current prescribing references。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Opioid 有風險，所以應立即停掉。」正確是 abrupt discontinuation / rapid taper can harm unless life-threatening risk exists。
- 誤讀：「Tolerance / withdrawal 就等於 OUD。」正確是 medically supervised opioid therapy 下 tolerance / withdrawal 不應單獨計入 OUD。
- 誤讀：「Buprenorphine 沒風險。」正確是風險較低不等於無風險，仍需 monitoring and interaction review。
