# Brainstem Localization 客觀徵象框架

## One-Sentence Definition

Brainstem localization 是用 cranial nerve level、ocular motor pattern、long tract signs、crossed signs、ataxia、consciousness 與 posterior circulation context 推定 lesion site 的臨床定位方法。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 brainstem lesion localization 的 bedside logic。
- 本頁不處理 acute reperfusion therapy decision。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Brainstem_Syndromes]]。

## Why It Matters

- Brainstem lesion 可快速影響 eye movement、bulbar function、respiration、consciousness、motor tract 與 sensory tract。
- Posterior circulation lesion 可能 patchy、多層次，若硬套單一 eponym 會漏掉重要病灶。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需要可解讀的 cranial nerve、pupil、eye movement、motor、sensory、coordination、speech / swallowing 與 level of consciousness examination。
- 若 mental status 不允許完整 history，客觀 physical findings 更重要。

## Mechanism

```text
Cranial nerve nuclei / fascicles have level-specific brainstem positions
+ long tracts pass through brainstem
-> lesion causes level-specific ipsilateral cranial nerve signs
-> long tract interruption causes contralateral motor / sensory signs
-> crossed signs localize to brainstem
```

## Observable Patterns

- Ipsilateral cranial nerve deficit plus contralateral hemiparesis or hemisensory loss supports brainstem localization.
- Coma、ataxia、vertigo、INO、unreactive pupils、LMN cranial nerve impairment、skew deviation support posterior circulation involvement in the source.
- Multiple findings may not fit a single transverse section because vertebrobasilar ischemia can be rostrocaudal and patchy.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Documentation should state the objective signs and inferred level rather than only naming Wallenberg, Weber, or other eponyms.
- Bulbar signs should trigger dysphagia and airway / aspiration risk thinking.
- Preserved consciousness with severe motor output loss should prompt locked-in syndrome communication testing.

## Fact

- Brainstem long tracts traverse rostrocaudally.
- Cranial nerve nuclei and exiting cranial nerves occupy distinct levels.
- Crossed cranial nerve and long tract deficits are characteristic of brainstem stroke syndromes.
- A prospective series cited in the source found classic eponymic brainstem syndromes in only a small subset of cases.

## Inference

- A lesion with only nonspecific dizziness and no objective brainstem signs is less localizing and needs broader differential diagnosis.
- A clean eponym label is less useful than describing the tract and cranial nerve findings.

## Assumption

- The exam signs are not explained better by peripheral nerve, neuromuscular junction, muscle, vestibular, metabolic, or medication effects.

## Uncertainty

- False localizing signs can occur, especially with brainstem compression.
- Imaging is still required when stroke, tumor, demyelination, infection, or structural lesion is suspected.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「沒有完整 Wallenberg syndrome 就不是 brainstem stroke」不成立。
- 「症狀像暈眩就一定是 peripheral vestibular」不成立；需要找 objective posterior circulation signs。
- 「只要有 cranial nerve sign 就一定在 brainstem」不成立；fascicle、nerve、NMJ、muscle 與 cavernous sinus 皆可能造成 mimic。
