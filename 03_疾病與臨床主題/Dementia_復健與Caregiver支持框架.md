# Dementia 復健與 Caregiver 支持框架

## One-Sentence Definition

Dementia 復健是以 disease stage、personhood、ADL / IADL、mobility、environment、behavior、caregiver capacity 與 quality of life 為核心的 long-term rehabilitation and support process。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 dementia rehabilitation、nonpharmacologic management、caregiver education 與 stage-specific activity design。
- 本頁不處理 antiamyloid therapy eligibility、藥物劑量或最新 FDA / guideline 狀態。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Aging_Dementia_and_Disorders_of_Cognition]]。

## Why It Matters

- Dementia 不是只用 cognitive score 管理；實際照護負擔多來自 IADL / ADL decline、behavior、mobility、fall risk、communication、environment mismatch 與 caregiver strain。
- Caregiver 不是旁觀者；在 activity simplification、cueing、home safety、behavior interpretation 與 exercise carryover 中是 intervention 的一部分。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先界定 dementia stage、baseline function、communication ability、safety risk、behavior trigger、caregiver availability 與 meaningful activity。
- 目標應以 preserved ability、comfort、participation、safety、caregiver feasibility 與 quality of life 設定。

## Mechanism

```text
Progressive neurocognitive disorder
-> memory, planning, judgment, communication, visuospatial, mood, and motor participation decline
-> IADL decline followed by basic ADL dependence and caregiver burden
-> stage-aware cueing, simplification, environmental modification, exercise, OT, and caregiver training
-> preserved participation and safer care within disease limits
```

## Observable Patterns

- Early stage：常因仍可獨立而被忽略；可能出現 memory、planning、word finding、orientation、vision / distance difficulty、falls、mood / emotion change。
- Middle stage：通常最長；症狀更明顯，behavioral issues 開始，日常活動需 assistance；先 IADL decline，後 basic ADL 受影響。
- Late stage：severe physical and cognitive disability，可能無法對話、purposeful movement 或 respond，需 total care。
- FAST stage 5 起需要 clothing selection assistance；stage 6 涉及 dressing、bathing、toileting、incontinence；stage 7 涉及 speech、ambulation、sitting、smiling、head control。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Dementia rehabilitation outcome should include cognition, staging, ADL / IADL, behavior, mobility, fall risk, caregiver burden, and quality of life.
- Nonpharmacologic treatment should be individualized; no single package is universally best.
- Exercise and motor rehabilitation remain relevant because inactivity worsens strength, endurance, flexibility, balance, gait, and fall risk.
- OT strategies include activity simplification, environment modification, adaptive aids, problem-solving, skill training, and caregiver education.

## Fact

- The source describes dementia as progressive decline in cognitive ability, physical function, behavior, and care needs.
- Patient-centered dementia care recommendations include knowing the person, accepting the person’s reality, supporting meaningful engagement, building caring relationships, creating supportive community, and evaluating care practices.
- Nonpharmacologic treatment categories in the source include exercise / motor rehabilitation, cognitive intervention, OT, psychological therapy, stimulation therapy, complementary approaches, and technologies.
- Physical performance measures used in dementia research / practice include 6MWT / 2MWT, 30-second chair stand, 5xSTS, TUG, POMA, FIM, SPPB, gait speed, BBS, Functional Reach, FICSIT, and strength measures.

## Inference

- A caregiver who understands what the person can and cannot do can reduce over-assistance, under-assistance, unsafe task demands, and conflict.
- Stage-specific simplification is more defensible than generic cognitive drills detached from daily life.

## Assumption

- Interventions are adjusted to medical stability, fall risk, communication ability, cultural context, home resources, and caregiver capacity.

## Uncertainty

- The source does not establish a single optimal combination, sequence, or dose of nonpharmacologic intervention for all dementia patients.
- Medication and antiamyloid sections require current verification before being used for treatment planning.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「Dementia progressive，所以復健沒有價值」不成立；目標可轉向 safety、participation、mobility、comfort、caregiver support 與 quality of life。
- 「Caregiver education 是出院衛教而已」不成立；它是 intervention delivery 的一部分。
- 「Behavioral issue 都是病人故意」不成立；需回到 cognition、environment、pain、fatigue、task mismatch 與 communication。
