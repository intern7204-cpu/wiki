# Nociceptive Neuropathic Nociplastic Pain

## One-Sentence Definition

Chronic pain 的 mechanism classification 至少要分 nociceptive pain、neuropathic pain、nociplastic pain，且同一病人可同時有多種機制重疊。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 Chronic Pain 來源中的 pain mechanism language。
- 本頁不是所有疾病的分型診斷標準，也不是 IASP 原始文件摘要。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Chronic_Pain]]。

## Why It Matters

- 不同 pain mechanism 會改變治療重點：tissue loading、nerve lesion management、central modulation、CBT-CP、sleep、exercise、medication。
- 把 nociplastic pain 誤讀成 psychogenic pain 會導致病人被否定，也會錯過 nervous-system modulation targets。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先排除 urgent pathology；mechanism classification 不可取代 red-flag evaluation。
- 同一病人可以 mixed pain；不要硬把所有症狀塞進單一分類。

## Mechanism

```text
Actual/threatened tissue damage → nociceptive pain
Somatosensory lesion/disease → neuropathic pain
Altered nociception without clear tissue damage or somatosensory lesion → nociplastic pain
Mixed drivers → overlapping clinical phenotype
```

## Observable Patterns

- Nociceptive pain：與 tissue load / inflammation / movement 較有關，somatosensory system 基本正常。
- Neuropathic pain：burning、shooting、tingling、allodynia、nerve territory or neurologic sign 可支持。
- Nociplastic pain：pain amplification、widespread symptoms、hypersensitivity、sleep / mood / fatigue burden，可缺乏 clear tissue lesion。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Pain note 應寫成 suspected mechanism mix，而不是只寫 pain location。
- Nociplastic component 強時，education、sleep、CBT-CP、graded activity、central modulation 與 avoidance reduction 更重要。
- Neuropathic component 強時，可考慮 neuropathic medication、neurologic workup 或 small-fiber evaluation。

## Fact

- Chronic pain is pain lasting at least three months and exceeding normal healing in the source。
- Neuropathic pain is due to lesion or disease of the somatosensory nervous system。
- Nociceptive pain reflects actual or threatened non-neural tissue damage and nociceptor activation with normally functioning somatosensory system。
- Nociplastic pain was adopted by IASP in 2017 and refers to altered nociception despite no clear tissue damage / nociceptor activation or somatosensory lesion。
- Overlap among pain mechanisms is possible。

## Inference

- Nociplastic pain validates a physiologic pain process even when standard imaging is unrevealing。
- Mechanism labels are useful only if they lead to different management and monitoring。

## Assumption

- The clinician has enough history, exam, and investigation context to propose mechanism rather than merely label unexplained pain。

## Uncertainty

- The source does not provide a single diagnostic test for nociplastic pain；classification remains clinical and probabilistic。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Nociplastic = psychological。」正確是 altered nociception without clear tissue or somatosensory lesion。
- 誤讀：「Neuropathic pain 一定沿著標準 dermatome。」正確是 phenotype and lesion evidence matter。
- 誤讀：「Mixed pain 表示分類沒用。」正確是混合分類可幫助設定多重治療 targets。
