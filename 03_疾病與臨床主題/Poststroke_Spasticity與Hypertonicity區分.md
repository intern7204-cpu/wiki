# Poststroke Spasticity 與 Hypertonicity 區分

## One-Sentence Definition

Poststroke spasticity 是 passive stretch 下 velocity-dependent reflex overactivity；hypertonicity 是較廣義的 passive movement resistance，可能包含非 reflex 成分。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只處理 Movement Dysfunction in Stroke 來源中的 spasticity / hypertonicity 概念與功能導向治療邏輯。
- 本頁不提供 botulinum toxin、phenol、baclofen、dantrolene 或 intrathecal baclofen 的處方劑量。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Movement_Dysfunction_in_Stroke]]。

## Why It Matters

- 若把所有 poststroke stiffness 都稱為 spasticity，會忽略 weakness、poor selective activation、contracture、pain、edema、joint malalignment、co-contraction 與 peripheral stiffness。
- Tone treatment 若未連到 function，可能改善被動阻力卻不改善 movement control。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 評估需在 passive state 測試 velocity dependence，並分開檢查 active movement、strength、selective control、range、pain、joint alignment、soft tissue length 與 task performance。
- 治療前需定義 functional goal，例如 hygiene、positioning、pain reduction、orthotic fit、gait clearance、hand opening、care burden 或 active motor practice。

## Mechanism

```text
UMN lesion
-> reflex excitability may increase
-> velocity-dependent catch during passive stretch = spasticity
```

```text
Poststroke immobility, malalignment, pain, soft tissue shortening, co-contraction, or reflex overactivity
-> increased resistance to passive movement
-> hypertonicity phenotype
-> requires component-level analysis
```

## Observable Patterns

- Spasticity：passive stretch 時速度越快越明顯的 reflex response。
- Hypertonicity：passive movement resistance，不一定有 velocity dependence。
- Movement dysfunction may occur from severe neurologic weakness even without hypertonicity.
- Atypical movements may reflect unbalanced muscle return, impaired sequencing / timing, excessive effort, and co-contraction rather than spasticity alone.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Modified tone score should not be the only endpoint; function, movement quality, comfort, hygiene, pain, care burden, orthotic fit, and active practice capacity matter.
- BoNT-A is described as temporary selective weakness and should be paired with intensive muscle reeducation and splinting in the source.
- Centrally acting agents may interfere with therapy through drowsiness, weakness, fatigue, or other CNS effects.
- Procedural or pharmacologic tone reduction should be paired with training if the goal is improved motor performance.

## Fact

- The source states the older belief that spasticity causes atypical movement patterns has been refuted.
- The source defines spasticity as velocity-dependent reflex in passive state.
- The source says hypertonicity is often used as a synonym for spasticity but this is incorrect.
- The source describes intrathecal baclofen as a treatment used for severe spasticity.
- The source states dantrolene can cause drowsiness, weakness, fatigue, and serious adverse effects with high-dose long-term use.

## Inference

- Tone reduction without strengthening, task practice, positioning, and movement reeducation may fail to improve active function.
- A patient with stiff-feeling limb but no velocity-dependent catch may need contracture, pain, edema, or alignment management rather than spasticity treatment.

## Assumption

- The examiner can distinguish passive resistance from active guarding, pain response, poor cooperation, or anxiety.

## Uncertainty

- Medication / injection choices, intervals, monitoring, and contraindications require current guideline, labeling, and patient-specific risk assessment.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「硬就是 spasticity」不成立。
- 「spasticity 降了，功能自然會好」不成立。
- 「abnormal movement pattern 都是 spasticity 造成」不成立；來源明確反對這個舊觀念。
