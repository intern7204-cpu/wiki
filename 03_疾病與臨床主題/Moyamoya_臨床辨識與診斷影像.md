# Moyamoya 臨床辨識與診斷影像

## One-Sentence Definition

Moyamoya 臨床辨識與診斷影像，是用 recurrent / triggered TIA、young stroke、unexplained deep ICH / IVH、basal ganglia collateral flow voids、distal ICA stenosis and DSA/MRA criteria，辨識並確認 moyamoya vasculopathy，同時排除 intracranial stenosis mimics。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理什麼情況要懷疑 moyamoya、初始 neuroimaging clues、CTA/MRA/DSA 選擇、diagnostic criteria 與 differential diagnosis。病因分層見 [[Moyamoya_Disease與Moyamoya_Syndrome_病因分層]]；治療、revascularization、antiplatelet、pregnancy 與 prognosis 見 [[Moyamoya_治療決策與Revascularization]]、[[Moyamoya_Antithrombotic與急性支持管理]]。

## Why It Matters

Moyamoya 的 ischemic symptoms 常因 fixed stenosis and impaired perfusion reserve 而反覆出現，且可被 crying、exercise、fever 或 hyperventilation 觸發。若只用一般 TIA / seizure / migraine / hypertension 框架處理，可能漏掉需要 vascular imaging、perfusional assessment and neurosurgical referral consideration 的進行性 vasculopathy。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Child, young adult or adult with ischemic stroke / TIA、deep ICH / IVH、seizure、headache、movement disorder 或 cognitive impairment。
- 特別注意：
  - recurrent same-territory transient deficits。
  - symptoms triggered by exercise, crying, coughing, straining, fever or hyperventilation。
  - ICH in caudate, thalamus or lateral ventricular system without typical hypertension / ICH risk factors。
  - MRI basal ganglia / thalamus flow voids, ivy sign or brush sign。
  - CTA/MRA distal ICA / proximal ACA-MCA stenosis with collateral vessels。

## Mechanism

```text
Distal ICA / proximal circle of Willis stenosis
-> reduced cerebrovascular reserve and dependence on collaterals
-> BP drop, hypocapnia, fever, exercise or emotional triggers can reduce effective perfusion
-> recurrent TIA / ischemic stroke

Fragile collateral vessels or collateral aneurysms
-> deep ICH, IVH or SAH
```

## Observable Patterns

### Clinical Trigger Pattern

- Ischemic stroke or TIA is the most common presentation in both children and adults。
- Hemiparesis、hemisensory loss and speech impairment predominate because anterior circulation is commonly affected。
- Recurrent symptoms suggest fixed regional stenosis。
- Triggered episodes suggest impaired perfusion reserve。
- Hemorrhagic presentation is less common than ischemic presentation but clinically important, accounting for approximately 25 to 30 percent of presentations, mostly in adults。
- Seizures are less than 10 percent of initial presentations but epilepsy can occur as a complication。
- Cognitive impairment can occur from recurrent ischemia; adults may have neurocognitive impairment without clinically apparent stroke。

### Brain Imaging Pattern

- Infarcts often involve cortical/subcortical regions and superficial or deep border-zone regions distal to stenotic vessels。
- ICH typically occurs in basal ganglia, thalamus and/or ventricular system。
- Asymptomatic cerebral microbleeds occur in at least 30 percent of adult MMD patients in cited studies; multiple microbleeds were associated with subsequent ICH in one study。
- Ivy sign：T2-FLAIR or postcontrast T1 subarachnoid serpentine hyperintensities reflecting slow retrograde collateral flow; not specific。
- Brush sign：prominent medullary vein hypointensity on susceptibility-weighted imaging; may reflect impaired reserve but is not specific。
- Multiple punctate or serpentine T2 flow voids in basal ganglia or thalamus are considered virtually diagnostic by this source。

### Vascular Imaging Pattern

- Characteristic findings include distal ICA and proximal ACA / MCA stenosis or occlusion plus basal collateral vessels。
- MRA is preferred initial imaging in children to avoid radiation and contrast exposure。
- CTA is commonly used in acute stroke evaluation and can identify moyamoya findings, although CTA is not specified in the 2021 Japanese MRA diagnostic criteria。
- DSA is the gold standard and is required for unilateral or nondiagnostic noninvasive findings。
- DSA provides surgical planning anatomy but is invasive; neurologic complications occur in approximately 3 per 1000 procedures。
- Vessel wall imaging can help distinguish moyamoya from atherosclerosis: moyamoya may show negative remodeling and concentric enhancement, whereas intracranial atherosclerosis more often lacks outer-diameter reduction and shows focal eccentric enhancement。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- A practical diagnostic pathway is: clinical suspicion -> brain MRI/vascular imaging -> determine whether noninvasive findings meet criteria -> DSA if unilateral, nondiagnostic, early-stage concern or surgical planning -> evaluate MMD vs MMS and mimics。
- Rehab and stroke teams should record triggers and recurrence pattern, because these are diagnostic clues rather than incidental symptoms。
- Screening asymptomatic relatives is not routine; consider only strong family history or condition predisposing to MMS, and even then benefit remains uncertain。

## Fact

- Diagnosis requires characteristic angiographic stenosis affecting distal ICAs or other proximal circle of Willis vessels plus prominent collateral vessels after excluding alternatives。
- DSA diagnostic findings include terminal ICA stenosis / occlusion on one or both sides and arterial-phase moyamoya vessels near the stenosis。
- DSA criteria allow unilateral or bilateral diagnosis。
- MRA diagnosis requires bilateral terminal ICA stenosis / occlusion, narrowed outer diameters of terminal ICAs and proximal MCAs, and collateral vessels in basal ganglia and/or periventricular white matter。
- For unilateral findings or stenosis without apparent outer-vessel narrowing on noninvasive imaging, DSA is required to exclude atherosclerosis and other vasculopathies。
- Differential diagnosis includes intracranial atherosclerosis, arterial dissection, RCVS, focal cerebral arteriopathy and vasculitis。
- In general, asymptomatic individuals are not screened for moyamoya; screening may be reasonable with strong family history, especially Eastern Asian families, or medical conditions predisposing to MMS。

## Inference

- A patient with recurrent limb-shaking or transient speech symptoms triggered by hyperventilation-like states should be evaluated for perfusion-dependent vasculopathy。
- Deep ICH / IVH without hypertension should not be closed as "spontaneous ICH of unclear cause" before vascular imaging is considered。
- Imaging reports should explicitly mention whether collaterals are basal moyamoya vessels, leptomeningeal collaterals or nonspecific vascular hyperintensities。

## Assumption

- Acute stroke stabilization and hemorrhage management are not delayed while definitive vascular diagnosis proceeds。
- Imaging interpretation is performed by clinicians familiar with moyamoya and intracranial vasculopathy mimics。
- The patient can safely undergo contrast or angiography if indicated, or alternatives are chosen。

## Uncertainty

- Noninvasive imaging may miss early-stage disease and very small collaterals。
- Ivy sign and brush sign are supportive but nonspecific。
- The utility of screening imaging in asymptomatic high-risk people is unclear。
- A normal single screening study does not exclude later development, especially in younger people。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRA 沒看到典型 bilateral criteria 就排除 moyamoya。」Noninvasive studies can miss early or unilateral disease；必要時 DSA。
- 誤讀：「deep ICH 一定是 hypertension。」若缺少 typical risk factors 或位置/影像不典型，moyamoya 是 differential。
- 誤讀：「ivy sign / brush sign 是 diagnostic。」兩者不 specific，需看 stenosis + collaterals。
- 誤讀：「hyperventilation EEG 是安全常規。」已知 moyamoya 應 minimize hyperventilation because it may trigger cerebral vasoconstriction。
