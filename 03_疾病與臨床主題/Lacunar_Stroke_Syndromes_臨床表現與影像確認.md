# Lacunar Stroke Syndromes 臨床表現與影像確認

## One-Sentence Definition

Lacunar stroke syndromes 是 acute subcortical neurologic patterns without cortical signs；它們提示 penetrating artery infarct，但 final diagnosis 仍需 CT/MRI topography、DWI timing and standard stroke evaluation。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 clinical lacunar syndromes and imaging confirmation。Lacunar infarct 的病因與 mechanism 邊界見 [[Lacunar_Infarcts_診斷與病因邊界]]。

## Why It Matters

Lacunar syndrome 的 bedside utility 很高，但 positive predictive value 不是 100%。過早把 syndrome 當 final diagnosis，會漏掉 nonlacunar infarct、multiple embolic lesions、parent artery occlusion、small ICH 或舊 lacune unrelated to current symptoms。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult acute stroke / TIA presentation。
- 可評估 cortical signs：aphasia、hemianopia、agnosia、neglect、apraxia。
- 可取得 brain imaging；若 CT nondiagnostic，需考慮 DWI MRI。

## Mechanism

```text
small deep lesion in compact subcortical pathway
-> motor, sensory, cerebellar-ataxic or corticobulbar deficit
-> absence of cortical signs
-> lacunar syndrome probability rises
-> DWI MRI / vascular imaging / cardiac evaluation confirms or redirects mechanism
```

## Observable Patterns

### Five Classic Syndromes

| Syndrome | Typical clinical pattern | Common location in source table |
|---|---|---|
| Pure motor hemiparesis | face, arm and leg weakness without sensory or cortical signs | internal capsule, corona radiata, basal pons, medial medulla |
| Pure sensory stroke | unilateral face, arm and leg numbness without motor deficit | thalamus, pontine tegmentum, corona radiata |
| Ataxic hemiparesis | ipsilateral weakness and limb ataxia out of proportion to weakness | internal capsule-corona radiata, basal pons, thalamus |
| Sensorimotor stroke | unilateral face, arm and leg weakness with sensory impairment | thalamocapsular region, sometimes basal pons or lateral medulla |
| Dysarthria-clumsy hand syndrome | facial weakness, dysarthria, dysphagia, mild hand weakness/clumsiness | basal pons, internal capsule, corona radiata |

### Pattern Rules

- Classic lacunar syndromes generally lack cortical signs：aphasia、hemianopia、agnosia、neglect、apraxia。
- Monoplegia, stupor, coma, loss of consciousness and seizures are typically absent.
- Symptoms usually evolve over minutes to hours; stuttering course and several-day evolution can occur.
- Lacunar infarction is the main ischemic stroke subtype associated with worsening motor deficits after hospital admission in this source.
- Pure motor hemiparesis is the most frequent syndrome in most clinical series.
- Capsular warning syndrome may reflect intermittent penetrating branch ischemia from diseased parent vessel.

## Imaging Confirmation

- Noncontrast CT is usually first-line in acute stroke, but CT sensitivity for small acute lacunar infarcts is low, about 30-44 percent in prospective studies.
- In the hyperacute phase under six hours, CT sensitivity is likely even lower; a visible lacune in that window may be chronic and unrelated.
- DWI MRI is more sensitive, can differentiate acute from chronic lacunes, and can identify multiple acute lesions.
- The source obtains brain MRI with DWI and conventional MRI when CT is nondiagnostic for clinically diagnosed lacunar infarction.
- Radiologic diagnosis relies on a small noncortical infarct on CT/MRI whose location matches the clinical syndrome.
- Acute DWI lesion size may overestimate final infarct size by approximately 40 percent at 30 days or more in the cited source.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- ED / ward language should separate "lacunar syndrome" from "confirmed lacunar infarct."
- DWI MRI has high value when CT is negative, multiple old lacunes exist, or current symptoms do not match a visible lesion.
- Clinical trials and registries should not classify lacunar stroke from bedside syndrome alone.

## Fact

- The source lists five classic lacunar syndromes: pure motor hemiparesis, pure sensory stroke, ataxic hemiparesis, sensorimotor stroke and dysarthria-clumsy hand syndrome.
- More than 20 lacunar syndromes have been described, but atypical syndromes have not been studied in large clinical series in the source.
- Lacunar syndromes generally lack cortical symptoms and signs.
- Hyperacute lacunar syndrome recognition may not reflect final diagnosis; one cited study within six hours reported 30 percent positive predictive value by CT.
- Five classic syndromes have been validated as predictive for lacunar infarction on brain imaging.
- One report found nonlacunar infarcts in 21 percent of clinically identified lacunar syndromes.
- Another DWI study found nonlacunar/embolic radiologic patterns in 41 percent of patients presenting with lacunar syndromes.
- CT false negative rate in acute lacunar syndrome ranges from 35-50 percent in the source.
- Multiple chronic subcortical lesions are common in patients presenting with lacunar syndrome.

## Inference

- The more the exam contains cortical signs, impaired consciousness, seizure or monoplegia, the less confidence one should have in classic lacunar syndrome.
- Multiple acute lesions on DWI should shift the diagnostic model toward embolism or another nonlacunar mechanism even if the bedside syndrome looks lacunar.

## Assumption

- A focused neurologic exam is reliable enough to detect cortical signs.
- MRI is feasible when CT does not explain the syndrome.

## Uncertainty

- Some lacunar syndromes overlap with brainstem or posterior circulation syndromes.
- Atypical lacunar syndromes are less well validated.
- Conventional MRI without DWI may not assign lesion age accurately.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 CT negative 解讀成沒有 lacunar infarct。
- 不可把 old CT lacune 自動歸因於 current symptoms。
- 不可把 lacunar syndrome 當作跳過 cardiac/vascular evaluation 的理由。
- 不可把 absence of cortical signs 當作 100% small vessel disease 證明。
