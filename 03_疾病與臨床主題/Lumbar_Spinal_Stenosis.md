# Lumbar Spinal Stenosis

## One-Sentence Definition

Lumbar spinal stenosis 在臨床上最重要的表現是 neurogenic claudication：standing / walking / extension 加重，sitting / flexion 改善，而不是影像上的 canal narrowing alone。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 lumbar spinal stenosis 的臨床辨識與 rehabilitation framework。
- 本頁不涵蓋所有 surgical decompression / fusion indication 或 vascular claudication differential。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Low_Back_Disorders]]。

## Why It Matters

- 許多老年人影像有 stenosis，但不一定有症狀。
- 若只看 canal diameter，會把 mechanical back pain、vascular claudication、hip pathology 或 nonspecific LBP 誤當 stenosis。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先辨識 walking tolerance、standing tolerance、downhill / extension provocation、sitting / flexion relief。
- 若有 progressive / profound neurologic deficit、cauda equina、severe lifestyle impairment，需 surgical evaluation。

## Mechanism

```text
Degenerative canal / foraminal narrowing
→ posture-dependent neural / vascular compromise
→ walking or extension-provoked leg symptoms
→ sitting or flexion relief
→ flexion-biased rehab, aerobic conditioning, pain control, or surgery if severe
```

## Observable Patterns

- Back and bilateral leg pain with walking or standing。
- Worse with downhill walking or lumbar extension。
- Relief with sitting, bending forward, or shopping cart support。
- Walking tolerance may be more useful than pain intensity alone。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Conservative care includes pain control, function improvement, flexion-based stabilization, abdominal / pelvic / gluteal strengthening, anterior hip muscle stretching, and aerobic training。
- ESI may provide short-term pain or walking tolerance benefit, but evidence is weak and steroid addition may have minimal advantage over anesthetic alone in cited evidence。
- Surgery is considered for intractable pain, progressive / profound neurologic deficit, or major lifestyle impairment。

## Fact

- The source states not all narrowing is symptomatic。
- Natural history is often favorable; cited four-year conservative outcomes: 70% unchanged, 15% improved, 15% worsened。
- Neurologic deterioration is rare and delaying surgery did not affect postoperative outcome in the source framing。
- Axial LBP dominant patients have poorer surgical response。
- Laminectomy is common; fusion is reserved for instability, spondylolisthesis, deformity, or recurrent stenosis contexts。
- Surgical complication rate is cited as 10-24% in the chapter。

## Inference

- Rehab goals should prioritize walking capacity, posture strategy, hip / trunk conditioning, and participation rather than changing MRI appearance。
- A flexion-relief pattern helps separate neurogenic claudication from many other LBP syndromes。

## Assumption

- Symptoms and imaging are concordant enough to consider stenosis clinically relevant；if not, alternate diagnoses remain active。

## Uncertainty

- Exact threshold for surgery varies by severity, function, comorbidities, imaging, and patient goals；this source gives a framework, not a complete decision aid。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRI 有 stenosis 就是病因。」正確是 symptoms must match posture-dependent neurogenic claudication。
- 誤讀：「Stenosis 一定會越來越差。」正確是 conservative natural history often stable。
- 誤讀：「Flexion relief 等於只能彎腰活動。」正確是它是 symptom-management clue，rehab 仍要建立 conditioning and function。
