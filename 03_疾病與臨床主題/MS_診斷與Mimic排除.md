# MS 診斷與 Mimic 排除

## One-Sentence Definition

MS diagnosis 需證明 CNS demyelinating disease 在 space 與 time 上 disseminated，並排除 NMOSD、MOGAD、ADEM、transverse myelitis、infection、vascular、autoimmune 與 metabolic mimic。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只整理 Multiple Sclerosis 來源中的 diagnosis / MRI / mimic logic。
- 本頁不是 DMT selection guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Multiple_Sclerosis]]。

## Why It Matters

- MS mimic 可符合 McDonald imaging pattern；若未排除 NMOSD / MOGAD 等疾病，治療方向會錯。
- MRI 不是單純「有 white matter lesions 就是 MS」；臨床 syndrome、DIS / DIT、CSF、evoked potentials 與 no better explanation 必須一起看。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Relapse / attack 需為 typical CNS inflammatory demyelinating event，持續至少 24 小時，且沒有 fever 或 infection。
- 至少一個 attack 應由 neurologic exam、visual evoked potential 或 MRI 支持。

## Mechanism

```text
CNS inflammatory demyelination
→ optic, brainstem, spinal cord, sensory, motor, cognitive, autonomic syndromes
→ clinical attack or MRI lesions
→ dissemination in space + dissemination in time
→ mimic exclusion
→ MS diagnosis
```

## Observable Patterns

- Clinical clues suggestive of MS include relapsing symptoms, partial or complete remissions, Lhermitte sign, internuclear ophthalmoplegia, fatigue, and heat sensitivity。
- Atypical features include steady progression except PPMS, onset before 10 or after 50, predominantly cortical deficits, dementia-like presentation, convulsions, and hyperacute onset within minutes。
- DIS in 2017 criteria：one or more T2 lesions in at least two of periventricular、juxtacortical、cortical、infratentorial、spinal cord。
- DIT：new T2 / gadolinium-enhancing lesion on follow-up MRI or simultaneous enhancing and nonenhancing lesions。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- MRI helps both diagnosis and DMT response monitoring。
- CSF-specific oligoclonal bands can meet DIT when clinical / MRI DIT is not met。
- Visual evoked potentials may help when clinical, radiologic, or CSF data are inconclusive。
- EDSS remains widely used but heavily weights ambulation at higher disability levels。

## Fact

- MS is a chronic inflammatory neurodegenerative CNS disorder。
- CSF oligoclonal bands are described as the most consistent immunologic abnormality in MS。
- T1 black holes correlate with chronic axonal damage and cognitive function。
- Contrast-enhancing lesions generally remain visible for about 4-12 weeks and indicate active disease in the source。
- Gadolinium-enhancing lesions are weakly correlated with disability accumulation。
- MRI is recommended for diagnostic evaluation but clinical criteria alone can suffice in classic cases。
- The source states that if imaging or other tests are negative, extreme caution is needed before making MS diagnosis and alternative diagnoses must be considered。

## Inference

- A patient with nonspecific MRI lesions and atypical symptoms should not be labeled MS until mimic workup is complete。
- Cognitive dysfunction in MS should not be dismissed when MRI suggests cortical atrophy, third ventricle widening, or black holes.

## Assumption

- Evaluation is done by a clinician familiar with demyelinating disease and with access to appropriate MRI sequences and serologic / CSF testing when needed。

## Uncertainty

- Diagnostic and treatment guidelines evolve; this page preserves one textbook chapter’s framing rather than a live guideline.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRI 有多發白質病灶就是 MS。」正確是需 clinical fit、DIS / DIT 與 no better explanation。
- 誤讀：「MS 只能靠 MRI 診斷。」正確是典型兩次 clinical attacks 可達診斷，但仍建議有 imaging access。
- 誤讀：「OCB 陽性就等於 MS。」正確是 OCB 可支持 DIT，但不能取代 mimic exclusion。
