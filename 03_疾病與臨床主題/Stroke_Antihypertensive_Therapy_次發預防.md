# Stroke Antihypertensive Therapy 次發預防

## One-Sentence Definition

Stroke antihypertensive therapy 次發預防是 stroke / TIA 後依 phase、stroke type、neurologic stability、ASCVD risk and tolerability 決定何時恢復/啟動降血壓藥、選什麼 regimen、以多快速度 titrate 到個別化 BP target。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 stroke / TIA 後 antihypertensive therapy 的 secondary prevention 邏輯。它不處理 acute reperfusion BP protocol、ICH first-hours anti-expansion protocol、SAH aneurysm securement 前後 BP protocol、hypertension guideline 全文或 vascular dementia cognition treatment。

急性 stroke BP 相關頁面見 [[Acute_Stroke_Initial_Assessment與Stabilization]]、[[Spontaneous_ICH_急性抗擴大與Neurocritical_Stabilization]]、[[Aneurysmal_SAH_急性治療與預後管理]]；整體 prevention bundle 見 [[中風次發預防]]。

## Why It Matters

Hypertension 是 recurrent stroke 的核心可修正風險因子；但 stroke 後降壓不是越早越低越好。急性 ischemic stroke 可有 perfusion-dependent tissue，慢性 hypertension 會改變 cerebral autoregulation，因此錯把長期目標套到急性期可能造成 hypoperfusion；反過來，急性期過後仍不恢復 BP control 會留下 recurrence risk。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult stroke or TIA patient。
- 先確認：
  - TIA、acute ischemic stroke、spontaneous ICH or SAH。
  - 是否在急性 reperfusion / anti-expansion / aneurysm management window。
  - Neurologic status 是 stable / improving 還是 fluctuating / progressive deterioration。
  - 是否能 safe swallow oral medications 或有 feeding tube。
  - Baseline BP、是否 long-standing uncontrolled hypertension、是否 previously treated。
  - ASCVD risk、heart failure、diabetes、CKD、age、frailty、postural hypotension、dementia / life expectancy、side effects。

## Mechanism

```text
Chronic hypertension
-> cerebral and systemic vascular injury
-> higher recurrent ischemic / hemorrhagic stroke and vascular event risk
-> long-term BP control lowers recurrence risk

Chronic hypertension + acute stroke
-> autoregulation curve shifts and/or acute tissue may be perfusion-dependent
-> abrupt or excessive BP lowering can cause ischemic symptoms
-> timing and speed of BP lowering must follow neurologic stability and stroke phase
```

## Observable Patterns

### Timing

| Clinical situation | Antihypertensive timing from this source |
| --- | --- |
| TIA returned to baseline without infarction evidence | initiate or reinstate oral therapy without delay |
| Acute ischemic stroke, stable/improving and able to receive oral/enteral medication | initiate or reinstate 24-48 hours after onset and during hospitalization |
| Acute ischemic stroke with fluctuating deficits or progressive deterioration | delay until stroke-related deficits stabilize or reach nadir |
| Spontaneous ICH | often IV antihypertensive treatment during acute phase, then transition to oral therapy when appropriate |
| SAH | start secondary prevention antihypertensive therapy when cerebral perfusion pressure is judged adequate |

### Regimen Selection

- If BP is <20/10 mmHg above goal：monotherapy is recommended。
- Initial monotherapy options：ACE inhibitor、ARB、calcium channel blocker or diuretic。
- If BP is >=20/10 mmHg above goal：combination therapy is recommended。
- This source uses angiotensin inhibitor plus long-acting dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker for initial two-drug therapy。
- Beta blockers should not be used solely to prevent recurrent stroke unless another compelling indication exists。

### Target and Speed

- Ischemic stroke / TIA with atherosclerotic disease：more intensive BP goal if tolerated。
- Ischemic stroke from cardioembolism or paradoxical embolus is not automatically evidence of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease；if otherwise lower risk, less intensive goal may be used。
- Spontaneous ICH secondary prevention：more intensive BP goal similar to ischemic stroke due to atherosclerotic disease。
- Recurrent SAH prevention：optimal target unknown；less intensive goal is reasonable if not otherwise high ASCVD risk。
- Known cerebrovascular disease or long-standing uncontrolled hypertension：gradual reduction, around 10 percent per day, unless hypertensive emergency exists。
- AHA/ASA 2021 ischemic stroke/TIA guideline summarized in this source：after first few days, established BP >=130/80 mmHg supports starting therapy; goal <130/80 mmHg is reasonable。
- AHA/ASA 2022 ICH guideline summarized in this source：goal <130/80 mmHg。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Rehab discharge planning should specify BP phase, target, measurement method, titration pace and medication rationale。
- Orthostatic symptoms, neurologic fluctuation and therapy intolerance are treatment-limiting signals, not mere inconvenience。
- Secondary prevention quality metrics should distinguish "antihypertensive prescribed" from "BP target and titration plan documented"。

## Fact

- Antihypertensive therapy reduces recurrent stroke risk among patients with prior stroke or TIA。
- Acute stroke BP management differs from chronic secondary prevention。
- Stable previously treated hypertensive patients should resume therapy after acute phase。
- Stable previously untreated patients with stroke/TIA and established BP above goal should start therapy。
- TIA patients can start or resume oral antihypertensive therapy without delay。
- Stable/improving ischemic stroke patients can start or resume therapy 24-48 hours after onset and during hospitalization。
- Unstable ischemic stroke patients should delay therapy until deficits stabilize or reach nadir。
- ACE inhibitors、ARBs、calcium channel blockers and diuretics are reasonable initial monotherapy options。
- Beta blockers should not be used for recurrent stroke prevention without another compelling indication。
- Gradual BP reduction is recommended in known cerebrovascular disease or long-standing uncontrolled hypertension unless hypertensive emergency exists。

## Inference

- A single discharge statement such as "control BP" is insufficient; a usable plan needs target, timing and titration conditions。
- Vascular cognitive impairment pages should link here for BP secondary prevention, but should still preserve their caution against symptomatic VaD SBP <120 mmHg as cognition treatment。
- Acute stroke order sets and rehab secondary prevention checklists should use different BP language。

## Assumption

- BP measurements are valid and repeated enough to define established BP above goal。
- Stroke mechanism and acute-phase restrictions have already been clarified。
- Contraindications, renal function, electrolyte risk, bradycardia, heart failure, orthostasis and medication interactions are reviewed。

## Uncertainty

- Optimal recurrent SAH BP target is unknown。
- "Gradual" BP reduction has no consensus definition。
- Intensive targets need individualization in frailty, postural hypotension, advanced dementia, limited life expectancy, institutionalization and symptomatic intolerance。
- This UpToDate topic is a clinical review, not a primary guideline.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「stroke 後看到高 BP 就立刻降到 <130/80。」急性 ischemic stroke first 24-48 hours 常有 permissive hypertension 邏輯。
- 誤讀：「出院前 BP 高但病人 stable，可以等門診再說。」本來源支持 stable / improving ischemic stroke patients 在 24-48 小時後住院期啟動或恢復治療。
- 誤讀：「beta blocker 也是降壓藥，所以可拿來防 recurrent stroke。」除非有其他 compelling indication，來源不支持 beta blocker 作 recurrent stroke prevention。
- 誤讀：「cardioembolic stroke 一律等同 ASCVD high-risk target。」來源不把 cardioembolic/paradoxical embolic stroke 本身視為 atherosclerotic disease。
