# Vascular Cognitive Impairment 血管風險與 Antithrombotic 邊界

## One-Sentence Definition

Vascular cognitive impairment / vascular dementia 的治療起點是 cerebrovascular risk modification and recurrent stroke prevention；但 antithrombotic therapy 必須依 clinical stroke/TIA、imaging infarction、stroke subtype 與 bleeding risk 分流，不能因 white matter lesions alone 就開立。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 vascular cognitive impairment / vascular dementia 中的 vascular risk and antithrombotic decision boundary。它不是完整 [[中風次發預防]] guideline，也不是 dementia 藥物頁；診斷與病因分層見 [[Vascular_Dementia_診斷與病因分層]]，認知藥物與預後見 [[Vascular_Dementia_認知藥物與預後限制]]。

## Why It Matters

把 vascular cognitive impairment 當作 dementia medication problem 會漏掉最重要的 recurrent vascular injury prevention；但反過來，把所有 MRI white matter lesions 都當成 aspirin indication，也會造成出血風險與過度治療。這個來源的關鍵是「treat vascular risk aggressively, but do not use antithrombotics without vascular event / infarction context」。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with cognitive impairment plus clinical or radiologic evidence of cerebrovascular pathology。
- 必須先釐清：
  - 是否有 clinical ischemic stroke or TIA。
  - 是否有 imaging evidence of brain infarction。
  - 是否只有 white matter lesions。
  - 是否有 prior intracranial hemorrhage、cortical superficial siderosis、multiple microhemorrhages、major systemic bleeding or aspirin contraindication。
  - 若有 stroke/TIA，stroke subtype 是 noncardioembolic、cardioembolic、lacunar or other mechanism。

## Mechanism

```text
Cerebrovascular disease / impaired cerebral blood flow
-> cognitive impairment spectrum
-> recurrent stroke and vascular injury increase cognitive decline risk
-> vascular risk factor treatment reduces future injury risk
-> antithrombotic therapy only when stroke/TIA or infarction context supports net benefit
```

## Observable Patterns

### Risk Modification Targets

- Hypertension：screen and treat；hypertension is a risk factor, but BP lowering has not been specifically tested as treatment of established VaD。
- Diabetes：glucose control is recommended for prevention of diabetes complications, not because it is proven to reverse VaD。
- Statins：follow secondary stroke prevention if ischemic stroke/TIA history exists；without clinical ischemic stroke/TIA, use cardiovascular risk-based statin indication。
- Recurrent stroke prevention：source generally follows ischemic stroke secondary prevention recommendations in VaD。

### Antithrombotic Boundary

- Prior clinical ischemic stroke/TIA：treat with appropriate antithrombotic according to stroke subtype。
- Noncardioembolic stroke/TIA：antiplatelet has established secondary prevention efficacy。
- Cardioembolic stroke：anticoagulation may be preferred。
- White matter lesions only, without clinical stroke/TIA and without imaging brain infarction：do not use antithrombotics in this source。
- Imaging infarction without clinical stroke history：aspirin 50-100 mg daily is often used despite limited evidence, but may be avoided when bleeding risk is high。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Cognitive diagnosis alone should not determine antithrombotic use。
- MRI report wording matters：white matter lesions and brain infarction are not equivalent treatment triggers。
- In symptomatic VaD, the source does not suggest intensively lowering systolic BP to less than 120 mmHg because cerebral perfusion compromise is a concern。
- Patients with VaD and prior stroke/TIA should still be managed by stroke mechanism, not by dementia label。

## Fact

- Vascular dementia refers to dementia primarily caused by cerebrovascular disease or impaired cerebral blood flow。
- Vascular cognitive impairment includes cognitive disorders in which cerebrovascular disease or impaired cerebral blood flow is a contributing causative factor。
- The source describes VaD as heterogeneous rather than a distinct disease。
- Etiology / diagnosis source states that neuroimaging evidence of cerebrovascular brain injury alone is not sufficient to diagnose vascular dementia。
- Patients with cognitive impairment and clinical or radiologic evidence of cerebrovascular pathology should be screened and treated for vascular risk factors, especially hypertension。
- Recurrent stroke is associated with greater risk of cognitive decline。
- Poststroke dementia is associated with higher mortality。
- Blood pressure lowering strategies have not been specifically tested for treatment of VaD。
- The source does not suggest intensively lowering systolic blood pressure to less than 120 mmHg in symptomatic VaD。
- Patients with VaD and clinical ischemic stroke or TIA history should receive antithrombotic therapy according to stroke subtype。
- If brain imaging shows white matter lesions only, without symptomatic ischemic stroke or imaging brain infarction, the source does not use antithrombotics。
- For imaging evidence of infarction without clinical stroke history, the source often uses aspirin 50-100 mg daily, but evidence is limited and contraindications may lead to avoiding antiplatelet therapy。
- Trials have not found improved cognitive outcomes from antiplatelet therapy in studied populations, possibly due to size or duration limits。

## Inference

- The most defensible clinical habit is to separate "vascular risk management" from "antithrombotic indication"。
- White matter disease should increase urgency of BP and vascular risk review, but not automatically produce an aspirin order。
- Stroke mechanism workup remains relevant even when the presenting concern is cognitive decline。

## Assumption

- Imaging has been reviewed well enough to distinguish white matter lesions, silent infarction, hemorrhage markers, and mixed pathology clues。
- Bleeding risk can be clinically assessed before antithrombotic decisions。
- This page applies to adults。

## Uncertainty

- Whether vascular risk treatment improves established VaD symptoms remains less certain than its role in preventing further vascular events。
- Aspirin for silent infarction is based on limited and indirect evidence。
- Mixed Alzheimer disease pathology complicates outcome interpretation and medication decisions。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「MRI 有 white matter lesions，所以要 aspirin。」來源明確不支持 white matter lesions alone 使用 antithrombotics。
- 誤讀：「VaD 的 BP 越低越好。」來源不建議 symptomatic VaD intensively lowering SBP <120 mmHg。
- 誤讀：「有 dementia 就不用管 stroke subtype。」若有 stroke/TIA，仍依 subtype 做 secondary prevention。
- 誤讀：「抗血小板可治療 cognitive decline。」來源未建立 antiplatelet 對 cognitive outcomes 的直接效益。
