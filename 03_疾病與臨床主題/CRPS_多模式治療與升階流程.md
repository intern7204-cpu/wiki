# CRPS 多模式治療與升階流程

## One-Sentence Definition

Adult [[CRPS_臨床辨識與治療限制|CRPS]] 治療是 rehabilitation-first 的 multidisciplinary escalation framework：先做 patient education、PT/OT、psychosocial assessment 與 individualized low-risk pain control，再依 response、progression、severity 與 chronicity 轉介 pain specialist 與考慮 interventional options。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁處理 adult CRPS 的 treatment escalation logic。它不是 [[CRPS_Budapest_Criteria與Mimic排除]]，不是 pediatric CRPS treatment，也不是 ketamine、sympathetic block、SCS、DRG stimulation 或 opioid 的 procedure-specific protocol。

核心邊界：

- Diagnosis 與 mimic exclusion 已完成。
- Pain control 的目的，是讓病人能參與 [[慢性疼痛復健的主動化治療框架]]。
- Procedure 不是診斷工具，也不是單一 curative treatment。

## Why It Matters

CRPS 常被兩種方向誤處理：一種是只給止痛藥、等 pain 完全消失才開始動；另一種是過早跳到 block、ketamine、SCS 等 procedure。來源的臨床框架相反：先把 education、function、PT/OT、psychosocial barrier 與安全止痛串起來，再對 refractory / progressive case 升階。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Adult patient with clinically diagnosed CRPS。
- Infection、DVT、compartment syndrome、vascular disease、fracture complication、peripheral neuropathy 等 mimic 已適當排除。
- 已評估 affected limb range of motion、edema、pain behavior、functional use、sleep、mood、medication risk 與治療可近性。
- 若有 progressive signs、severe pain、chronic CRPS 或 initial treatment response 不佳，需較早 pain management referral。

## Mechanism

```text
CRPS diagnosis
-> explain pain as neuropathic / central mechanism rather than ongoing local tissue damage
-> reduce fear and avoidance enough to attempt limb use
-> early PT/OT preserves ROM, prevents contracture and restores function
-> pharmacotherapy reduces pain enough to tolerate rehabilitation
-> psychosocial treatment reduces disability-maintaining barriers
-> refractory or progressive disease triggers specialist escalation
```

## Observable Patterns

### Universal Initial Components

- Patient education。
- PT/OT initiated as quickly as practical after diagnosis。
- Psychosocial assessment。
- Symptomatic pain management selected by age、comorbidity、concurrent medication、drug interaction and side effects。

### PT/OT Content Range

來源列出的方法包括 general exercise、strengthening、functional activities、gait retraining、TENS、postural control、pacing / prioritizing / planning、goal setting、relaxation、coping skill、hydrotherapy、sleep hygiene、edema control、vocational support、self-management facilitation and splinting。

Specific CRPS-focused techniques include graded motor imagery、pain-exposure PT / graded exposure、tactile / thermal desensitization、mirror visual feedback、immersive virtual reality、functional movement techniques、stress loading, and contact allodynia re-education。

### Pharmacotherapy Role

Medication is used to enable active rehabilitation. Early CRPS options in the source include:

- NSAID trial, with renal/GI/cardiovascular and older-adult risk adjustment。
- Neuropathic pain adjuvant such as gabapentin、pregabalin、amitriptyline or nortriptyline。
- Short-term bisphosphonate course for early CRPS with pain and abnormal bone scan uptake。
- Topical lidocaine 2-5% or capsaicin cream for selected mild to moderate pain。

### Higher-Risk or Lower-Certainty Options

- Calcitonin：weak/conflicting evidence, low risk, possible option in selected mild/moderate symptoms。
- Oral glucocorticoids：very weak evidence and unfavorable risk/benefit in most cases；chronic CRPS usually does not respond。
- Alpha-adrenergic agents：expert-experience-based option for sympathetically maintained pain；hypotension can limit alpha blockers。
- Ketamine infusion：low- to moderate-quality evidence, nonstandardized dosing/delivery, usually last option after sympathetic blocks and/or traditional neuropathic pain medication；oral ketamine is discouraged by the source。
- Opioids：controversial in chronic noncancer pain, limited high-quality CRPS data, and escalating dose may shift risk above benefit。
- IVIG：later multicenter trial did not show benefit for chronic CRPS pain reduction。

### Interventional Escalation

Pain specialist referral is appropriate for progressive signs, unsatisfactory initial response, severe CRPS, or chronic CRPS。Options include trigger/tender point injection、sympathetic nerve block、SCS / PNS / DRG stimulation、epidural clonidine、sympathectomy、intrathecal baclofen for dystonia, and selected adjunctive modalities。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Treatment plan should record both pain and function endpoints；pain score alone is insufficient。
- If medication reduces pain but limb use and participation do not improve, the plan has failed its main purpose。
- Graded motor imagery and mirror therapy should be described as low-quality evidence options, not guaranteed treatments。
- Bisphosphonate consideration is most source-consistent in early CRPS with abnormal bone scan uptake, not all chronic CRPS。
- Sympathetic block may be therapeutically useful in selected patients but should not be used to confirm diagnosis。
- Neuromodulation should remain an expert-center option after traditional modalities fail。

## Fact

- The source suggests a multidisciplinary approach for CRPS management。
- Treatment goals are restoration of affected-limb function, decreased pain/disability, improved quality of life, and minimized medication toxicity。
- PT/OT are first-line treatments, though relevant studies have methodologic limitations。
- The source suggests therapist referral immediately after diagnosis。
- PT can be performed twice daily at home and ideally begins before movement limitation occurs。
- Graded motor imagery has small randomized trial support, but one prospective observational study found no pain improvement despite use。
- A 2023 meta-analysis found low-quality evidence supporting mirror therapy、graded motor imagery、pain exposure therapy and aerobic exercise。
- Psychosocial / behavioral management is suggested when CRPS duration is more than two months at presentation, treatment response is insufficient, or psychologic / psychiatric comorbidity is suspected。
- Early pharmacologic options are presented as Grade 2C in the source summary。
- Supporting data for gabapentin in CRPS are limited; pregabalin has not been directly studied in CRPS in this source。
- Bisphosphonates may reduce short-term pain in selected early CRPS with abnormal bone scan uptake, based on several small randomized trials。
- Topical lidocaine or capsaicin has only limited CRPS-specific efficacy data。
- Glucocorticoid evidence is very weak; chronic CRPS usually does not respond。
- Ketamine infusion showed pain benefit through weeks 1-11 in one trial, but no statistically significant reduction by week 12; side effects included psychomimetic symptoms, nausea and vomiting。
- Sympathetic block evidence is conflicting and generally weak。
- SCS plus PT reduced pain and improved health-related quality of life more than PT alone for up to two years in one small study, but did not improve functional outcome measures and did not show pain difference from three to five years。
- Sympathectomy has not been compared with placebo or sham surgery in randomized controlled trials and can cause increased pain, new neuropathic pain and bothersome sweating。

## Inference

- CRPS management should be written as a staged function-restoration plan, not as a drug menu。
- If fear-avoidance blocks movement, psychosocial treatment is a functional intervention, not a claim that CRPS is psychogenic。
- Interventional pain treatment should be judged by whether it increases tolerated rehabilitation and participation。

## Assumption

- Adult CRPS diagnosis has been made and mimics have been excluded。
- Local treatment setting can safely monitor adverse effects from NSAIDs, anticonvulsants, antidepressants, bisphosphonates, glucocorticoids, ketamine or opioids when used。
- Any invasive procedure is done by clinicians with relevant pain management expertise。

## Uncertainty

- No single medication or procedure has high-certainty evidence as a universal CRPS treatment。
- Specific PT/OT modalities cannot be ranked confidently from this source alone。
- Ketamine protocols, sympathetic block selection and neuromodulation sequencing require procedure-specific sources。
- This source is an adult UpToDate review, not a pediatric CRPS treatment source。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「CRPS 先止痛，止痛後再復健。」來源方向是 early PT/OT plus pain control to tolerate activity。
- 誤讀：「Gabapentin / pregabalin 是 CRPS 特效藥。」來源對 gabapentin 證據保留，pregabalin 未直接研究。
- 誤讀：「Bisphosphonate 可用於所有 CRPS。」來源最支持 early CRPS with abnormal bone scan uptake。
- 誤讀：「Sympathetic block 有效就證明 CRPS。」block response 不是 diagnostic confirmation。
- 誤讀：「Ketamine 或 SCS 是早期 routine treatment。」來源把這些放在 refractory / specialist escalation。
- 誤讀：「Psychological therapy 表示 CRPS 是 psychological。」psychosocial care 是 chronic pain disability management。
