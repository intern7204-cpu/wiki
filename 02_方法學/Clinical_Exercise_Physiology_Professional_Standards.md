# Clinical Exercise Physiology Professional Standards

## One-Sentence Definition

Clinical Exercise Physiology Professional Standards 是 Clinical Exercise Physiologist 在健康照護脈絡中安全、倫理、以人為中心、能處理風險地提供 exercise-based care 所需的最低國際能力框架。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這是 professional standard / competency framework，不是 disease-specific exercise protocol。
- 它定義「Clinical Exercise Physiologist 應該具備什麼能力」，不是證明某一種 exercise intervention 有療效。
- 它可支撐 education、credentialling、accreditation 與 scope discussion，但不能直接取代本地法規、給付規則或醫療院所制度。
- 在 wiki 中，本頁用來補強 [[治療性運動處方]] 的 professional-practice 邊界。

## Why It Matters

把 exercise prescription 放進臨床照護時，問題不再只是 FITT。真正需要被標準化的是：

- 是否在 scope of practice 內執行。
- 是否能辨識風險與 contraindications。
- 是否能處理 medications、procedures、comorbidity 對 resting / exercise physiology 的影響。
- 是否有 documentation、referral、team collaboration 與 patient-reported outcome measurement。
- 是否能把 behavior change、health literacy、self-management 與 person-centred care 放進介入。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 適用於 Clinical Exercise Physiology 作為 health profession 的能力框架。
- 適合用於 program design、curriculum mapping、credential discussion、quality assurance 與跨國 standards comparison。
- 不適合作為單一病人的 immediate treatment order。
- 不適合作為特定疾病或運動模式的 efficacy citation。

## Mechanism

```text
Clinical exercise used in disease / injury care
→ higher safety and accountability requirement
→ need for professional standards
→ four competency domains
→ education / credentialing / practice governance
→ safer and more consistent exercise-based care
```

## Observable Patterns

### Four Domains

1. Professional practice。
2. Foundational knowledge。
3. Assessment and client management。
4. Case formulation and design / delivery of evidence-based interventions。

### Professional Practice

- Scope of practice、code of conduct、ethical practice。
- Legislation、regulations、standards、privacy、confidentiality。
- Effective, concise, respectful, informative documentation and reports。
- Culturally safe、sensitive、respectful、equity / diversity / inclusion、person-centred care。
- Interprofessional collaboration and onward referral。
- Risk management and mitigation。
- Reflective practice。

### Foundational Knowledge

- Anatomy、functional anatomy、human physiology、exercise physiology。
- Exercise prescription and delivery for clinical populations。
- Biomechanics、growth and development、health and exercise assessment。
- Motor learning/control、nutrition、physical activity and health。
- Health/exercise psychology、research methods、data analysis。
- Biopsychosocial care、value-based care、cultural determinants of health。
- Effects of medications、diagnostic procedures、medical/surgical interventions on resting and exercise-related physiology and adaptations。
- Contextual learning、behaviour change、engagement、adherence、self-management。

### Assessment and Client Management

- Distinguish、record、report、action changing risk factors and adverse signs/symptoms before, during, and after assessment/intervention。
- Use client-centred service delivery and communication methods。
- Refer to relevant health care professionals when indicated。
- Formulate screening processes to evaluate and stratify risk。
- Determine absolute and relative contraindications to exercise。
- Use standardized and individualized measurements, including patient-reported outcome measures。

### Design and Delivery of Evidence-Based Interventions

- Formulate meaningful, goal-oriented, evidence-based exercise prescriptions and recommendations。
- Design、prescribe、deliver、monitor safe and effective movement / physical activity / exercise-based interventions for complex presentations。
- Manage risk、evaluate progress、adapt recommendations and interventions。
- Educate and engage clients through communication strategies。
- Enable self-management during treatment。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 對臨床：合格的 exercise prescription 不能只寫強度和時間，還要能交代 risk stratification、contraindications、monitoring、documentation、referral 與 self-management。
- 對教育：curriculum mapping 不應只列 exercise physiology 知識，還要檢查 clinical documentation、ethics、cultural safety、behavior change、PROMs 與 interprofessional referral。
- 對研究：若研究聲稱 intervention 可被 clinical exercise professional 執行，方法段最好描述 screening、safety monitoring、contraindications、adverse response handling 與 intervention adaptation。

## Fact

- Reeves et al. 2026 使用 modified e-Delphi process 建立 international Clinical Exercise Physiology professional standards。
- Final standards 包含 4 domains / 20 elements。
- 21 位 expert working group members 被 recruited。
- Round completion 分別為 19、13、15、14 位。
- Consensus threshold 預設為 80% agreement。
- 18 of 20 final elements reached core consensus。
- Reflective practice 與 self-management strategy 未達 80% inclusion threshold，但 steering committee 保留。
- External stakeholder feedback 來自 27 individuals / 7 countries。

## Inference

- Clinical Exercise Physiology 的臨床定位接近 risk-aware allied health practice，而不是單純健身指導。
- 在 PM&R / cardiopulmonary rehabilitation / chronic disease management 中，exercise prescription 的品質應同時看 clinical governance 與 intervention dose。
- 若一張處方無法說明風險、監測與轉介條件，它不符合這類 professional standards 的精神。

## Assumption

- International professional standards 可以被各國教育與 credentialing system 合理轉譯。
- Local systems 願意建立 accreditation、registration、continuing professional development 與 practice governance。

## Uncertainty

- 本來源未測試標準導入後是否改善 patient outcomes。
- Western-country expert dominance 可能限制全球文化與制度轉譯。
- 不同國家對 Clinical Exercise Physiologist、physiotherapist、sports scientist、exercise specialist 的角色邊界可能不同。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 professional standards 當成 clinical guideline。
- 不可把 international consensus 當成台灣本地法規或院內職責。
- 不可把 exercise prescription 縮成 FITT；FITT 是 dose language，不是完整 professional practice。
- 不可把 Clinical Exercise Physiology 等同於 performance coaching；臨床脈絡需要 risk management、contraindication handling、documentation 與 referral。
