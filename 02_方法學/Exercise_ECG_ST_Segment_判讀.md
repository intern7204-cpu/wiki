# Exercise ECG ST Segment 判讀

## One-Sentence Definition

Exercise ECG ST segment 判讀，是在 resting ECG 可判讀的前提下，把 exercise and recovery phase 的 ST depression / elevation、lead distribution、symptoms、BP response and arrhythmias 合併解讀為 ischemia and risk signal。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只處理 exercise ECG 中 ischemic ECG changes and recovery ECG abnormalities。
- 測試適用條件、contraindications、protocol and endpoint 拆在 [[02_方法學/Exercise_ECG_Testing_適用條件與安全流程]]。
- 本頁不是 coronary angiography localization rule，也不是 stress imaging interpretation。
- 若 baseline ECG 有 LBBB、ventricular pacing、WPW、digoxin / LVH / hypokalemia-related ST-T abnormalities 或 >1 mm resting ST depression，exercise ECG ST interpretation 的可靠性下降，需考慮 imaging modality。

## Why It Matters

Exercise ECG 判讀容易出現三種過度簡化：

- 只看 ST depression 數字，不看 slope、lead、baseline ECG、workload and symptoms。
- 把 ST depression 當成 culprit vessel localization。
- Exercise phase 一結束就停止思考，忽略 recovery-only ST depression and recovery ventricular ectopy。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Resting ECG 必須能判讀 exercise-induced ischemic ST changes。
- Exercise ECG 應與 resting / standing ECG 對照；recovery ECG 應與同姿勢 resting baseline 對照。
- ECG acquisition quality 要足夠；high workload artifact and handrail gripping 可能干擾 interpretation。
- Interpretation 必須放在 pretest probability、exercise adequacy、symptoms、BP response and achieved workload 內。

## Mechanism

```text
Exercise increases rate-pressure product and myocardial oxygen demand
→ coronary oxygen delivery may become insufficient
→ subendocardial ischemia alters action potential and injury currents
→ TQ / ST segment displacement appears on surface ECG
→ horizontal/downsloping ST depression is a more specific ischemic pattern
→ recovery may reveal delayed ST depression or prognostically relevant ventricular ectopy
```

## Observable Patterns

### ST Depression

- Abnormal ischemia criterion：≥1 mm horizontal or downsloping ST depression in one or more leads, persisting 80 ms after the J point。
- Upsloping ST depression 可較早出現，但 specificity 較低；納入 upsloping criteria 會提高 sensitivity but reduce specificity。
- V4、V5、V6 對 subendocardial ischemia ST depression 最敏感；V5 常是單一最佳 lead。
- Lateral precordial ST depression 通常不能 localize coronary artery。
- Inferior-only ST depression, especially II and aVF, 常是 false positive and of little diagnostic value for coronary disease。
- Multiple-lead and greater ST depression 通常提示 more extensive myocardial ischemia。

### Sensitivity / Specificity Tradeoff

- ST depression criterion 愈嚴格，specificity 上升但 sensitivity 下降。
- 來源引用的 meta-analysis 中，exercise ECG ST depression 對 obstructive CHD 的 mean sensitivity and specificity 約 68 percent and 77 percent，但範圍很大。
- 若 abnormal criterion 限定為 ≥1 mm horizontal or downsloping ST depression，mean sensitivity and specificity 約 60 percent and 90 percent。

### Resting ST Depression

- 若 resting ST depression 來自 LBBB、LVH、digoxin or other baseline abnormality，exercise-induced ST depression 不是可靠 ischemia indicator。
- 若沒有上述因素，resting ST depression 本身可能見於 coronary disease；exercise ECG 仍有 diagnostic value，但通常 sensitivity 較高、specificity 較低。
- Minor resting ST depression intensified to ≥1 mm flat/downsloping ST depression during or after exercise 在來源引用研究中具有 prognostic value。

### ST Elevation

- Exercise-induced ST elevation uncommon，除非位於 pre-existing Q wave lead suggesting prior transmural MI；此時常和 wall motion abnormality 有關。
- Normal resting ECG 下的 exercise ST elevation 可提示 transmural ischemia，常見於 variant angina or severe / multivessel CHD。
- 和 ST depression 不同，ST elevation lead location may localize culprit artery；例如 anterior ST elevation suggests LAD disease, inferior ST elevation suggests lesion in or proximal to posterior descending artery in cited single-vessel disease study。
- ST elevation in leads without pre-existing Q waves and not in aVR may be arrhythmogenic。
- ST elevation in aVR 在來源引用研究中與 left main or ostial LAD severe obstructive CAD 有關，sensitivity and specificity 約 75 and 81 percent。

### Arrhythmia and BBB

- Exercise-induced ventricular ectopy occurs in 7-20 percent of patients undergoing exercise ECG for known or suspected CHD。
- Mortality association may be most relevant to frequent ventricular ectopy during recovery。
- Atrial ectopy is common but does not appear to be an independent adverse outcome predictor in this source。
- Baseline RBBB with upright ST / T in V5-6 時，V5-6 horizontal/downsloping ST depression can indicate myocardial ischemia。
- Baseline LBBB masks ischemia interpretation；exercise-induced transient LBBB is uncommon and may indicate CHD risk or conduction system disease, but can also be rate-related and misread as VT。

### Recovery Phase

- Recovery-only ST depression occurs in a minority of patients but has diagnostic / prognostic significance similar to exercise-phase ST depression in cited studies。
- 在一個 review 中，recovery-only ST depression 的 positive predictive value for significant angiographic CAD was similar to exercise-phase ST depression。
- Continuous monitoring during recovery is necessary because ventricular arrhythmias can occur during recovery and may carry mortality signal。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Exercise ECG report should include peak ST depression, slope, timing, recovery findings, arrhythmias, symptoms, BP response and achieved workload。
- Normal / abnormal 比 positive / negative 更能避免過度簡化。
- 若 ECG abnormality 只在 recovery 出現，不應降級為 incidental finding。
- 若 baseline ECG 不可判讀，應在 report 中明確說明 exercise ECG ischemia interpretation is limited，而不是硬給 ischemia negative。

## Fact

- ≥1 mm horizontal/downsloping ST depression persisting 80 ms after J point 是來源採用的 common abnormal criterion。
- Upsloping ST depression 的 predictive value 有 conflicting data。
- Recovery ST depression can increase test sensitivity without reducing predictive value in cited data。
- Frequent ventricular ectopy during recovery, rather than exercise-only ectopy, was associated with increased mortality after adjustment in a large referred cohort cited by the source。

## Inference

- Exercise ECG 判讀品質取決於 protocol adequacy and recovery surveillance；只保留 peak exercise ECG 容易漏掉 delayed ischemia signal。
- 在 rehabilitation context，arrhythmia and BP response may change exercise safety even when ischemic ST criterion is not met。

## Assumption

- 病人是 adult clinical population with known or suspected CHD，且測試目的包含 ischemia diagnosis / prognosis。
- ECG lead placement and signal quality adequate。

## Uncertainty

- ST depression 的 diagnostic performance 會因 abnormal criterion、population、pretest probability and angiographic reference standard 大幅變動。
- aVR ST elevation 的 left main / ostial LAD implication 需和 symptoms、overall ECG pattern and downstream testing 合併判斷。
- Ventricular ectopy 的 clinical management 需另查 stress-test prognosis / arrhythmia source。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把 upsloping ST depression 寫成和 horizontal/downsloping ST depression 一樣 specific。
- 不可用 ST depression lead distribution 直接定位 culprit artery。
- 不可因 exercise phase 沒有 ST change 就省略 recovery ECG interpretation。
- 不可在 LBBB / paced rhythm 等 baseline ECG 不可判讀情境下宣稱 exercise ECG ruled out ischemia。
