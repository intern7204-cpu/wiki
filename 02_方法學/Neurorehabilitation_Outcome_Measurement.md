# Neurorehabilitation Outcome Measurement

## One-Sentence Definition

Neurorehabilitation outcome measurement 需依 target level 分別量測 impairment、activity / disability、participation、psychosocial needs 與 HRQoL，並用連續測量修正 goals and interventions。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 Neurological Rehabilitation 來源中的 outcome measurement framework。
- 本頁不列出所有疾病特異量表，也不評比各量表 psychometrics。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Neurological_Rehabilitation]]。

## Why It Matters

- 若只看 FIM / BI，會漏掉 cognition、communication、participation、real-world activity、caregiver burden、QoL 與 patient priorities。
- 若只看 impairment，可能低估病人實際生活中的 disability 或 participation restriction。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先決定 intervention target：pathophysiology / impairment、activity / disability、participation、psychosocial domain 或 HRQoL。
- 量表需能在該族群、嚴重度、語言、環境與臨床目的下解讀。

## Mechanism

```text
Clinical question
-> choose measurement level
-> collect baseline
-> deliver intervention
-> repeat measurement
-> revise goal, dose, strategy, or setting
```

## Observable Patterns

- Impairment measures：strength、ROM、tone、sensation、cognition、language、balance component 等。
- Activity / disability measures：BI、FIM、mobility tests、walking speed、2-minute walk、6-minute walk。
- Participation / handicap：home, work, social role, community mobility, caregiver burden。
- HRQoL：disease-specific or generic quality-of-life instruments。
- Real-world activity：self-report may differ from actual behavior; wearable monitoring can add data.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Select measures that match the question: service effectiveness, individual progress, trial endpoint, discharge readiness, community participation, or home program adherence.
- Clinic walking speed and walking endurance tests are useful but do not fully describe daily activity.
- Floor / ceiling effects and sensitivity limitations must be considered when interpreting FIM or BI.

## Fact

- The source states outcome measurement is essential for service effectiveness, individual intervention monitoring, and trials.
- Measurement levels include pathophysiology / impairment, disability / functional activity, handicap / participation, and HRQoL.
- BI and FIM measure assistance needs for ADLs.
- FIM has floor / ceiling and sensitivity limitations in the source.
- Walking speed and 2- or 6-minute walk tests provide useful performance data but limited insight into daily behavior.

## Inference

- A rehabilitation program should not claim community participation success based only on inpatient ADL score.
- Wearable sensors may be useful when the clinical question is daily movement quantity, type, or quality.

## Assumption

- Repeated measures are collected under comparable conditions or condition changes are documented.

## Uncertainty

- The best measure set differs by diagnosis, stage, impairment severity, clinic resources, and decision need.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「FIM 上升等於所有 outcome 都好」不成立。
- 「10-meter walk speed 好就代表日常活動量足夠」不成立。
- 「量表只是研究用」不成立；來源把 measurement 放在 service and intervention monitoring 的核心。
