# Neurorehabilitation 目標設定與 Team-Based Care

## One-Sentence Definition

Neurorehabilitation team-based care 是以 shared functional goals、coordinated practice、patient / caregiver self-management 與 home/community transition 連接 neurologic impairment 到 participation 的照護流程。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁整理 Neurological Rehabilitation 來源中的 goal setting、team structure、discipline roles 與 care transition。
- 本頁不替代任何單一疾病的 clinical practice guideline。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../09_來源摘要/Neurological_Rehabilitation]]。

## Why It Matters

- 神經復健不是各科分別做自己的項目；若 goal、cueing、practice strategy 與 discharge plan 不一致，病人學到的是互相矛盾的行為。
- Home / community transfer 早在 inpatient stage 就要規劃，否則功能進步可能無法轉移到真實生活。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需要明確描述 pathology、impairments、activity limitations、participation restrictions、environment、caregiver capacity、medical complications 與 patient priorities。
- Goals 應 relevant、motivating、explicit、attainable、measurable，且 patient agreeable。

## Mechanism

```text
Team assessment
-> shared functional problem list
-> explicit short-term goals
-> agreed cueing and practice strategies across disciplines
-> repeated practice across therapy, nursing, family, and home program
-> improved transfer to daily life
```

## Observable Patterns

- Physician integrates neurologic, musculoskeletal, cardiopulmonary, and other impairments into functional planning.
- Rehab nursing carries over skills and monitors bowel / bladder, skin, pressure sore risk, and complications.
- PT addresses voluntary motor control, sensation, ROM, strength, balance, endurance, mobility, devices, orthoses, pain, contracture, spasticity, and deconditioning.
- SLP addresses speech, language, cognition, and swallowing assessment / intervention.
- Neuropsychology, social work, recreation, orthotics, patient, family, and caregiver contribute to participation and community transition.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- A progress note should connect discipline-specific intervention to shared functional goal.
- Caregiver education and home practice should be documented as part of the rehabilitation dose.
- Discharge planning should include environment, equipment, services, caregiver capacity, self-management, and follow-up measurement.

## Fact

- The source describes rehabilitation training as active learning and self-management using motivation, guidance, goal setting, progressive practice, feedback, and social support.
- Interdisciplinary approach is framed as problem-solving for functional outcomes rather than discipline-bound activity.
- Stroke, TBI, and SCI team approaches are described as reducing complications in the source.
- Stroke units reduce death, institutionalization, and dependency in the source.

## Inference

- A team conference that only lists discipline updates without a shared functional decision is structurally weak.
- Inpatient milieu can be used as a therapeutic environment when all team members reinforce the same strategies.

## Assumption

- Patient goals are elicited in a way that accounts for cognition, communication, mood, culture, family role, and prognosis.

## Uncertainty

- Best setting and service intensity vary by diagnosis, severity, medical stability, family support, community resources, and payer system.

## Limitations / Common Misreadings

- 「有 PT/OT/ST 就是 team care」不成立；核心是 shared goal and coordinated strategy。
- 「Goal 寫 improve mobility 就夠」不成立；需 explicit and measurable。
- 「出院規劃是最後一天的事」不成立；community transfer 是 neurorehabilitation 的核心。
