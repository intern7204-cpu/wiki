# Neurorehabilitation 藥物效應與治療時機

## One-Sentence Definition

Neurorehabilitation 中的 drug therapy 是會改變 arousal、motor output、tone、balance、orthostasis、cognition、pain、seizure risk 與 therapy timing 的功能變項。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁根據 [[../09_來源摘要/Impact_of_Drug_Therapy_on_Patients_Receiving_Neurological_Rehabilitation]] 建立 medication-aware rehab frame。
- 本頁不提供處方、停藥、換藥或 dosing recommendation；任何調整需由 prescriber / pharmacist 處理。

## Why It Matters

- 同一位病人上午和下午表現不同，可能不是 motivation 問題，而是 drug timing、wearing off、sedation、orthostasis、hypoglycemia、electrolyte、pain medication 或 polypharmacy。
- Rehabilitation clinician 若不記錄藥物效應，functional gain / failure 可能被誤讀。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 需取得 medication list、dose timing、recent changes、renal / hepatic function、nutrition status、drug interaction risk、therapeutic target、adverse effects。
- 急性 hypotension、syncope、new confusion、seizure、bleeding、severe sedation、hypoglycemia 或 suspected toxicity 需即時回報。

## Mechanism

```text
Drug pharmacokinetics + pharmacodynamics
-> timing and intensity of therapeutic / adverse effects
-> mobility, tone, balance, alertness, cognition, BP, HR response, pain
-> therapy participation, motor learning, safety, outcome measurement
```

## Observable Patterns

- PD：on/off fluctuation、wearing off、dyskinesia、orthostasis、hallucination or cognitive changes。
- Antiseizure medication：sedation、ataxia、vertigo、cognitive slowing；abrupt discontinuation risk。
- Cardiovascular medication：orthostatic hypotension、HR response blunting、weakness、dizziness、electrolyte-mediated muscle effect。
- Anticoagulants：bruising / bleeding risk affecting handling and fall consequences。
- Antispasticity / muscle relaxants：tone reduction may improve movement but sedation / weakness can impair function.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Therapy notes should document medication timing when performance varies predictably.
- Scheduling therapy during optimal "on" windows can be therapeutic in PD, while also documenting wearing-off patterns for the team.
- Research and outcomes should record medication changes as confounders.

## Fact

- 來源定義 pharmacokinetics 為 absorption、distribution、metabolism、elimination。
- 來源定義 pharmacodynamics 為 drug effects and mechanisms。
- 來源指出 disease、age、diet、gender、genetics、drug interactions can alter medication response。
- 來源指出 antiseizure medications may cause drowsiness, ataxia, vertigo, and cognitive effects.
- 來源指出 antihypertensive and cardiovascular drugs can cause orthostatic symptoms, dizziness, weakness, electrolyte issues, and altered exercise tolerance.

## Inference

- Rehab failure after a medication change should trigger medication-effect review before escalating therapy intensity.

## Assumption

- Medication management remains collaborative and led by prescribing clinicians; rehabilitation role is observation, safety, documentation, and communication.

## Uncertainty

- Drug availability, indications, safety warnings, and pharmacogenomic applications are time-sensitive and must be verified with current references.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 「復健人員不用管藥」不成立；藥會改變功能與安全。
- 「看到副作用就自己停藥」不成立；應回報 prescriber / pharmacist。
- 「功能退步一定是病情惡化」不成立；medication timing and adverse effects can mimic decline.
