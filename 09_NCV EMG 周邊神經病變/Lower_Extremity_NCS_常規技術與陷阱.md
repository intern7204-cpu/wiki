# Lower Extremity NCS 常規技術與陷阱

## One-Sentence Definition

Lower extremity NCS 常規技術與陷阱，是用 tibial、peroneal、femoral、sural、superficial peroneal、saphenous、lateral femoral cutaneous、plantar studies 與 soleus H reflex 的 standard recording / stimulation / distance / comparison rules，避免把正常變異、technical limitation 或 APN 誤判為 pathology。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 routine lower-extremity NCS technique。
- 本頁不建立 peroneal neuropathy at fibular neck、tarsal tunnel syndrome、polyneuropathy、sciatic neuropathy、lumbar plexopathy、femoral neuropathy、meralgia paresthetica 或 S1 radiculopathy 的完整 diagnostic criteria。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Routine_Lower_Extremity_NCS_Techniques]]。
- Upper-extremity, facial, blink reflex, and phrenic NCS 已另頁處理：[[Upper_Extremity_NCS_常規技術與陷阱]]。

## Why It Matters

- Lower-extremity NCS 的 amplitude drop、small sensory responses 與 body habitus limitation 比想像中常見；直接套 normal table 容易 false positive。
- Peroneal neuropathy at the fibular neck 可能因省略 below-fibular-neck stimulation 或未記錄 TA 而漏掉 focal slowing / conduction block。
- Tarsal tunnel evaluation 常面對很小的 plantar responses；沒有 averaging 與 side-to-side comparison 時，low / absent response 不應被過度診斷。
- H reflex 對 proximal pathway 有用，但 delayed / absent H reflex 不等於特異定位到 S1 radiculopathy。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 檢查前先定義 localization question：peroneal at fibular neck、tibial across ankle、polyneuropathy screening、sciatic / plexus / root pattern、femoral neuropathy、lateral femoral cutaneous neuropathy 或 S1 pathway。
- Standard distance、controlled temperature、recording montage、stimulation site、pulse duration 與 side-to-side comparison 必須明確。
- 若 sensory / plantar response 不見或需要高刺激，先考慮較短距離、electrode repositioning、averaging、body habitus 與 age effect。
- 若症狀單側，affected-unaffected comparison 應優先納入判讀。

## Mechanism

```text
Localization question
→ choose lower-extremity nerve-specific protocol
→ apply recording / stimulation / distance / pulse settings
→ verify supramaximal stimulation and avoid co-stimulation
→ account for normal amplitude-drop exceptions, small response, age, height, and obesity
→ use side-to-side comparison and actual-distance conduction velocity
→ identify focal slowing, conduction block clue, APN clue, distal tibial neuropathy clue, plexus / root clue, or technical limitation
```

## Observable Patterns

- Tibial motor AHB study uses medial ankle and popliteal fossa stimulation with 9 cm distal distance.
- Tibial CMAP often has an initial positive deflection if G1 is not over the motor endplate; G1 should be slightly repositioned.
- Tibial popliteal-fossa CMAP amplitude is often lower than medial-ankle amplitude; normal controls may drop up to 50%, so conduction block must be interpreted cautiously.
- Peroneal motor EDB study uses ankle, below-fibular-head, and lateral-popliteal-fossa stimulation with 9 cm distal distance.
- Peroneal fibular-neck evaluation should always include ankle, below-fibular-neck, and above-fibular-neck stimulation; ankle plus above-fibular-neck alone can miss focal slowing.
- Excessive lateral-popliteal-fossa stimulation can co-stimulate tibial nerve.
- Peroneal EDB proximal amplitude higher than ankle should trigger APN review.
- Peroneal below-fibular-head amplitude may normally be lower than ankle; average drop is 14%, and rare normal drops can reach 20-30%.
- Peroneal TA recording is especially valuable in suspected peroneal neuropathy at the fibular neck and may show conduction block / focal slowing more clearly than EDB.
- Femoral motor rectus femoris study mainly compares side-to-side amplitude for femoral neuropathy, lumbar plexopathy, and severe L4 radiculopathy; it is difficult in obesity and often requires high current.
- Superficial peroneal sensory and sural sensory studies use 14 cm standard distance, but shorter distances may be needed; if distance is shortened, use onset-latency conduction velocity based on actual distance rather than standard peak latency.
- For lower-extremity neuropathy screening, this source prefers sural sensory over superficial peroneal sensory.
- Saphenous sensory response may be difficult or absent in normal controls, especially older than age 40; side-to-side comparison is required before abnormal interpretation.
- Lateral femoral cutaneous sensory study may require moving stimulation lateral and medial around ASIS because nerve course varies; low / absent response requires side-to-side comparison when symptoms are unilateral.
- Medial / lateral plantar motor studies record AHB and ADQP and are useful for distal tibial neuropathy across the ankle.
- Medial / lateral plantar sensory potentials are very small and difficult to obtain even in normal controls; averaging and side-to-side comparison are often required.
- Medial / lateral plantar mixed nerve studies are technically easier than orthodromic sensory studies and are preferred for distal tibial neuropathy across the ankle in this source.
- Soleus H reflex uses tibial stimulation in the popliteal fossa with cathode rostral and 1000 microseconds / 1 ms pulse duration.
- H reflex appears at low stimulation intensity, usually at 25-34 ms with triphasic morphology; side-to-side latency difference >1.5 ms is abnormal.
- H reflex can be delayed or absent in polyneuropathy, tibial neuropathy, sciatic neuropathy, lumbosacral plexopathy, or S1 radiculopathy.
- Normal tables assume controlled temperature and standard distances; height, age, and lab-specific norms matter.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- A lower-extremity NCS report should state actual distance when standard distance is shortened, especially sensory studies.
- Peroneal EDB amplitude pattern should be checked for APN before labeling conduction block or technical failure.
- Tarsal tunnel workup should prefer plantar mixed study over difficult orthodromic plantar sensory study when following this source.
- Low or absent saphenous, lateral femoral cutaneous, or plantar potentials should be reported with age, body habitus, technical, and side-to-side context.
- H reflex abnormality should be phrased as a proximal pathway abnormality with differential localization, not as a stand-alone S1 diagnosis.

## Fact

- Lower-extremity normal value tables in this source assume controlled temperature and standard distances.
- Motor and sensory amplitudes are measured baseline to negative peak.
- Sensory and mixed distal latencies are peak latencies, but sensory and mixed conduction velocities are calculated from onset latency.
- Affected-unaffected limb comparison may be more useful than normal value tables.
- Late responses must be normalized for height in tall or short patients.

## Inference

- Lower-extremity protocol metadata should be treated as part of the diagnostic result.
- When standard sensory distance is shortened, "normal peak latency" cannot be used as if the protocol were unchanged.
- In unilateral lower-extremity complaints, side-to-side comparison often carries more interpretive value than isolated normal-table classification.

## Assumption

- The examiner can achieve supramaximal stimulation and can reposition electrodes or stimulator when response morphology is poor.
- The lab has reference values tied to its own technique, or it reports when external normal values may not apply.

## Uncertainty

- The source does not define full diagnostic criteria for peroneal neuropathy, tarsal tunnel syndrome, polyneuropathy, radiculopathy, plexopathy, femoral neuropathy, or meralgia paresthetica.
- Several lower-extremity studies are limited by obesity, age, small responses, and stimulation discomfort.
- A delayed or absent H reflex is not lesion-specific.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Tibial proximal amplitude drop = conduction block。」正確是 normal controls 可在 popliteal stimulation drop up to 50%，需 side-to-side 與 technical context。
- 誤讀：「Peroneal ankle + above fibular neck 已足夠。」正確是還要 below-fibular-neck stimulation，否則可漏掉 fibular-neck focal slowing。
- 誤讀：「Proximal peroneal amplitude 比 ankle 高就是技術錯誤。」正確是要考慮 APN。
- 誤讀：「短距離測到 response 後仍可直接用 14 cm peak latency normal table。」正確是要用 actual distance 和 onset latency 算 conduction velocity。
- 誤讀：「Absent plantar / saphenous / lateral femoral cutaneous response 一定 abnormal。」正確是正常人也可 small / absent，尤其 older adults 或 technical difficulty；需 side-to-side。
- 誤讀：「H reflex abnormal = S1 radiculopathy。」正確是 polyneuropathy、tibial、sciatic、plexus 與 S1 都可能造成 delayed / absent H reflex。
