# Upper Extremity NCS 常規技術與陷阱

## One-Sentence Definition

Upper extremity NCS 常規技術與陷阱，是用 nerve-specific recording site、stimulation site、distance、limb position、internal comparison 與 safety checks，讓 median、ulnar、radial、facial、phrenic NCS 的 waveform 可以被可靠解讀。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 routine upper-extremity, facial, blink reflex, and phrenic NCS technique。
- 本頁不建立 CTS、UNE、PIN neuropathy、plexopathy、facial palsy、brainstem lesion 或 diaphragm weakness 的完整 diagnostic criteria。
- 本頁只使用單一來源：[[../10_來源摘要/Routine_Upper_Extremity_Facial_Phrenic_NCS_Techniques]]。
- Lower extremity NCS 需另頁處理。

## Why It Matters

- NCS 的數字不是脫離 protocol 的事實；錯誤距離、錯誤 electrode、錯誤 elbow position 或 co-stimulation 會直接改變 latency、velocity、amplitude 與 conduction block 判讀。
- Ulnar across-elbow evaluation 若省略 below-elbow stimulation，會漏掉 across-elbow focal slowing。
- 在 polyneuropathy 或 unilateral symptoms 下，internal comparison 或 side-to-side comparison 可能比單純套 normal table 更有用。
- Phrenic NCS 有 device / catheter safety issue，不是普通 low-risk motor study。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 檢查前先界定 localization question：median at wrist、ulnar across elbow / Guyon's canal、radial spiral groove / PIN、plexus / cord / trunk、facial nerve、brainstem reflex arc 或 phrenic nerve。
- Standard distance、controlled temperature、recording montage 與 stimulation site 必須明確記錄。
- Paired internal comparison studies 必須使用 identical distance。
- 若症狀單側，side-to-side amplitude / latency comparison 應納入判讀。
- 若病人有 external pacemaker、internal jugular catheter、implanted cardiac pacemaker 或 cardioverter-defibrillator，phrenic NCS 需特別風險處理。

## Mechanism

```text
Localization question
→ select nerve-specific routine study
→ apply standard recording / stimulation / distance / position
→ prevent co-stimulation and distance error
→ compare absolute values, internal paired latencies, and side-to-side values
→ identify focal slowing, conduction block clue, plexus clue, cranial reflex abnormality, or technical limitation
```

## Observable Patterns

- Median motor APB wrist distance is 7 cm；antecubital fossa stimulation is just medial to brachial artery pulse。
- Median motor CMAP amplitude larger at antecubital fossa than wrist should raise MGA suspicion, after excluding co-stimulation.
- Median motor palmar palm/wrist CMAP amplitude ratio >1.2 implies some conduction block across the wrist, but conduction velocity is unreliable because of short distance and recurrent thenar branch course.
- Median sensory digit 2 or 3 standard distance is 13 cm; proximal sensory response is normally smaller and harder to record because of temporal dispersion and phase cancellation.
- Median sensory palmar study compares wrist-to-digit and palm-to-digit segments; palm/wrist SNAP amplitude ratio >1.6 implies some conduction block across the wrist.
- Wrist-to-palm median sensory conduction velocity is normally faster than palm-to-digit 3; CTS reverses this pattern with relative wrist-to-palm slowing.
- Ulnar motor ADM study uses wrist, below-elbow, and above-elbow stimulation; all three are required.
- Ulnar below-elbow stimulation should be 3 cm distal to medial epicondyle; >4 cm distal makes the nerve deep and difficult to stimulate.
- Ulnar across-elbow distance should be measured as a curved line with elbow flexed 90-135 degrees, not as a straight line.
- Straight-elbow ulnar study causes factitious across-elbow slowing by underestimating true nerve length.
- Below-elbow ulnar stimulation commonly needs higher current because the nerve lies deep to flexor carpi ulnaris.
- Below-elbow ulnar CMAP amplitude >10% smaller than wrist should trigger MGA review.
- Dorsal ulnar cutaneous sensory study is spared in Guyon's canal lesions and may be abnormal in some UNE; absent response should prompt superficial radial stimulation check for rare dorsal hand anomalous innervation.
- Deep ulnar motor branch FDI recording may be more useful than ADM for focal ulnar slowing across the elbow and for Guyon's canal lesions.
- FDI G2 electrode must be on thumb MCP joint; placing G2 on index MCP produces an initial positive CMAP deflection.
- Lumbrical-interossei motor comparison: median second lumbrical and ulnar first palmar interosseous are recorded with the same electrodes and same distance; latency difference >0.5 ms is definitely abnormal in this source.
- Lumbrical-interossei comparison is useful when CTS is suspected with coexistent polyneuropathy and absent sensory / mixed potentials.
- Median-ulnar digit 4 and median-radial digit 1 comparisons use identical distances; normal latency difference is <0.5 ms.
- Median-ulnar palmar mixed latency difference normally is <0.4 ms at 8 cm; short distance makes exact measurement critical.
- Radial motor EIP CMAP usually has an initial positive deflection from nearby radial-innervated muscles; this does not require moving G1 to chase a motor point.
- Radial motor proximal distance measurement is often inaccurate by surface tape and should use obstetric calipers near the spiral groove.
- Radial sensory abnormality can support radial neuropathy or posterior cord / upper-middle trunk plexus involvement; radial sensory is spared in PIN neuropathy.
- Medial antebrachial cutaneous SNAP may be abnormal in medial cord / lower trunk plexopathy and is typically absent or very low in true neurogenic thoracic outlet syndrome.
- Lateral antebrachial cutaneous SNAP may be abnormal in musculocutaneous nerve, lateral cord, or upper trunk lesions.
- Proximal upper-extremity stimulation at Erb's point or cervical root level can be technically difficult; side-to-side comparison is necessary.
- Phrenic motor study can accidentally stimulate spinal accessory nerve or brachial plexus if stimulator placement is wrong.
- Phrenic amplitude is slightly larger during inspiration and study is difficult in obese individuals.
- Facial whole-nerve stimulation at stylomastoid foramen can require higher current and be uncomfortable; separate branch stimulation is often easier and more comfortable.
- Facial branch studies should be done bilaterally because the contralateral side often provides the baseline.
- Blink reflex uses supraorbital stimulation and bilateral orbicularis oculi recordings; two to five traces are superimposed to determine minimal R1 and R2 latencies.
- Normal tables assume controlled temperature and standard distances; local lab normal values are preferred.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- A report should not just state "median / ulnar NCS done"; it should make enough protocol assumptions explicit for interpretation.
- Ulnar across-elbow slowing is only meaningful if elbow position and curved-distance measurement are correct.
- For CTS in polyneuropathy, lumbrical-interossei comparison can retain diagnostic value when sensory responses are absent.
- For plexus localization, antebrachial cutaneous sensory studies provide cord / trunk-level clues that routine median / ulnar / radial distal studies may miss.
- Phrenic study should include a safety caveat when relevant devices or catheters are present.

## Fact

- Normal value tables in this source assume normal controlled temperature and standard distances.
- Motor and sensory amplitudes are measured from baseline to negative peak.
- Sensory and mixed nerve distal latencies are peak latencies, but sensory and mixed nerve conduction velocities are calculated from onset latency.
- Some values may need adjustment for extremes of height or age.
- Affected-unaffected limb comparison may be more useful than normal value tables.
- Ideally, each laboratory should develop its own normal values.

## Inference

- Protocol metadata should be treated as part of the diagnostic result, not background information.
- If internal comparison and absolute normal table disagree, the reason may be distance, temperature, co-stimulation, age / height adjustment, or side-to-side asymmetry rather than a single disease label.
- For focal neuropathy, a technically incomplete study can be falsely reassuring even if all recorded values are numerically normal.

## Assumption

- The examiner can maintain standardized distances and can reposition electrodes / stimulator when response morphology suggests artifact.
- The lab has reference values tied to its own technique or clearly comparable published technique.

## Uncertainty

- The source does not define full diagnostic criteria for CTS, UNE, radial neuropathy, plexopathy, facial palsy, brainstem lesion, or diaphragm dysfunction.
- Some studies are technically difficult; failed supramaximal stimulation or body habitus limitation should be reported rather than over-interpreted.
- Normal values are one reference set and not universal.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Routine NCS 是固定套餐。」正確是 routine protocol 仍需符合 localization question、standard distance 與 technique。
- 誤讀：「Ulnar wrist + above-elbow stimulation 已足夠。」正確是必須做 wrist、below-elbow、above-elbow，否則可能漏掉 across-elbow slowing。
- 誤讀：「Straight elbow 測距和 flexed curved distance 一樣。」正確是 straight elbow 會低估 true nerve length，造成假性 slowing。
- 誤讀：「Absent dorsal ulnar cutaneous SNAP 一定是 ulnar pathology。」正確是還要考慮 rare dorsal hand anomalous innervation。
- 誤讀：「Normal table 是最終裁判。」正確是 normal table 需 controlled temperature、standard distance、age / height context；side-to-side comparison 有時更有用。
- 誤讀：「Phrenic NCS 只是另一個 peripheral motor study。」正確是需注意 external pacemaker、nearby catheter / implanted device 與 body habitus limitation。
