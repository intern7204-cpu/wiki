# EDX 異常神經支配變異判讀

## One-Sentence Definition

EDX 異常神經支配變異判讀，是在 NCS / EMG 出現不合解剖預期的 amplitude、latency 或 muscle involvement pattern 時，系統性排除 technical artifact，並用 targeted stimulation 證實 MGA、APN 等 anomalous innervation。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁聚焦 anomalous innervation 如何改變 EDX interpretation。
- 本頁不整理 routine upper / lower extremity NCS protocol，也不建立 CTS、UNE、PNFN、CIDP 或 GBS 的完整 diagnostic criteria。
- 本頁以 [[../10_來源摘要/Anomalous_Innervations]] 為主要來源，並用 [[../10_來源摘要/Routine_Upper_Extremity_Facial_Phrenic_NCS_Techniques]] 與 [[../10_來源摘要/Routine_Lower_Extremity_NCS_Techniques]] 補足 routine NCS 中應觸發 variant review 的 protocol clue。
- Anomalous innervation 不是 technical error；它是正常 anatomic variant，但可以製造看起來像 technical error 或 pathology 的 waveform pattern。

## Why It Matters

- 把 MGA 誤讀成 forearm conduction block，可能把病人錯誤歸類為 acquired demyelinating neuropathy，甚至導向不必要的 immunomodulating therapy。
- 把 proximal MGA 誤讀成 UNE，會讓單一 waveform pattern 取代 clinical-electrophysiologic correlation。
- 把 APN 誤讀成 stimulation error 或 peroneal conduction block，會讓 peroneal neuropathy at fibular neck 的定位變混亂。
- Needle EMG 的 muscle pattern 也會被 anomalous innervation 改寫；沒有先做 NCS，肌肉分布可能被錯誤定位。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 先確認 temperature、supramaximal stimulation、co-stimulation、electrode placement、recording montage 與 distance measurement。
- 若 amplitude pattern 不合一般生理預期，不能只在 "technical limitation" 停止；需依 nerve / muscle pattern 檢查可能的 anatomic variant。
- 疑似 MGA 時，需依 recording muscle 選擇 median-to-ulnar 或 ulnar-to-median confirmatory stimulation。
- Routine upper-extremity protocol 中，median motor proximal amplitude larger than wrist、ulnar below-elbow CMAP >10% lower than wrist、或 absent dorsal ulnar cutaneous SNAP 都應觸發 variant / artifact review。
- 疑似 APN 時，需刺激 lateral malleolus posterior region 並記錄 EDB。

## Mechanism

```text
Variant motor / sensory pathway
→ routine distal and proximal stimulation activate different fiber sets
→ unexpected amplitude drop, proximal amplitude increase, positive dip, or absent response
→ can mimic conduction block, entrapment neuropathy, demyelinating polyneuropathy, or technical error
→ targeted stimulation proves variant pathway
→ reinterpret NCS and needle EMG localization
```

## Observable Patterns

- MGA 是 median-to-ulnar motor fiber crossover；sensory fibers spared。
- MGA 來源報告 prevalence 約 15-30%，可 unilateral 或 bilateral。
- MGA 最常供應 FDI，也可供應 hypothenar muscles、ulnar-innervated thenar muscles，或混合型。
- Routine ulnar motor study recording abductor digiti minimi：wrist CMAP 大於 below-elbow CMAP 可見於 hypothenar MGA，但需先排除 wrist co-stimulation 與 below-elbow submaximal stimulation。
- Source 指出 wrist-to-below-elbow ulnar CMAP amplitude drop up to 10% 可由 normal temporal dispersion 解釋；超過此範圍需檢查 MGA。
- Hypothenar MGA confirmation：median nerve stimulation at wrist and antecubital fossa while recording hypothenar muscles；MGA 時 antecubital fossa stimulation 可出現 CMAP，且 amplitude 約等於 ulnar wrist-to-below-elbow drop。
- Proximal MGA 可少見地模仿 UNE conduction block，尤其當 below-elbow stimulation site 太 distal。
- Source 建議 ulnar below-elbow stimulation site 放在 medial epicondyle distal 3 cm，不應更遠端。
- Routine upper-extremity source 也強調 below-elbow site 太遠端會使 nerve deep and difficult to stimulate，並增加 interpretation error。
- Ulnar study recording FDI：FDI MGA 可造成 wrist-to-below-elbow ulnar amplitude drop；confirmation 需看 median antecubital fossa stimulation amplitude 是否高於 median wrist stimulation。
- Median motor study recording thenar muscles：MGA to ulnar-innervated thenar muscles 可造成 median antecubital fossa stimulation amplitude 高於 wrist stimulation。
- MGA plus CTS：可見 prolonged distal median motor latency、proximal positive dip、spuriously fast median forearm conduction velocity；source 指出 median forearm CV 很少超過 70-75 m/s。
- Severe CTS plus MGA 可出現 proximal median latency shorter than distal median latency。
- MGA 會改變 needle EMG：proximal median lesion 可異常影響 ulnar-innervated hand muscles；proximal ulnar lesion 可 paradoxically spare 部分 ulnar hand muscles。
- APN 是 lower extremity 常見 anomalous innervation，來自 distal superficial peroneal nerve，走 lateral calf、posterior to lateral malleolus，供應 EDB lateral portion。
- Routine peroneal motor study recording EDB：APN 可使 below-fibular-neck / lateral-popliteal-fossa CMAP amplitude 高於 ankle stimulation。
- Routine lower-extremity protocol 也指出 peroneal EDB below-fibular-head or popliteal-fossa CMAP amplitude higher than ankle should trigger APN consideration。
- APN confirmation：刺激 posterior to lateral malleolus while recording EDB；若 APN 存在會有 small CMAP。
- APN plus peroneal neuropathy at fibular neck 可形成 ankle low、fibular-neck high、lateral-popliteal-fossa low 的 low-high-low pattern。
- MGA plus UNE 可形成 forearm "block" 加 across-elbow block 的 multiple block-like pattern，模仿 acquired demyelinating polyneuropathy。
- Riche-Cannieu anastomosis 可造成 absent median motor response with normal thenar bulk / strength，或 median sensory abnormal with relatively normal thenar needle EMG；但 source 對其臨床重要性標示為 debated。
- Rare dorsal hand cutaneous variant 可讓 dorsal ulnar cutaneous sensory response 看似 absent，可用 superficial radial stimulation with dorsal ulnar cutaneous territory recording 證實。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 非 entrapment site 的 apparent conduction block 不應直接升級為 acquired demyelinating neuropathy；先確認 MGA / APN、technical artifact 與 nerve-specific protocol。
- 若 UNE 只有 across-elbow conduction block 而缺乏 clinical symptoms 或其他 EDX support，需回頭檢查 proximal MGA 與 below-elbow stimulation site。
- 若 peroneal EDB response proximal amplitude 高於 ankle，應 routine check APN，否則會混淆 PNFN 判讀。
- EDX report 應把 anomalous innervation 當作 physiologic interpretation 的一部分，而不是只寫成 incidental note。
- 自動 NCS 判讀若沒有 anomalous innervation rule，會系統性高估 conduction block、technical artifact 或 demyelinating neuropathy。

## Fact

- Anomalous innervations can be mistaken for technical abnormalities or actual pathology.
- MGA is a median-to-ulnar motor crossover and does not involve sensory fibers in this source.
- MGA can innervate abductor digiti minimi, FDI, ulnar-innervated thenar muscles, or combinations.
- The source reports MGA in 15-30% of patients.
- A >10% ulnar CMAP amplitude drop from wrist to below-elbow in routine ulnar motor studies should trigger median stimulation to check MGA.
- Ulnar below-elbow stimulation site should be 3 cm distal to the medial epicondyle in the source's protocol discussion.
- APN can be demonstrated by stimulation posterior to the lateral malleolus while recording EDB.
- MGA plus CTS can produce a positive proximal deflection and factitiously fast median forearm conduction velocity.
- NCS is needed to properly interpret needle EMG findings when anomalous innervation is present.

## Inference

- Anomalous innervation should be part of the standard differential for unexpected amplitude patterns, not an afterthought.
- A conduction block diagnosis should carry less confidence when it rests on a single segmental amplitude drop without confirmatory clinical, technical, and anatomic checks.
- In anomalous innervation, "normal" muscle innervation maps are conditional assumptions, not guaranteed facts.

## Assumption

- The examiner can perform additional stimulation sites and compare waveform morphology without excessive co-stimulation.
- The lab follows consistent ulnar and peroneal motor technique and records which muscles and stimulation sites were used.

## Uncertainty

- Rare variants such as Riche-Cannieu anastomosis and dorsal hand cutaneous anomalies have less robust evidence in this source.
- Coexistent entrapment neuropathy and anomalous innervation can overlap, requiring cautious interpretation rather than binary labeling.
- Source is a textbook chapter; it does not quantify diagnostic accuracy for each confirmatory maneuver.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Forearm ulnar conduction block = acquired demyelination。」正確是先排除 MGA、technical error 與 temporal dispersion。
- 誤讀：「Across-elbow CMAP drop alone = UNE。」正確是若缺乏臨床或其他 EDX support，需考慮 proximal MGA 與 stimulation-site error。
- 誤讀：「Peroneal proximal amplitude 比 ankle 高就是技術錯誤。」正確是 APN 是常見 lower-extremity anomalous innervation，需刺激 posterior to lateral malleolus。
- 誤讀：「Needle EMG muscle pattern 可直接照標準 nerve map 解讀。」正確是 anomalous innervation 可讓 median / ulnar lesion pattern 反常。
- 誤讀：「Anomalous innervation 是 disease。」正確是它通常是 normal anatomic variant；問題在於會混淆 EDX localization。
