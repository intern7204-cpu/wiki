# EDX 解剖與神經生理基礎

## One-Sentence Definition

EDX 解剖與神經生理基礎，是用 peripheral nerve anatomy、DRG 位置、fiber type、myelination、motor unit、NMJ 與 volume conduction 解釋 NCS / EMG 到底量到什麼，以及不能量到什麼。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只處理 EDX 的 foundational anatomy / neurophysiology。
- 本頁不整理特定疾病的完整 diagnostic criteria。
- 本頁不取代 nerve-specific NCS protocol、technical artifact review、diagnostic statistics 或 disease-specific interpretation。
- 本頁的核心任務是把 EDX 數值拉回「anatomic substrate + recorded fiber population + recording physics」。

## Why It Matters

- NCS / EMG 不是直接看見病灶，而是用可記錄到的 large-fiber / muscle electrical events 推回 localization 與 physiology。
- 如果不先理解 DRG、myotome / dermatome overlap、large-fiber selection 與 volume conduction，就容易把 normal SNAP、normal routine NCS 或 waveform morphology 誤讀成過度確定的診斷。
- 這頁補足 [[NCS_軸突損失與脫髓鞘判讀]] 的底層假設：latency / velocity、amplitude、duration 與 morphology 各自對應不同生理來源。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- EDX interpretive chain 必須先有 clinical localization question。
- 檢查者需知道 nerve root、plexus、peripheral nerve、muscle、motor point 與 sensory territory 的基本解剖。
- Routine NCS 結果只能代表被記錄到的大直徑、快速傳導纖維；不能外推到所有 peripheral nerve fiber types。
- 波形判讀前要先確認 recording electrode 是否在合理位置，尤其 motor CMAP 是否以 active electrode 放在 motor point 為前提。

## Mechanism

```text
Clinical symptoms and signs
→ anatomic localization: root, plexus, peripheral nerve, NMJ, muscle
→ lesion relation to DRG
→ selected NCS / needle EMG target
→ recorded fiber population: large myelinated fibers and motor units
→ conduction physiology: axon diameter, myelin, saltatory conduction
→ recording physics: volume conduction, near-field / far-field potentials, electrode position
→ latency / velocity / amplitude / morphology interpretation
```

### DRG and Sensory Response Logic

```text
Sensory neuron cell body in DRG
→ peripheral sensory axon remains intact when lesion is proximal to DRG
→ sensory symptoms can coexist with preserved SNAP
→ normal SNAP does not exclude radiculopathy or CNS lesion
```

### Myelin and Conduction Logic

```text
Schwann-cell myelin
→ internodes insulated and nodes of Ranvier depolarize
→ saltatory conduction increases velocity
→ large myelinated fibers dominate routine NCS latency / velocity
→ demyelination can markedly slow conduction or cause conduction failure
```

### Volume Conduction Logic

```text
Intracellular action potential
→ extracellular current field through tissue
→ near-field recording depends on source-electrode distance
→ electrode placement changes amplitude and morphology
→ waveform shape is not pure pathology
```

## Observable Patterns

- Lesion proximal to the DRG：可有 sensory symptoms / sensory loss，但 routine SNAP 可以 preserved。
- Peripheral nerve lesion distal to DRG：較可能影響 sensory conduction response。
- Single-root lesion：因 dermatomal / myotomal overlap，通常不造成 complete anesthesia，也不會讓單一肌肉完全 paralysis。
- Peripheral nerve lesion：因同時包含多個 myotomes / dermatomes 的 fibers，可造成較明顯 motor and sensory deficit。
- Routine NCS：主要量 large fast myelinated fibers；small-fiber pain、temperature、autonomic dysfunction 可在 routine NCS 正常時仍存在。
- Mixed nerve study：可包含 Ia muscle afferents，因此 conduction velocity 可比 routine motor 或 cutaneous sensory studies 更快。
- CMAP / SNAP / MUAP：多屬 volume-conducted near-field potentials；amplitude 受 source 到 electrode 距離影響。
- Sensory / mixed nerve waveform：常呈 triphasic waveform；onset latency 應在 first positive peak，若太小或缺失則用 initial negative deflection。
- Motor CMAP：active electrode 正確放在 motor point 時應有 initial negative deflection；若有 initial positive deflection，需先考慮 off-motor-point recording。
- Stimulus artifact：屬 far-field potential，可在不同刺激距離下同時出現，不代表 propagated nerve response。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 報告中遇到 sensory symptoms + normal SNAP，應把 proximal-to-DRG lesion 放進 localization reasoning，而不是直接排除 sensory pathology。
- Small-fiber symptoms 不能用 normal routine NCS 排除；若臨床懷疑 small-fiber neuropathy，需另行設計 appropriate testing。
- 判讀 demyelination 前，應理解 routine latency / velocity 主要代表 fastest large myelinated fibers；amplitude / area 則更接近 activated fiber or muscle population。
- Motor waveform 出現 unexpected initial positivity 時，應先檢查 motor point / electrode placement，再解讀為 pathology。
- Mixed nerve study 的敏感性與速度差異要從 fiber composition 解釋，不能和 routine sensory / motor study 混成同一類數字。

## Fact

- EDX study 是 clinical examination 的延伸。
- DRG 位於 spinal cord 外、靠近 intervertebral foramen。
- Anterior horn cell axons 形成 motor roots；DRG central projections 形成 sensory roots，peripheral projections 成為 peripheral sensory fibers。
- Motor and sensory roots distal to DRG unite to form mixed spinal nerve。
- Dorsal and ventral rami both contain motor and sensory fibers。
- Adjacent myotomes and dermatomes overlap substantially。
- Routine motor and sensory NCS latency / velocity measurements reflect the largest and fastest fibers of the studied nerve。
- Standard NCS does not record small myelinated A-delta / B fibers or unmyelinated C fibers。
- Human peripheral myelinated fibers typically conduct around 35-75 m/s；unmyelinated fibers conduct around 0.2-1.5 m/s。
- A motor unit includes one anterior horn cell, its axon, and all muscle fibers it innervates。
- CMAPs, SNAPs, and MUAPs are extracellular recordings of intracellular electrical events through volume conduction。
- Near-field potential amplitude depends on source-electrode distance。

## Inference

- EDX interpretation should explicitly state whether a conclusion is based on anatomic localization, large-fiber physiology, motor unit sampling, or recording morphology.
- Normal routine NCS is a statement about recorded large fibers, not a global statement that peripheral nerve function is normal.
- Off-motor-point recording can mimic abnormal waveform morphology, so technical review belongs before disease labeling.

## Assumption

- The lab uses technically valid recording and stimulation setup.
- The interpreter has access to appropriate normal values and knows which fiber population each test samples.
- The clinical question is specific enough to guide root / plexus / nerve / muscle selection.

## Uncertainty

- This source does not provide disease-specific sensitivity / specificity.
- Routine EDX cannot fully characterize small-fiber or autonomic pathology.
- Waveform morphology may remain ambiguous if electrode placement, anatomy, edema, or artifact cannot be adequately controlled.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀：「Normal SNAP 排除 sensory lesion。」正確是 proximal-to-DRG lesion 可保留 SNAP。
- 誤讀：「Normal routine NCS 排除 small-fiber neuropathy。」正確是 standard NCS 不記錄 A-delta、B、C fibers。
- 誤讀：「Root lesion 應該造成 anesthesia。」正確是 dermatomal overlap 讓單一 root lesion 通常只造成 altered or decreased sensation。
- 誤讀：「Waveform morphology 都是病理。」正確是 volume conduction 與 electrode position 會改變 morphology。
- 誤讀：「Mixed、sensory、motor conduction velocity 可直接互相比。」正確是 fiber composition 不同，mixed nerve studies 可包含更快的 Ia afferents。
