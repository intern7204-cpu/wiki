# Nurturing Care 健康與營養服務整合

## One-Sentence Definition

`Nurturing care` 健康與營養服務整合，是把 responsive caregiving、early learning、safety and security 與 caregiver well-being 放進 antenatal、postnatal、well-child、sick-child、inpatient、home visit 與 nutritional rehabilitation 的日常接觸，而不是另外開一條孤立的發展服務。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁討論的是 service workflow，不是重新定義整個 [[早期發展與終身健康]]。
- 核心場域是 maternal、newborn、child health 與 nutrition services。
- 主要對象是 managers 與 service providers。
- 本頁整合 guideline 與 implementation guide：[[WHO_ECD_Guideline_0至3歲Nurturing_Care建議]] 提供 recommendation 層級，practice guide 提供服務現場 workflow。
- 它不是替代訓練課程，也不是單一 programme manual；它是把 nurturing care framework 轉成服務現場可做的 implementation approach。
- Specialist mental health、child protection、rehabilitation、developmental disability service 仍需 referral network；primary care 不能假裝全包。

## Why It Matters

- Health and nutrition services 本來就頻繁接觸孕婦、嬰幼兒與照顧者。
- 若這些接觸只處理 growth、immunization、feeding、acute illness 或 medication，就會漏掉早期發展中可被觀察、示範與練習的 caregiving input。
- 最可操作的轉換是：把每次接觸變成 provider observe -> model -> praise -> coach -> problem-solve -> refer 的短迴圈。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Facility 要可近、歡迎兒童與照顧者，並有安全、可清潔的 play / counselling 空間。
- Provider 需要 interpersonal communication skill：open-ended question、active listening、observation、praise、empathy、shared decision-making、coaching。
- Protocols 需要納入 developmental monitoring、home environment observation、caregiver mental / physical health、safety risk 與 referral triggers。
- Managers 需要處理 provider time、training、mentoring、turnover、supervision checklist 與 referral mapping。
- 有 safety、abuse、severe caregiver mental health 或 developmental disability concern 時，要有 escalation pathway。

## Mechanism

```text
Routine contact in health / nutrition service
-> provider sees caregiver-child interaction and caregiver load
-> provider models responsive interaction and coaches a small practice
-> caregiver practises in a real context with praise and problem-solving
-> home routines include more responsive care, play, communication, safety, and self-care
-> child has better developmental input while health / nutrition needs are also managed
```

## Observable Patterns

- Antenatal care：問 caregiver 對 baby 的感受、擔心、胎動回應、父親或其他照顧者參與、second-hand smoke、safe water 與居家準備。
- Postnatal care：利用 breastfeeding / holding / soothing 觀察與 coaching eye contact、soft voice、touch、hunger cue recognition 與 father involvement。
- Well-child care：把 weighing、immunization、growth monitoring、home health record 與 waiting-room playbox 轉成 play / communication / screen-time / safety discussion。
- Sick-child care：把 responsive caregiving 連到 illness sign recognition、feeding during illness、medicine administration、comforting 與 follow-up。
- Inpatient care：避免不必要 caregiver-child separation，支持 caregiver rooming-in、play、talking、feeding、comforting、discharge readiness。
- Nutritional rehabilitation：結合 responsive feeding、play、communication 與 caregiver support，因為 poor nutrition 與 developmental delay 常互相放大。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 臨床紀錄若只寫「衛教發展刺激」，仍太空泛；較好的寫法是記錄 provider 實際觀察到的 caregiver-child interaction、示範了什麼、caregiver 練習了什麼、下一次追蹤什麼。
- Program design 不應只問「要不要篩檢 milestone」，還要問 referral network、provider coaching skill、supervision、family access 與 caregiver well-being support 是否存在。
- 對 low-resource 或 humanitarian context，低成本 play material、brief counselling、poster / radio / text message 可作為補充，但不能完全取代面對面的 skill coaching。
- WHO 2020 guideline 將 first-3-years responsive caregiving、early learning、nutrition integration 與 maternal mental health psychosocial support 列為 strong recommendations；但 programme dose、active ingredient 與 long-term outcome 仍需保守處理。
- Hirve et al. 2023 支持把部分 early development intervention 放進 health-system delivery：HCP-ECD 對 0-36 months cognitive / motor outcomes 與 HOME inventory 有正向 pooled signal；但 maternal mental health、language、socioemotional、behaviour outcome 不明確，EF / adaptive function 未被報告。

## Fact

- Nurturing care 包含 good health、adequate nutrition、safety and security、opportunities for early learning、responsive caregiving 五個 interrelated components。
- 來源指出 health and nutrition services 常已涵蓋 good health 與 adequate nutrition，但 responsive caregiving、early learning、safety and security 常未被充分辨識。
- Responsive caregiving 是 caregiver notice、understand、respond to child signals 的能力，並被來源視為 supporting other components 的 foundational capacity。
- The guide uses universal、targeted、indicated 三層 support。
- The guide explicitly states that simply encouraging primary care providers to assess developmental milestones is unlikely to be effective when referral networks and indicated supports are not ready.
- Managers can modify facilities, protocols, training, supervision, caregiver-participation policies, crisis adaptation, and referral maps.
- Providers can use antenatal、postnatal、well-child、sick-child、inpatient、home visit、nutritional rehabilitation contacts to strengthen caregiving practice and caregiver well-being.
- WHO 2020 guideline recommends responsive care and early learning during the first 3 years of life, integration of responsive care and early learning into optimal nutrition interventions, and integration of psychosocial maternal mental health interventions into ECD services.
- WHO 2020 declined to make a recommendation for caregiving interventions specifically targeting socioemotional and behavioural development because evidence was insufficient and HIC-only.
- HCP-ECD review 中，eligible interventions 由 primary-level healthcare providers 面對面交付，常見場域是 home visit；內容包含 responsive caregiving、early learning support 或 motor stimulation。
- HCP-ECD review 未發現 visit-number dose-response；唯一 subgroup signal 是 ECD-predominant interventions 比 non-ECD-predominant interventions 有較大 cognitive effect，但 interpretation 受 heterogeneity 與 power 限制。

## Inference

- 在服務設計上，nurturing care 的最小可行單位不是一張衛教單，而是一次 provider-assisted practice。
- 如果 provider 無法 observe caregiver-child interaction，就很難知道要 coach 哪個 skill。
- 若 caregiver mental health、sleep、family support 與安全風險未被納入，要求照顧者「多互動、多陪玩」可能只會增加負荷。

## Assumption

- Provider 有時間與訓練做 observation / coaching。
- Facility 或 community 至少有基本 referral map。
- Suggested practices 已依家庭語言、文化、disability access 與資源條件調整。

## Uncertainty

- 來源是 practice guide，沒有為每項建議提供 GRADE certainty。
- WHO 2020 guideline 雖提供 strong recommendations，但 early learning、nutrition integration 與 maternal mental health evidence block 的 outcome certainty 不均一，不能把所有 child outcomes 都寫成 high-certainty。
- HCP-ECD systematic review 對 cognitive outcome 的 certainty low；motor outcome certainty 在 abstract 與 results section 標示不完全一致，因此應保守描述。
- 不同 health system 的 staffing、financing、space、training capacity 差異會大幅影響可行性。
- 在 humanitarian / health crisis context，remote communication 可補強資訊傳遞，但對 skill acquisition 的效果可能不如面對面 coaching。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：有做 growth monitoring 就等於支持 nurturing care。錯；growth monitoring 要連到 caregiver coaching、developmental monitoring、feeding interaction 與 referral。
- 誤讀 2：設 play corner 就完成 early learning intervention。錯；play space 需要 trained staff / volunteer 示範、清潔、安全與 caregiver practice。
- 誤讀 3：問 milestone 就是 developmental care。錯；沒有 referral capacity 與 family-centred support 時，篩檢資訊可能無法轉成幫助。
- 誤讀 4：照顧者健康是 adult issue。錯；來源將 caregiver well-being 明確放進 child nurturing care support。
