# MMC 運動神經功能層級與步行預後

## 一句話定義

MMC 的 mobility prognosis 主要不是由影像上 defect 高低單獨決定，而是由 **functional motor level、quadriceps/iliopsoas strength、sitting balance、spasticity/flaccidity、以及後續 shunt/tethered cord/obesity/contracture burden** 共同塑造。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁聚焦：
  - motor level-based prognosis
  - Hoffer ambulation logic
  - FMS longitudinal tracking
  - bracing / assistive-device implication
- 這頁不處理：
  - prenatal surgery selection
  - bladder and bowel program
  - hydrocephalus differential

## Why It Matters

- 家庭最早會問的常常是：`Will my child walk?`
- 若回答只用「可能會」或「大概不會」，沒有 functional framework，臨床價值很低。
- motor level 預後會直接改變：
  - orthosis prescription
  - wheelchair planning
  - home/school participation expectation
  - long-term energy expenditure discussion

## Preconditions or Conditions

- anatomic lesion level 有用，但 functional level 可能比 expected 好，也可能左右不對稱。
- spasticity or flaccidity 會改變 function。
- mobility can decline with growth, obesity, shunt failure, tethered cord, contracture.

## Mechanism

```text
lower motor level burden + stronger hip flexor / quadriceps + better sitting balance
→ better standing / transfer / ambulation potential
but
secondary complications over time
→ mobility may decline even if early childhood walking was achieved
```

## Observable Patterns

- lower thoracic lesion：
  - childhood 多為 therapeutic standing / nonfunctional ambulation
  - adulthood typically complete wheelchair reliance
- high lumbar lesion：
  - distance ambulation largely wheelchair dependent
  - may have limited household ambulation
- midlumbar lesion：
  - household ambulation or limited community ambulation
  - adolescence/adulthood often partial wheelchair reliance
- low lumbar / sacral lesion：
  - more likely community ambulation
  - adulthood may still need crutch/cane or show reduced endurance / pain with distance

## Clinical / Research Implication

- early upright mobility tools like parapodium or RGO 的價值，不只在走路本身，也在 peer interaction 與 cognitive/psychosocial opportunity。
- 但本章也明說，parapodium / RGO 多半不會一路用到 adolescence/adulthood。
- 長期追蹤不能只問 `still walking?`，要用工具看 decline pattern。

## Fact

- most individuals with sacral lesion are community ambulators。
- most with thoracic lesion are nonambulators。
- most important predictors of ambulation are quadriceps strength, iliopsoas strength, and sitting balance。
- Hoffer ambulation categories divide function into:
  - community ambulators
  - household ambulators
  - nonfunctional ambulators
  - nonambulators
- Functional Mobility Scale (FMS) can track mobility over time and help detect meaningful decline。

## Inference

- 如果 child 的 FMS 或 real-world mobility 下降，不能先假設只是長大變懶；在 MMC，要主動找 treatable complication。

## Assumption

- orthosis / assistive device prescription 有搭配 goal setting，而不是只為了追求「看起來比較像 walking」。

## Uncertainty

- 本章提供的 prognostic grouping 是非常實用的 bedside framework，但並不是精準個體預測模型。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不要把 early therapeutic ambulation 誤讀成 future functional community ambulation。
- 不要把 anatomic level 直接當成 final function，不看 actual muscle testing、sitting balance 與 secondary complications。
