# MMC 神經性膀胱與腎功能保護

## 一句話定義

在 MMC，神經性膀胱管理的第一原則不是先追 continence，而是 **以 renal preservation 為上位目標，及早建立 baseline imaging + urodynamics + creatinine + proactive CIC / pharmacologic plan，避免 renal scarring 與 chronic kidney disease**。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁聚焦：
  - neurogenic bladder in MMC
  - renal-protective surveillance
  - CIC / medication / detrusor botulinum logic
- 這頁不處理：
  - general neurogenic bowel algorithm
  - fetal surgery selection
  - detailed adult urology follow-up interval

## Why It Matters

- up to 98% of children with lumbosacral MMC show signs or symptoms of neurogenic bladder。
- untreated neurogenic bladder 可導致：
  - urinary incontinence
  - UTI
  - vesicoureteral reflux
  - renal scarring
  - eventual renal failure

## Preconditions or Conditions

- all children with MMC should undergo baseline urodynamic evaluation。
- renal ultrasound is recommended to detect hydronephrosis or abnormal anatomy。
- in neonatal period, renal/bladder ultrasound, urodynamics, and serum creatinine should occur within 3 months of birth。

## Mechanism

```text
MMC-related neurogenic bladder
→ abnormal sensation / storage / detrusor behavior
→ high bladder pressure, retention, reflux, infection, incontinence
→ renal scarring and chronic kidney disease risk
```

## Observable Patterns

- children may have reduced or absent bladder sensation。
- detrusor areflexia and hyperreflexia are both seen。
- only 4 of 112 children in one cited cohort had normal bladder function。
- 4%–9% of infants with MMC show high-grade hydronephrosis on ultrasound within the first year。
- 10%–30% of children with MMC are born with evidence of renal disease。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- proactive treatment is preferred over waiting for obvious complication。
- CIC is the typical preventive backbone。
- caregivers need training early; self-catheterization should be taught as independence allows。

## Fact

- goals of treatment are to preserve renal function and improve bladder function / continence。
- CIC initiation indications in neonatal period include:
  - hydronephrosis
  - vesicoureteral reflux
  - inability to void
- anticholinergics reduce intravesical pressure；oxybutynin is commonly used despite age-related approval limits。
- mirabegron can improve voided volume, continence, and compliance with bladder program。
- this source states there is no evidence that alpha blockers help neurogenic bladder management in children with MMC。
- detrusor botulinum toxin A can improve continence, bladder capacity, and maximum cystometric capacity, but effect is temporary。
- catheterizable channels such as Mitrofanoff can improve access and quality of life。

## Inference

- 在 MMC，`孩子現在沒抱怨` 不能當作 bladder safe 的證據，因為 sensation 本身可能就不足。
- continence improvement 若犧牲 upper tract safety，不是成功治療。

## Assumption

- family 能取得 trained CIC teaching、follow-up imaging、與 pediatric urology coordination。

## Uncertainty

- 這篇來源沒有給出完整 standardized surveillance interval，也沒有處理所有 medication sequencing 的當代 guideline 差異。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不要把 incontinence 當成本頁唯一 outcome；腎功能保護才是上位目標。
- 不要把 baseline normal creatinine 當作後續不需 surveillance。
- 不要把這頁當成 bowel program page；bowel 需要另做單一概念頁。
