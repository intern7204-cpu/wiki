# Disability-Inclusive Nurturing Care：發展遲緩與發展障礙兒童

## One-Sentence Definition

`Disability-inclusive nurturing care` 是把發展遲緩或 developmental disability 兒童納入 [[Nurturing_Care_健康與營養服務整合]] 的完整五構面，同時用 individualized、family-centred、coordinated care 移除服務與參與障礙。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理的是 `nurturing care` 如何納入 children at risk of or with developmental delays and disabilities。
- 它不是 [[發展遲緩與Intellectual_Disability]] 的 etiologic workup 頁，也不是 CP、hearing loss、ASD 或 ID 的 condition-specific treatment guideline。
- 核心問題不是「孩子有沒有診斷」，而是「孩子與照顧者是否能取得 health、nutrition、safety、responsive caregiving、early learning 與 participation support」。
- 來源最適用於 5 歲以下兒童與其照顧者，也可作為 early childhood policy / programme design 的 disability-inclusion framework。
- 本頁證據層級是官方 thematic brief；它可支撐 service design 與概念框架，不能單獨決定治療劑量、診斷門檻或 programme effectiveness。

## Why It Matters

- 發展遲緩或 developmental disability 兒童常因 poverty、discrimination、limited access to services、caregiver support 不足而錯失 nurturing care。
- 依 ICF 邏輯，disability 不只是 impairment；attitudinal、physical、systemic barrier 會讓 impairment 轉成更大的 participation restriction。
- 若服務只提供診斷或單點 therapy，卻沒有處理 caregiver stress、referral navigation、inclusive childcare / early learning、assistive technology、stigma 與日常 routines，child 的實際參與仍可能被卡住。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 服務系統採取 twin-track approach：所有兒童都能使用 inclusive universal services，另對需要更多支持者提供 targeted / indicated services。
- Provider 能以 family-centred approach 聽取 caregiver concerns，不把照顧者當成被動執行者。
- 有 coordinated care：case management、transdisciplinary team、referral / follow-up system 與跨部門資訊交換。
- 早期支持不以 formal diagnosis 為前提；已經觀察到 developmental risk 或 family need 時，就要開始 family-centred support。
- 服務設計必須處理 accessibility、language、culture、poverty、caregiver mental health、transportation、cost、AT availability 與 community stigma。

## Mechanism

```text
Child with developmental delay / disability
-> faces impairment plus social, physical, and service barriers
-> caregiver faces stress, information gaps, cost, isolation, and navigation burden
-> ordinary nurturing care opportunities shrink
-> child receives less responsive caregiving, early learning, safety, nutrition support, and participation

Disability-inclusive nurturing care
-> inclusive universal services + targeted / indicated support
-> caregiver receives coaching, mental health support, peer / social support, and clear referrals
-> barriers to daily routines and participation decrease
-> child can use more health, learning, communication, play, feeding, mobility, and social participation opportunities
```

## Observable Patterns

- Universal services actively ask why families are not using clinics, early learning spaces, childcare, worship spaces, parks, libraries, or community programmes.
- Developmental monitoring goes beyond milestone boxes and includes child functioning, home environment, caregiver-child interaction, risk factors, strengths, and referral needs.
- ECI services are family-centred, strength-based, home- and routine-grounded, multisectoral, integrated, and transdisciplinary.
- Transdisciplinary teams help families set individualized goals, understand rights, coordinate referrals, and use daily activities as intervention contexts.
- Assistive technology access includes product, fitting, training, maintenance, affordability, and caregiver support.
- Caregiver-mediated intervention teaches practical communication, play, movement, responsive feeding, self-care, peer support, and inclusion strategies.
- Community-level work reduces stigma and modifies spaces so children can participate rather than remain hidden or isolated.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Clinical note 應避免只寫「已衛教早療」；較有用的是記錄 family barriers、caregiver concerns、child participation、provider coaching、referral plan 與 follow-up responsibility。
- 對 developmental concern，沒有 formal diagnosis 不應成為 family-centred support 的等待理由；同時仍需依 red flags 轉介適當 medical / rehabilitation / developmental evaluation。
- Programme evaluation 不應只看 referral count；應追蹤 families 是否真的完成 referral、取得 AT、改善 caregiver confidence、降低 stigma / access barriers，並增加 child participation。
- 研究上要分清楚：本 thematic brief 支持 disability-inclusive framework，但 CST、LEAP-CP、Baby Ubuntu、AAC project 等 programme effectiveness 需回到各自原始研究。

## Fact

- Nurturing care 包含 good health、adequate nutrition、safety and security、opportunities for early learning、responsive caregiving。
- 來源將 responsive caregiving 視為支持其他 nurturing care components 的 foundational component。
- 來源定義 developmental delay 為對實際或校正年齡 expected milestones 的 significant variation。
- 來源定義 developmental disability 為影響 developing nervous system 並造成 motor、cognitive、language、behaviour 或 sensory functioning impairment 的 health conditions，且 barriers / context 會限制 participation。
- 來源明確採用 ICF 觀點：disability 不是 health condition 本身，而是 impairment 與 societal、physical、environmental barriers 的互動結果。
- 來源列出三個 guiding principles：individualized care、family-centred care、coordinated care。
- 來源主張 services 應包含 universal、targeted、indicated 三層，並以 twin-track approach 並行 mainstream inclusion 與額外支持。
- 來源指出 early identification 能促成 timely intervention，但不應要求 diagnosis 先完成才提供 family-centred services。
- 來源描述 ECI services 最好是 family-centred、strengths-based、home-based、grounded in daily routines，且 delivery planning 與 caregivers 共同完成。
- 來源指出 caregivers 的 physical / mental health、safety 與資源可近性會影響其照顧能力。

## Inference

- `Disability-inclusive nurturing care` 的最小單位不是「轉早療」四個字，而是把 child function、caregiver load、access barriers、日常 routines 與 referral follow-up 接起來。
- 如果 universal service 沒有 accessibility 與 provider bias training，它實際上不是 universal。
- Caregiver coaching 和 caregiver mental health support 是 child intervention 的一部分，不是附加福利。
- AT 若沒有訓練與維護，只把產品交給家庭通常不足以改變 participation。

## Assumption

- 當地有可用的 health、rehabilitation、education、social protection、child protection 或 community resources，或至少有明確建置計畫。
- Provider 有足夠訓練、時間與 supervision 做 observation、coaching、referral coordination。
- 家庭能以其語言、文化與資源條件理解並使用服務。
- Programme design 會納入 children with disabilities、caregivers、disabled persons' organizations 與 community actors 的聲音。

## Uncertainty

- 本來源不是 formal guideline，未為每項 action 提供 GRADE certainty。
- 來源提到的 country examples 和 specific programmes 不能在未讀原始研究時視為效果已確立。
- 不同國家的 workforce、financing、insurance、transportation、AT market、早療制度與 stigma pattern 差異很大。
- 對 long-term functioning、family quality of life、cost-effectiveness、deinstitutionalization safety 與 caregiver mental health outcome 的確切效果仍需原始研究或 systematic review 支持。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：有 referral list 就等於 coordinated care。錯；coordinated care 還需要 case management、follow-up、team communication 與家庭可理解的下一步。
- 誤讀 2：孩子有 disability，所以 nurturing care 只剩 rehabilitation。錯；health、nutrition、safety、responsive caregiving、early learning 仍同時存在。
- 誤讀 3：沒有 diagnosis 就不能開始支持。錯；來源明確反對把 diagnosis requirement 當成 family-centred support 的前置門檻。
- 誤讀 4：把責任交給 caregiver 就完成 caregiver-mediated intervention。錯；caregiver 需要技能、資源、mental health support、peer support 與服務可近性。
- 誤讀 5：universal service 天然公平。錯；若有 stigma、language、transport、cost、physical accessibility 或 provider bias barrier，universal service 會實際排除高需求家庭。
