# MMC 胎兒修補與功能結果

## 一句話定義

MMC 胎兒修補不是「越早關起來越好」這麼簡單，而是 **在嚴格篩選條件下，於 26 週前進行的 fetal intervention，用較高的 maternal / prematurity risk 去交換較好的 child motor、shunt、hindbrain herniation 與後續部分 self-care / bladder outcome**。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁聚焦：
  - prenatal screening
  - MOMS / MOMS2-style prenatal repair outcome
  - maternal-fetal-child trade-off
- 這頁不處理：
  - lifelong bladder protocol
  - shunt malfunction differential
  - detailed ambulation classification

## Why It Matters

- prenatal diagnosis 會直接改變：
  - parental counseling
  - fetal surgery eligibility discussion
  - delivery planning
  - neonatal team readiness
- prenatal repair 不是只改影像，而是會改 school-age mobility 與部分 self-care trajectory。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- gold standard screening / diagnosis：fetal ultrasound at 18–22 weeks gestation。
- maternal serum AFP screening window：15–22 weeks，open NTD detection rate 約 65%–80%。
- fetal MRI 可在 fetal surgery consideration 時幫助 delineate defect。
- MOMS trial prenatal group inclusion criteria 包括：
  - surgery before 26 weeks
  - uncomplicated singleton pregnancy
  - isolated MMC
  - normal karyotype
  - maternal BMI < 35 kg/m2
  - no additional major structural anomaly

## Mechanism

```text
earlier in-utero closure of open MMC
→ less ongoing neural exposure / CSF leakage / hindbrain distortion burden
→ less shunt need and better later motor outcome
but
maternal uterine surgery + premature delivery risk increase
```

## Observable Patterns

- prenatal group：
  - less hindbrain herniation
  - lower shunt placement
  - better motor scores
  - more independent walking
  - less bracing / assistive-device burden at school age
- trade-offs：
  - more prematurity
  - more maternal surgical complications
  - more later tethered cord release

## Clinical / Research Implication

- prenatal repair 應被表述為 **selected-risk exchange**，不是 universal upgrade。
- 若家庭只聽到「walk more independently」，但沒同步聽到 prematurity / maternal hysterotomy burden，就不是完整 counseling。

## Fact

- MOMS trial prenatal repair group had lower Arnold-Chiari II presence than postnatal group（64% vs 96%）。
- shunt placement rate was lower in prenatal group（40% vs 82%）。
- no difference in infant mortality at 30 months。
- prenatal group had better Bayley / Peabody motor outcome and more independent ambulation。
- average gestational age at delivery in prenatal group was 34.1 weeks。
- maternal complications occurred in about one-third, including dehiscence / thinning of hysterotomy site。
- by school age, shunt placement remained lower in prenatal group（49% vs 85%）and shunt revision lower（23% vs 60%）。
- by school age, CIC requirement was lower in prenatal group（61.5% vs 87.2%）and volitional voiding higher（24% vs 4.2%）。
- tethered cord release was more common in prenatal group by school age（27% vs 15%）。

## Inference

- prenatal repair 改善 child outcome 的訊號是真實存在的，但代價不是小到可以忽略，因此 suitability 必須建立在 high-selectivity program，而不是單純「有 MMC 就修」。

## Assumption

- counseling 能同時涵蓋 maternal risk、future pregnancy consequence、child functional outcome、以及 center experience。

## Uncertainty

- fetoscopic 與 hybrid approach 的 long-term comparative effectiveness 尚未在本章形成 clear standard。
- urologic improvement 雖存在，但是否足以改變 long-term reconstructive surgery burden，這篇來源並未證實。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不要把 prenatal repair 說成 cure。
- 不要把 MOMS trial 的結果直接外推到所有 lesion、所有 maternal condition、所有 center。
- 不要只看 30 個月 outcome；school-age follow-up 仍有新增 trade-off，例如 tethered cord release。
