# MMC 水腦分流與Chiari II警訊

## 一句話定義

在 MMC，hydrocephalus、VP shunt、與 Chiari II malformation 不是附帶名詞，而是 **直接決定 airway safety、swallowing、認知、neurologic stability 與死亡風險的核心 surveillance axis**。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁聚焦：
  - hydrocephalus burden
  - shunt / ETV-CPC strategy
  - symptomatic Chiari II red flags
  - shunt malfunction recognition
- 這頁不處理：
  - fetal repair candidacy
  - bladder protocol
  - general mobility prognosis

## Why It Matters

- symptomatic Arnold-Chiari II malformation 是 MMC infant leading cause of death。
- shunt failure 沒有 single pathognomonic finding，延遲辨識的代價很高。
- airway sign，尤其 stridor，不是耳鼻喉小問題，而可能是 threatened brainstem / airway sign。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- hydrocephalus may be clinically silent at birth。
- ongoing collaboration among caregivers and clinicians is required across childhood and adolescence。
- symptomatic Chiari II must prompt urgent evaluation and treatment。

## Mechanism

```text
MMC-associated hindbrain / CSF-flow abnormality
→ hydrocephalus and Chiari II burden
→ shunt dependence or CSF diversion need
→ shunt failure / progressive brainstem dysfunction
→ airway, swallowing, neurologic, and cognitive deterioration
```

## Observable Patterns

- clinically apparent hydrocephalus at birth：only about 10%
- hydrocephalus manifesting within first week：about 85%
- neonatal VP shunt need historically up to 80%
- symptomatic Chiari II red flags：
  - poor secretion control
  - inspiratory stridor
  - apnea
  - bradycardia
  - dysphagia
  - cyanosis
  - nystagmus
  - vocal cord paralysis
  - hypotonia
  - opisthotonus
  - new/worse spasticity
  - upper limb weakness
  - torticollis
- shunt failure common symptoms：
  - headache
  - nausea/vomiting
  - neck pain
  - fatigue
  - irritability / lethargy
  - change in school performance
  - swelling / redness along shunt tract
  - in MMC, low back and hip pain may mimic tethered cord

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 新的 stridor、apnea、bulging fontanelle、mental-status change，不應先被當成 nonspecific illness。
- symptomatic Chiari II 時，作者主張應先檢查 hydrocephalus / shunt failure；必要時再做 posterior fossa decompression。

## Fact

- historically 75%–80% of patients with MMC required shunt placement。
- ETV-CPC overall success in HCRN data is about 45%–50%。
- ventricles tend to remain larger after ETV-CPC than after shunt placement。
- hydromyelia / syringomyelia may worsen with hydrocephalus or shunt failure and can present with pain, numbness, weakness, scoliosis, spasticity, hand decline, or dysesthesia。

## Inference

- 對 MMC patient，任何新的 neurologic / airway / functional decline，都必須把 shunt-related problem 放進前段 differential，而不是等到影像非常典型才想起來。

## Assumption

- family 已知 child 的個人化 shunt-failure pattern，因為本章明確指出很多 child 的 presentation 會有自己獨特表現。

## Uncertainty

- 哪些 less-severe hydrocephalus child 最適合 ETV-CPC 而非 shunt，這篇來源沒有給足夠細的 stratification algorithm。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不要把 absent classic symptom 當成 shunt failure excluded。
- 不要把 new low back / hip pain 直接當 orthopaedic issue；在 MMC，這也可能是 shunt-related presentation。
