# Preschool SR/EF Intervention Design 特徵

## One-Sentence Definition

Preschool SR/EF intervention design 的核心不是選一個看起來流行的 programme label，而是把目標 outcome、incremental cognitive challenge、movement / engagement、feedback、small-group fit、teacher training、fidelity 與 sustainability 對齊。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁討論 3-6 歲 typically developing preschool children 的 self-regulation / executive function intervention design。
- 不是 ADHD、ASD、ID、language disorder 或 trauma-specific treatment guideline。
- 不把 play、mindfulness、SEL、physical activity、curriculum 或 digital training 任一類寫成全域最佳。
- 與 [[Preschool_EF_Cognitive_Training與Transfer]] 不同，本頁涵蓋 preschool classroom 中較廣義的 SR/EF approaches。

## Core Design Logic

```text
Target SR/EF outcome
-> select activity vehicle
-> build incremental cognitive challenge
-> add engagement, movement, social co-regulation, and feedback
-> train / coach the interventionist
-> monitor fidelity and dose
-> measure both acute gains and real-world transfer / maintenance
```

## What The Source Supports

- Preschool SR/EF abilities can be intentionally enhanced, but effect depends on target, content, implementation, context, interventionist, and measurement.
- Some approaches had comparatively more consistent signals: mindfulness、mediated structured play、physical activity。
- Features seen more often in high-efficacy interventions included cognitive challenge、movement、interventionist expertise、fidelity / professional development、practice opportunity and feedback。
- Physical activity support is strongest when activity is cognitively injected：child has to remember rules, inhibit prepotent responses, shift, coordinate with peers, or solve a movement problem。
- Small groups may work well because they preserve social interaction / co-regulation while allowing more individualized challenge than whole-class curricula.
- Digital task training should be framed narrowly: the most consistent signal was for working memory, not broad SR/EF or school readiness.

## Fact

- Muir et al. 2023 included 85 studies and 12,595 children aged 3-6 years.
- 59 studies targeted EF only, 17 targeted SR only, and 9 targeted both SR and EF.
- Median intervention dose was 9 hours, but dose ranged from 15 minutes to year-long curricula.
- 60 studies (70.6%) reported statistically significant results for at least one outcome.
- Across 208 evaluated effects, 102 (49.0%) were significant immediately after the intervention.
- Only four studies included follow-up assessments several months after intervention.
- Movement appeared in 53 studies (62.35%); 77.4% of movement-including interventions achieved one or more significant effects.
- Physical activity interventions had 14 significant effects out of 20 evaluated effects.
- Mindfulness interventions had 17 significant effects out of 26 evaluated effects.
- Mediated structured play had 26 significant effects out of 41 evaluated effects.
- Digital task training had 7 significant effects out of 21 evaluated effects.
- The review did not conduct meta-analysis because intervention heterogeneity was high and the aim was to identify effective characteristics rather than pooled average effect.

## Inference

- 若 clinical / school concern 是 classroom participation，不應只買 working-memory app；需要把 EF demand 放回 group routine、feedback、teacher scaffold 與 daily practice。
- 若 intervention 只有 cognitive challenge 但沒有 engagement、feedback、context carryover 或 teacher fidelity，transfer 可能很弱。
- 對 preschool child，movement + rule + social turn-taking 常比靜態 worksheet 更符合 SR/EF practice 的 developmental ecology。

## Assumption

- Preschool classroom 有可執行的小組或全班活動時間。
- Teacher 或 facilitator 有足夠 training、coaching 與 fidelity support。
- Outcome measure 能區分 task-based EF gain、teacher-rated behaviour change 與真正 participation transfer。

## Uncertainty

- Long-term maintenance 與 fadeout 不清楚。
- 不同 intervention approach 的直接比較不足，不能做硬性排名。
- Child baseline、sex、income、language background 等 moderators 在來源中訊息不一致。
- Fidelity reporting 不一致，導致很難知道失敗是 intervention 無效，還是 implementation 不足。

## Clinical / Educational Use

- 先問 target：要改善 inhibitory control、working memory、cognitive flexibility、planning、behavioural SR，還是 classroom participation。
- 再問 activity vehicle：play、movement、mindfulness、SEL、curriculum、music、math、language 或 digital task training。
- 接著檢查 active ingredients：是否有逐步增加難度、明確規則、turn-taking、stop-go、memory load、shifting、feedback、reflection。
- 最後看 implementation：誰帶、受過什麼 training、有沒有 coaching、fidelity 如何監測、是否能嵌入 daily preschool routine。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：某一類 approach 最好。錯；來源結論是沒有單一 approach 對所有情境都最佳。
- 誤讀 2：movement 越多越好。錯；較合理的機制是 cognitively engaging movement，而不是單純 activity volume。
- 誤讀 3：短期 post-test 變好就代表 school readiness 改善。錯；follow-up 很少，transfer outcome 需要另證。
- 誤讀 4：teacher-led intervention 低成本所以一定可行。錯；teacher PD、coaching、ratio、time、leadership support 與 fidelity 是核心限制。
