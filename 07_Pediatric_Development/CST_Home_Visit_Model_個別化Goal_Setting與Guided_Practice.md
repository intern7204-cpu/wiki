# CST Home Visit Model 個別化Goal Setting與Guided Practice

## One-Sentence Definition

CST home visit model 是把 CCI / FCI observation、caregiver interview、goal-setting worksheet、tailored coaching、target routines 與 independent practice planning 串成一個讓 CST group strategies 進入個別家庭日常 routines 的臨床執行迴圈。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只處理 WHO CST home visit guide for facilitators 中的三次 home visits。
- 它不是 CST efficacy review，也不是 ASD / GDD intervention guideline。
- 目標族群依來源為 2-9 歲、具 developmental delays or disabilities 的兒童 caregivers，特別聚焦 social / communication domain；child 不必已有正式 diagnosis。
- Home visit 的核心不是「到家裡關心一下」，而是用 home context 觀察 child / caregiver interaction，設定小步驟目標，coach caregiver practice，並把策略移到日常 play and home routines。

## Why It Matters

- Group session 可以教通用策略，但 child interests、caregiver style、home materials、family priorities、behavior triggers、practice barriers 都需要在 home visit 中被具體化。
- 若少了 home visit，CST 容易退化成「照顧者知道策略但不知道怎麼放進自己家」。
- Home visit 同時是 safety checkpoint：facilitator 需要注意 caregiver mental health、child maltreatment、material deprivation、family service needs，並在超出 CST 範圍時 consult supervisor or refer。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Facilitator 必須接受 CST practical training and supervision；reading the guide is not enough。
- Home visit 需要 goal-setting worksheet、CCI / FCI materials、已完成的前次 worksheets、caregiver home practice notes，以及可用的 service / support information。
- Home visit 2 preferably 排在 session 5 和 session 6 之間，因為它要在 communication sessions 後提供 coaching。
- CCI / FCI 觀察結果要回到 goal-setting and coaching decision，不可被誤當成獨立 diagnosis。
- 若 guide 不足、family has complex needs、suspected neglect or abuse、或 facilitator 尚在初期經驗階段，需 consult supervisor。

## Mechanism

```text
Home visit 1
-> caregiver interview + CCI + obligatory FCI
-> identify current child skills and caregiver spontaneous strategy use
-> set child targets, caregiver targets, and target routines

Home visit 2
-> review key messages and home practice
-> observe caregiver-child guided practice
-> coach and demonstrate selected strategies
-> revise targets when progress, mismatch, or barrier appears

Home visit 3
-> consolidate selected strategies
-> revise targets for longer-term goals
-> select routines for ongoing practice
-> discuss caregiver self-care, service supports, and independent practice
```

## Observable Patterns

- Home visit 1 before session 1：get to know the family、assess child / caregiver needs、interview caregiver、administer FCI、observe CCI、establish goals。
- Home visit 2 between sessions 5 and 6 preferred：review home practice、plan guided practice、observe caregiver-child interaction、coach caregiver、demonstrate selected strategies、revise child and caregiver targets。
- Home visit 3 after session 9：consolidate 2-3 key strategies、review progress and challenges、revise longer-term targets、select new or expanded routines、support independent practice。
- CCI 每次 home visit 使用，用來觀察 child communication、play、behavior、caregiver interaction style、progress and support needs。
- FCI 在 home visit 1 obligatory，在 home visit 3 optional，用來觀察 baseline and possible end-point child communication、play and behavior。
- Child targets 分為 communication、play、daily living skills、behaviour regulation。
- Caregiver targets 分為 environmental strategies、look and listen、routine building、understanding communication、responding to communication、teaching new skills、behaviour regulation、response to challenging behaviour、expanding routines。
- Target routines 包含三個 play routines 與三個 home routines；communication targets 會嵌入 play and home routines。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 臨床轉介 CST-like program 時，應確認是否保留三次 home visits、CCI / FCI observation、goal-setting worksheets、coached practice、target revision、supervision and referral pathways。
- 在評估服務 fidelity 時，不應只數 group sessions；還要看 home visit 2 是否在 communication modules 後進行，以及 caregiver 是否真的在 home routine 中接受 coaching。
- 對 developmental delay / disability child，home visit goals 應是小步驟、可觀察、可在家庭 routines 中練習；不能直接把 caregiver long-term hope 寫成短期 target。
- 若出現 suspected maltreatment、neglect、severe caregiver distress、material deprivation 或 health need，CST facilitator 應 consult supervisor and refer；CST 本身不是 child protection 或 mental health service 的替代品。

## Fact

- WHO CST home visit guide 是三次 home visits 的 facilitator reference manual，需搭配 specific training and supervision 使用。
- CST standard course structure 是 9 group sessions 加 3 home visits，由 trained and supervised non-specialist facilitators 執行。
- Home visit 1 在 session 1 前，目標是建立關係、了解 family、assess needs、observe interaction、establish goals。
- Home visit 2 preferably 排在 session 5 和 session 6 之間，目標是 re-evaluate goals and provide tailored support。
- Home visit 3 在 session 9 後，目標是 re-evaluate goals、provide tailored support、make longer-term plans and support independent practice。
- CCI 和 FCI 各為 12 minutes clinical tools；CCI 每次 home visit 使用，FCI 在 home visit 1 obligatory、home visit 3 optional。
- Clinical use 中 video-recording 對 monitoring and supervision 有幫助但非 obligatory；research purposes 中 video-recording may be required or is required depending on component。
- Goal-setting component uses caregiver interview and CCI / FCI observations to identify child targets and caregiver targets。
- Coaching component 是 adult learning methodology，facilitator 在 caregiver-child interaction 中主動協助、調整或示範；duration 可從數分鐘到 12-15 minutes。
- Home visits include assessment of family needs, including child / caregiver health, caregiver mental health, potential child maltreatment, and material deprivation.

## Inference

- Home visits 是 CST fidelity 的關鍵節點，因為它們把 group-level strategies 轉成 child-specific and caregiver-specific practice。
- CCI / FCI 最有用的位置是在 goal-setting and coaching loop 中；若只拍影片或填表而沒有修正 targets，會削弱其臨床功能。
- Target routines 是 intervention dose 的容器；同一個洗手、點心、收拾、玩具或家務 routine 可以同時承載 communication、play、adaptive skill、behavior regulation and caregiver strategy goals。
- 對高風險家庭，CST 的安全性取決於 supervisor consultation、referral mapping and child protection pathway，而不是 facilitator 個人能力。

## Assumption

- Facilitator 能從 observation 中辨識 child current level，而不把偶發或不穩定表現誤當 mastered skill。
- Caregiver 願意或能夠在 home routine 中接受練習與回饋；若 caregiver anxious、sad、overloaded 或被家人干擾，需要調整 coaching intensity。
- Home materials and routines 足以支撐至少部分 daily practice。

## Uncertainty

- 本來源沒有量化三次 home visits 各自對 outcome 的獨立貢獻。
- 不同文化、住家安全、隱私限制、家族權力結構或資源匱乏情境下，home visit 的最佳 adaptation 仍需 local evaluation。
- 若 child 有 severe aggression、self-injury、medical pain、sleep disorder、feeding problem、trauma or abuse concern，本來源不能取代 specialist evaluation or protection pathway。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：Home visit 是可有可無的追蹤。錯；來源把三次 home visits 放在 standard course structure 中，且用於 assessment、goal-setting、coaching and independent practice。
- 誤讀 2：CCI / FCI 是診斷工具。錯；來源描述它們是 clinical tools for observing interaction and informing goals，不是正式 developmental diagnosis。
- 誤讀 3：Goal-setting 就是問 caregiver 希望孩子最後變成什麼。錯；來源要求把 caregiver hopes 轉成符合 current skill level 的 small next steps。
- 誤讀 4：Coaching 就是 facilitator 示範給 caregiver 看。錯；coaching 的支持量需依 caregiver confidence、child response and task difficulty 調整，目標仍是 caregiver practice。
- 誤讀 5：如果發現 neglect or abuse，facilitator 自己處理即可。錯；來源要求 consult supervisor and referral，facilitators should not carry this responsibility by themselves。
