# WHO ECD Guideline 0至3歲 Nurturing Care 建議

## One-Sentence Definition

WHO 2020 的 0-3 歲 ECD guideline 把 [[Caregiving_Quality_作為早期介入靶點|responsive caregiving]]、early learning、nutrition-service integration 與 maternal mental health psychosocial support 列為改善 early childhood development 的核心 health-sector recommendation bundle。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這頁討論的是 WHO 2020 guideline 的 first-3-years ECD recommendations。
- 它是 guideline-level recommendation bundle，不是單一 parenting programme、單一訓練手冊或單一 clinical protocol。
- 適用對象是 infants / young children、parents / caregivers、health workers、programme managers、policy-makers 與 implementation partners。
- 它強化 [[Nurturing_Care_健康與營養服務整合]]，但不取代 formal [[Early_Intervention_總論]]、developmental evaluation、child protection 或 specialist mental health care。

## Core Recommendations

1. Responsive caregiving：所有 infants and children 在生命前三年都應接受 responsive care；parents and other caregivers 應被支持去提供 responsive care。Strength strong；certainty moderate。
2. Early learning：所有 infants and children 在生命前三年都應與 parents and other caregivers 一起有 early learning activities；caregivers 應被支持去參與。Strength strong；quality moderate。
3. Nutrition integration：optimal nutrition interventions 應納入 responsive care 與 early learning support。Strength strong；quality moderate。
4. Maternal mental health：support maternal mental health 的 psychosocial interventions 應整合進 early childhood health and development services。Strength strong；certainty moderate。

## Mechanism

```text
Routine health / nutrition / early-childhood contact
-> caregiver receives support for responsive care, play, communication, feeding interaction, and mental health
-> daily relationship becomes more responsive and developmentally stimulating
-> child receives better repeated developmental input while health and nutrition needs are handled
-> ECD outcomes may improve, especially cognition / motor / language signals in selected evidence blocks
```

## What The Evidence Actually Supports

- Responsive caregiving interventions improved ECD and caregiver outcomes in the guideline review, with no harmful impacts found, but long-term data were unavailable.
- Early learning interventions had modest pooled effects on cognitive development and motor development; language and behaviour-problem effects were not clearly significant.
- Combined caregiving and nutrition interventions in LMICs improved cognitive, language, and motor outcomes compared with standard care, and improved developmental outcomes compared with nutrition alone in several comparisons.
- Growth outcomes were mixed; HAZ and WAZ were generally not significantly improved, while some WHZ comparisons showed benefit.
- Psychosocial maternal mental health interventions improved maternal anxiety and depressive symptoms; child-development outcomes were less consistently reported.
- Caregiving interventions targeting socioemotional and behavioural development did not receive a WHO recommendation because the evidence was insufficient and from HICs only.

## Clinical / Service Use

- 在 well-child visit、home visit、nutrition counselling 或 nutritional rehabilitation 中，不應只寫「衛教」，而要寫出觀察到的 caregiver-child interaction、示範的 responsive care / early learning practice、caregiver 當場練習與下次追蹤點。
- 對 malnutrition 或 nutritional risk child，nutrition plan 應同時處理 responsive feeding、play、communication 與 caregiver support。
- 對 caregiver depressive / anxiety symptoms，ECD service 需要 psychosocial support pathway；若有 severe depression、psychosis、suicidality、intimate partner violence 或 child safety concern，必須升級轉介。
- 對已有明確 developmental delay、regression、hearing / vision concern、neurological red flag 或 functional impairment 的 child，health-sector ECD support 只能作為 bridge，不可取代 EI / specialist referral。

## Fact

- WHO 2020 guideline 是 WHO 第一份 specifically related to early childhood development 的 guideline。
- Guideline 使用 systematic reviews 與 GDG deliberation 形成 recommendation。
- 四項 recommendation 都是 strong recommendation。
- Responsive caregiving 與 early learning 的定義在研究中不一致，且介入策略常有 overlap。
- 來源明確說研究沒有設計來比較哪一種 caregiving intervention 較好，因此 programme-to-programme comparison 要保守。
- Maternal mental health psychosocial interventions 主要改善 perinatal anxiety / depressive symptoms；child outcome measurement 較少。
- Socioemotional / behavioural caregiving intervention 被列為 future research priority，而不是 clinical recommendation。

## Inference

- 這份 guideline 的臨床意義不是增加一個獨立門診，而是把 first-3-years ECD content 放進既有健康、營養與照顧者支持接觸點。
- 對服務設計者，最小可行單位應是 provider-observe、model、coach、problem-solve、refer 的短迴圈，而不是單向 leaflet。
- 對臨床紀錄，最好把 responsive caregiving / early learning 寫成可觀察行為與練習內容。

## Assumption

- Health system 有足夠 training、supervision、time、space、referral map 與 culturally adapted materials。
- Caregiver mental health、basic needs、family safety 與 disability access 會被同步評估。

## Uncertainty

- Long-term educational、functional 與 participation outcomes 不足。
- 最有效的 active ingredient、dose、delivery platform、provider cadre 與 scale-up fidelity 仍未定。
- Outcome tools 的 reliability / validity 在不同 sociocultural contexts 仍是限制。
- 來源於 2020 年出版，且原文規劃五年 review；政策使用前應確認是否已有新版或補充 guidance。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：strong recommendation 等於所有 outcome 都 high-certainty。錯；部分 outcome certainty low 或 very low。
- 誤讀 2：nutrition 做好就自然改善 ECD。錯；來源支持 nutrition intervention 要結合 responsive care 與 early learning。
- 誤讀 3：maternal mental health support 直接等於 child developmental treatment。錯；maternal symptom evidence 較明確，child outcome evidence 較少。
- 誤讀 4：health worker 做一次衛教就完成 guideline。錯；guideline 支持的是有內容、有支持、有整合的服務設計。
