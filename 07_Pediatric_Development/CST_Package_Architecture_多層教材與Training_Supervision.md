# CST Package Architecture 多層教材與Training Supervision

## One-Sentence Definition

CST package architecture 是 WHO 把 caregiver-mediated intervention 做成可進入社區服務的多層設計：2-9 歲 developmental delays or disabilities 兒童的 caregivers，可透過 9 group sessions、3 home visits、adult-learning methods、trained non-specialist facilitators、supervision and stepped-care referral pathway 接受支持。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁只處理 WHO CST Introduction 對整個 package 的架構定義。
- 它不是 CST adaptation guide、facilitator manual、participants' guide、home visit manual 或 CST efficacy review。
- CST target population 是 caregivers of children aged 2-9 years with developmental delays or disabilities，尤其 social / communication delays or impairments；child 不必已有 formal diagnosis。
- CST 目標是提升 caregiver 在家中促進 child engagement、communication、positive behaviour and daily living skills 的能力。
- Package architecture 的核心不是一本教材，而是 course structure、home practice、home visits、facilitator training、ongoing supervision and referral boundary 的組合。

## Why It Matters

- 對等待 developmental evaluation 或 ASD diagnosis 的家庭，CST 的 no-diagnosis entry criterion 可能降低支持延遲。
- 但若把 CST 簡化成「發講義」或「上一次家長課」，就會移除它的主要 mechanism：caregiver practice、home routine embedding、coaching、supervision and stepped-care escalation。
- 對 service planning 而言，真正要確認的是 package 是否仍保留 training-supervision infrastructure，而不只是課程名稱是否叫 CST。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- Child 已被辨識出 developmental delay or disability；formal diagnosis 不是進入 CST 的必要條件。
- 需有 trained and supervised facilitators；來源明確把 CST 放在 non-specialist delivery 但 supervision-supported 的架構中。
- 需有可執行的 group sessions、home visits、home practice review and caregiver coaching。
- 需有 supervisor 或 master trainer 能處理 fidelity、group dynamics、home visit difficulties、additional family needs and urgent risk concerns。
- 需能在 community-based health / social services network 中處理 referral，例如 child health、caregiver mental health、suspected maltreatment、material deprivation 或更高層級 specialist services。

## Mechanism

```text
Developmental delay or disability is identified
-> child can enter CST without waiting for formal diagnosis
-> 9 group sessions teach shared strategies through adult learning
-> 3 home visits individualize goals, observe caregiver-child interaction, and coach practice
-> caregivers practise strategies in everyday routines
-> trained non-specialist facilitators receive supervision
-> families step up to additional or specialist services when needs exceed CST
```

## Observable Patterns

- Standard course structure 是 9 core group sessions 加 3 home visits。
- Group sessions 通常 2.5-3 hours，weekly or every two weeks。
- Home visits 排在 start、middle、end：session 1 前、sessions 5/6 之間、final group session 後。
- Home visits 通常 1.5-2 hours，通常在 caregiver home；若距離、安全或其他因素不適合，可在 community setting。
- 每個家庭通常可邀請 two caregivers 參與。
- Sessions 2-9 常見結構包括 wellness activity、key-message review、home-practice discussion、caregiver story、teaching activities、facilitator role-play demonstration、home-practice planning、caregiver role-play with coaching and feedback、review and close。
- Content sequence 由 engagement、play/home routines、communication、daily living skills、positive/challenging behaviour，走到 caregiver well-being、problem-solving and ongoing goals。
- Facilitator training 包含 staged classroom training、field experience、debriefing、advanced skills training、additional supervised co-delivery and ongoing supervision。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 臨床轉介 CST-like program 時，應確認：
  - 是否接受 developmental delay / disability 但尚未 formal diagnosis 的 children。
  - 是否保留 9 group sessions and 3 home visits。
  - facilitator 是否接受 CST-specific practical training and ongoing supervision。
  - 是否有 daily home practice、role-play、coaching and review。
  - 是否有 supervisor consultation and referral pathway 處理 caregiver mental health、child maltreatment、material deprivation or medical needs。
- 研究或 implementation reporting 應把 field-testing feasibility / acceptability 與 clinical efficacy 分開寫，不能把 Introduction 的可行性敘述過度外推成 definitive efficacy。

## Fact

- WHO 2022 CST Introduction 是 CST package 的第一部分。
- CST package 目標是增加 caregivers 促進 child development and well-being 的能力，透過 structured play and home routines 執行。
- CST intended audience 是 caregivers of children aged 2-9 years with developmental delays or disabilities，尤其 social / communication delays or impairments。
- Child 不必有 formal diagnosis 即可接受 CST。
- CST designed structure 是 9 core group sessions and 3 home visits。
- Group sessions 通常在 health facility、school 或 community-based facility 舉行。
- Home visits 分別在 group session 1 前、sessions 5/6 之間、final group session 後。
- CST designed to be delivered by trained non-specialists，並整合到 community-based health and social services network。
- CST 應作為 stepped care approach 的一部分：先提供最有效、least resource-intensive treatment，再依需要 step up to specialist services。
- Facilitator supervision 被描述為 essential，至少應在每次 home visit 後進行 formal check。
- Supervision 內容包括 child developmental competencies、target skills、caregiver goals、additional family needs、home visits/coaching difficulties、group dynamics、facilitator workload and facilitator self-care。
- 來源提到 field-testing in countries supported feasibility、acceptability、relevance、improved parenting skills and caregiver well-being。

## Inference

- CST 的 package-level active design 不是單一技巧，而是「group learning + home coaching + supervised delivery + routine-based practice」。
- No-diagnosis entry criterion 的臨床價值在於降低服務門檻；但它不能取代 formal developmental evaluation、medical assessment、speech-language evaluation 或 child protection pathway。
- Non-specialist delivery 是 scalability mechanism；若拿掉 training and supervision，就不是來源描述的 CST delivery model。
- Stepped care 的意思不是 CST 能處理所有複雜個案，而是 CST 可作為低門檻支持，並在需要時銜接 higher-intensity or specialist services。

## Assumption

- Local system 能辨識 developmental delay / disability，且有足夠 referral pathway 處理超出 CST 範圍的問題。
- Facilitators 有時間完成 training、home visits、travel、documentation、debriefing and supervision。
- Caregivers 能在家庭日常 routines 中找到可行練習機會，或能透過 service adaptation 降低 attendance and practice barriers。

## Uncertainty

- Introduction 沒有量化每個 package component 對 child outcome 的獨立效果。
- Field-testing 結果支持 feasibility and acceptability，但不能直接視為所有場域的 efficacy 證據。
- Virtual delivery、brief versions、additional modules and local adaptations 對 fidelity and outcomes 的影響需另行評估。
- 在 referral resources 稀缺的地區，stepped care pathway 可能被設計出來，但未必有足夠服務可承接。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：CST 只能等 ASD 或 GDD formal diagnosis 後才能做。錯；來源明確說 child does not need a diagnosis。
- 誤讀 2：CST 是一本給 caregiver 看的自學手冊。錯；來源定義的是包含 group sessions、home visits、training and supervision 的 package。
- 誤讀 3：Non-specialist delivery 代表 anyone can deliver CST after reading materials。錯；來源要求 facilitator training、field experience、debriefing and ongoing supervision。
- 誤讀 4：Home visits 可刪掉而仍維持同一個 CST package。錯；Introduction 把 3 home visits 放在 standard course structure 中。
- 誤讀 5：Field-testing 等於已證明所有 outcome。錯；本來源較直接支持 feasibility、acceptability、relevance and caregiver-related signals，不是完整 efficacy review。
