# Preschool EF Cognitive Training 與 Transfer

## One-Sentence Definition

Preschool EF cognitive training 是讓 3-6 歲 child 重複練習 working memory、inhibitory control、cognitive flexibility、planning 或 problem solving task；目前較能支持的是 EF task improvement，不是 broad learning / behavior treatment。

## Definition and Boundary

- 這裡只討論 child-facing cognitive training。
- 不包括 physical exercise、drama、art、curriculum-based EF program、mindfulness、neurofeedback、parent training 或 multimodal early intervention system；這些 broader preschool SR/EF approaches 另見 [[Preschool_SR_EF_Intervention_Design特徵]]。
- `near transfer` 指訓練目標與測量 EF domain 相同。
- `far EF transfer` 指轉移到未直接訓練的 EF task。
- `non-EF transfer` 指 learning、behavior、adaptive / social-emotional outcome；這需要獨立證據，不能由 EF task improvement 自動推出。

## Why It Matters

- preschool EF 仍在快速發展，EF components 可能比 school-age / adult 更不模組化。
- 因此，單一 EF practice 可能影響其他 EF task performance。
- 臨床上最容易出錯的地方，是把 EF task gain 說成「孩子學業、行為、ADHD 症狀都會改善」。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- child 年齡約在 3-6 歲。
- intervention 必須是明確 cognitive training，不是泛稱「益智活動」。
- outcome 要分清：
  - trained EF task
  - untrained EF task
  - learning / behavior / participation
- 若 child 有 sleep disorder、hearing / vision problem、language disorder、trauma、anxiety、ASD、ID 或 ADHD concern，需先做 broader developmental differential。

## Mechanism

```text
Preschool EF systems still overlapping
-> repeated cognitive task practice
-> improved trained EF performance
-> possible transfer to other EF tasks because preschool EF tasks share control demands
-> weak / unproven transfer to learning, behavior, or everyday participation unless separately measured
```

## Observable Patterns

- Overall EF task effect is low-to-medium positive.
- Near EF transfer and far EF transfer can both be significant.
- Non-EF outcomes may remain unchanged even when EF task outcomes improve.
- Group format may show larger effects than individual format in this source.
- Longer total training time matters more than simply counting sessions.
- ADHD-symptom and low-SES subgroups show larger point estimates, but evidence base is small.

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 可以把 EF cognitive training 視為 preschool EF task practice 的一種工具。
- 不應把它寫成 ADHD treatment、school readiness cure 或 behavior therapy substitute。
- 若目標是 classroom participation，仍要回到 [[Executive_Function_總論]]、[[Executive_Function_支持策略]]、sleep、language、parent / teacher scaffold 與 differential diagnosis。
- 若目標是在 preschool classroom 改善 SR/EF，Muir et al. 2023 顯示不能只看 cognitive training；movement、play、mindfulness、SEL、curricula 與 physical activity 都可能透過不同 design features 發揮作用。
- 若目標是 early learning 或 caregiving-mediated development，證據邊界應另見 [[Positive_Parenting_Interventions_對Early_Cognition與Language]]、[[Parenting_Interventions_生命前三年ECD與Caregiving_Outcomes]] 與 [[HCP_ECD_Interventions_由健康照護者交付的早期發展介入]]。

## Fact

- Scionti et al. 2020 納入 27 papers、32 studies、123 EF effect sizes。
- Muir et al. 2023 納入 85 preschool SR/EF intervention studies，顯示 digital task training 的效果較不一致，最一致的訊號偏向 working memory，而不是 global SR/EF transfer。
- Overall EF training effect: `g = 0.342`, 95% CI `0.252 to 0.451`, `p < 0.001`。
- Near EF transfer: `g = 0.352`, 95% CI `0.252 to 0.451`, `p < 0.001`。
- Far EF transfer: `g = 0.318`, 95% CI `0.186 to 0.449`, `p < 0.001`。
- Near vs far EF transfer difference was not significant: `p = 0.619`。
- Non-EF outcomes were not significant overall: `g = 0.169`, 95% CI `-0.047 to 0.383`, `p = 0.122`。
- ADHD-symptom subgroup: `g = 0.785`, 95% CI `0.451 to 1.120`，但只有少數研究。
- Low-SES subgroup: `g = 0.430`, 95% CI `0.219 to 0.641`。

## Inference

- preschool child 的 EF task transfer 可能比 older children 更容易出現，因為 EF components 尚未高度分化。
- 對 at-risk preschoolers，EF cognitive training 可作為 assessment-informed intervention 的其中一環，但需要更大 trial 才能變成強建議。

## Assumption

- 訓練有足夠 engagement、fidelity 與總練習時間。
- 測驗不是只測到 task familiarity 或 examiner effect。
- 家庭與學校環境沒有完全阻斷 carryover。

## Uncertainty

- 遠端 EF transfer 有多少來自真實能力變化，有多少來自 preschool EF task impurity，仍不清楚。
- Long-term maintenance 與 everyday function transfer 尚未確立。
- 對 ASD、language impairment、developmental coordination disorder 或 complex adversity child 的效果，本來源沒有足夠資料。

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不要把 `far transfer` 讀成「學業與行為都會變好」。
- 不要把 larger ADHD-symptom effect size 讀成 ADHD treatment standard。
- 不要把 computerized EF app 當成已證實優於非電腦訓練；本來源沒有支持這個結論。
- 不要把 broader SR/EF systematic review 讀成「任何 SR/EF 活動都有效」；來源強調 target、implementation、fidelity 與 follow-up 限制。
- 不要把 session number 當成唯一 dose；total minutes 和訓練品質才是需要追蹤的條件。
