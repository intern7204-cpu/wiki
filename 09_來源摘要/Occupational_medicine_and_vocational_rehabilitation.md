# Saby, Zappaterra, Aragaki — Occupational Medicine and Vocational Rehabilitation

## 一句話定義

這章把 occupational medicine 與 vocational rehabilitation 接到 PM&R 熟悉的語言上：**ICF、return to work、workplace modification、safety culture、work conditioning / hardening / FRP**。

## 核心機制

### 主要貢獻

### 1. return to work 被明確放進 biopsychosocial / ICF 框架

- work ability 不等於 injury healed。
- patient factor、job demand、employer support、policy、mental health 都會決定 outcome。

### 2. workplace intervention 不是附屬品

- early coordination、modified duty、schedule adjustment、ergonomics、assistive technology 都有實證基礎。
- 多領域介入通常比單一治療更能改善 work outcome。

### 3. 職場安全與制度面有完整骨架

- OSHA、hierarchy of controls、ergonomics、preplacement exam、ADA accommodation 都被整理進來。
- 這使本章不只是臨床治療，也涵蓋 systems practice。

### 4. job-specific rehabilitation 工具被放進實作路徑

- FCE、work conditioning、work hardening、FRP 的適應症與用途有清楚區分。

## 限制與未定論

### 限制 / caveat

- 章節著重美國制度與 workers' compensation 脈絡，跨國適用時要小心制度外推。
- 各州規範、payer rule 與 legal detail 仍需本地化。

### frontmatter contradictions

- Return to work is not determined solely by tissue healing or impairment severity; workplace context, accommodations, and psychosocial factors also shape outcome.
- Workers' compensation treatment guidelines standardize care but do not eliminate the need for individualized clinical reasoning and documented deviation when justified.
