## One-Sentence Summary

Rheumatologic rehabilitation should combine disease classification, joint-specific biomechanics, inflammatory activity, functional limitation, patient education, exercise, joint protection, orthoses, assistive devices, and selective modalities.

## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Rheumatic disease rehabilitation

#### One-Sentence Definition

Rheumatic disease rehabilitation is ICF-based management of pain, stiffness, inflammation, deformity, weakness, mobility, ADL, participation, and environment in immune and nonimmune musculoskeletal disease.

#### Known Facts

- Rheumatic diseases include immune-related and nonimmune-related musculoskeletal disorders.
- OA features include pain, limited morning stiffness, reduced function, crepitus, restricted movement, and bony hypertrophy.
- Symptomatic OA should mainly be based on clinical findings; imaging abnormalities may be asymptomatic.
- RA is chronic systemic inflammatory disease affecting joints and extraarticular tissues.
- Rehabilitation management includes detailed evaluation, patient education, exercise, orthoses, and physical modalities.
- Septic arthritis is included as a high-risk entity requiring diagnosis and management rather than routine rehabilitation framing.

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Inflammatory / degenerative / metabolic / infectious joint disease
-> pain, swelling, stiffness, weakness, deformity, ROM loss
-> gait / hand function / ADL / work / participation limitation
-> disease-specific medical care + rehab evaluation + exercise + joint protection + orthoses
-> improved function and reduced disability
```

#### Inferences

- Rehabilitation should not delay medical workup for inflammatory or infectious arthritis.
- Imaging should be interpreted against symptoms, exam, disease activity, and function.

#### Assumptions

- Diagnosis and disease activity are either known or being actively clarified with rheumatology / orthopedics.

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Biologic and targeted synthetic DMARDs evolve quickly.
- Prolotherapy / PRP / transarterial microembolism are described as promising or emerging but not established by most guideline bodies in the source.

## Clinically Useful Points

- OA management requires holistic assessment: joint distribution, pain severity, gait / ADL impact, falls risk, comorbidities, social support, expectations, previous treatment, and modifiable risk factors.
- RA rehab differs by acute versus chronic stage.
- Joint protection, assistive devices, adaptive equipment, environmental modification, and vocational rehabilitation are part of the rehabilitation plan.
- Red hot swollen joint should not be treated as routine OA flare until septic arthritis is considered.

## Research-Useful Points

- Outcomes should distinguish pain reduction, disease activity, structural progression, ADL, gait, hand function, work, and participation.
- Studies of procedures for OA should avoid conflating symptomatic relief with disease modification.
