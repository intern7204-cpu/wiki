## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: CPET in Pulmonary Hypertension

#### One-Sentence Definition

CPET in pulmonary hypertension 是透過 VE/VCO2 slope、PETCO2、VD/VT、peak VO2、VO2/work slope、O2 pulse、HR response 與 gas exchange pattern，非侵入性評估 pulmonary vascular disease 對 exercise limitation、risk 與 follow-up 的影響。

#### Known Facts

- PAH 是由 pulmonary arterioles remodeling、fibrosis、in situ thrombosis 等造成 PVR 增加，導致 RV afterload 增加與 right heart failure risk。
- RV-arterial coupling 是 PAH patients symptoms、clinical status 與 prognosis 的主要決定因子。
- Early PAH 的主要症狀可為 effort dyspnea；其機制是 RV 在 exercise 時無法在 increased afterload 下維持足夠 cardiac output。
- CPET 被來源描述為 assessing functional capacity and mechanisms of exercise limitation 的 gold standard，並可支持 early detection、differential diagnosis、prognostic stratification 與 follow-up。
- 來源指出 ESC/ERS PH guideline 強調 CPET 在 initial diagnostic phase 與 follow-up 的角色。
- PAH CPET abnormalities 可分成 ventilatory abnormalities、gas exchange abnormalities、cardiovascular abnormalities。
- PAH patients exercise 時可出現 increased dead space ventilation，VD/VT ratio >30%；healthy subjects exercise 時 VD/VT normally decreases。
- PAH 可有 high VE/VCO2 and VE/VO2 ratios；moderate PAH 中 ratios 可能 flat，severe PAH 中可增加。
- VE/VCO2 relationship slope 在 PAH 中增加；嚴重時可能 steep 到無法辨識 respiratory compensation point 的 normal upward deflection。
- PAH gas exchange abnormality 包含 reduced PETCO2 at rest and during exercise、low PaCO2、positive P(a-ET)CO2 that increases with effort、higher P(A-a)O2。
- PAH cardiovascular CPET abnormalities 包含 reduced peak VO2、AT、VO2/work slope、peak HR、O2 pulse。
- PAH 中 O2 pulse 常降低且 flattened；cardiac output rise during exercise 更依賴 HR increase，而非 stroke volume increase。
- At the same peak VO2 limitation，PAH patients compared with left HF patients have smaller SV response and steeper HR-VO2 slope。
- EOV is frequent in post-capillary PH / HF but not typical of PAH。
- In PH diagnostic phase，high VE/VCO2 and low PETCO2 increase likelihood of pulmonary vascular disease；both normal makes PH unlikely in the cited framework。
- In systemic sclerosis with PAH suspicion，one cited study found peak VO2 threshold 13.8 mL/kg/min had sensitivity 87.5% and specificity 74.8%；peak VO2 >18.7 mL/kg/min excluded PAH in that cohort。
- In CTEPH / CTED study，peak exercise VD/VT >45% had sensitivity 92% and specificity 83% for CTEPH；peak A-a O2 gradient >32 mmHg had sensitivity 92% and specificity 67%。
- The review quotes ESC/ERS risk categories: peak VO2 >15 mL/kg/min and VE/VCO2 slope <36 as low risk; peak VO2 11-15 and VE/VCO2 slope 36-44 as intermediate risk; peak VO2 <11 and VE/VCO2 slope >44 as high risk。
- Right-to-left shunt during exercise can be identified by abrupt sustained PETO2 increase, simultaneous PETCO2 decrease, RER / VE/VO2 / VE/VCO2 increase and pulse oximetry decline。
- Delayed HR recovery <18 beats/min in first post-exercise minute has prognostic value in PAH。
- CPET in clinical trials requires expertise and standardization；inadequate experience may weaken endpoint reliability。

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
pulmonary vascular remodeling / obstruction
  -> increased PVR and mPAP
  -> RV afterload increase
  -> impaired RV-arterial coupling during exercise
  -> limited stroke volume augmentation and CO reserve
  -> early tissue O2 delivery limitation and lactic acidosis
  -> increased ventilatory drive, dead space and V/Q mismatch
  -> high VE/VCO2 slope, low PETCO2, abnormal VD/VT, reduced peak VO2 and O2 pulse
  -> diagnostic clue, severity staging and prognostic stratification
```

#### Inferences

- In PAH, VE/VCO2 / PETCO2 abnormalities are not merely generic deconditioning markers；they map to pulmonary vascular resistance, dead space and RV-pulmonary artery coupling。
- The PH CPET pattern differs from HF: PAH tends toward very low PETCO2, high VE/VCO2, smaller SV reserve and HR-dependent CO augmentation；HF / post-capillary PH more often shows EOV。
- CPET can guide whether dyspnea should prompt RHC, pulmonary vascular work-up, follow-up observation or broader differential diagnosis, but it does not replace RHC for definitive PH diagnosis。

#### Assumptions

- Guideline-derived cutoffs quoted in this review need confirmation from ESC/ERS guideline before high-stakes treatment decisions。
- CPET diagnostic performance depends on standardized protocol and expert interpretation。
- PAH / PH subgroup matters: IPAH, SSc-PAH, CTD-PAH, CTEPH, PH-LHD, COPD-PH and IPF-PH do not share identical CPET meaning。

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Evidence for mPAP 20-25 mmHg treatment decisions remains insufficient；specific PAH therapy is still described as indicated for mPAP >=25 mmHg and PVR >3 WU in this source’s discussion。
- CPET can reduce unnecessary RHC in some workflows, but cannot replace RHC as diagnostic gold standard。
- Clinical trial use of CPET has been inconsistent when sites lack standardization / expertise。
- Sex-specific CPET prognostic parameters are based on small data in the source。

## Clinically Useful Points

- PH suspicion on CPET should focus on pattern: high VE/VCO2 slope, low PETCO2, increased VD/VT, low peak VO2, low O2 pulse and abnormal HR / O2 pulse kinetics。
- Normal VE/VCO2 / PETCO2 pattern lowers PH likelihood in the cited framework, but does not replace full clinical evaluation。
- In SSc / CTD, CPET may help decide RHC referral and reduce unnecessary procedures when integrated with screening algorithms。
- In post-PE persistent symptoms, VD/VT, A-a gradient, VE/VCO2 slope and PETCO2 can support CTEPH / CTED work-up。
- Low peak VO2 and high VE/VCO2 slope are risk stratification anchors in PAH follow-up。

## Research-Useful Points

- CPET should be integrated with invasive or noninvasive hemodynamic assessment to study RV-pulmonary artery coupling。
- Future studies are needed for borderline PH, especially mPAP 20-25 mmHg and high-risk groups such as SSc。
- CPET endpoints in PAH trials require standardized execution and expert interpretation。

## Conflicts With Existing Knowledge

- 與「PH CPET 只是 low VO2」衝突：來源強調 VE/VCO2、PETCO2、VD/VT、O2 pulse、HR response 與 gas exchange pattern。
- 與「PAH 和 HF 的 CPET pattern 可完全互換」衝突：來源指出 PAH 的 stroke volume response 更小、HR/VO2 slope 更陡，EOV 更偏 post-capillary PH / HF。
- 與「CPET 可以取代 RHC」衝突：來源將 CPET 放在 diagnostic support、likelihood stratification 與 follow-up，而非 definitive diagnosis。
