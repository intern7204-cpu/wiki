## Core Concepts Extracted

### Concept: Sports medicine and adaptive sports rehabilitation

#### One-Sentence Definition

Sports medicine rehabilitation restores sport participation by matching tissue diagnosis, biomechanics, training load, emergency planning, RTP criteria, and disability-specific equipment / injury patterns.

#### Known Facts

- Team physician duties include medical eligibility, injured athlete care, RTP, emergency preparedness, training program oversight, supervision, and liability protection.
- Event administration requires chain of command, emergency action plan, equipment, protocols, environmental assessment, and communication.
- Training principles include specificity, individuality, periodization, overload, and tapering.
- Athletic injury rehabilitation progresses from acute symptom control to recovery and then functional sport-specific RTP.
- Kinetic chain assessment looks beyond the painful tissue to flexibility, strength, proprioception, neuromuscular control, and sport mechanics.
- Adaptive sports require classification, equipment knowledge, and cause-of-disability injury patterns.

#### Mechanism Chain

```text
Sport demand + training load + biomechanics + tissue capacity + environment
-> injury or medical event
-> acute management + diagnosis + tissue healing phase + kinetic-chain rehab
-> functional progression + sport-specific mechanics + RTP decision
-> safe participation or modified participation
```

#### Inferences

- RTP clearance is a medical-risk decision, not just a therapy discharge milestone.
- Adaptive athletes need both sport-specific and impairment-specific surveillance.

#### Assumptions

- The clinician understands sport demands, athlete goals, event context, and relevant legal / organizational rules.

#### Uncertainties / Limitations

- Concussion protocols, transgender athlete eligibility, anti-doping restrictions, and PPE policy change over time.

## Clinically Useful Points

- PEACE and LOVE framing emphasizes education, optimal loading, vascularization, exercise, and caution with anti-inflammatory overuse.
- Overtraining syndrome involves sustained performance decrement, fatigue, mood disturbance, poor sleep, and increased illness / injury.
- Exercise-associated collapse has broad differential, including benign and life-threatening conditions.
- Wheelchair and limb-deficient athletes have disability-specific injury / equipment concerns.

## Research-Useful Points

- RTP research should measure reinjury, performance, mechanics, psychological readiness, and sport-specific workload, not only pain.
- Adaptive sports injury studies need classification and equipment variables.
