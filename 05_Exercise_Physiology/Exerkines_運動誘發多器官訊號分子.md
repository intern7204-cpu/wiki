# Exerkines 運動誘發多器官訊號分子

## One-Sentence Definition

Exerkines 是 acute 或 chronic exercise 後釋放或改變的 signalling moieties，透過 endocrine、paracrine 或 autocrine pathways 連接 skeletal muscle、heart、liver、adipose tissue、BAT、immune system、nervous system、bone 與其他 tissue。

## Definition and Boundary

- 本頁處理 exerkines 作為 exercise-induced inter-organ communication 的概念。
- 本頁不把 exerkines 寫成 routine clinical lab、補充品靶點或「exercise pill」。
- 本頁不取代 exercise prescription；運動處方仍要回到 FITT、specificity、safety、progression 與 patient goal。
- Myokines 是 skeletal muscle-derived exerkines；exerkines 比 myokines 更大。

## Why It Matters

- Exercise 的全身效益不能只靠 calories burned、VO2、lactate 或單一 organ 解釋。
- Exerkines 提供一個 systems-biology language，說明 exercise 如何同時影響 cardiometabolic health、inflammation、brain function、muscle remodeling、bone、immune surveillance 與 resilience。
- 這個概念也提醒研究者：若 exercise exposure、timing、sampling、diet、circadian state、fitness、sex、age、comorbidity 沒控制好，分子訊號很容易被誤讀。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 已明確定義 exercise exposure：acute vs chronic、aerobic vs resistance vs HIIT、intensity、duration、frequency、training status。
- 已明確記錄 sampling context：during / immediately after / 1-2 h after / resting state、fed-fasting state、post-exercise diet、circadian timing。
- 已區分 measurement target：plasma、tissue、extracellular vesicle cargo、RNA、protein、metabolite、lipid、epigenomic signal。
- 已區分 evidence layer：human、animal、cell model、association、causal mediation、therapeutic manipulation。

## Mechanism

```text
Exercise exposure
-> mechanical + metabolic + neural + inflammatory + vascular perturbation
-> multiple tissues release or alter signalling moieties
-> endocrine / paracrine / autocrine signalling
-> acute homeostasis: fuel use, cytokine balance, vascular tone, immune mobilization
-> chronic adaptation: metabolic flexibility, lower resting inflammation, tissue remodeling, fitness, healthspan / resilience signals
```

## Observable Patterns

- Acute exercise often produces transient cytokine and metabolic changes; IL-6、IL-8、IL-1RA、IL-10 are classic examples in the review.
- Chronic training can change resting humoral factors, but the direction may differ from acute exercise.
- IL-6 是 context-dependent：acute exercise-induced transient IL-6 不等於 chronic elevated resting IL-6。
- Extracellular vesicles may carry proteins、nucleic acids、lipids as exercise-related crosstalk signals, but collection and isolation methods strongly affect interpretation。
- Brain-related candidate pathways include BDNF、irisin / FNDC5、cathepsin B、adiponectin、kynurenine metabolism、GPLD1、clusterin-like signals, but BBB crossing and human causal pathways remain uncertain。
- Adipose / metabolic pathways include lipolysis、WAT browning hypotheses、12,13-diHOME、BAIBA、FGF21、TGFβ2、fetuin-A、follistatin、fractalkine and others, with uneven human validation。

## Clinical / Research Implication

- 臨床上，exerkines 目前最適合用來解釋 exercise as medicine 的多器官機制，不適合拿來當 routine test。
- 研究上，先定義 exposure，再解釋 signal；不要看到某分子上升或下降就直接寫成 health benefit。
- Translational claim 至少要問三件事：human 是否一致？acute / chronic 是否一致？這個 signal 是 mediator、marker，還是 epiphenomenon？

## Fact

- Chow et al. define exerkines as signalling moieties released in response to acute and/or chronic exercise acting through endocrine, paracrine, and/or autocrine pathways.
- The review states that exerkines can arise from skeletal muscle, heart, liver, WAT, BAT, nervous system, and other tissues.
- Myokines are skeletal muscle-derived exercise signals; they do not cover the full exerkine category.
- Exerkines can include cytokines, hormones, neurotransmitters, proteins, lipids, metabolites, nucleic acids, and extracellular-vesicle cargo.
- IL-6 is identified as the most extensively studied myokine since contracting muscle IL-6 release was demonstrated in 2000.
- Exercise response varies by timing, fed-fasting status, post-exercise diet, exercise mode, duration, intensity, underlying fitness, genetics, and phenotype.
- The review cites HERITAGE data: aerobic capacity response heritability around 47%, about 20% non-response for improved aerobic capacity, and 7-15% adverse response in selected cardiometabolic variables.
- Acute and chronic exerkine responses may not parallel each other.
- Animal and human exerkine responses may diverge.
- Omics platforms and extracellular-vesicle analysis are important but methodologically demanding.
- The review explicitly warns that “exercise in a pill” remains wishful thinking.

## Inference

- Exerkines should be treated as a research framework for multi-organ adaptation, not as a single causal pathway.
- A useful exercise mechanistic study should document exposure and sampling as carefully as outcome measures.
- Exerkine claims in clinical rehabilitation should be phrased as plausible mechanisms unless supported by human intervention and mediation evidence.

## Assumption

- The exercise signal being discussed was measured with reliable sampling and assay methods.
- The measured molecule has a plausible tissue source and target mechanism.
- Clinical translation requires evidence that manipulating the pathway changes a patient-relevant outcome.

## Uncertainty

- Which exerkines are causal mediators versus correlated markers remains unresolved for many pathways.
- The extent to which circulating exerkines cross the blood-brain barrier remains uncertain for multiple brain-related claims.
- Whether exercise-induced WAT browning is clinically meaningful in humans remains controversial.
- The optimal exercise type, dose, and timing to produce specific exerkine profiles remain incompletely defined.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 誤讀 1：找到 exerkine 就等於找到 exercise benefit 的主因。錯；association 不等於 mediation。
- 誤讀 2：myokine 和 exerkine 一樣。錯；myokine 只是 skeletal muscle source 的 subset。
- 誤讀 3：acute exercise 某分子上升，chronic training 就應該也上升。錯；兩者可能不同甚至相反。
- 誤讀 4：animal model 顯著，就可直接用在人類臨床。錯；來源明確把 animal-human inconsistency 列為 contentious question。
- 誤讀 5：exerkine therapeutics 已可取代 exercise。錯；來源只說有潛力，並明確指出 exercise in a pill 仍是 wishful thinking。
