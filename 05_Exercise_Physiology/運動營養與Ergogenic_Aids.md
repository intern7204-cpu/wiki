# 運動營養與Ergogenic Aids

## One-Sentence Definition

運動營養與 ergogenic aids 的核心不是「哪個補充品最強」，而是判斷目標運動的限制因子是否真的是該介入能改變的代謝、神經、緩衝、熱負荷或底物供應機制。

## Definition and Boundary

### What It Is

- 以 exercise intensity、duration、training status、event demand 與 limiting mechanism 為中心的運動營養 / 補充品判讀框架。
- 本頁目前主要根據 Hargreaves & Spriet 2020 的 muscle metabolism review 建立骨架。

### What It Is Not

- 不是 supplements 推薦清單。
- 不是臨床病人的飲食處方。
- 不是 antidoping、腎臟病、心血管疾病、兒童、孕婦或藥物交互作用的完整安全規範。

### Commonly Confused Concepts

- More fat oxidation ≠ better high-intensity performance。
- Lactate buffering ≠ lactate 是廢物。
- Acute performance benefit ≠ long-term training adaptation。
- Athletic ergogenic aid ≠ rehabilitation patient 可直接使用。

## Why It Matters

很多運動營養論述會把單一底物或補充品說成萬用答案；但實際上 performance limitation 可能來自 PCr availability、glycogen depletion、blood glucose、acidosis / ion handling、central drive、oxygen cost、heat stress 或 gastrointestinal tolerance。若限制因子判錯，補充品即使有生理效果，也可能不改善目標表現。

## Preconditions or Conditions

使用本框架前先定義：

1. 運動型態：single sprint、repeated sprint、2–4 min high-intensity、endurance、stop-and-go sport、resistance training。
2. 強度域：moderate、heavy、severe / supramaximal。
3. 時間尺度：秒、數十秒、數分鐘、60–90 min 以上、數小時。
4. 個體狀態：trained / untrained、sex、diet history、heat acclimation、medical risk。
5. 目標：acute performance、training adaptation、fatigue resistance、rehabilitation tolerance。

## Mechanism

```text
Event demand
→ dominant limitation: PCr / glycogen / blood glucose / acidosis / oxygen cost / central drive / heat strain
→ intervention target
→ benefit only if target matches limitation and safety context
```

## Observable Patterns

### Carbohydrate availability

- Carbohydrate is the key fuel for high-intensity exercise because it can support both aerobic and anaerobic ATP production quickly。
- Carbohydrate loading improves performance mainly in events longer than about 60–90 min。
- Carbohydrate ingestion during prolonged exercise maintains blood glucose and carbohydrate oxidation and delays fatigue, but does not prevent fatigue。

### Creatine

- Creatine monohydrate can increase total muscle creatine and PCr stores。
- Most relevant to repeated high-intensity exercise and resistance training contexts。
- Mechanism is improved rapid ATP resynthesis via increased PCr availability。

### Caffeine

- Lower-dose caffeine ergogenic effects may be mediated more through adenosine receptor effects in central and peripheral nervous system than through fat oxidation。
- High-dose caffeine can cause adverse effects such as anxiety、jitters、insomnia、GI discomfort。

### β-alanine and bicarbonate

- β-alanine increases carnosine and may enhance high-intensity performance by improving buffering capacity。
- Bicarbonate / alkalosis may help high-intensity exercise through extracellular buffering and ionic regulation。
- These are most relevant when acidosis / strong-ion disturbance contributes to fatigue.

### Nitrate / beetroot

- May reduce oxygen cost and improve muscle efficiency in some contexts。
- Effects appear less apparent in well-trained athletes。
- Mechanism may involve excitation–contraction coupling rather than simple mitochondrial efficiency improvement.

### High-fat / ketogenic / ketone approaches

- High-fat availability can increase fat oxidation and reduce carbohydrate oxidation, but high-intensity performance may suffer because rapid ATP provision still depends heavily on carbohydrate。
- Ketone ester performance evidence is mixed to negative in trained athletes in the source.

### Antioxidants and heat strategies

- ROS can be both signaling molecules for adaptation and contributors to fatigue when excessive。
- Antioxidant supplementation may be equivocal and may attenuate adaptation in some reports。
- Hyperthermia can impair performance via central neural drive、cardiovascular function、muscle metabolism、dehydration；acclimation, cooling, and fluid strategies target this mechanism.

## Clinical / Research Implication

### 臨床 / rehab 使用

- 對 rehabilitation patient，不應直接套 athletic supplement strategy。
- 若目標是提升 therapy tolerance，先確認限制因子是 nutrition inadequacy、cardiopulmonary limitation、medication effect、pain、sleep、deconditioning、neurologic impairment 還是 metabolic substrate issue。
- 有腎臟病、心血管病、arrhythmia、hypertension、pregnancy、pediatric、polypharmacy 時，supplement safety 必須另外查證。

### 運動表現 / 研究使用

- 每個 intervention 都應明確寫出：event、dose、timing、population、outcome、adverse effects、evidence strength。
- Acute biomarker change 不等於 chronic training benefit。

## Fact

- Hargreaves & Spriet 2020 將 regular training and nutrition 視為兩個主要提升 fatigue resistance 的策略。
- Carbohydrate loading benefits events longer than about 60–90 min。
- Creatine can increase total muscle creatine and PCr stores and support repeated high-intensity performance。
- Ketone ester evidence in trained male athletes from several laboratories did not support consistent performance benefit in the source。
- Lactate is not the main negative factor for force / power; acidosis and ionic disturbance are more relevant fatigue candidates。

## Inference

- Supplement decisions should begin with event physiology, not supplement popularity。
- “Training low” or low carbohydrate availability might have adaptation logic in selected contexts, but competition requiring high intensity usually requires carbohydrate availability.

## Assumption

- This page uses an athlete-oriented review as a physiology framework; clinical rehabilitation prescriptions require separate disease-specific evidence.

## Uncertainty

- Long-term safety and benefit of some supplements remain incomplete。
- Sex-specific and menstrual-cycle-specific metabolism data remain insufficient for many high-intensity / elite contexts。
- Effects differ by training status and baseline diet.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- Do not interpret this page as recommending supplements。
- Do not apply high-fat / ketogenic claims to high-intensity performance without checking event demand。
- Do not assume antioxidant supplementation is always beneficial; ROS are part of adaptation signaling。
- Do not equate ergogenic aid evidence in athletes with safety in medical populations.
