# Low-Intensity Endurance Training 健康成人證據邊界

## One-Sentence Definition

Low-intensity endurance training (LIT) 是結構化、主要低於 LT1 / VT1 或保守固定百分比錨點的 endurance training；在 healthy working-age sedentary / untrained adults 中，它能改善 aerobic fitness，並對部分 cardiometabolic risk markers 有小幅效果。

## Definition and Boundary

本頁只處理 Nuuttila et al. 2026 這篇 systematic review / meta-analysis。它不是 athlete endurance training guideline，也不是疾病族群的 rehabilitation prescription。

來源中的 LIT 定義：

- 優先：training exclusively below LT1 or VT1。
- 若未以 threshold 定義：<=60% VO2max、<=60% VO2 reserve、<=75% HRmax，或 <=60% HRR。
- 介入至少 3 週。
- 結構化 endurance training，不包含 resistance training、nutrition intervention、ergogenic modalities 或 pharmacologic agents。

適用族群：

- Healthy adults aged 18-65 years。
- 主要為 sedentary or untrained adults。
- 不包含 diagnosed disease、cardiometabolic medication、pregnancy、BMI >35 kg/m2 或 trained / competitive athletes。

## Why It Matters

LIT 常被兩種方向誤讀：一種說低強度太輕、沒有訓練效果；另一種說 Zone 2 / upper LIT 是所有健康與 mitochondrial adaptation 的最佳強度。Nuuttila et al. 2026 支持的中間結論是：LIT 對 untrained healthy adults 有效，尤其是 VO2max / Pmax；但精確強度、反應大小與 cardiometabolic effects 仍有明顯異質性。

## Preconditions or Conditions

- 使用前要先標示目標 outcome：VO2max、VT1、Pmax、blood pressure、lipids、glucose 或 adherence。
- 若目標是 VO2max，強度在 LIT domain 內不能完全忽略。
- 若目標是 cardiometabolic risk markers，不應期待大幅、穩定、單一 biomarker 改變。
- 若使用固定 %HRmax / %VO2max，必須承認可能錯估 LT1 / VT1。
- 若族群是 athlete、cardiac rehab、pulmonary disease、frailty、pregnancy、renal impairment 或 neurologic disease，本頁不可直接外推。

## Mechanism

```text
sedentary / untrained baseline
  -> structured low-intensity aerobic exposure
  -> repeatable oxygen transport and utilization demand
  -> central + peripheral aerobic adaptation
  -> VO2max / Pmax / VT1 improve with moderate-to-large pooled effects
  -> lipid and blood pressure markers may improve, but with smaller and heterogeneous effects
  -> higher intensity within LIT strengthens VO2max response
  -> without progression, early adaptations may plateau
```

## Observable Patterns

| Outcome | Source-supported pattern |
|---|---|
| Relative VO2max | Large effect; ES 0.94, high GRADE certainty |
| Absolute VO2max | Large effect; ES 0.84, high GRADE certainty |
| Pmax | Large effect; ES 1.09, high GRADE certainty |
| VT1 | Moderate effect; ES 0.74, moderate GRADE certainty |
| Total cholesterol | Small decrease; moderate GRADE certainty |
| LDL | Small decrease; moderate GRADE certainty |
| HDL | Small increase; low GRADE certainty |
| Triglycerides | Small decrease; low GRADE certainty |
| Blood pressure | Small decrease; DBP moderate, SBP low GRADE certainty |
| Glucose | Trivial effect |

## Clinical / Research Implication

- Clinical implication: for healthy sedentary / untrained adults, LIT is a reasonable entry-level aerobic training strategy when tolerability and adherence matter.
- Prescription implication: for VO2max improvement, consider progressing toward the upper LIT range if safe and tolerated, rather than keeping all work at the lowest possible intensity.
- Monitoring implication: use VO2max / submaximal threshold / functional capacity plus symptoms and adherence; do not judge success only by glucose or lipid change.
- Research implication: future trials need better LT1 / VT1 anchoring, adherence reporting, implemented intensity reporting, and background activity monitoring.

## Fact

- Nuuttila et al. 2026 is a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- The source included 50 studies after screening 9702 records.
- Intervention groups included 824 participants; control groups included 708 participants.
- Participants were healthy adults aged 18-65 years and mainly sedentary or untrained.
- None of the included studies reported results among trained or competitive athletes.
- Mean training frequency was 3.8 sessions/week.
- Mean session duration was 41 minutes.
- Mean intervention length was 12.2 weeks.
- Only one included study used LT1 or VT1 to determine exercise intensity.
- LIT improved VO2max, Pmax, VT1, total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides, SBP, and DBP compared with control, with trivial glucose effect.
- Higher LIT intensity was associated with greater VO2max improvement.
- Sensitivity analyses did not change the significance or magnitude of outcomes after excluding high-risk-of-bias studies or estimated-max intensity prescription studies.
- GRADE certainty was high for VO2max and Pmax, moderate for VT1 / total cholesterol / LDL / DBP, and low for HDL / glucose / triglycerides / SBP.

## Inference

- LIT should be described as effective but not maximal: it is enough to improve many untrained adults, but not necessarily the fastest or strongest stimulus for every outcome.
- The upper end of LIT may be a pragmatic target for improving VO2max when safety and recovery allow, but this does not prove Zone 2 as a universal optimum.
- Small cardiometabolic effects can still be clinically meaningful in aggregate, but individual biomarker response should be expected to vary.
- If a patient does not improve after initial LIT, the next question is progression, adherence, baseline activity, measurement reliability, and outcome choice rather than "exercise does not work."

## Assumption

- The fixed percentage anchors used by many included trials were intended to approximate sub-LT1 / sub-VT1 exercise.
- Structured LIT can be used as a feasible physical activity bridge for sedentary adults.
- In this evidence set, fitness outcomes are more robust and less biologically noisy than fasting cardiometabolic biomarkers.

## Uncertainty

- The exact minimum effective intensity below LT1 / VT1 was not established.
- Because only one study used LT1 / VT1 prescription, some included "LIT" protocols may not have stayed in the intended domain for all participants.
- The evidence does not define optimal progression strategy: intensity, volume, frequency, or combinations remain open questions.
- The evidence does not directly apply to athletes, frail older adults, chronic disease populations, cardiac / pulmonary rehab, pregnancy, or BMI >35 kg/m2.

## Limitations and Misreadings

- 不可把本來源寫成「Zone 2 是最佳或唯一有效強度」。
- 不可把本來源寫成 athlete training evidence。
- 不可把 fixed %HRmax / %VO2max 當成等同 LT1 / VT1。
- 不可把 glucose 沒有顯著改善外推成 LIT 沒有 cardiometabolic value。
- 不可忽略 heterogeneity、prediction intervals、risk of bias 與 adherence reporting 不足。
